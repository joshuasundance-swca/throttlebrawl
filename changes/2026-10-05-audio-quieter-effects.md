Effects are quieter by default. The Effects slider now starts at 70%, down from 90%, so the engine, hits, crashes and wind sit lower against the music and the bark voices. Before: master 80%, music 60%, effects 90%, voices 80% (the effects were 7.0 dB over the music and 2.0 dB over the voices). After: master 80%, music 60%, effects 70%, voices 80% (2.7 dB over the music and 2.3 dB under the voices, so the order is music, then effects, then voices). A saved level of exactly 90% for Effects moves to 70%, once. That is the old default, and most devices that have played hold it, because the settings record is saved whole (dismissing the what's-new card saves it), so without this the new default would reach new devices only. Any other saved Effects level stays as it was, and so do the other three sliders. After that one move the device's record is marked, so if you drag Effects back up to 90% yourself, it stays at 90%.

For devs: `src/save/index.ts` (`DEFAULT_SETTINGS.volumes.effects` 0.9 to 0.7; the new `effectsDefault: 0.7` field marks a record written by this build; `migrateEffects`, chained after `migrateRadio` in `load()`, moves a record with no marker and Effects at exactly 0.9 to the default, on load, and the next save writes the marker, so it never runs twice; sanitising alone never migrates), `src/audio/tuning.ts` (new `DEFAULT_VOLUMES`, frozen: the one starting level for audio's stand-in `index.ts` and its engine `system.ts`, which used to hold two copies of the literals; exported from `src/audio/index.ts`), `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/product-spec.md` (the default levels). The dB figures are the bus gains with the squared taper (0.9 squared is 0.81, 0.7 squared is 0.49: 4.4 dB lower), worked out in `src/app/default-mix.test.ts`, not heard or measured by ear. Tests: `src/save/save-mix.test.ts` (the defaults are what was set; a missing level takes the default; a saved level a build with the marker wrote, 90% included, loads as saved and is not rewritten; and the migration: a record with no marker and 90% loads as 70% with the other volumes unchanged, 0, 0.3, 0.7, 0.85, 0.95 and 1 stay, near-90% values stay, a 90% chosen after the move sticks through fresh loads, and a record this build saves carries `effectsDefault` 0.7), `src/audio/default-mix.test.ts` (the stand-in and the engine start from the constant), `src/app/default-mix.test.ts` (save and audio agree; the ladder; the old default as a negative control). `tests/e2e/audio-mix.spec.ts` now reads the record's defaults and holds the engine flat out within 4 dB over and 3 dB under the music (it was 1 to 8 dB over at the old default); it was not run on the dev machine (CI runs it), and its window is the old figure minus the 4.4 dB cut with margin, so it is the check to watch. The engine, cue and wind levels in `audio/tuning.ts` are unchanged. Not phone-verified, and not listened to.
