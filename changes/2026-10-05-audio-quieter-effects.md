Effects are quieter by default. The Effects slider now starts at 70%, down from 90%, so the engine, hits, crashes and wind sit lower against the music and the bark voices. Before: master 80%, music 60%, effects 90%, voices 80% (the effects were 7.0 dB over the music and 2.0 dB over the voices). After: master 80%, music 60%, effects 70%, voices 80% (2.7 dB over the music and 2.3 dB under the voices, so the order is music, then effects, then voices). Your saved levels are not touched: a device that has already saved its volumes keeps them, including a saved 90% for Effects. That is most devices that have played: the settings record is saved whole, and dismissing the what's-new card saves it. So on a phone that has played before, drag Effects down in Settings to hear this; only a new device, or a saved record with no Effects level, starts at 70%.

For devs: `src/save/index.ts` (`DEFAULT_SETTINGS.volumes.effects` 0.9 to 0.7), `src/audio/tuning.ts` (new `DEFAULT_VOLUMES`, frozen: the one starting level for audio's stand-in `index.ts` and its engine `system.ts`, which used to hold two copies of the literals; exported from `src/audio/index.ts`), `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/product-spec.md` (the default levels). The dB figures are the bus gains with the squared taper (0.9 squared is 0.81, 0.7 squared is 0.49: 4.4 dB lower), worked out in `src/app/default-mix.test.ts`, not heard or measured by ear. Tests: `src/save/save-mix.test.ts` (the defaults are what was set; a saved level, the old 90% included, loads as saved and is not rewritten; a missing level takes the default), `src/audio/default-mix.test.ts` (the stand-in and the engine start from the constant), `src/app/default-mix.test.ts` (save and audio agree; the ladder; the old default as a negative control). `tests/e2e/audio-mix.spec.ts` now reads the record's defaults and holds the engine flat out within 4 dB over and 3 dB under the music (it was 1 to 8 dB over at the old default); it was not run on the dev machine (CI runs it), and its window is the old figure minus the 4.4 dB cut with margin, so it is the check to watch. The engine, cue and wind levels in `audio/tuning.ts` are unchanged. Not phone-verified, and not listened to.
