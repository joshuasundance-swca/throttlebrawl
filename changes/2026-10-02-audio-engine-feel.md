The engine is quieter and livelier. It sits about 10 dB lower than before, a few dB over the music instead of drowning it (the Engine level slider goes higher if you miss it). Gear changes now sound like gear changes: the revs drop a step and the note dips for the clutch instead of whooping up from idle every time, and a downshift blips. Snapping the throttle open flares the revs, and shutting it at high revs pops and crackles the exhaust (the pops have their own slider).
