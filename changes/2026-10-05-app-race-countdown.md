Every race now starts with a 3, 2, 1, GO countdown. The riders, traffic and cops hold at the grid while a big number shows in the middle of the screen, with a short beep on each beat, then GO shows as the race starts. Anything you press on the grid is ignored; hold the throttle through GO if you want the jump. Pausing during the count stops it where it is. The beeps go through the effects volume, so they follow the quieter default mix and the Effects slider, and GO's sound is the one the race already played.

For devs: the hold is the app's, not the sim's (`src/app/countdown.ts`, stepped by the loop in `src/app/index.ts`): a held step is not a sim step, so the sim stays at tick 0 for everyone, nothing is recorded until GO, and replays and determinism are untouched. It counts in loop steps, 60 to a beat, so the lockstep seam drives it. `src/ui/countdown-view.ts` draws the number inside the HUD's road-ahead box (centred in it, so it covers no other HUD piece by construction), and `countBeat` is a new cue (`src/audio/`, `audio.countdownBeat`) a little softer and an octave under GO. Under the test flag it is off unless a spec sets `window.__countdown = true` (docs/architecture.md, "Testing seams"). `src/app/countdown.test.ts` pins the count in steps; `src/audio/countdown-cue.test.ts` pins the beep under GO; `tests/e2e/ui-menu-first.spec.ts` plays it: tick 0 and nothing recorded through 3, 2, 1, a press on the grid dropped, GO and the race running, and the number inside the screen and the road-ahead box and off every other HUD piece at 568x320, 640x360, 915x412 and 1366x768, with a negative control. Browser specs were not run on the dev machine (CI runs them). Not phone-verified.
