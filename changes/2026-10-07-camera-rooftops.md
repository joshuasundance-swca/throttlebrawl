The picture now agrees with a rider on a roof and a rider who leaves the course. On a roof beside a building front the chase and far cameras stay beside you instead of hanging off over the street, and no camera ends up inside a building or under a pitched roof's slope: a tower beside a low roof, or the slope behind you as you ride down one, pulls the camera in along its arm until it is clear, and a crash that leaves you under a balcony's slab brings the camera down under it. The helmet view leans out a little less toward a wall you are rubbing, so it never pokes through it. A crash on a roof is framed on the roof, not on the road far below it. Your shadow and the chalk mark of where you will land lie on the roof, tipped to a pitched roof's slope, and shrink to stay on it at an eave instead of floating in the air. A crashed rider's shadow falls on the roof he is over, not on the road inside the building. Leaving the course onto the ground, or off a low drop onto it, now says so in one plain line at the top, "OUT OF BOUNDS. BACK ON THE ROAD IN 4 SECONDS.", once a fall, in the same strip as the other notices, so it is never over a control. A splash into water keeps its gator or fisherman and a high drop stays a clean cut-away, with no line. For developers: the cameras read the race's structures plan as data (`CameraContext.structures`, `src/camera/solids.ts`, `src/camera/fronts.ts`), render reads it for the marks (`src/render/roof-fit.ts`; `Renderer.structures()` hands it on), the cue is `outOfBoundsLineFor` in `src/app/ticker-feed.ts`, and nothing the sim reads changed, so every race hashes as before. The fronts the plan does not hold (the row and painted houses, the gardens) keep the sidewalk rule, and a camera with no plan is the camera as it was. Not phone-verified.
