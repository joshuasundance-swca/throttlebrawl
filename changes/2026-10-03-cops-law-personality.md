Every region's cops now chase their own way. Sgt. Pruitt gets more relentless the longer he chases. Trooper Dalrymple finally rides: he waits on the long Keys bridges with a radar, and lets you go if you pass him under 65 mph. Deputy Lindqvist never rams you, but writes you a $75 citation for every 3 seconds he rides alongside, and the bill comes at the finish. Officer Meter chases on a pursuit budget, and gives up when it runs out ("Pursuit budget exhausted. Fiscal year resets in July."). Each region has an END OF JURISDICTION sign part-way along the race: cross it with the law on you and they pull over, and your heat drops to nothing. The cops now speak their lines.

In the career, every race now has the cop patrol and the heat meter, as free play already did, and crossing the END OF JURISDICTION sign wins a cop escape. Pacific Northwest roadblocks come more often: a cop trailing far behind you is now sent ahead to the roadblock.
