Three more of the Keys' places read at race speed. The cruise ship at the end of Duval Street is bigger, as big as the biggest ships that call at Key West, and carries what a cruise ship shows over a roofline: a funnel with a yellow band, a mast, rows of orange lifeboats and three coiling water slides. From down the street you now see a ship over the roofs, not a small white block. The shallow water between the islands is patched with darker seagrass and paler sand in bands you can see from the road, where it was one turquoise. And the ospreys' nesting posts are common along the shore and mangrove stretches, stand close to the road, and are taller with a bigger nest, so you find one every few hundred metres.

1. Ship. The model was already a real ship's size (290 by 36 m, 60 m high); the street was the problem: the chase camera is 4 m up and Old Town's fronts (6 to 9 m) hide a ship's lower 35 m, so only the top tier showed. The feature now says `scale` 1.25 (362.5 by 45 m; box s 1417 to 1779.5, d -82 to -37, so the stern is 72 m nearer and the hull 3 m past the pier's tip; `farM` 450), in the road file and in the bake config. New `render/cruise-ship-top.ts` makes the top deck in code (a funnel, a mast and radar, 18 lifeboats, 3 slides; 432 triangles, one more piece of the landmark layer, no new draw call), hooked in by `EXTRAS` in `landmarks.ts`. The test casts a ray through every 8th pixel against the ship and the street's fronts (their boxes): the ship fills 1.0 to 1.3 % of the screen at 500 m from the road's end and 4.3 to 7.2 % at 400 m on seeds 1 to 3, against 0.3 % and 0.7 to 0.8 % before. `cruise-ship.test.ts` asks the scale, the top deck and the funnel's height (81 m at the cap) and its old waterline and box checks pass at the new scale.
2. Flats. The patches were 160 m wide on a 56 m grid with faint tints, so 0 to 6 % of the views over the flats had both a darker and a paler band (a band: the 5th or 95th percentile of the sea's luminance against the untinted water's, below 0.88 or above 1.08). `SEA_BANDS` is now 60 m patches (a second octave 0.55 of that), a sand tint of 1.3, 1.2, 0.95 and a seagrass tint of 0.5, 0.72, 0.62, starting at noise 0.04, on a 28 m grid 32 squares each side: 74 to 75 % of the views (Seven Mile 64 of 87, Bahia Honda 27 of 36, three seeds, every 500 m) show both. The sea is 2,312 triangles (was 968), still one draw call. The lattice's tints are now kept between re-lays, so a re-lay costs a row and a column (0.3 to 0.6 ms in node, against 3 to 12 ms for the same walk over the plan at the finer grid); `Math.hypot` is out of the plan's inner loops.
