You can punch and kick. A tap punches the nearest rider straight away; drag off the button (or use U and O on the keyboard) to pick a side, and swipe down (or K) to kick. A kick has a proper wind-up and only lands if the rival is right alongside when it connects, and mashing the button won't launch everyone: after a kick there's a half-second breather, and pressing again in it throws a punch instead. A kick that lands shoves the rival sideways, and each hit you land or take freezes the action for a split second so it feels like it connected. Enough hits knock a rider off their bike. The hit-stop and knockback strength are sliders in the tuning panel.
