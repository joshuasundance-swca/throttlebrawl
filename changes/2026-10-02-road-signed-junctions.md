The junction choices are signed now. Before each region's shortcut (the Keys boat-ramp cut, the Pacific Northwest's Logging Spur, San Francisco's Stair Alley) a road sign on the right says where the split is and which side to keep to ("BOAT RAMP: KEEP RIGHT. Trailers only. Nobody checks."), so you can choose your way before you reach it. Every hand-made route now names its shortcut, which the race and the career map can show.
