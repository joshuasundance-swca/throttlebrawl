The junction choices are signed now. Before the Pacific Northwest's Logging Spur and San Francisco's Stair Alley, a road sign on the right says where the split is and which side to keep to ("LOGGING SPUR: KEEP RIGHT. Shorter by a hill. Longer by a log truck."), so you can choose your way before you reach it. The routes now name these two shortcuts, which the race and a later career map can show.
