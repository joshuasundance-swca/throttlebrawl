Three place fixes from the live check of the place work. Duval Street has a town behind its shopfronts and people on its sidewalks: where two shopfronts stood apart you used to see the sea behind them, and now you see ground and a second row of houses; and the party blocks have people standing on the pavement under the balconies, so they show in the picture all the way down a block at riding speed. Chuckanut Drive's bay is water as far as the map draws it: the water past the drop ran out kilometres short of the shore south of Larrabee, and the far view was green ground; it now follows the real shoreline. San Francisco's sea lions are on the waterfront and the piers (where the joke is), and the doodles are in the neighbourhoods; none stand at the Twin Peaks summit, the Hawk Hill overlook, or the top and bottom of Lombard Street. That last one is a default the maintainer may veto: say "cut this" and the sea lions go back to the waterfront-only list or out of the game.

For devs (playtest 4, run B's live check, punch items 4, 6 and 7; the identity sheets' D2, H1 and C8; `[default]`, not phone-verified, and no browser was run).

- **Duval's city** (`render/scenery.ts`, `render/road-mesh.ts`, `render/roadside.ts`). `WIDE_LAND_M` gains `oldtown: 72`: Old Town's land reaches 72 m past the sidewalk, not 24 m (the two streets are 143 m apart, so each strip ends where the other's begins; wider overlapped the other street's land at a different height, which `duval.test.ts` found at 100 m). A wide strip is not laid where a landmark stands beyond the usual strip (`landmarkBeyond`): the cruise ship's berth at the Gulf end stays water (`cruise-ship.test.ts` found it under ground at first). `RoadsideRule.behind` is a new option: a street front that stands only where the rules it names leave more than half of a building's length open. The Keys kit's last rule, `oldtown-back` (last, so no other rule's placements move), stands the Duval kit's conch houses 22 m past the sidewalk (`OLDTOWN_BACK_M`) behind the front row's gaps. A first-row check in `duval.test.ts` and `duval-street.test.ts` keeps to the front row; `portland-blocks.test.ts` allows the second wide theme.
- **Duval's pavement** (`render/balcony-crowd.ts`, `render/party-lights.ts`). `sidewalkWalkers` stands a group of two people every 7 m on each side of every party block, 0.55 to 1.15 m in from the pavement's back edge, facing the road: six boxes, 60 triangles each, in the balconies' own mesh (`WALKERS`, drawn within 100 m), so no draw call is added. They are scenery, never a hitbox; they stand at the back of the pavement, under the balconies, so a rider who rides the sidewalk against the wall passes through one (a thing to know, not to fix here). `PartyLights.walkers()` lists them; `revellers()` still lists only the balconies' people.
- **Chuckanut's bay** (`packs/region-pnw/assets/backdrop/pacific-northwest/region.json`). Checked first on main: #580 gave the bay side its drop and parapet, and `bellingham-bay`'s east edge was a straight line from (48.66, -122.495) to (48.6, -122.5), 0.3 to 4 km off the shore, so past the drop the far view was `floorColour` green. The floor's east edge now follows OpenStreetMap's `natural=coastline` (Overpass, 2026-10-06, simplified to 80 m, 63 points, chosen as the first tolerance whose outline has no crossing: the ear-clipping triangulation in `backdrop.test.ts` covers it exactly). `backdrop/chuckanut-bay.test.ts` asks it of the map's own shore (`backdrop/chuckanut-coast.json`, the same coastline simplified to 12 m, 290 points, 8 KB, ODbL like the bakes, and a row in `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md`): 321 of 321 sample points of the sea off the bay side (every 100 m of road, 150 m to 3 km out, 150 m or more seaward of the shore) are inside the floor, with the floor as it was 106 of 321 (the control); none of the 41 landward points and none of the 303 road points are. Not changed, and the reason this is not the whole answer: from the chase camera the sea first shows 206 to 382 m past the lip (median 303 m, computed from the baked road, the shelf's lip and a camera 2.6 m up) and the haze is full at 480 m, so the water is a fogged band 98 to 274 m deep. Two things would widen it, both the maintainer's look to decide: a narrower shelf (the lip nearer the road) or a longer haze on the bluff. I recommend asking on the phone before touching either.
- **San Francisco's strays** (`packs/region-sf`, `tools/road/tracks`, `tools/gis`). The region's `animals` list is the doodle alone, so the sea lion is a zones-only kind; the nine waterfront zones (the `sf-wf-*` roads and the pier row) name `kinds: [sea-lion, e-scooter-commuter, dog-walker-sf]`; the summit lookout, the Hawk Hill overlook, `lombard-top` and `lombard-bottom` name the two kinds of people. The generators (`sf-waterfront.ts`, `sf-hills.ts`) and the bake configs and networks carry the same words as the baked road files (edited by hand, not re-baked, as Duval's zones were in T10.4). `tests/sim/sf-animals.test.ts` reads the animals standing at the start of a race on each of nine routes and eight seeds: before, 131 of 171 sea lions stood off the waterfront and 21 animals in the four zones; now 0 and 0, with 69 sea lions on the waterfront (more than one waterfront road) and 204 doodles in the neighbourhoods (more than three zones), so the check can find both.
