Clinton, Iowa joins Quick Play with Rural Rush, a first race on the broad rural bends of Iowa Highway 136. Race the familiar regulars through ordinary traffic, with a fictional local patrol. This is a bounded first course, with one pass and no regional career yet.

The road follows OpenStreetMap geometry and USGS terrain. Lane widths, grass verges, roadside art and signs are gameplay adaptations rather than a survey of the real roadside.
