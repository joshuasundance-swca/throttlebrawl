Rivals are better in traffic: they finish going around a slower car before cutting back in, never steer into a car alongside, get out of the way of oncoming cars instead of braking in front of them, and give room to a faster car coming up behind. In 50 test races they bump into traffic about 40 % less often. Knocking a rival into a car is still very much allowed.
