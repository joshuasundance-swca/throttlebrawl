You can race the Pacific Northwest and San Francisco now. Pick one on the menu, above the Race button, then press Race. The first time, the region's road loads for a moment first. Each region has its own road, its local rivals and its local cop, and its own traffic: log trucks and elk in the Northwest, cable cars in San Francisco. The roadside signs and billboards now show in every region, the Keys included. A race in a new region still starts on the region's standard length, and its colours are still the Keys' until the renderer learns the region palettes. The locals' trash talk comes later too: for now only the regulars from the Keys talk.
