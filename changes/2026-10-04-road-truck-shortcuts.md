Two shortcuts you can only reach by jumping a ramp truck (playtest 3: "the static one could be used to get to shortcuts"). Ride up the parked truck, steer right in the air, and clear the low wall beside the road to land on a road of its own with no traffic and a boost pad. Miss the truck, or never steer, and you just stay on the main road. Rivals and cops never take them.
- Pacific Northwest, long race only: the **Mill Yard Cut**, a concrete haul road through the sawmill yard on the last of the Sawmill Flats, behind the sign "MILL YARD CUT-THROUGH. Authorised vehicles only. Airborne is authorised."
- San Francisco, downtown race: the **Plaza Cut**, a paved cut-through where the headquarters plaza opens on Campus Way, behind the sign "PLAZA ENTRANCE: DELIVERIES ONLY. Founders arrive by ramp." The car carrier is parked in the kerb lane.

Neither saves distance (both stand on straight road: the route calls them `alternate`), so the prize is the clear road and the pad, not metres; a bigger prize is the maintainer's call. Both signs are new and can be cut like any sign. Not seen on a phone or in a browser: the checks below ride the sim by ticks.

What changed, for the next lane:
- `tools/road/tracks/pnw-c1.ts` and `sf-downtown.ts` (and their re-baked pack files): the last road of each is cut into the road up to the truck, the split connector, the stretch beside the cut, the merge connector and the finishing piece, with the cut as a named branch (`aiTake: 0`, signed). The first piece keeps its id and its s, so the career's pins and checkpoints stay. The long PNW route and the SF route now finish on the new last piece (`pnw-sawmill-end`, `sf-dt-campus-end`). Two signs in the regions' files (`mill-cut`, `dt-plaza-cut`).
- Why the cuts look the way they do: the road lint wants a junction on the road it leaves, the sim's hand-over between overlapping roads would carry a rider on the ground across, and with the off-road switch on a rider on the ground reaches the verge. So the cut's reference line starts half a metre inside the road's edge with its lane starting on that line, the wall also lines the main road beside the cut for the first 155 m, and the cut is held parallel for that stretch with two `via` points. The docs say all this (architecture: jumpable walls; content-packs: route branches).
- Shared edits that ride along: one type line in `src/road/compile.ts` (a track's `named` branch may carry `aiTake`); the Seven Mile and other bakes are untouched. `tests/sim/road-setpieces-live.test.ts` now treats a pad or truck in no slot as a fixed piece (the seed never moves it, so it is no candidate). `src/render/downtown.test.ts` finds the headquarters' road as the route's finish road, not the last road in the list (the cut's roads now come after the avenue's). `tools/road/pnw-c1.test.ts` and `sf-downtown.test.ts` count the new truck, pad and sign and leave the cut's roads out of the avenue checks.
- New `tools/road/truck-shortcuts.test.ts`: on both networks, the structure (named, signed, `aiTake` 0, one shortcut lane, one pad, a truck in no slot, a zone past the lanes' edge, the wall's span), a rider on the ground never gets on (9 rides each), 24 flights at 24 to 42 m/s from three spots on the ramp, steering right from the lip or 8 ticks later, all land on the cut inside its lane and ride it back onto the road with no crash or wobble, a rider who does not steer or presses right only once down stays on the road, and no rival or cop is let in. Its first version failed on exactly the three traps above, so the checks can see them.
