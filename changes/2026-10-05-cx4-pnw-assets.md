The Pacific Northwest's own art for playtest 3 (Codex batch CX4), and Bridge City's Hawthorne lift towers now draw.

- **The atlas.** `packs/region-pnw/assets/textures/atlas/pacific-northwest.png` (12,563 bytes): 20 greyscale facade tiles (brick bonds, cast-iron arcades, ribbon windows, granite bands, steel-sash warehouse windows, timber siding, shingles, corrugated metal, stucco, transoms) and 23 picture tiles (eight food-cart fronts and eight storefronts with blank panels, four murals without words, truss, rivet and Gothic tracery panels). No lettering of any kind: `cx4-pnw.test.ts` fails the sheet if a tile draws glyphs.
- **Portland's landmark kit** (`models/landmarks/pdx-landmarks`, 49,220 bytes), placed by the game's generic landmark path (`pdx-landmarks#<node>`):
  - `pdx_lift_tower_lod0/1`: one end of a vertical-lift bridge as a portal over the road. Origin on the deck's centreline at the road surface; the towers stand outside the 19 m deck, nothing enters |x| < 10.5 m from 1.5 m under the deck to 30 m over it, sheaves at 36 m (`top_m`), piers down to 20 m (`foundation_m`). Bridge City's two Hawthorne features (T9.4) already name this node, so they draw now.
  - `pdx_truss_bay` (40 m) and `pdx_lift_span` (64 m): through-truss bays for the game's decks, clear of the road to 6.5 m.
  - `pdx_bascule_pier`: a river pier with an operator's house on each side of the deck.
  - `pdx_st_johns_tower_lod0/1`: a 125 m Gothic suspension tower from the waterline, its portal clear of a 62 m deck.
  - `pdx_roof_sign`: a three-storey brick building with a steel roof frame, a blank board and an invented neon leaping salmon bolted above it. The board's face is a text surface, `pdx_roof_sign_words`; no letter is baked anywhere.
  - `pdx_plaza`: a 56 m brick square with amphitheatre steps and a plain weather column topped by a rain cloud (not the real square's artwork).
- **The roof sign's words stay out of the model.** "STILL RAINING" (the maintainer's pick in round 3) is for the game to paint on `pdx_roof_sign_words`, so the in-game veto can cut it. It is not in a pack file yet: the landmark layer does not paint landmark text surfaces, and a region `signs` entry must carry a kicker under the board rule (`boards.test.ts`), which would add words to his pick. The change that paints the text chooses its home. No feature places the sign or the square yet either, so neither shows in a race until a render or content change does.
- **Downtown Portland** (`models/scenery/pdx-downtown`): a cast-iron front, a brick loft, a mid-century office block, pink granite tower modules that stack exactly (7 m base, 14 m mid, crown), three food carts with blank name boards (the names are pack text) and a bike rack. Nothing places them yet (T12.4).
- **PNW traffic**, at the pack types' own sizes: `pdx-streetcar` (20 m, two sections and a pantograph), `wagon-with-kayaks`, `camper-van` (with a pop-top and a canoe) and `log-truck` (a tall conventional cab, bunks and a pole trailer). They draw once the region's `traffic-models.json` names them.

The coordinator also redrew the sign's neon as open strokes: the closed loops twisted where the tube doubled back and, once welded, came out inside out.
