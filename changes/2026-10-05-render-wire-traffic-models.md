Traffic looks like its place. Portland's streetcar, the Pacific Northwest's log trucks, camper vans and kayak-topped wagons, San Francisco's driverless robotaxis and its startup shuttle, and Old Town's pedicabs are now real vehicle models instead of boxes and plain shapes. The Pacific Northwest's mossy wagons, its most common car, draw as a proper car too. Each keeps its own size, so nothing about how it drives or what it hits changes.

For devs: render-only data, no sim, save or replay change. `packs/region-pnw/assets/traffic-models.json` gains the log truck, camper van, kayak wagon, Portland streetcar (each its own model in the pack) and the mossy wagon (the shared sedan); `packs/region-sf/assets/traffic-models.json` gains the robotaxi and swaps the startup shuttle from the shared city bus to its own model; `packs/base/assets/traffic-models.json` gains the pedicab. Every model draws at 1.00 of its type's size on both axes except the mossy wagon (0.97). The draw cost holds: no traffic pool draws more than 2 calls over its boxes (Pacific Northwest whole mix +2, pdx-blocks and pdx-deck +1, San Francisco +2, the Keys' worst +2), so the composed peak is 105 of the 120 allowed (the test caps it at 106; it was 105 before). The mossy wagon taking the sedan is what keeps the Pacific Northwest whole mix at +2 (the camper van's model is the one more call); the motorhome keeps its shape. Not wired, because no row can take them yet (waived by name in `src/render/vehicles.test.ts`, which now fails on any built vehicle model that no row draws and no waiver explains): the Keys tour tram (18 m, against the island tram's 7.5 m, which cannot grow because the rival AI sizes every vehicle by the race's largest; it needs a short tram model), the semi, the SUV, the San Francisco streetcar and the trolleybus (no such traffic types exist), and the city bus (nothing uses it now the shuttle has its own). The test helpers now find a model in any pack (a region's own sit in its pack). `docs/content-packs.md` and `docs/architecture.md` say so. Not phone-verified; no browser was run. The sizes and draw counts come from `vehicles.test.ts` and `draw-headroom.test.ts` over the committed models.
