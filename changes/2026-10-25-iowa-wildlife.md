Iowa's IA 136 race now has wildlife. A deer crossing sign warns of whitetail deer on the West Bends, and there are more on the East Bends. A wild turkey sign comes before a flock on the Middle Bends, and more turkeys work the county dirt road on the detour. Deer step clear in their own time. Hit one at speed and you crash, as with the PNW elk; a slow bump is only a wobble. Turkeys scatter. A landing that pays in Iowa (the county stunt carrier, for one) now puts an Iowa one-liner on the ticker, where it showed none before.

The deer and turkeys use the existing dog and bird shapes at their own size and colours. They stand only in these authored roadside spots. Eagle Point's routes and the county farmsteads are unchanged.

For developers: the IA 136 delivery-truck ride test now has a 120 s time limit, as the county rides do. The animals added time to its five races and replay: 98 s on CI against the 90 s default.
