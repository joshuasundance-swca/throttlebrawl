The career's rivals now level up as you climb. Every career race takes its field from its tier: the pace follows the best bike open at that tier, rivals ride that bike (or the one below it in a region's first tiers), and they get tougher, hit harder, fight more and use their signature move more often with each tier. The cops ride the same bike, capped just under its top speed so you can always outrun them on a straight, and their fines (and a citation habit's cash) climb with the tier. Buying a bike never makes the field easier. From Season 2 a node's remix applies too: a new time of day, sometimes the long length, a redrawn field, some events swapped between a race, a hunt and an escape, the tier bosses handed to the rivals you wronged most.

The map has a Start Season card once every region boss has fallen: one tap starts the next season (it resets the maps and keeps your cash, garage, paints and grudges). The garage has a New career button (two taps, so a stray one does nothing): it keeps the old career as a backup code, listed under "Kept careers" with Show code and Restore. Restoring, or loading any code, keeps the career it replaces as a backup of its own, so no career is lost; the newest 3 are kept. A career with no race run yet has nothing to keep, so New career does nothing there.

For devs: `RaceSetup.fieldLevel` and `RaceSetup.eventPatch` are applied in `buildSimConfig` (`src/app/config.ts`): `withEventPatch` patches the event before anything reads it (field, time of day, modifiers, prizes, length), then the level sets `event.paceMps`, the rival and cop bike, rival `healthMax`/`power`, the cops' top-speed cap, `law.fineCash` and a habit's `cashEach`, and `event.level`. A race given neither builds the config it always built. `withEventPatch` repeats the career's `applyEventPatch` because app/ cannot import it in first-load code; `tests/sim/app-field-level.test.ts` holds the two equal on every Keys node. `career-flow.ts` gains `careerRaceSetup`, `startNewCareer` and `restoreCareer`; `CareerCallbacks` gains `onStartSeason` and `onNewCareer` (plain buttons; the career UI task dresses them). `tests/sim/career-harness.ts` still builds Season 1 races without a field level. Not phone-verified; the buttons were not driven in a browser.
