Takedowns. Knock a rider off within two seconds of hitting them, into a car, a wall or out of health, and it counts as your takedown, with its style cash. A takedown into traffic or scenery that involves you plays a short slow-motion beat (under a second, and you can still steer), at most once every eight seconds; the slow-motion setting turns it off. Steering into someone as you kick now sends them further, up to about a metre and a half more. Every number is a slider in the tuning panel's combat group.
