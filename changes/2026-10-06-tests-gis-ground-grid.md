The unit tier's slowest file, `tools/gis/region-routes.test.ts`, runs in about a third of its time and checks the same things (the maintainer: "Why do our tests and everything take so long?"). Its scenery check builds each real road's scene for 10 seeds and asks what lies straight under every footprint point of every land spot. Until now each point cast a three.js Raycaster through the chunk meshes around it, and the Raycaster tests every triangle of every mesh near its ray. On a seed with 200 land spots or more, the test now reads that seed's ground triangles into a grid of 8 m cells once (world corners in one Float64Array) and tests only the triangles in the point's cell. It uses three's own `Ray.intersectTriangle`, culls back faces as the Raycaster does and keeps the highest hit within the same 400 m. Seeds with fewer spots (Seven Mile has 89 a seed, the Golden Gate 25) still use the Raycaster, and a seed with no land spots skips the lookup. Every seed builds its own grid, because the ground meshes differ by seed (Samish, the Gorge, Russian Hill and Key West each draw 4 different sets over the 10 seeds).

What protects it:
- **Same answers.** Before the switch, both lookups ran on all 11 networks and on the Gorge negative control. They found identical spot counts and identical off-land lists. Example: the Gorge control flagged 151 spots and 76 far-forest spots with both. The comparison ran again after main brought San Francisco's apartments (#577), and the answers were still identical. With the planted bug below in place, they also agreed: Gorge 82, Samish 40, Portland 1198, Russian Hill 6 and Twin Peaks 78 off-land with both. That comparison was then removed.
- **Planted bug.** The bug: the scatter ignores the bridge tags (it treats the `none` theme that a bridge leaves as forest and skips the land reach). The old test failed on the Gorge, Samish, Portland, Russian Hill and Twin Peaks, and the new one fails on the same five, with the same 42 flagged lines in its failure output: 25 caught by the bridge check and 17 by the ray finding `road-water`.
- **New negative control.** Without one, a grid that answered `road-land` everywhere would pass every clean scene. The new control walks Russian Hill's roads at 1,520 points, on the road and 5 m and 40 m past each verge. The grid must name the same surface as the Raycaster at every point and must find the road and the sea as well as land. It fails both on a grid that always answers land (332 points differ) and on one that keeps the lowest hit (1,477 differ).

Time on the dev machine, the file alone with 2 workers, on main with this change merged: 96.4 s before, 35.3 s after. The lookups alone (all 11 networks, 10 seeds, the grid build included) took 75.0 s with the Raycaster and 4.8 s as now chosen per seed (measured before the merge). `tests/timings.json` now gives this file 87.1 s instead of 237.8 s. That number is an estimate: main's measured CI time scaled by the local before-and-after ratio (237.8 x 35.3 / 96.4). The next timing refresh replaces it with a measured one. No rule, seed, network or threshold changed.
