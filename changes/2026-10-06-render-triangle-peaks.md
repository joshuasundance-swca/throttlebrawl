Russian Hill and the lake drive off I-5 by Lake Samish drew too much at their busiest moments: the playtest's live check counted up to 158,360 triangles in a frame against the game's limit of 150,000, the kind of frame where a phone stutters. Both now sit well inside it, and so does every other road, without taking away anything you see: the ground beside the road, the road's own surface and its painted lines are drawn with fewer, longer pieces where they run straight and even, and distant blocks of houses switch to their simple far shapes where they were always meant to. And on Russian Hill, the stretches of open lawn on California Street, Columbus, Union and by the street signs on Hyde are row houses again, standing just behind the people on the sidewalk (the same goes for Lombard Street, Twin Peaks and the invented hill streets).

For devs (playtest 4 run B's live check, mustFix 1 and punch item 5).

- **The check now finds each route's peak view.** The budget was held only at fixed checkpoints: the browser specs' three ticks of one seeded race, and `scene-cost.test.ts`'s chase camera every 25 m one way. Now `scene-cost.test.ts`, `scene-cost-sf.test.ts` and `scene-cost-pnw.test.ts` (one per pack's routes, sharing `scene-cost.test-util.ts`, so CI runs them side by side) sweep every 5 m of every road a route allows, main path and branches, in both directions, from six views at their widest: the phone's low chase and far chase at the top-speed field of view, the helmet, the look-back and the takedown framing to each side (`camera/index.ts`'s defaults, read as text, since render may not import camera). The still scene now also counts the backdrop, the region's staged scenes, the boards, and the roadside as the renderer builds it (off the scenes' and landmarks' ground, with Lake Samish's docks). The share is unchanged: 110,000 triangles and 80 draw calls of still scene in every view. The helmet view's draw calls are printed, not held (81 on Key West's Smathers Beach and 82 on the Gorge's Crown Point loops, 1 and 2 over the share; about 116 of 120 with what moves); a follow-up.
- **It would have failed both places.** On the code before this change the sweep counted 131,505 still triangles on Russian Hill (Leavenworth looking back) and 121,957 on I-5 (the East Shore drive, the takedown framing), against 110,000; the old 25 m chase checkpoints counted 100,151 and 99,172, under it. At run B's real-time takedown frame on Hyde Street (153,442 triangles) the sweep's takedown view counted 120,000 to 122,000 still, so what moved cost about 33,000 of the 40,000 the share leaves. A negative control puts a 151,600-triangle block 40 m behind the Seven Mile's start: the old checkpoints peak at 33,528 and never see it; the sweep peaks at 175,812.
- **What was cut, all of it drawn the same.** `geometry.ts` `keptSamples` keeps only the samples a strip's shape needs: a sample is left out where every point of it lies within 1 cm of the straight strip between its kept neighbours, and the colour (or which surfaces a row has) is the same. It is used by the ground band (`verge.ts`, under the 1.5 cm between the band and the land), the painted lines and cable slots (`road-mesh.ts`, floating 2.8 to 3 cm over the road), and a plain road's lanes and shoulders, all on the same kept samples so their shared edges still meet vertex for vertex (a road with a shortcut or a gore keeps every sample). The land is untouched: its strip meets its skirt vertex for vertex. And `scenery-merge.ts` switches a block to its far stand-ins once its middle is half a block past `render.sceneryLodM`, not its nearest edge: a 160 m block of terraces drew every house in full out to about 330 m.
- **Houses behind the sidewalk (punch item 5).** A pedestrian zone on a city street (Nob Hill's people on California Street, d 5.6 to 12.6 m) or a board on the sidewalk covers the line the terraces' fronts stand on (8.7 m), and the scatter kept every house off it and 3 m round it: 40 to 100 m of lawn. Now a house there stands 0.3 m behind the zone's or board's far edge (`scenery.ts`, `zoneEdge` from `road-mesh.ts`), and no building ever stands inside one, so no pedestrian is drawn in a house. Only where a zone or board reaches the fronts' line; elsewhere nothing moves. New test `terrace-sidewalk.test.ts` on every San Francisco network with row houses, seeds 1 and 3; without the fix it fails ("osm-sf-california nob-hill has open lawn: expected 0 to be greater than or equal to 6").
- **Headroom, the still scene's worst view (`scene-cost*.test.ts`, seed 1), before and after:** Russian Hill 131,505 to 104,019 triangles (5,981 under the share) and 77 draw calls (78 in the helmet view); I-5 121,957 to 83,029 (26,971 under) and 70 (72); Gorge 114,393 to 93,639; Twin Peaks 112,741 to 85,899; the rest 56,375 to 88,064 (Key West 99,048 to 74,332). Every route draws 77 calls or fewer in the held views (23 routes). Frame estimate with what moves: Russian Hill about 137,000 of 150,000.

Also run: `scenery-merge`, `verge`, `verge-hairpins`, `verge-railing`, `scenery`, `sf-city-kit`, `road-chunks`, `draw-headroom`, `render`, `feel`, `land-seam`, `gap-ends`, `brick-road`, `sf-land`, `road-split`, `speed-cues`, `junction-sweep`, `interstate-roadside`, `staging-deck`, `waterfront`, `chinatown-northbeach`, `gorge-identity`, `bridge-supports`, `road-barriers`, `pnw-shore`, `duval-street`, `landmarks-sf-identity`, `sf-headlands`, `mission`, `barrier-looks`; typecheck; lint.
