On a phone, the first tap now asks for fullscreen, turns the game sideways and starts the sound, and the game keeps the screen sideways when you come back to fullscreen. If your phone won't lock sideways, a "turn your phone sideways" screen holds the race until you do. Locking the phone or switching apps pauses the race, and leaving fullscreen pauses it until your next tap. Long-pressing no longer pops up the browser's menu, and pulling down no longer reloads the page.
