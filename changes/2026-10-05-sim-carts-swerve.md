Golf carts now swerve out of the way. A rider coming up fast behind a golf cart (or the Stump Social marshal's cart in the Pacific Northwest) used to see it shuffle about 5 cm, which nobody could see, and then plough into it. Now, where the sidewalk or verge beside the road has room for it, the cart pulls right off the road onto it, as the bicycles and scooters already did, and rolls back to its line once you are past. Where there is no room (Duval Street's narrow kerb, the Seven Mile bridge's rail) it still hugs the edge, as before. A solid rear-end into a cart is still a crash: this only gets it out of the way, it does not make it soft.

Why: the maintainer's playtest-4 note, "golf carts and similar things should swerve out of the way", and his answer for the cart you still clip: "Keep it a crash". Only two vehicle types are meant by "golf carts and similar" (the Keys golf cart and the Pacific Northwest marshal's cart): every other slow vehicle is on rails, driverless or a set piece.

Numbers: ten seeded Key West races (spring break, the dev bot, the rivals and the cops all counted): crashes into a golf cart 3 with the dodge switched off, 0 now; 217 carts met. The audit's fifteen-race count of the old rules was 9 cart contacts, 4 of them crashes. Not phone-verified.

For devs: `src/sim/traffic/kerb-yield.ts` splits `isLightKerb` into `takesVerge` (every kerb type, wherever the verge is at least its width plus 0.4 m) and `softContact` (unchanged: 0.8 m wide or less), and adds `vergeOffsetFor`, which keeps a kerb rider's outer edge inside the smashables' line (`propLineM`, 0.9 m, asserted equal to `SMASH.gapM`): the light types ride exactly where they did (0.2 m past the edge), the 1.3 m cart rides with its outer edge on the line, 0.4 m of it still over the road's edge, about 1 m further out than before on a Keys shoulder. A rider dead on the cart's own line at speed can still graze it as it passes (a wobble, as for any vehicle), but no longer meets it end-on. `traffic.kerbYield` 0 still turns the dodge off; no new key, no RNG draw. Seeded Keys races change (the cart moves), which only the live bands see: `src/sim/traffic/kerb-yield.test.ts` (a shoulder-road scenario at 20 and 35 m/s, on the cart's line and on the shoulder's inner half, with the rear-end still a crash with the dodge off) and `tests/sim/traffic-cart-live.test.ts` (ten Key West seeds, crashes at most 2 and never above the dodge-off count, before and after printed). The other Keys-seeded sim tests (`keys-traffic`, `career-headless-keys`, `career-ladder`, `keys-long-race`) pass unchanged. Not checked: how a cart on the verge looks beside props and waiting pedestrians (no render run), and the Stump Social marshal (same code path, not raced).
