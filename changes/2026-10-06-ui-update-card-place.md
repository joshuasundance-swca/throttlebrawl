The "game was updated" card on a result screen no longer covers the result. On the career result at phone size it sat over the title ("5TH OF 5. CLEARED.") so you could not read it. It is now the first line of the result screen itself: it takes its own room, scrolls away with the screen and lies over no title, button or road, on the race result and the career result alike, at every Text size. Its words are also exact now: "It reloads when you go back to the menu" after a race, and "when you go to the map" after a career race (Race again, Retry and Next never reload; the old "when you leave this screen" was wrong for them). The offline notes now say plainly that a very first visit needs the network until the game has saved itself (41 to 52 seconds on the dev machine).

For devs: `placeReloadOffer` in `src/ui/index.ts` seats `#reload-offer` as the first child of `#results` or `#career-results` (the career's result rebuilds its children, so it re-seats after `showResults`), and the card lost its `.notice` top-centre layer; `reloadOfferText` in `src/ui/format.ts` holds the words, and `src/app/states.test.ts` holds that going back is the only event out of a result that reloads. `window.__reloadOffer = true` (test flag only, `testReloadOffer` in `src/app/index.ts`, listed in `docs/architecture.md`) stands in for a deploy. `tests/e2e/ui-reload-offer.spec.ts` measures the card on both result screens at 915x412, 854x480, 640x360 and 568x320, at the three Text sizes, scrolled to the top, middle and end (no word, control, title or card under it, not fixed, sticky or absolute, every control hittable), with a negative control that fixes the card at the top again and must name the covered title. `docs/engineering.md` ("Offline") is updated. Not phone-verified; the browser spec runs in CI only (no local browser, per AGENTS.md).
