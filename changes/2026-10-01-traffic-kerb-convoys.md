Traffic learns some local habits. Bicycles, e-bikes, scooters and golf carts ride at the kerb (on the shoulder where there is room), and cars pass them instead of queuing behind. E-scooters weave as they go. RVs can travel in convoys of up to four, nose to tail. Some cars never change lanes at all. The new regional vehicles that use these arrive in the region packs next.
