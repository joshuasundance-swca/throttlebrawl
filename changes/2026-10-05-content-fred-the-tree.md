Fred the Tree now grows on the old Seven Mile Bridge. He is the real little pine that sprouted from a crack in the old deck, probably from a bird's dropping, and has outlasted a good few hurricanes; a younger one has come up beside him, and the locals hang lights on both of them every year, so he wears a few. Ride the old road west of Moser Channel and he stands on the side facing the new highway, a little over five metres tall against the sea, where he is easy to spot at riding speed and from the highway too. He is outside the rail, so he is something to wave at, never something to hit. Our thanks to the real Fred and his Elves; the reference is the Wikipedia article "Fred the Tree" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_the_Tree), which gives where he grows (24.6896 N, 81.2048 W), what he is (a casuarina) and his story.

For devs: a code-made landmark, `keys-landmarks#fred_the_tree`, through T11.2's landmark path: `src/render/fred.ts` (new, a flat-shaded triangle soup of about 220 triangles: the ledge, the trunk, three crown tiers, the sapling, the lights) and a composite in `src/render/landmarks.ts` (`COMPOSITES`, plus `MeshBuilder.addSoup`), so no model file, no Blender and nothing in `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md` (original work; the maintainer's reference is credited above). Placed by the bake: `tools/gis/networks/osm-keys-seven-mile.json`, line `old`, a `landmarks` entry `fred-the-tree` (the real point moved 6.4 m south, which is where the bake measures the box's `d`, because the bake takes `d` from the point). Re-baked with `tbgis network`; of the 14 Seven Mile road files only `osm-sm-old-west.json` (the feature) and the network's notes changed, and the other roads' files and the sources' timestamps were left as committed. The box is 4 by 1 m at d -6.4 (the shoulder ends at 5.5), so the `landmark-clear` lint passes without `overRoad`, and the sim never meets it. One piece, one level out to 820 m, inside the layer's one draw call. Tests: `src/render/landmarks-fred.test.ts` (new: height band, unit normals, nothing over the drive lanes, only crown 2 m or more up over the shoulder, drawn in the one call and gone past 820 m) and a case in `tools/gis/routes-keys-pt3.test.ts` (the real station, the highway side, clear of every lane, small). Also run: `src/render/landmarks.test.ts` and `tools/gis/region-routes.test.ts` (the road lint, the land and scenery sweeps). The Seven Mile now fetches the 17 KB `keys-landmarks` kit with its race (the composite reads none of its nodes, but a placement needs its kit loaded); that is a lazy chunk, nothing in the first load.
