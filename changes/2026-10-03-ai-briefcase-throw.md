Rivals who pick up Kevin's briefcase now throw it. Before, a rival threw it only when you were close enough to punch, so most of them rode the whole race carrying it: in 73 live races rivals threw it 3 times. Now a rival with the briefcase throws it soon after you (or another rival) are within its throw range, up to 14 m ahead of it. In the test races half the throws came within 1.8 s, and the slowest took 4.2 s. The more aggressive the rival and the harder the difficulty, the sooner it comes. Their personalities still decide: Deacon Vane won't throw while he is calm, Chad Speedwell only throws when it looks good, and The Mayor rides away instead once his fight has turned. Cops are still never a rival's target. A rival beside or behind you kicks instead of wasting the throw. Races where no rival holds the briefcase play exactly as before.
