The Keys' career now races on the real streets. In the first tier, Rental Return becomes Last Light on Duval: Key West's Duval Street at dusk, through the cruise-ship crowd, the pedicabs, an island tour tram and a lot of roosters, with Mallory Square's pier at the finish where you can finally see it. In the third tier, Speed Monitored by Aircraft moves onto the Seven Mile Bridge at noon: two cops are on you, and the old bridge is how you shake them. The law stays on the highway, so ride the old road over the repair platforms and the missing span, and the chase never reaches you. Fall short of a jump and you wake on the highway, where the law is waiting. Hurricane Party now starts at dusk. Duval has its own four signs, two billboards and two things to smash that belong to the cruise lines and the city, never to the shops.

For devs: `keys-t1-rental-return` is now `keys-t1-last-light-duval` (node `last-light`, on `osm-duval-street`), `keys-t3-speed-monitored` rides `osm-seven-mile-run` (a 3,500 m escape by distance; its node pins `osm-sm-bridge`, claims the highway and opens the old road), and `keys-t4-hurricane-party` is `dusk`. The Keys region lists `noon`, `golden-hour` and `dusk`. Five new traffic types, all tagged `key-oldtown` and in no region-wide list: `pedicab`, `island-tram`, `rooster`, `cruise-day-tripper`, `street-performer`; the Old Town's vehicles are the new `key-oldtown` traffic area, the people and roosters come from roadside zones' `kinds` on the baked Duval and Whitehead roads, where the six boards also stand in `billboard` slots (and the bake config has the matching features). The tram is 7.5 m, not the plan's 14 m: the rival AI sizes every vehicle by the race's largest traffic type, so a longer tram would change how every Keys rival rides. Mallory Square's pier was 191 m from Duval's centre line; it now stands 21 m off the finish on the Gulf side, square to the road (`yawDeg` -90), with a 24 m forecourt kept clear in front of it so nothing can stand between the road and the pier. I moved it by hand in `osm-duval-street.json` and in `tools/gis/networks/osm-keys-duval.json` (an `at` computed from the new spot plus the real line's 8 m offset), and edited the report row (the bake config's feature `params` now take a list of words, a one-line change in `tools/gis/src/tbgis/config.py` with its own test, so a zone's `kinds` can live in the config as well as the baked road); I did not re-bake, which would fetch the map again and change the bake's hashes, so the next re-bake should be checked against `tools/gis/routes-keys-pt3.test.ts`. The AWAITING_EVENT list in `tests/sim/career-content.test.ts` loses its two Keys routes (the Portland and San Francisco ones stay for their own tasks). New `tests/sim/keys-real-world.test.ts` asks the rules: a node pins a road of its own route, the escape asks more than the road to the old road's turn-off, a rider on the old road wins and is never busted (3 seeds), a zone's `kinds` names a real pedestrian or animal, Old Town kinds stay on Old Town streets, no Old Town vehicle enlarges the AI's largest vehicle, and a bot's tour of Duval meets every new kind over 4 seeds. Not phone-verified; `night` is left out of the Keys' times of day until someone has looked at how dark it reads.

Keeper fix: the Keys now draw noon, golden hour or dusk in a quick race, and the browser check that every look paints its own sky had relied on the old single golden hour (at dusk the region's pink sky leans every look toward it). The check now races on the first seed whose time of day is the golden hour, found through a new test-handle read, `freePlayTimeOfDay(seed)`, instead of whatever seed 1 draws.
