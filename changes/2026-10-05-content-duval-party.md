Duval Street is now a party street. Ten crowds line it in five blocks, one on each side, and they are people with a plan: bar hoppers with a neon lei and a cup held high, a birthday party where everyone wears the same sash, door greeters who call every rider "boss", all among the cruise-ship day-trippers and the cooler tourists. Pedicabs are far more common. Ride through a block and a bar's music comes out of its open front and swells as you come up to it: a cover band, a steel pan or a karaoke machine with a singer a quarter-tone flat, fading again between blocks. At dusk the shopfronts light up. Every blank shop board along the street now reads as an invented business in neon (Sandal Observatory, The Unplanned Parrot, Buy One, Regret Two, Lime & Punishment, three names for each board), and strings of coloured lights hang in front of the party blocks. By day the same boards are plain signwriting and the strings stay down. Six new boards stand on the street, from a bar-crawl company ("SIX BARS, NO PLAN.") to the city's own notice ("SIDEWALK CAPACITY: 40. Current occupancy: all of them."). The joke is the tourist plan and the city's paperwork; nobody is played as drunk. Any name can be cut in the game like any sign.

For devs (playtest 4, P4-16; the Duval kit from T12.1 and the existing pedestrian, soundscape and board systems).

- **Crowds** (`sim/peds`): a `roadsideZone` may carry `params.everyM` (metres of kerb per person, 3 to 25) and `params.maxPeds` (at most 16); a zone without them spawns as before, a bad number is ignored, and the zone still rolls from its own seeded stream (`zone-crowd.test.ts`). Ten zones on `osm-duval-street` (`party-1-l` to `party-5-r`, 120 to 180 m, 44 % of the street on each side, 9 people each, 90 standing at the start of a race) carry `dressing: party` and a `music` style. Three new people (`bar-hopper`, `birthday-party`, `door-greeter`), tagged `key-oldtown` and in no region-wide list; the first two draw as a new `reveller` figure (`traffic-figures.ts`, one more instanced mesh while one is in view). The Old Town's pedicab weight goes 1 to 3.
- **Music** (`audio/soundscape.ts`, `soundscape-voices.ts`, `system.ts`): `musicAt` reads a zone's `params.music` (`cover-band`, `steel-drum`, `karaoke`) under the rider, full inside the zone and fading over 60 m outside it; the Keys' director chains one 1.5 s phrase after another, handed over 0.25 s early and scheduled on the audio clock so the beat holds, and the voices play it through a low-pass, with four variations a style. The `audio.soundscape` slider scales it. Presentation only (`bar-music.test.ts`).
- **Names** (`render/text-surfaces.ts`, `roadside.ts`): the roadside layer now hands its street fronts' text surfaces to the text layer (`RoadsideLayer.surfaces()`), so the Duval kit's nine blank shop boards are painted from 27 new signs tagged `site`, `surface` and `new`. A board may have several names (`<id>`, `<id>-2`, `<id>-3`), one picked per building, so a cut name blanks only its buildings; neon in a colour of its own when the race is at dusk or night (`isLitTime`, in `look.ts`), plain signwriting by day. `board-slots-on-land.test.ts` accepts the alternates of a painted surface.
- **Lights** (`render/party-lights.ts`, a lazy chunk, in `first-load.test.ts`'s list): posts and a string of bulbs in front of the shopfronts over each party zone, at dusk and after only; one mesh and one draw call, an index of the near spans refilled as the camera moves, never over the road (`duval-party.test.ts`).
- **Budget** (`scene-cost.test.ts`, now counting the name boards and the strings): Duval's route peaks at 56 draw calls (share 80, cap 120) and 101,082 triangles (share 110,000, cap 150,000), still scene only; the strings are about 1,000 triangles and one draw call in view. First-load JavaScript is untouched except the 7-line `isLitTime` in `look.ts`.
- **Words**: the six boards are in `region.json` (four billboards, two signs) with six slots on `osm-duval-street`. The zones and slots were added by hand to the baked road and to its bake config (the bake was not re-run, as in T10.4); `tools/packs/invented-brands.test.ts` now also refuses the real Key West bars and events a party lane could reach for. `docs/content-packs.md` (zone params, text surfaces, "As built, Duval as a party street"), `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/tone-guide.md` (a party street) are updated.
- **Not done here**: pedicabs do not glow (their figure is unchanged), and the strings do not sway. Not phone-verified, and no browser was run.
