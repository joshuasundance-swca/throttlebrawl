Pause now really stops the race, and Resume picks it up where you left it. The pause screen's Restart button starts the race again from the grid, and Quit takes you back to the menu. The volume sliders and Mute now change the sound, the left-handed option moves where your thumbs work as well as where the buttons are drawn, and all of these settings are saved on your device. If you paused before switching apps, the game stays paused when you come back.
