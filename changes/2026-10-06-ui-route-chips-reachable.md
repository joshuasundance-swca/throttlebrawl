Every road in the menu's "Which road" row can now be reached with a tap, at every text size. The row of road chips ran off the right edge of the screen (at the largest text on San Francisco, five of the nine were off-screen) and it scrolled only if you happened to swipe it. Now, when the row has more than it shows, a "more roads" arrow stands at each end that has more (dimmed at the end that has nothing past it), and a tap pages the row so the next chip lands whole in view. A chip wider than the row wraps to two lines, and a road you pick that sat half out of the row slides fully into view. On a first visit the "What's new" card no longer stands over the chips: the menu gives way to it, so the road chips keep their own room beside the card. Not phone-verified.

For devs: `ui/routes.ts` gets `scrollCue`, `pageScrollLeft` and `scrollLeftToShow` (pure, unit-tested in `ui/routes.test.ts`, including the widest region's measured chip widths and a control that a row that fits shows no cue) and the picker wraps the row in `.route-scroll` with `#route-before` and `#route-after` (they show only while the row scrolls, take room beside the row so they never cover a chip, and re-measure on a resize of the row or of any chip, so a text size change is seen). `ui/changelog-screen.ts`: beside the menu (a screen over 380 px high, or upright) the menu is `flex: 1 1 0; max-width: 600px` and the pickers `max-width: 100%`, so the card keeps its width and nothing overlaps; the short landscape stacking is unchanged. New `tests/e2e/ui-route-chips.spec.ts`: 915x412, 844x390, 732x412, 740x360, 640x360 and 568x320, Normal, Large and Largest text, card up and dismissed; every chip made whole by tapping the arrows, the last pickable, no arrow where the row fits, the card clear of both pickers and on the screen; negative controls: a plain scrolling row with no arrows fails the cue and the paging, and a box planted over the chips fails the placement check. The unit tests failed first (the functions did not exist); the browser spec is not run locally (CI runs the browser tiers). `docs/content-packs.md` ("The menu's route picker") is updated. Budgets: no draw calls or triangles touched (menu DOM only); first-load JavaScript headroom is reported in the PR.
