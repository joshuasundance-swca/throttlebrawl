Pile-up checks separate physical contact from random incidence. Scripted riders on the real base road hit a bike parked by the actual crash and hand-back: a fast square-on hit must crash, while a slow hit or the old rule must wobble. All 64 original natural races still enforce the unchanged upper-share limit and an observed dropped-bike contact supplies the old-rule control. A sample with no natural pile-up is reported as such, without changing gameplay frequency or collision rules.
