The rivals talk now. Every bark is spoken as its subtitle shows: 247 lines for the eleven rivals and the three cops (the cops' lines wait until cops start talking), each with one voice of their own, from Deacon Vane's slow tent-revival rumble and Dial-Up's wired screech to Kevin's flat office menace and Old Growth's one sentence a mile. The music and the engine dip a little while someone talks, and the subtitle stays up until the line is finished. The Voices slider and the Voices switch in the settings control them. "Cut this" on a bark silences its voice along with its words, for good. The voices are AI-made from synthetic reference voices (no real person's voice), so some takes will miss: tell us which, and they get cut or redone.
