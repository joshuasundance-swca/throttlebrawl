The "did not load" card no longer hides the buttons under it. It used to sit over the top of the screen: on the career screen it covered the Map and Garage tabs, and on a small phone's menu it covered the title and all three region buttons, with its Retry button where two of them should be. Now it is the first line of the screen that raised it, above the title, so everything under it moves down and stays visible. It goes away when you leave that screen, and while the game shows "Loading ..." for another try. Its words now say what went wrong: "Check the connection" only when the connection failed; when the game server answered with an error, it says the server had a problem and to try again shortly. If the server asked for a wait, Retry stays off and counts the wait down ("Retry in 9 s") instead of asking again at once. The short notices (a save that could not be read, a race the career will not open yet) follow the same rule, and in a race they show on the top ticker.

For devs: one rule for every transient card, written once in `src/ui/transient-cards.ts` and in `docs/architecture.md` ("Transient cards"): `TRANSIENT_CARDS` lists each card (`reload-offer`, `load-retry`, `ui-notice`, `whats-new`, `look-offer`) and every screen it can appear on; `placeCards` in `src/ui/index.ts` seats the flow cards first in the screen's column (`cardHost`), and the slot model (`createCardSlots`) drops a card when its screen changes or a busy line goes up. `tests/e2e/ui-reload-offer.spec.ts` grew into `tests/e2e/ui-transient-cards.spec.ts`: through the new `window.__uiCards.raise(id)` seam (test flag only) it raises every card on every screen it can appear on (start, menu, race options, career, a race, both results) at 915x412, 854x480, 640x360 and 568x320, at the three Text sizes and three scroll positions, and `cardFindings` fails any overlap with a control, title, card or word (or, in a race, a HUD piece or the road ahead). Two negative controls put the K2 card and the old update card back at the top centre and must be named over the region buttons and the career result's title. A last test fails San Francisco's map files for real (a 429 asking for 30 s, then a dropped connection) and checks the words, the Retry wait, the busy line and that the card goes with its screen. The words come from `failureOf` and `loadFailedText` in `src/app/load-retry.ts`; content/'s data fetches now throw `HttpLoadError` (status and raw Retry-After), and platform/ exports its Retry-After parser (`retryAfterMsOf`). The `Screen` type moved to `src/ui/screen.ts` so the spec can import the registry in Node. The browser spec runs in CI only (no local browser, per AGENTS.md). Not phone-verified.
