The Pacific Northwest's career now climbs. Every tier has its own boss to beat before the next tier opens: Juniper Moss in Light Drizzle, Kevin from Accounting in Steady Rain (a new grudge match, "Reply All, Again", on the sawmill haul), Deacon Vane in Atmospheric River and Old Growth in The Big Cut. Five races are new: Tulip Traffic (a race on the I-5 by Lake Samish, with the back road along the lake as the bonus), "Reply All, Again", Rainout (a full pack on the sawmill haul), Fire Watch (four takedowns on Chuckanut Drive) and Weigh Station Closed (an escape on the I-5). The rivals ride faster tier by tier, and the money is tighter: a first-tier win pays about $2,000, Old Growth about $9,000, and the flat podium bonus on his race is gone. The shop changes with it: the Grand Tourer 1100 (about 136 mph, $25,500) opens in Steady Rain and the Supersport 900 (about 148 mph, $31,000) in The Big Cut, the dirt bike costs $6,000, and the Streetfighter and the Superbike now belong to the other regions' shops. Paints cost $1,500 to $2,500, and a new one, Wet Cedar Gloss, sits at $15,000 for the late game. Each tier also states how hard its field rides: rivals about 10% easier to knock down in Light Drizzle, about 20% harder by The Big Cut.

For devs: this is T10.2, the Pacific Northwest's phase 1. `packs/region-pnw/careers/pnw-circuit.json` is the second chapter (global tiers 5 to 8) and names each tier's `boss` and `field` block (the progression plan's values for those tiers), with `requiredWins: 2` per tier and 15 nodes for now: the tier-3 drift event ("Crown Point Loops") waits for the drift content task, which needs the career's drift-only style objective and the drift bot's cash figure, so that tier holds two regular races and its boss. The ten old events are retuned to the purse, style and pace tables (W(g) for g 5 to 8, a tier boss 1.5 W, the region boss 2.5 W, optional bonuses 0.15 W); each event's `paceMps` is the tier's share of the best step-up bike open there (Streetfighter 750 at tier 1, Grand Tourer 1100 at tiers 2 and 3, Supersport 900 at tier 4), so the pace is right before the career applies a field level. The region has dawn and noon only, so Rainout (planned for dusk) races at dawn until the real-world content task adds a dusk. `grand-tourer-1100` and `supersport-900` are new bike files in `packs/region-pnw/bikes/`, each with a byte copy of an existing model under its own id (`touring-flagship.glb`, `superbike-1000.glb`) in `packs/region-pnw/assets/models/bikes/`, and a catalog row each in `tools/blender/catalog/pnw.mjs` that reuses the source bike's script; `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md` has a row. Tests: the new `tests/sim/career-pnw-ladder.test.ts` holds this region to the ladder (tier bosses and fields, the purse by global tier, the pace as a share of the best open bike, one new step-up bike every second tier, models in the pack, one shop per bike) and failed first on the old file. `career-polish.test.ts` races the Pacific Northwest's boss on the best bike the region sells, as the career puts a player there: on the starting bike the boss now rides out of reach, and the test saw no fall into traffic.
