The bark veto browser checks now read the ticker's class, content reference, words and box together. A style chip replacing a bark between separate checks can no longer be mistaken for the line to cut. A controlled check rejects a visible COMBO chip without a content reference.

Mouse presses and touches inside and outside the steering zone start in that same observation task. This checks steering-zone rejection while the observed bark is still present. Accepted presses let the production pointer watcher capture and hold the actual current bark, preserving its race ID and tick. The ticker item is never replaced. The saved settings flag is checked against the real replay's race seed and tick range. The 499/500 ms threshold, steering-zone exclusion, selected line, saved veto and page-error checks remain strict. Browser execution is left to CI; not phone-verified.
