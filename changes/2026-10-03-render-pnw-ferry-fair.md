The Pacific Northwest's forest road has three new places. Every race there now starts on a ferry dock: the bridge is out, so you ride up the ramp onto a moored car ferry, under its passenger deck, past parked pickups and a coffee cart, and roll off the far ramp. A fast bike hops off the top of each ramp. The Logging Spur shortcut now runs through a fresh clear-cut, with stumps you can hit and a log deck either side of its jump. The road it skips is the log trucks' road now. On Espresso Row it is the Stump Social, Fir County's logging festival: the main street is closed, there is a chainsaw-carved bear on every corner, the crowd gets out of your way, and only the marshals' golf carts drive there. The races there are about 400 m longer.
