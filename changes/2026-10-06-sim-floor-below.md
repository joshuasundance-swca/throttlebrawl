Nothing changes to ride yet; this gives the camera and the shadows what they need for the next change. A rider in the air, or tumbling overboard, now carries `floorY` in the snapshot: what lies straight below it, as a world height (a truck's roof or a parked pickup's top when it is over one, a ramp truck's deck, the road's surface, or past a rail the sea, the bluff's floor, or the old Seven Mile Bridge's deck; for a body falling past a rail, the water it falls to). The chalk landing mark meets a barrier by the same rule the rider does: a flight over a rail into the sea has no mark, one toward the old bridge has its mark on that deck, one under the rail's top is held at the edge.

For devs: `floorOf` and the reworked `touchdownOf` in `src/sim/riders/index.ts` (and `floorField` in `src/sim/create.ts`, which adds the water level for a tumble that is overboard); `overBarrier` in `src/sim/riders/gap.ts` is now `overStep` (the pure decision, with `roadUnder`, `overFalls` and `handYaw` shared) plus a wrapper that applies it, and behaves exactly as before (its suite is unchanged and green). `riderLimits` takes a `reading` flag so a snapshot never creates the verge state. Both new reads write nothing: a race hashes as before, and a snapshot read every tick leaves every tick's hash the same (`tests/sim/flight-floor.test.ts`, with the old Seven Mile Bridge, the sea, Chuckanut's bluff and the held-at-the-rail control; `src/sim/riders/floor.test.ts` for the roofs, with the old rules as the control). Not phone-verified; no budget touched (sim only).
