The Pacific Northwest's radio is faster, fuller and has more to it, and every region gets a new station (playtest 4: "PNW seems very simple and slow"). Drizzle 89.3 (grunge), Salal 102.7 (indie folk) and Stump Radio 1240 AM (stoner rock) keep their sound but now play whole songs instead of one short loop. Each song has a riff or an intro, a verse, a build, a chorus whose hook comes back, and a solo or a break, at a quicker tempo with busier drums. Salal's chorus now drives: a stomp on every beat, a shaken tambourine, banjo rolls and a mandolin. Two new Pacific Northwest stations: Wet Basement 1450 AM plays driving garage rock (a combo organ, a fuzz guitar, a stop-time break and a rave-up), and Intermittent 95.5 plays rainy-night synth for Portland (rain on the glass, a sequencer bass, a gated snare, a lead that comes back after a breakdown). The Keys get Brackish 1090 AM, swamp blues in a slow shuffle with a harmonica, a slide guitar and a washboard. San Francisco gets Down Round 88.5, a swinging jazz combo with a walking bass, an electric piano and a muted horn that plays a tune and then a solo. Pivot FM's medleys can now cut to the new bands too. All of it is written in code from each song's seed, every new song can be cut with "cut this" like any other, and the R key tunes through the new stations. Done, not phone-verified.

For devs: `src/audio/radio-compose-pnw.ts` holds the rewritten grunge, folk and stoner composers (sixteen-bar songs; tempo ranges 112-148, 116-148 and 100-126 bpm; the Pacific Northwest station files' track tempos moved into them, the track ids unchanged). The four new bands are `src/audio/radio-compose-extra.ts` and `src/audio/radio-rigs-extra.ts` (presets `garage-band`, `darkwave-band`, `swamp-band`, `jazz-band`; new layers `rain`, `ep`, `harp`, `scrape`), with the station files `packs/region-pnw/stations/pnw-wet-basement.json`, `pnw-wipers.json`, `packs/base/stations/keys-wetland.json` and `packs/region-sf/stations/sf-down-round.json`. The folk rig gains a tremolo mandolin and the stoner rig a ride. `src/audio/radio-p4.test.ts` reads both dials from the station files and holds every Pacific Northwest station to San Francisco's: at least as many note onsets a second as its calmest station, no lower tempo floor than its slowest band, sixteen bars or more and as many sections as its most varied song; it also caps every band's sources a second at the Keys' surf band at its busiest (the phone budget). Before this change the Pacific Northwest's stations averaged 4.2, 5.1 and 2.6 onsets a second against San Francisco's 6.7 to 7.5; now 6.5 to 8.2. Rig trims were set by an offline render so every band's first eight seconds sit within about 2.3 dB of each other (the e2e check allows 3.5); `tests/e2e/audio-radio.spec.ts` renders the four new bands and stations, and the Keys' R-key test and `app-wire-seams.spec.ts` now read the dial from the station files instead of a fixed list. The first-load JavaScript grew only by the four station files and the genre names (the composers ride in the radio's lazy chunk).
