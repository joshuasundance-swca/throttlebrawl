Wheelies have their own button now (playtest 4: "Wheelie control conflicts with accelerating"). A small ▲ button sits by your right thumb, left of HIT and over BRAKE: hold it to lift the front and keep it up, let go to drop it. The stick is only the throttle again, so you can wheelie at full gas, and the old double-tap of the stick is gone (it also popped wheelies by accident when you flicked the stick). The skill is how long you hold: one long hold carries the front up through the green on the wheelie gauge, into the red, and over the top, a loop-out, about 3 seconds after the pop. Let go and press again to keep it up as long as you like. On a keyboard hold H; on a gamepad hold R3 (click the right stick). Auto-throttle no longer turns wheelies off.

For devs: the sim's balance is the hold model (`src/sim/riders/wheelie.ts`): a press arms the pop, which fires on the first tick of that press the rider can (6 m/s, no wobble, U-turn or drift), at any throttle; while held, the front's aim climbs at `riders.wheelieRise` (new, 0.25 rad/s, on the tuning panel) from where the press found the front, and released it is 0. The throttle no longer enters the balance (`wheelieStep` lost its throttle argument), and `WHEELIE_MIN_THROTTLE` and `WHEELIE_TARGET_MAX` are gone. Measured in `wheelie.test.ts` by sim ticks: a held button is in the sweet band from tick 11 and stays there for at least a second (to tick 115), high from tick 115, a loop-out at tick 173; let go after 30, 60 or 90 ticks, the front is down cleanly 52 to 56 ticks later; the same press and release at throttle 0, 0.4 and 1 gives the same angle tick for tick; riding by the gauge keeps it up 6 s with 4 presses. The moves audit's numbers are why: the thumb-height band was 9.4 px wide in mid-stick and a full swipe looped out 0.6 s after the pop. The hood and trunk launch tests now ride the wheelie by the gauge (hold under 0.5 rad, let go over 0.7) instead of holding a throttle. The input flag and the replay are unchanged: `InputFlag.wheelie` is still the level-held bit 256 every recording carries, and the new slider is sim tuning, recorded in the header like the others; `tests/sim/wheelie-replay.test.ts` replays a course of held-button wheelies to the same hash and events.

Input: `ActionState.wheelie` is held while the touch button, H (`KeyAction` `wheelie`) or R3 (`PadButtonAction` `wheelie`, `PAD.r3`, remappable from the settings like the others) is held; a touch press shorter than a tick is latched. `input/devices/wheelie-tap.ts`, its tests and the `input.wheelieTapMs` and `input.wheelieTapPx` sliders are gone (a saved preset naming them skips them). Not Shift for the key: five quick Shift presses, the hold-and-release rhythm, open the Sticky Keys prompt on Windows. The keyboard legend has a `H — wheelie (hold)` row, and W's row is just "ride".

Layout: the button is `touch-wheelie` in the Classic preset (`packs/base/hud/classic.json`), placed by a settle rule in `core/layout.ts` (`placeTouchButtons`) that input/ hit-tests and ui/ draws, so the two agree: it keeps 6 px from HIT and BRAKE and stays out of the road ahead, shrinking about its corner where it must (58 px on a 915 by 412 phone, 33 to 39 px on 16:9 phones), else lifting clear of the buttons. The road-ahead box moved to core (`ROAD_AHEAD`; ui's `LOOK_AHEAD` is it). `src/core/touch-buttons.test.ts` sweeps 13 screens in both hands (clear of both buttons, off the road ahead, on screen, at least 32 px, nearer the attack button than the brake is), with a negative control. The other per-PR layout checks now count it as a touch button: the wheelie gauge's sweep (`ui/moves-meter.test.ts`, every grid point still the top tier), the tuning panel's (`ui/tuning/place.test.ts`; the panel now stops above it: 60 to 237 px at 915x412, 60 to 194 px at 568x320, still at least 120 px to use on every screen), and the browser checks (`ui-style-popups`, `ui-screens`, `ui-ticker` list it; `.touch-button` pieces were already measured). `tests/e2e/input-devices.spec.ts` holds the button through Chrome's touch emulation and waits for bit 256 on and off. A hold on the button is a control, not an in-game veto press. Known lists stay empty. Browser specs were not run on the dev machine (CI runs them). Not phone-verified.
