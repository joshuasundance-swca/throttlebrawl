Marked dirt shortcuts, signposted at the split.
- In the Keys, keep left at the end of the Mangrove Cut for the Sandbar Flats, a sand track across the shallows north of Tarpon Flats, over a hummock and back at Conch Row ("SANDBAR: NOT ADVISED. Advice not taken."). It is on the Keys' Long race and the career's longer Keys events.
- In San Francisco, hug the right at the end of Painted Row for the Park Cut, a flat dirt path across the park, with garden fences along it, that skips the Fogline Climb's hill ("PARK CUT. Flatter. Muddier. Unfunded.").

Each is shorter but loose. The Pacific Northwest's fire road waits for a fix in how races handle junctions.
