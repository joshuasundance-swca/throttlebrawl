A marked dirt shortcut in every region, each signposted at the split.
- In the Keys, keep left at the low end of the Pelican Bridge for the Sandbar Flats, a sand track across the shallows that skips the Sandbar Causeway ("SANDBAR: NOT ADVISED. Advice not taken.").
- In the Pacific Northwest, hug the right off the end of the trestle for Fire Road 9, a dirt track through the trees, over a water bar, that skips Espresso Row ("FIRE ROAD 9. Not a road. Not on fire.").
- In San Francisco, hug the right at the end of Painted Row for the Park Cut, a flat dirt path across the park, with garden fences along it, that skips the Fogline Climb's hill ("PARK CUT. Flatter. Muddier. Unfunded.").

Each is shorter, but loose. The sandbar and the park cut are on the standard races; the fire road is on the long haul.
