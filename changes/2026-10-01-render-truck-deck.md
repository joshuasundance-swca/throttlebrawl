The ramp truck now looks the way it rides. Past the top of the ramp the game has always given you a flat deck all the way to the front of the truck, but the model had a car parked on that deck and a cab sticking up at the front, so a slow rider seemed to drive through the car and float past the cab. The car on the top deck is gone (the truck still carries the one underneath), the cab sits lower, and the deck now runs on over the cab to the front bumper, exactly where the ride is.
