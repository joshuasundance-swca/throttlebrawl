Less waiting after a crash. The tumble hands you back as soon as you have nearly stopped sliding, never later than about three and a half seconds. Skip never takes longer than running would have, so skipping next to your bike is quick. And you climb back on rolling, at about 30 km/h, instead of from a standstill. The run back to the bike is the same as before.
