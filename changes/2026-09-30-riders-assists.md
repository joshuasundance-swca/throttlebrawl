Three assists are now in the game engine. A steering helper, at light or strong, gently turns you back when you drift toward the shoulder or the wall. It never steers you onto a set line, and you can still ride the shoulder if you steer hard. Auto-throttle keeps the gas on for you, and the brake still works. A lower overall game speed plays the same race slower: at 0.8 the bikes are 80 % as fast, and jumps keep the same arc, so the boat-ramp shortcut still works at the slowest setting. The settings screen that turns these on comes with the menu work; until then they are off. For now the lower speed slows the bikes only. Traffic and pedestrians follow in their own changes.
