There's a lead pipe on the road now, three of them along each race. Ride over one to pick it up, and your attack button swings it instead of punching: a slower wind-up, but it hits harder and reaches further. Kicks still kick. The best part is the steal: when a rival winds up a pipe at you, hit attack while it's held out (the pipe glints) and you snatch it right out of their hand. Too early or too late and you just throw a punch. Anyone who wrecks drops their pipe where they went down, and the next rider past can grab it.
