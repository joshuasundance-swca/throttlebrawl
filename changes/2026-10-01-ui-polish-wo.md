Three small fixes. Pressing R to change the radio now remembers your choice at once, even if you never pause. The "Cut." note in the pause menu's radio panel is now bigger, upright and on a yellow strip, so it reads on a phone. On a narrow upright screen the rivals' speech bubble now uses the screen's width, so a line takes at most two rows instead of three or four.
