Kicks now shove. A landed kick pushes the rider about a lane sideways, like a forced swerve, maybe into traffic or onto the shoulder, while a punch staggers them for a moment instead of pushing them. A swipe down on the attack button now becomes a kick even when it takes a moment, so a natural swipe no longer comes out as a punch; if the punch already connected, the kick follows it. An attack you press while still reeling from a hit is no longer lost: it goes off as soon as you recover. Health slowly comes back after about five seconds out of a fight. Rivals' kicks push you about as far as before, so the rivals are no harder. Every one of these numbers is a slider in the tuning panel's combat group.
