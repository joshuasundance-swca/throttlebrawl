Kicks now shove. A landed kick pushes the rider about a lane sideways, like a forced swerve, maybe into traffic or onto the shoulder, while a punch still only nudges them and now makes them wobble for a moment. An attack you press while still reeling from a hit is no longer lost: it goes off as soon as you recover. Your health slowly comes back once you have backed off: about five seconds out of a fight with nobody riding right beside you. Rivals' kicks push you about as far as before, so the rivals are no harder. Every one of these numbers is a slider in the tuning panel's combat group.
