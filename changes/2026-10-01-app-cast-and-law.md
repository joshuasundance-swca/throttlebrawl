The cops and rivals built last run now ride in the real game. Sgt. Pruitt starts every race with his baton and swings it at you when he catches up; press attack as he winds up and you take it off him. If he busts you, the results screen shows the fine. The rivals' riding styles are on by default ("Rival style quirks" in the tuning panel turns them off): Dial-Up weaves across the road when nobody is near him, Deacon is slow off the line and hits hard, and each rival has their own feud and favourite weapon. The new weapons (the bike chain, the taser and the rest) keep their own behaviour, uses and stun.
A pedestrian now also dives clear of a rider thrown backward in a crash (before, they read him as already past).
