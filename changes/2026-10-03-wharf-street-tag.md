The waterfront's side streets are tagged `wharf-street` instead of `side-street`. Chinatown and North Beach (merged first) already use `side-street` for a block's cross street, with its own verge (14 m) and theme (`crossing`), and the Chinatown layer loads on any network that carries the tag; one tag cannot hold both meanings, so the waterfront's (20 m of shoulder, the `wharf` theme) took a name of its own. The waterfront's road files were re-baked: only the tag changed (9 lines in 5 files).
