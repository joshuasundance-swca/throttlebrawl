The road is wider. Each of the two travel lanes on the hand-made coastal road grew from 3.4 m to 4 m, so there is more room to weave between cars and trucks. The shoulders are still there on both edges, and you can still ride them. The boat-ramp shortcut, the people by the road and the cop's parking spot moved out with the road's edge.
