Jumps are forgiving now: the bike follows the bend and lines itself up with the road in the air, a crooked landing wobbles or slides straight (losing some speed) instead of throwing you off, and only a really bad landing crashes. On a seeded batch of 360 jumps flown the way a thumb flies them, 153 used to end on the ground and none do now.
