Past a barrier you meet what you see. Fly over Chuckanut's sandstone parapet and come down on the grassy shelf beyond it, and that is a quick reset on the grass, not a 70 m fall into the sea through the ground you were looking at; clear the shelf and you go over the cliff where the bluff really falls away. The same holds everywhere: the land beside a mangrove key, the slope below a hill road, a catwalk beside a bridge or the ground in a junction's corner is ground you come down on, and you only fall where the game draws water or a drop. For developers: with the course's honest edges on, `beyondAt` (src/road/beyond.ts) gives what lies under a rider out past an edge each tick from the road scene's own ground rules, now read from the network in src/road/drawn-ground.ts (the land strip rule, moved from road/land.ts, plus the terrain skirt, the shelf, the roadside zones' land, the split zones' paint and the lake bank); the crash's `floorY` and `dropM` are that ground's, and crashed bodies meet the same rule at the barrier line. The land reach is now worked out per sample as asked for (Lake Samish's whole network took a second). Because the sim reads it, drawn-ground.ts builds into the sim chunk, not the planners' lazy chunk: scripts/sim-chunk.mjs and scripts/first-load.test.ts change with it (a small shared-config edit only this change needs), and the first-load JavaScript measured 488.2 KB gzip on this branch (11.8 KB under the 500 KB budget). tests/sim/beyond-drawn.test.ts holds the sim's floor to the drawn scene on every route network (without this change it fails at Chuckanut: 32,662 points wrong on 75 roads, against 758 on the 8 listed), with the roads still off listed and why: Lake Samish's one water level stands under falls where the sea is drawn, which needs the lake's outline in the sim. The fixture test in src/sim/riders/gap.test.ts now expects a drop past an untagged deck's wall, where nothing is drawn. The old rules (`riders.courseEdges` 0, every recording made before) are unchanged. Not phone-verified.
