Quick Play setup offers Race, Hunt, Escape and Grudge from the selected region's existing events.
The objective appears before you ride and on the HUD, and challenge results say whether you met it.
Hunts keep enough rivals, Escapes keep the law on, and Grudges keep their named opponent.
Retry keeps your event and setup. These rides leave career progress and cash unchanged.
