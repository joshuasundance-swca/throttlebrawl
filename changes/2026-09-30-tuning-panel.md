The hidden tuning panel is real now. Press the backquote key to open it (on a phone it opens from the pause screen with a three-finger tap, or a long-press on the build number, once the pause screen lands). It stays open, see-through, while you race, so you can drag a slider and feel the change straight away. Sliders sit in groups: hit-stop, knockback, steering, shake, speed and traffic, and each one shows up as soon as the part of the game it tunes exists; for now that is steering and the camera distance. There is also a frame-rate cap: every frame, every 2nd frame or every 3rd frame, with the frame rate your screen gives for each, so you can try a battery-saving 30 fps against full speed (the cap takes effect once the game loop is wired to it). "Copy preset as JSON" copies your settings to paste in chat, and shows them in a box too in case the phone blocks copying. "Reset" goes back to the shipped feel.
