More now offers Measure frames, with explicit Start, Cancel/Stop and Close controls. The frame diagnostic collects an isolated repeated race, keeps the full eligible-frame distribution, and stops at the canonical 15-minute target or 20-minute wall deadline. Interruptions preserve partial results and the last measurement draw. Debug exports include the complete diagnostic evidence without treating it as a phone performance pass; copying a running result leaves the diagnostic running. A delayed tool load cannot open over a newer menu action, and the overlay scrolls on small screens.
