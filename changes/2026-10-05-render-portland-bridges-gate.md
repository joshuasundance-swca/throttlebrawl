Bridge City's bridges look like the real ones, and Old Town has its Chinatown gate. Ride the Burnside Bridge and the two operator houses of its bascule stand either side of the deck at each end of the lifting span, with steel trusses running out from them over both approaches. On the Hawthorne Bridge a steel lift span hangs between the two lift towers, and truss spans run on from the towers, two to the west and seven to the east. A little way along West Burnside a red-and-green gate stands over a side street on your right, its plaque facing the road: CHINATOWN. UMBRELLAS WELCOME. The street under it is open, with no building across it.

The plaque's words are invented and in the pack as a vetoable sign. One taste call for the maintainer: the joke is the weather, and it is easy to cut (long-press the board, or pick it from the recently seen list; the plaque stays blank).

For devs (playtest 4, P4-19 and P4-20: "Bridge City should look like downtown Portland ... as content-rich as the others"; builds on CX4's and CX5's `pdx-landmarks` kit, #506 and #535, and on #519's second row).

- **Where they stand.** The positions are OpenStreetMap's own tags for each bridge, read from the Overpass API and checked against the baked roads (the extract is not in the repo): the Burnside Bridge's movable (bascule) span runs from lon -122.6680141 to -122.6671101 (70 m), its two through-truss approaches are 94 and 95 m, the Hawthorne Bridge's truss runs 72 m west of the lift span and 273 m east of it, and the Chinatown Gateway is OSM node 2544562055, 25 m north of West Burnside's line. Sixteen structures are `overRoad` entries of the bake config's `landmarks` list (`tools/gis/networks/osm-pnw-portland.json`), with `landmark` features on the baked bridge roads and a row each in the bake report, all written by hand from the same points as the lift towers' were. Two bascule piers, four truss bays on the Burnside Bridge, the lift span, nine truss bays on the Hawthorne Bridge; each bay is the kit's 40 m, and they run end to end from the piers and towers. The gate is a road feature of West Burnside, `chinatown-gate`, at s 229.3, d 25.1, `yawDeg` 90, with the free-form param `crossStreet`.
- **A bay stands by its middle** (`src/render/landmarks.ts`, `LandmarkLayer.single`). The kit models a bay from its origin along +Z (`bay_m`), where a tower stands about its origin; a node that says `bay_m` is now shifted back half its length, so its length fills the footprint from `s0` to `s1`. A node without `bay_m` is placed as before (the landmark tests hold both, with the control). The Golden Gate's bays are composed in code and are unaffected.
- **A gate opens a street** (`src/render/downtown.ts`, `planPortland`). A landmark with `crossStreet: true` opens a cross street (18 m, `PDX_STREET_M`) centred on it along its side, with the street's asphalt under the gate; the front row ends short of it and starts again past it, no lot takes a building across it, and the block's own cross street gives way when the gate's is about to open (within half a block). The second row and the street-end fronts follow the street list as before.
- **Tests.** `tools/gis/routes-pnw-pt3.test.ts`: the bascule piers stand at the ends of the real 70 m span, the lift span lies between the towers' inner faces, and each bridge's structures lie in its bridge run, overlap nothing, with the bays touching end to end. `src/render/landmarks-pnw.test.ts`: all of them draw from the kit in the one landmark call (worst view standing at a structure: 3,346 triangles, against the 9,560 the Golden Gate's landmark view holds), none stands in the lanes' clearance (a floor beam sits under the deck; the control finds the roof sign's building there), and the road under each bay is within 0.6 m of its axis (worst 0.43 m). `src/render/portland-blocks.test.ts`: the gate's cross street is open for four seeds with the control that without `crossStreet` no asphalt is there; the plaque faces West Burnside (the control: unturned, it looks along the road); the gate is clear of every building and of every road and its sidewalk; and every blocks side with 30 m of the full wide strip (26 sides) has a second row behind its front, with the control that on the old 24 m strip none does. `src/render/landmarks.test.ts`: the bay-by-its-middle rule.
- **Cost** (`src/render/scene-cost.test.ts`, run on this branch and on origin/main in a throwaway copy): the busiest Bridge City view is 70 draw calls on both, 84,987 triangles against 84,513 (the share is 80 draws and 110,000 triangles, the frame's caps 120 and 150,000), so about 10 draws and 25,000 triangles of headroom. The structures add no draw call (they join the landmark mesh). First-load JavaScript (`scripts/perf.mjs --size-only` after `vite build`, on this branch and on origin/main in the same throwaway copy): 451.7 KB gzip of 500 here against 452.1 KB on main, so 48.3 KB of headroom and no growth (the change is in two lazy chunks, `landmarks.ts` and `downtown.ts`; the 0.4 KB difference is build noise, not a saving).
- **The second row and the city floor.** #519 already stands the second row (the sheet that asked for it was written before that). This change adds the all-sides test above and finds the second row on every side that has the room. The two Morrison connector pieces' left sides (24 and 42 m of land, held short by other roads' land) have none, and cannot. A paved city floor out to 300 m, as San Francisco's downtown has, is not built: behind the second row the terrain skirt drops about 10 m, out of sight behind buildings 30 to 60 m tall, and a flat floor at the lots' level would cross other roads at other heights and the river. It needs the skirt's foot from `road-mesh.ts`, a larger change in shared code.
- **Not done, not checked.** No browser was run, so the look (how a truss reads against the road scene's own bridge fascia and rails, the plaque's size from the road) is checked by geometry, not by eye, and is not phone-verified. The east truss runs 7 bays (280 m) over OSM's 273 m, the Burnside approaches 2 bays (80 m) over 94 to 95 m: a bay is the kit's whole 40 m. The baked features and report rows are hand-written from the baked roads' own points, so a re-bake from the real extract may move a structure by the 2 to 3 m the lift towers moved (3.5 and 3.1 m).
