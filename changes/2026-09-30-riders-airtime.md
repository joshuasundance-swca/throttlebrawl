Bikes can fly. Hit a ramp lip fast enough and you leave the ground, keep your speed through the air (you can nudge your heading a little), and come down again. Land straight and you ride away clean; land crooked and you wobble, or crash if you were really sideways. The tuning panel gains the landing crash speed. The ramp shortcut itself arrives with the road lane's junction work; until then the ordinary road has no lips, so nothing takes off by accident.
