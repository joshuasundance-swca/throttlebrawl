Each region now has a sound of its own under the engine. In the Keys, every expansion joint on a bridge thumps under your wheels, one after another, so you can hear your speed in the rhythm, and gulls cry over the water and sailboat halyards clink in the marinas. In the Pacific Northwest, rain patters on your helmet, harder the faster you ride, and log trucks bark their engine brakes at the bends. In San Francisco, a two-tone foghorn sounds across the bay, and cable-car bells ring only on the cable-car streets. The new Regional sounds slider in the audio settings turns it down or off.
