Lake Samish now has a parked ramp truck on I-5's outer shoulder. Pull onto its deck and steer right in the air to land on East Shore Drive, then follow the shore road back to the race. The through lanes remain open for traffic and riders who stay on I-5.

The trailer's visible side rails are solid edges, not landing surfaces. A bike rising from that truck's ramp can climb the small rail lip; an incoming or falling bike still hits the rail, and the cab remains solid.
