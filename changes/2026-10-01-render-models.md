Roadside scenery now stands on sandy land beside the road, never on a bridge, the road or the water: palms, mangrove clumps, bait shacks and a line of power poles, picked by what each stretch of road is (beach, mangroves, marina and so on). Small boats bob offshore. The Pacific Northwest and San Francisco get land and poles but no palms. Where the scenery stands follows the race's seed. The game can now draw the new 3D models (the palms, the ramp truck, the boats and the rest) in every look, with the ramp truck's ramp exactly where the jump launches you.
