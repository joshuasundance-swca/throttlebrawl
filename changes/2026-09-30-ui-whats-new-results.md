The menu now tells you what changed since you last played: a small card beside the Race button lists the new things, and "What's new" in the menu opens the full list of changes, newest first. The first time you open the game it just says hello. The results screen now counts your takedowns and style cash, and risky riding will flash NEAR MISS, ONCOMING or AIRTIME with the cash it earned once style scoring switches on.
