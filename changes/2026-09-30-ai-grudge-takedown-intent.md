Rivals play dirtier. When a car is coming the other way, or you're riding near the bridge rail, heavy hitters like Deacon Vane work their way round to your far side so their hits shove you into it. A rival who holds a grudge against you comes looking for you for the rest of the race, even one who normally just races; grudges get noted once knocked-off rivals start getting up and shaking a fist.
