Rivals play dirtier. Heavy hitters like Deacon Vane now work their way round to your far side so their hits shove you toward oncoming traffic, or toward the rail on the bridge, and they kick more when a kick would do it. A rival who holds a grudge against you comes looking for you for the rest of the race, even one who normally just races; grudges get noted once knocked-off rivals start getting up and shaking a fist.
