Wheelies and drifts now show on screen. While a wheelie is up, a thin bar stands beside your thumb: green is the sweet band, red above it means the front is about to loop out, amber below it means it is dropping, and a white marker rides the bar at the front's angle. It only shows while the front is up, and it moves out of the way of the buttons, the readouts and the road ahead wherever your thumb lands. On a keyboard or gamepad it sits where the stick would be.

Your drift chain now runs on the top line like the other live meters: "DRIFT" with the chain's multiplier and the cash it would bank ("DRIFT ×2 +$140"). When the chain banks, the line lands on its award. If a crash, a wobble or a hit knocks it out first, the line shows "DRIFT LOST" and the cash is gone. Paid wheelies and banked drifts also pop their cash on the top line now ("WHEELIE +$140", "DRIFT +$210"); until now they paid but never showed (the results screen counted them, the top line never said so).

For devs: `src/ui/moves-meter.ts` holds the drift meter (`createDriftMeter`, `driftOutcome`, `meterLine`), the gauge's view and DOM (`#hud-wheelie`) and its placement (`placeGauge`, the settle rule, tiered: clear of every slot and the road ahead, else every slot, else the buttons and text only). `STYLE_WORDS` in `src/ui/race-feed.ts` gained `wheelie` and `drift`, and `src/ui/race-feed.test.ts` holds it to every kind in the sim's `STYLE_KINDS`, so a kind the sim can pay can never be without a ticker word again. `tests/sim/moves-meter-pins.test.ts` pins the gauge's bands and the chain multiplier to the sim's own numbers and runs a real paid wheelie through the tally and the gauge's view. The HUD layout check (`tests/e2e/ui-style-popups.spec.ts`) now shows the gauge in the heat and toast moments with the known list still empty. The gauge stands beside the ring ui already draws, so `InputSystem.stickBase()` is not used here; `InputSystem.onMoves` (the loop-out warning buzz) still needs its one-line call in `app/index.ts`, which this change leaves to the app lane. A rider who finishes with a chain still open loses it in the sim, and the strip says so with "DRIFT LOST". Done, not phone-verified.
