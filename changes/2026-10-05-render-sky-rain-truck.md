Three small fixes from the last live check. The Keys' sky no longer has a lone tall cloud with a flat top on the horizon that read as a mushroom cloud: the sky now has a few wide, puffy fair-weather clouds, each broader than it is tall, with no narrow stalk under a wider top. The Pacific Northwest's drizzle no longer leaves two or three streaks falling under the car ferry's deck: how much rain is drawn now follows where the camera is (none from 4 m in under the deck), instead of a fade timed by frames that could lag when frames were slow. And the moving ramp truck now turns up in San Francisco's own quick race, Fogline Hill Sprint, which never got one before: the truck is placed by the road, so a route that has a straight stretch for it gets it on every seed, and the stretch may now sit beside a shortcut as long as the last 200 m of it lie past where the shortcut rejoins.

For devs: `src/sim/modifiers/setpieces.ts` (`initSetPieces` picks the moving ramp's spot among every spot of its window that fits, like a piece tied to a road tag; `roomFor` takes a `tail`, `MOVING.rampTailM` in `moving.ts`), `src/render/backdrop/shapes.ts` and `data.ts` (the `thunderhead` cloud style is gone, a `cumulus` style replaces it, and a pack naming an unknown cloud style is refused), the Keys' backdrop `region.json`, `src/render/roofs.ts` (`roofCover`) with `rain.ts` and `index.ts`. `docs/architecture.md` (Moving ramp trucks) and `docs/content-packs.md` (moving-ramp, Backdrop, Surface) are updated. The moving-ramp test now rides the region's own race and runs its jump check on three seeds; its approach starts after the last tight bend of the run-up, which a Key West street has. Not phone-verified: the checks are headless (a cloud's silhouette has no waist, where the truck is placed on each route, the rain count for a frame under the deck); the clouds' look wants a glance on the phone, and the rain fix is a reading of the cause (a fade timed by frames), not a reproduction in a browser.
