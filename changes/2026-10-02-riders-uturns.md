You can turn round now. Slow down to under about 27 mph, then hold the brake and steer all the way to one side: the bike pivots round on the spot or in a tight circle, and you ride back the other way. Once it is round you can let go of the brake and gas out of the turn; to turn again, centre the bars first. A kerb you meet mid-turn only stops you, it never throws you off. Riding back down the race earns no near-miss or oncoming cash; turn round again and you race on from wherever you are. Rivals and cops never turn round, and hard braking into a tight bend above that speed feels exactly as before.
