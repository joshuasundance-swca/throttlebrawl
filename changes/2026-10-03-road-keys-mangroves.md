The Keys get a mangrove boardwalk and a secret island. Halfway down the Mangrove Cut, keep left at the BOARDWALK sign: a plank walk runs straight through the swamp, with mangroves on one side and a channel on the other, where tour airboats roar along beside you. It is about as quick as the road, and the Mangrove Run's bonus now pays for riding it. Out on the sandbar, past a sign that says there is nothing out there, hug the left edge: an unmarked fork loops out across the shallows to Unlisted Key, a palm island with a tiki bar nobody has found. On the career map it is a "?" until you find it, and finding it pays $500.

Under the hood: the road compiler can now build a branch off another branch, through via points; the Keys' self-test hash and seeded Keys races change, because the Keys road has new pieces. Junction signs (and the island's signs) stay at their own spot and no longer turn up in other roads' random sign slots. Done, not phone-verified.
