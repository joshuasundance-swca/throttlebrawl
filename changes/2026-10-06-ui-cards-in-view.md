When a career ride fails to load, you now see why. The "did not load" card used to appear at the top of the career screen while the screen was scrolled down to the event, so it was out of sight, and Ride just stayed there with no message. Now the screen scrolls to show the card, on every screen that can raise one. Five more fixes from the same check:

- If the game server asks the game to wait ("Retry in 30 s"), picking the region again or tapping Race now shows the same countdown, instead of asking the server for every map file at once.
- When the game's files for this version are gone from the server (usually because a newer version replaced them), the card says so and offers Reload for the newest version, instead of "Try again shortly" and a Retry that could never work.
- If you leave the menu after a region failed to load, then come back, the menu still says why that region has no roads to pick, and what to tap. While a region's roads load, it says "Loading ...".
- The notice that your settings come from a newer version no longer disappears if you tap the start screen quickly: it is shown on the menu.
- On a small phone at the largest Text size, the menu's bottom row of buttons now fits the screen instead of being cut off at both sides, and the version number at the bottom of the menu no longer sits on top of Start career and Race when you scroll.

For devs: the transient-card rule (`src/ui/transient-cards.ts`, `docs/architecture.md` "Transient cards") gains "in view": `reveal` in `src/ui/index.ts` scrolls a card into view when it is raised, comes back after a busy line, or shows on its screen, and `inViewFindings` judges it. A notice on the start screen has no timer there and is carried to the menu (`createCardSlots().screenChanged` returns what it carried). The loader keeps the host's wait: `retryingFetch` (`src/platform/retry-fetch.ts`) asks for no `assets/` file until a Retry-After has passed, sitting out up to 8 s and answering a longer wait at once with its own 429 and the time left. `failureOf` in `src/app/load-retry.ts` reads a 404 or 410 as `gone` (Reload, through `offerLoadRetry`'s new `reload` argument and `offerRetry`'s `action: 'reload'`), and `routesNote` plus the route picker's new note (`setRoutesNote`) put a word in the route row's place while the picked region's roads are not in. The cards wrap to their column (`width: fit-content`; their one-line `max-content` width widened the menu's column at 568x320), the menu's column is capped at the screen's width, and the menu footer gives way (`keepFooterClear`) while the menu scrolls or something lies under it. `tests/e2e/ui-transient-cards.spec.ts` now raises each card with its screen scrolled to the end and the top and checks it is in view, checks every screen (and the menu on its own) with the new `screenFitFindings` (`src/ui/screen-fit.ts`) for anything off the screen's sides or under the footer, with negative controls for both and for a card scrolled away, and adds real-failure tests for a failed career ride on a scrolled career, the loader's wait on a pick and a Race tap, a 404's Reload, the route-row word after leaving the menu, and the boot notice carried to the menu. The browser spec runs in CI only (no local browser, per AGENTS.md). Not phone-verified.
