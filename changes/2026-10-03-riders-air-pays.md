Air that pays. Land a jump straight and the bike surges forward for a second, with a local one-liner over your bike ('TEN OUT OF TEN, SAYS A PELICAN'; the rivals get the surge too). Come down within a bike length of a rival and you land on him: a heavy hit that knocks him off only if he is already hurt. In the air the camera tips forward and a chalk mark shows where you will touch down, red if you would land crooked. On the biggest jumps, hold the brake and the kick together to sit back in a lawn chair and read the newspaper; let go in time and it pays more than a backflip, hold it too long and you land holding the newspaper. Live on the game link: hit the car-carrier ramp truck at speed, then try S and K together in the air (on the phone, the brake button plus a held swipe down on the attack button).
