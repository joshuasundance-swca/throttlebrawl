San Francisco's streets stop being one kind of building. The row-house terraces of Russian Hill, Lombard Street and Twin Peaks now have taller Edwardian and Mediterranean flats, apartment blocks (one with a fire escape, one a six-storey mid-century slab) and corner buildings with a shop at street level, turned so their windows and side door face the gap where a cross street would be. Each corner shop has a sign with an invented name ("FOGLINE LAUNDRY & BAR", "THE LAST GOOD DELI", "ALMOST OPEN CAFE"). On Russian Hill the copper-green flatiron stands beside Kearny Street as you come up to the Columbus turn, its prow pointing up the road. On the Mission route the old adobe chapel stands past the finish line, on the left, its front looking back at you. And Coit Tower is back where the real one is, on Telegraph Hill at the end of the boulevard: you meet it at the top of the climb, not at the foot of the hill.

For devs: CX6's models, placed (run C task C1; identity sheets R3, R4 and M3; B1's note that the near Coit Tower stood about 200 m from the real one).
- **Apartments (R3).** `sf-apartments` is a new model kind (`sfApartments`) that loads with the row houses, and a new scenery kind `apartment` (variants in the order of `APARTMENT` in `scenery.ts`). `upgradeTerrace` runs on the row houses the scatter has just placed on a side, so every check a house passed still holds. At the end of a run of houses (the next plot has none), two plots become a corner building 2 times in 10, its open side to the gap; otherwise two plots become a block 7 in 100; otherwise a house becomes a flat 8 in 100 (`APARTMENT_RATE`). Over six seeds of Russian Hill, 73 % of the plots stay row houses. A spot carries `plots`, and `halfAlongOf(spot)` is its width (the geometry sweeps use it). The corners' signs are the new region signs `sf-corner-l-sign` and `sf-corner-r-sign` (each with `-2` and `-3` names; tags `site`, `surface`, `new`; vetoable), painted by `apartmentSurfaces` in `text-surfaces.ts` from `index.ts`.
- **The flatiron (R4).** `columbus-flatiron` on `osm-sf-kearny` (s 410 to 440, d 11 to 29, `farM` 250), and in `osm-sf-russian-hill`'s config as a `landmarks` entry on the line `hills`. The baked Kearny ends 55 m north of the real tower, so the prow stands 35 m short of the turn.
- **The mission chapel (M3).** `mission-chapel` on `sf-mi-last-coat-alley` (s 615 to 651, d -20 to -8, `yawDeg` 180, `frontM` 18). I chose the finish end of the route as "the Dolores end": the sheet names only "one end", and the finish is where a rider's eye is. The mural walls now stop 1 m short of any landmark footprint on their side (`mission.ts`).
- **Coit Tower.** Moved to `osm-sf-lombard-telegraph-hill` (s 381 to 403, d -30 to -8), at its real height (the scale 1.25 is gone), 23 m from the boulevard's baked end. The config's `at` and the report are edited to match, by hand (the OSM extract is not in the repo). The price: from the Lombard flats the terraces hide it (it is 10 to 40 degrees off the road's line), and on the climb its top is out of the frame; only the last 40 m show it whole (`landmarks-sf.test.ts` measures this). This is a taste call for the maintainer (docs/content-packs.md lists the levers).
