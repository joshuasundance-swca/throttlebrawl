A cop only busts you when he is the one who knocks you off: his baton or taser finishes you, or his hit sends you into a car or the scenery. Crashing on your own next to a cop is no longer a bust, so a fall in front of him just costs the time to get going again. The old rule is still a switch in the tuning panel, in case the next playtest wants it back.
