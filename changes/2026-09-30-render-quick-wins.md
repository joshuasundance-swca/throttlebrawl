The game can now draw and sound the two new stunts. Speed-boost pads are glowing cyan patches on the road with arrows, a boosting bike spits a cyan flame out of its back, and a boost makes a rising whoosh. The car-carrier tow truck is a parked truck with two cars on board and a pale ramp you can see from behind, which rises to its top deck. They appear on the track once the road lane places them.
