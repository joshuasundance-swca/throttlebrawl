The Pacific Northwest no longer drizzles at every hour. Rain now belongs to the light and to the race: the noon and dusk skies rain, the dawn fog is dry, and a few races say their own. Chuckanut Drive is dry at noon (the bay under a marine layer), and the Gorge's two dawn races and Rainout rain. Bridge City's neon dusk keeps its rain. A dry race has no drizzle on the screen and no rain on the helmet; a menu race's own Dry or Rain pick still beats all of it. Of the 17 Pacific Northwest races, 6 are now dry and 11 wet (counted by the new test, which asserts a band, not these numbers).

San Francisco has traffic areas now, so the Golden Gate's lanes (the bridge deck, and Hawk Hill, Conzelman Road, Vista Point and the toll plaza) take the city's cars, the shuttle, the box truck and the robotaxi, and no rental e-scooter riders or delivery e-bikes, who ride the kerb. The rest of San Francisco keeps its mix.

For devs: `raceSky` in `src/app/regions.ts` is the one answer (pick, then the event's `weather` in its own light, then the light's palette `rain`) and `showRegion` in `src/app/index.ts` uses it; its cache now keys on the event as well, since two events on one road can differ. The event schema takes an optional `weather` of `dry` or `rain`, left out of the sim hash (`SIM_EXCLUDED_FIELDS`), so replays are not renewed by it. Audio: `setRoad(road, wet)` and a `dry` flag on the soundscape input silence the Pacific Northwest's helmet rain in a dry race; the default is wet, as before. Content: the region's base palette lost its `rain`, the `noon` and `dusk` options gained it; four events got a `weather`. San Francisco's `traffic.areas` are `gg-deck` (a new deck tag, written by the bake config's `deckTags` on `osm-sf-golden-gate` and by hand into the baked `osm-sf-gg-bridge`, since the OSM extract is not in the repo; the python lint list and `region-routes.test.ts` know it) and `headlands`. Both use only types already in San Francisco's mix, so no new model, pool or draw call; the type roll is the same roll as before, so SF races outside those roads spawn as they did. Tests: `src/app/regions.test.ts` (the weather rules, with a control per rule), `src/audio/soundscape.test.ts`, `src/content/schema/schema.test.ts`, `tests/sim/pnw-real-world.test.ts`, `tests/sim/sf-real-world.test.ts` (checked to fail with the area's tag renamed) and `tools/gis/routes-sf-pt3.test.ts` (config and baked road agree). Docs: `docs/content-packs.md` (Event, Palette, scenery tags). Not done: a scenic-byway area for Chuckanut and semis for I-5 (sheets H5, I2), the Hawk Hill zone's people, a dry option for the other regions. Not phone-verified; no browser was run.
