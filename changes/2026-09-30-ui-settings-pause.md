The settings screen now has tabs (Sound, Race, Controls, Display), and you can pick mph or km/h under Display. The pause menu gains "Controls and HUD", which opens the controls settings without leaving the race. The attack button now says "▼ KICK" under HIT: swipe down on it to kick. The menus no longer hide behind a rival's speech bubble. More settings (difficulty, assists, tilt, vibration, slow motion and the rest) appear on their own as each feature is switched on.
