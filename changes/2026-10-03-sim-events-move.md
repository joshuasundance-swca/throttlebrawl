Weird events that move (the pitch deck's #9). Road events no longer just stand there:

- In the Keys, a pickup towing a boat loses the trailer as you close in, and the boat slides across into the other lane. Four small red signs tell you first: YOUR BOAT / IS NOT / IN THE WATER / CHECK LANE TWO.
- In the Pacific Northwest, a log truck on a downhill sheds six logs that roll to rest across both lanes. Riding over one is a hop (rivals hop them too).
- In San Francisco, on a cable street only, a cable car ahead loses its grip and rolls back down at you, shoving the cars behind it. On any other road it never takes the race's event slot.
- A lane vote in every region: a green gantry splits two events (GATOR CROSSING | PARADE in the Keys, LOGGING PARADE | LOOSE HAY in the Pacific Northwest, PRODUCT LAUNCH | ROADWORK in San Francisco). The side you ride under picks the one that turns up about 400 m on, and its half of the gantry lights up.
- The Keys' gator crossing: a crossing guard with a stop paddle and four gators taking their time across both lanes.

Still one or two road events a race. Each moving moment fires a new `setPieceBeat` event, so sound and barks can follow (the cable car's bell is the audio lane's follow-up). Every event sign now shares one post mesh, so a sign costs one draw instead of two. Done, not phone-verified.
