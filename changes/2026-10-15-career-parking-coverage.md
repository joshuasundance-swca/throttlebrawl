Career checks follow authored career maps and career-event primary routes. Quick Play regions and
additional selectable routes need no new campaign chapter or node. Every authored map still registers
in chapter order; every node's selected length and route must exist in its region and reach the race
config. Event types, progression, secrets and garage checks remain.

Cop parking checks now run races from new regions in the default shard alongside the Keys, while
keeping the dedicated Pacific Northwest and San Francisco shards. Coverage requires every authored
race exactly once, and the existing travel-lane parking checks still run on each selected race.
