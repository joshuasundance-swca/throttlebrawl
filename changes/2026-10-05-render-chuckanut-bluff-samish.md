Chuckanut Drive and Lake Samish look like their places. On Chuckanut, where the bay side is a real cliff, the road runs along a shelf: a low stone parapet, the red-barked madrones behind it, and then the sandstone drops away to the water, so the bay and the islands open up below you instead of a wall of firs. The uphill side is cut sandstone, mossy and ferny, with boulders fallen at its foot and the firs above it. On I-5's Lake Samish loop, East Shore Drive runs beside the lake: cabins with green roofs stand between the road and the water, small docks with rowboats run out from the bank, and the lake itself lies there at its real height.

For devs: the identity sheets' shared seam, "a bluff/lake land theme with a drop and no skirt" (H1, I3), and Codex CX6's wiring items 7 and 8. Three new tags, each a land theme that outranks the `forest` beside it (`src/render/scenery.ts`): `bluff` (a 10 m shelf past the verge that drops sheer, no terrain skirt; its verge 4 m of dirt to a `hard` edge, `src/road/cross-section.ts`), `rock-cut` (the forest's verge; the scatter's trees stand 9.5 m back, behind the cut, `THEME_NEAR_M`) and `lake` (a 20 m strip that drops at its edge; 4 m of soft grass). The drop is the promenade seawall's (`SEAWALL_LAND_M`); a drop narrows to half on the inside of a bend too tight for it (`src/render/road-mesh.ts`). CX6's `models/scenery/pnw-shore` is the scenery kind `pnwShore` (`src/render/models.ts`), loaded for a network with any of the three tags; the Pacific Northwest kit draws it (`src/render/roadside.ts` `PNW_KIT`, six rules at the end of the list, after B9's, so no other rule's seeded placements move): the parapet in 6 m sections end to end with its face on the hard edge, the 20 m bluff sections with their lip at the drop's edge, the cuts in runs of 24 to 36 m whose solid body keeps the boulders out of the rock (`body`), the boulders past the verge between the runs, the cabin with its porch to the road, and the dock with its root at the shore at the water's level (`waterline`). A section stands only where its theme covers its whole length (`whole`), and the parapet, the bluff's lip and the boulders stand under a tree's crown (`low`). B9's madrones (#578) stand on the bluff's shelf too, behind the parapet (their rule takes the `bluff` theme as well as the forest). The props merge into the roadside's stretches, so they add no draw call.

The tags are B9's side runs (the bake config's `sideRuns`), which a network bake now writes too (`tools/gis/src/tbgis/network.py`; a run off its road is refused, and a branch's connector never takes its neighbour's runs). Their ranges, by the baked road's own metres: Chuckanut's `bluff` (right) only inside B9's `bay-bluff` runs, the road's drop side as `tbgis.drops` found it, and only where a USGS 3DEP probe 30 and 60 m to each side of the baked road found the deeper drop of a cliff, the ground falling 15 m within 30 m and 30 m within 60 m while the left side rose: 3.15 km in 5 runs on Samish Cliffs and Oyster Creek, of 3.76 km of `bay-bluff`; `rock-cut` (left) where the ground rose 6 m within 15 m and 15 m within 30 m, 2.56 km in 8 runs; East Shore Drive's `lake` (right) where the OSM outline of Lake Samish lies within 30 m of the baked road, s 890 to 1595 and 2590 to 2940. Both bakes were redone through `tools/gis`; the files differ from main only in these tags and their provenance (a control re-bake with no config change first matched the committed files apart from provenance).

The lake is a water `floor` in the network's backdrop file (`packs/region-pnw/assets/backdrop/pacific-northwest/networks/osm-pnw-samish.json`), at 82.85 m, the level 3DEP gives the lake's surface (12 of 12 probes), in the network's own metres: the OSM outline joined to an apron along the `lake` runs that reaches under the shore strip, less every road's lanes, verges and a metre, 3.34 km². The renderer reads the network's own water when the race loads (`src/render/backdrop/water.ts`, `loadNetworkWater`) and places the roadside again once it is in, so the docks stand on the water as drawn (half a metre under the piece's `y`, `FLOOR_UNDER_M`); with no water floor, no dock is placed and nothing else moves.
