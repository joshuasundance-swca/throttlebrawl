Each region now hides a pirate radio station. Ride near one spot on the road and the radio crackles through a burst of tuning static into a station that is not on the dial: Contraband Cay in the Keys (dub), Static Cedar in the Pacific Northwest (ambient, foggy and drumless) and Zero Day in San Francisco (game-console chiptune). Ride on and your own station comes back. A radio switched off stays off.
