The existing weapon glint now follows the simulation's selected steal opportunity for you. It stays
quiet during an early weapon wind-up, a held-weapon kick or while you are not ready, instead of
glinting throughout every armed wind-up. No marker or automatic action was added.

The start card explains finishing your swing, riding alongside and making a fresh attack press as
the weapon glints. Holding the button does not repeat the attempt; a snatch replaces a weapon you
already carry. The two help rows span the existing card columns so the instructions can wrap.

The old test expecting a glint throughout an armed wind-up is replaced with selected-holder and
no-candidate controls, including absent previews and real rider rigs. Existing browser font checks
also examine the start instructions and their scrollable entry at normal and saved largest text.
