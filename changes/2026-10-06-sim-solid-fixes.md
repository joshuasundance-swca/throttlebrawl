Street furniture is more forgiving, and the rivals and cops now see it. Only a hydrant, pole, trunk or planter dead ahead of your front wheel crashes you at speed; clipping one with your bar or shoulder is a wobble and you slide past. Key West's Old Town now has its palm planters and frangipanis in a row by the kerb, so the middle of its sidewalk is a clear line to ride. Coming down from a jump onto a scooter rider no longer crashes you; you only wobble, as you do when you clip one on the ground. Rivals and cops no longer ride into street trees, lamps and planters they never saw: they steer round them, slow for the bends on those streets so they are not carried onto the sidewalk, and ride back off a sidewalk slowly. A rival knocked into a tree in a fight still goes down.

For devs: three fixes from the live check of #610, #618 and #619 (scratch notes only).
- **Kerb riders from above** (mustFix 1): `contacts()` in `src/sim/traffic/index.ts` ran the roof branch before the light kerb rider's soft contact; a light kerb rider (`kerbSoft`, `dodges`) now skips the roof, and a drop on one is its soft contact with `hit: 'top'`. `src/sim/traffic/air-height.test.ts`: 12 and 3 m/s drops onto a scooter rider never crash and never ride its top; control: the same 12 m/s drop onto a sedan crashes on its roof.
- **The AI sees the furniture** (mustFix 2): `furnitureSeen` in `src/sim/ai/sense.ts` gives each solid piece as an obstacle that stands still. The rivals (`driveRider`) and the cops (`trafficGuard`) keep their picked line clear of it, go round it and never steer into one alongside; only moving traffic blocks the way across to a line (with the pieces too, a rival on a tree-lined sidewalk never found a way back). On a street lined with it they keep to `bendSpeed` (the speed that holds each bend ahead, from the new `holdSpeedMps` in `src/sim/riders`), off the road among it to 8 m/s (`offRoadAmongFixed`), and boxed in behind a piece they crawl at 3 m/s rather than stop. `tests/sim/ai-furniture.test.ts`, 3 seeds each on Russian Hill and Duval, furniture on and off, the full world with road events off: on main's AI, rival crashes were 87 with the furniture on (41 on furniture, 20 of them not a takedown) against 39 off, and cops crashed on furniture 7 times. Now: 29 on (6 on furniture, all takedowns) against 39 off, and 0 for the cops. Controls: with the furniture off nothing of it is met; with it on, rivals still meet it. In a scratch A/B on Duval (5 seeds), the bend rule cost the rivals 0.1 % of their mean finish time; the furniture itself costs them about 5 to 9 % on Russian Hill and Duval, against it off.
- **Forgiving** (punch item 1): `SOLID_GRAZE_M` in `src/sim/riders/furniture.ts` (the bike's half width, 0.4 m) replaces traffic's `GRAZE_M` (0.3 m) for fixed things in `contactAxis`, so square on means the piece is in the bike's own line. Old Town's planters (across 0.6 to 0.7 m past the drawn verge, square to the street, `r` 0.6) and frangipanis (0.5 to 0.7 m, `r` 0.5) moved to the kerb, in `src/road/furniture.ts` and its mirror in `src/render/roadside.ts` (a small render edit that only this change needs). Contact-to-crash for the S1 sidewalk rider, solid pieces only, seeds 1 to 8, before and after: downtown 38 % and 22 % (blind and swerving) to 27 % and 7 %; Russian Hill 23 % and 34 % to 11 % and 17 %; Duval 55 % and 41 % to 55 % and 18 %. A blind rider whose line runs along the new planter row still meets each planter square on. `tests/sim/furniture-sidewalk.test.ts` now holds each place's swerving share to 30 % or less (Russian Hill and Duval were 32 % on its seeds) and the middle of Duval's sidewalk clear (it met 35 planters and frangipanis square on before). `src/sim/riders/furniture.test.ts`: a lamp 0.15 m off the bike's line is a graze (it was a crash). Docs: `docs/content-packs.md` ("Contact outcomes: one rule") and `docs/architecture.md`. Not phone-verified, and the new planter row has not been looked at in a frame.
