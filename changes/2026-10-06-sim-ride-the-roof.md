You can land on a truck and ride it. Coming down on anything solid whose top is big enough for your bike (a car's or a truck's roof, the car on a ramp truck's top deck, a parked pickup, the ferry's coffee cart and stair towers, a log pile, a parked car on the waterfront) is now a landing, judged exactly like one on the road: land a flip clean and you ride away on top of it, land nose first and you crash. On a moving truck you ride along with it: hold the brake to stay put, give it a little gas to ride forward, let go and the wind slowly rolls you off the back. Steer off the side or ride off an end and you drop to the road at the truck's speed. If the truck brakes hard you roll on over its roof, and if it stops dead you are thrown off. Smaller tops (a hydrant, a lamp, a bench, a planter, a stump, a carved bear, a scooter rider) are still things you hit: come down on one hard and you crash, softly and you wobble off it. Nobody on the road can kick you while you are up on a roof, and two riders on the same roof can fight. A ride on top of a vehicle of a second or more pays a little style cash (ROOF RIDE) when you leave it. The chalk mark shows where you will come down on a roof. Building fronts are still walls, however high you fly: landing on building roofs is decided and comes next. Not phone-verified.

For devs: one rule, in `src/sim/riders/supports.ts` (the maintainer, 2026-10-06, `[decided]`: "landing on vehicles shouldn't necessarily be a crash", "include landing on big solid things beyond vehicles"). A top holds the bike when the rider's own box (`riderHitbox`, 2.0 by 0.8 m) fits on it (`holdsBike`); supports are traffic vehicles (their rigid drawn box at `vehicleHeightM`; none for a ghost), ramp truck bodies (`truckBodyTop`; new `truckBodyBox` and `truckVelocityS` in `features.ts`), solid hazards that are not light, and solid street pieces drawn no taller than a rider's head whose footprint holds the bike. `src/sim/riders/index.ts`: `stepAirborne` lands a rider coming down from above onto a support through `land()`, which judges it in the support's frame and puts the rider on it (`data.on`, `data.object`, the vehicle as target); `stepGrounded` rides it in the support's frame (a signed speed over it; throttle, brake and coasting on it; the air drag on the speed through the air; the support's velocity carried; a change in that velocity rolls the bike on, a 3 m/s change in a tick wobbles, a `traffic.solidHitMps` one is a crash with `hit: 'jolt'`), and riding off an edge is a take-off at the world velocity. A top too small to hold the bike, met from above, is decided by the fall speed (`topContact`, `hit: 'top'`). The chalk mark (`touchdownOf`) and the air controls' ground forecast count tops. `src/sim/traffic/index.ts` skips the vehicle a rider stands on, measures contacts with other vehicles at his world velocity, holds a roof the riders missed for one tick, pushes a rider off a small roof, and pays no near miss up on a support. `src/sim/tumble/index.ts` starts a crash on a support at the world velocity. `src/sim/combat/index.ts` adds `combat.reachHeightM` (1 m; absent, any height): hits, bumps of the attack and snatches reach only that far up or down, and the rivals (`src/sim/ai/index.ts`) do not chase a fight out of reach; the landing hit reads it too; rider bumps (`src/sim/riders/contact.ts`) skip pairs 1.6 m apart in height. `src/sim/race/style.ts` pays `roofRide` (`race.styleRoofScale` 0.5 × the oncoming cash a second, from `race.styleRoofMinS` 1 s) and runs no oncoming stretch up on a support. The switch is `riders.supports` (on; absent, the old rules). The support state is the system state `supports`, written only when a rider first lands on a support, so a race where nobody does hashes as before.

Tests, written failing first, each with a control: `src/sim/riders/supports.test.ts` (an 11.2 m/s drop onto a box truck lands clean and rides, 0.5 m from where it landed after 1 s on the brake; the old rule crashes it; a nose-first drop crashes as a landing; the maintainer's case, a hood launch to 9.5 m with two backflips onto a 3.4 m box truck, lands clean (pitch 0.08 rad off) and rides 12 ticks before rolling off its end, where the old rule crashed at 10.96 m/s; 10 s on a semi on the brake carried 200.0 m, then off its tail with the wind and a clean landing; a side drop at 18.6 m/s lands clean; a semi braking hard rolls the rider 10 m forward and off its front with no jolt; a hard stop throws him at 20 m/s; the ramp truck's top car and a parked pickup are landed on and ridden off, where the old rule crashed or passed over; a hydrant from above crashes at 12.2 m/s and wobbles at 3.2 m/s, nothing under the old rule; the chalk mark lands 0.11 m from the real landing on a moving truck; a semi ride replays to the same hash tick by tick; a jump over a car gives the same state with the switch on and off). `src/sim/combat/reach-height.test.ts`, `src/sim/riders/contact.test.ts`, `src/sim/race/style.test.ts` and `src/sim/traffic/air-height.test.ts` (its old roof cases now pinned to the switch off, with the new rule beside them).
