The producer's asks ("Take a rival down before the bridge. +$300", and "Got it.") and the landing one-liners ("TEN OUT OF TEN, SAYS A PELICAN") now run on the top ticker with the rest of the pop-ups, instead of in a box low on the screen and a plate under your bike. A landing line no longer covers your rear wheel, on any frame. Long-press a landing line on the strip to cut it, as with a rival's line; it also shows up in the pause menu's "recently seen" list, and so do the signs and billboards you rode past. The slow-frames note adds a line to the ticker saying the Classic look is in the pause menu. A wheelie that climbs too high now buzzes the phone, as it was always meant to.

For devs: `src/app/ticker-feed.ts` is the app side of the ticker: `landingLineFor` picks the line on the player's own landing that paid (the same rule the old overlay used), `producerAskItem` and `producerThanksItem` build the asks, `withoutLandingLines` hands the renderer a catalog with an empty landing pool (so `src/render/air-pays.ts` never draws a line; deleting that code is a later task), and `createSeenPoll` reads `renderer.visibleContent()` every 30 frames of a race into `narrative.noteSeen`. `app/index.ts` also calls `input.onMoves(snapshot.moves)` after every sim step (null at a race start). The learn-by-riding prompts stay in the career prompt box for now, and the floating slow-frames toast is still ui's; both are ui follow-ups. The layout check (`ui-style-popups.spec.ts`) now measures the strip as a landing line against the player's bike on every frame of a landing, fails if it ever covers it, and checks the pause list for the line. With signs and billboards in it, the pause list fills to its 20 rows and scrolls on a phone held sideways, as the pause screen was built to (playtest 1c); `tests/e2e/ui-screens.spec.ts` counted the scrolled-out rows as text leaving the screen, so its overflow check now lets text pass that sits inside an on-screen scrolling column, side to side, and prints how many it let pass (the keeper's fix on this PR). Not phone-verified; no browser was run locally.
