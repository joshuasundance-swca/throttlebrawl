The bikes launch much harder. Flat out from a standstill, the starter bike now reaches 60 mph in about 3 seconds instead of 6, and 90 mph in about 9 instead of 13. Top speed, braking and how much the shoulder slows you are unchanged, and the rivals get the same kick off the line. The tuning panel has a new "Launch punch" slider; set it to 1 for the old feel.
