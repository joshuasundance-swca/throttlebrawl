Flips and tricks. In the air, pull back (brake) to lift the nose and hold it for a backflip; hold a kick (swipe down and keep your finger down, or K) to tip forward into a front flip; steer hard to lay the bike over for a whip; land nose-high for a wheelie landing. Each trick landed pays style cash on top of the airtime (a backflip pays double the airtime, a double backflip four times). Holding the gas, the bike flies level and lands lined up. Land mid-flip and you go flying.
