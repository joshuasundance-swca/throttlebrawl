Pivot FM. In San Francisco, when Pivot rides near you, his own station takes over your radio with a burst of static, and every eight bars it pivots to a completely different genre: surf, then chiptune, then dub, then grunge, each one the start of a real song from the radio's own bands. Ride away from him and your station comes back. Knock him down and it goes to dead air; a moment later he announces, in his own voice: "We're excited to announce our next chapter." Once he is back on his bike, the station returns with a new track. It never takes a radio that is off, and it is not on the dial. The line can be cut like any other bark (long-press it, or use the pause screen's recently seen list), and so can each of its six tracks.
