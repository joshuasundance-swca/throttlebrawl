On San Francisco's Twin Peaks and headlands roads, the motorcycle can cross connected grass beside a soft verge and return to the road without hitting an unseen course edge. The drawn strip still ends at real gaps and drops. Headlands batteries, brush and rock outcrops, and Presidio trees now have matching physical contacts that remain visible at low scenery settings. Large batteries stand clear of curved lanes for their full drawn length.
