A crash on a hairpin no longer hands you back facing the wrong way. On the Twin Peaks route, a crash at the foot of the climb could slide you round the bend far enough that you got back on the bike facing downhill, and the race never ended. You now always get back on facing the way the race goes (a rider who was riding the wrong way still keeps going the wrong way).

Behind the scenes: the camera's crest check on Twin Peaks had only been passing because the test bot was riding that route backward after this crash. Ridden the right way, the hilltops there are gentler, so the check now counts a crest from a 0.6 m drop instead of 0.8 m.
