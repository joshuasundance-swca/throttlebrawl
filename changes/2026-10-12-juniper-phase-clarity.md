Juniper's shoulder check, cut-in and pull-away now have distinct body language. She sits out of
the cutting crouch as she pulls away, and her earned punches and kicks keep their full animation
when they overlap the move.

For devs: this is a cut-in pose change only. Her speed, timing, targeting and combat rules are
unchanged. Focused rig tests cover phase contrast, riding contacts, neutral reset and whole-rig
combat precedence. Shipped-model rendering and phone recognition are separate checks.
