Traffic both ways in the sim (M1 traffic-1). Cars and trucks follow each other in their lanes with Intelligent-Driver-Model car-following, so they brake behind a stopped rider, a crash or a stalled car. They are populated deterministically within 400 m of the sim anchors (the player and the rivals), never spawn inside reaction range (fastest anchor top speed plus fastest cruise speed, times 2 s: 125 m at M1 speeds), and are recycled to the front of the window when they fall out of it. Two tuning sliders, `traffic.densitySame` and `traffic.densityOncoming` (0 to 3, default 1, one vehicle per 120 m of lane), set each direction's density, so oncoming traffic can go to zero. A first touch of a car wobbles you (a `wobble` event), a second touch while still wobbling or any touch of a truck crashes you (a `crash` event with `data.cause` `traffic` for tumble-1), and passing a vehicle within 1 m untouched fires `nearMiss`. Vehicles only drive roads that have drive lanes and no shortcut lane, preferring the route's roads at a junction. Rare seeded lane changes happen where a direction has two lanes (the M1 road has one each way).

The three vehicle types (Rental Sedan, Pickup With a Kayak, Box Truck) ship as `draft`, so the public game (prod) has no traffic yet while dev and staging builds do: the renderer does not draw vehicles yet, and invisible cars that crash you would be worse than none. Flip them to `live` once render-1 draws vehicles. The region's traffic mix lists them with weights that equal the sim's per-category defaults (car 5, truck 2), because `SimConfig` does not carry the region's mix weights yet. All numbers are `[default]` starting values.
