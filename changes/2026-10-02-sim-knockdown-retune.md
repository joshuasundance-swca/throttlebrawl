Rivals go down sooner. A fresh rival now takes about three kicks, five punches or two swings of the lead pipe or the driftwood club to knock off, instead of six kicks or ten punches, and never just one. Weapons hit harder than fists. Rivals hit you exactly as hard as before. The tuning panel has three new combat sliders: "Punch and kick damage", "Weapon damage" and "Rival damage on you".
