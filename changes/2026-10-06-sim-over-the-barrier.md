In the air you can now go over a barrier. A rail or wall holds you only below its top: fly higher than it and you sail over, and what lies beyond decides what happens. Over a bridge rail into the sea you splash, wait out the splash penalty and wake on the bridge where you went over. Over Chuckanut's parapet, or over the Golden Gate's railing, it is a long drop with the same penalty and the same wake-up on the road. Off the new Seven Mile Bridge, fly toward the old bridge beside it and you can land on it (a landing judged like any other, and not a shortcut found). Over a wall with ground behind it, you come down at the road's edge. Below a barrier's top it holds you exactly as before. Building fronts are still walls at any height: buildings are not part of the game's physics yet. Rivals and cops obey the same rule. A crashed rider's tumbling body follows it too, so the Golden Gate's railing no longer stops one.

For devs: the rule's road half is `src/road/beyond.ts` (`edgeTopAt`: a barrier's `heightM`, a derived hard edge's drawn top by `EDGE_TOP_BY_TAG`, 0 at a water edge, Infinity at a `BUILDING_FRONT_TAGS` front or an untagged hard edge, null at a ground edge; `pastAt`: water, drop or ground from the tags; `WATER_LEVEL_M`/`waterLevelOf`: sea level but Lake Samish 82.85 m, chosen 0 where nothing is drawn under a drop). The sim half is `overBarrier` and `overFall` in `src/sim/riders/gap.ts`, which replace `airWallSkip`: the `jumpable` flag is retired into the height rule (still read, it changes nothing; `jumpableWallAt` is gone). An over-the-edge fall is a `crash` with `cause: 'over'`, `overboard`, `past`, `dropM`, `high` (over the new tuning key `riders.highDropM`, 25 m `[default]`), `floorY` and the crossing; sim/tumble carries `over`, `past`, `dropM` and `high` on `railOver`, `splash` and `respawn` for the presentation lane (a high drop is the clean cut-away, a low one into water the splash). The tumble's barrier line now uses the same rule (any rail or wall above its top with water or a drop past it; a water edge with nothing standing still holds a body) and falls to the network's water level. Another road under a flying rider is found with `RoadNetwork.surfaceUnder` and handed over once the rider is inside its limits; sim/race's shortcut stamp gives up a run that leaves by air (`hoppedRoads`), and combat's shove no longer snaps a rider out past the edge back (`outPastEdge`). The road lint now refuses a barrier with no `heightM` (every barrier in the packs had one; none filled in). New state is written only when a rider goes over, so a race where nobody does keeps none. Tests: `tests/sim/over-barrier.test.ts` (the Seven Mile rail at 3 m and, as the control, at 0.6 m; onto the old bridge; Chuckanut's bluff; the Golden Gate's railing; Duval's shopfronts at 8 m; record and replay; the no-new-state check), `tests/sim/over-barrier-roads.test.ts` (every route network's barriers and edges, and the water level against the backdrops), `src/road/beyond.test.ts`, `src/sim/riders/gap.test.ts`, `src/road/validate-gap.test.ts`; `tools/road/truck-shortcuts.test.ts` now reads the static-truck shortcuts' 1.2 m wall as any wall (its rides still land every flight that clears the truck on the cut, now by the handover onto the road past the wall). Docs: architecture ("Over the barrier", "Over the rail"), content-packs ("Barriers"), product-spec, tone-guide (the Golden Gate). The chalk mark's touch-down forecast (`touchdownOf`) still marches over the road's plane past the edge; not phone-verified.
