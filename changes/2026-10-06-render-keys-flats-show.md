The Keys' shallow water now shows its seagrass and sand. Over the flats there are darker green-teal beds of seagrass and paler aqua patches of sand, each with a clear edge, and the open sea past them stays one sea. Before, the patches were there in the data and too faint to see: the live check found the water one turquoise on the Seven Mile, at Bahia Honda and at Spanish Harbor, at noon and at golden hour.

For devs: playtest 4 run C's fix check, punch item 1 (not fixed by #616), lane H3. #616's test counted the views whose mesh vertex colours held both a dark and a pale band; it never asked whether the band shows. The new test asks that.
1. The test. `tests/sim/sea-view.test-util.ts` works out the frame the way the app draws it, for the real follow camera on the 915 by 412 phone screen at 38 m/s. What the land hides: every triangle of the road scene (deck, road, shoulders, land strips, rails, the scene's own scenery at full roadside density) goes into a depth buffer (a small rasteriser with the near plane clipped), and a sea pixel counts only where no triangle is in front of the sea's depth. The colour seen: the default (ink, `kodak`) look's water colour times the mesh's vertex tint (bilinear over the lattice), times the look's exposure, mixed toward the haze by three's fog (a smoothstep from 220 to 700 m of view depth, checked against `look.setupScene`), through the film grade. A patch shows where that colour differs from the same pixel with no tint by at least 12 delta E (CIE76; 2.3 is a just-noticeable difference) over a connected area of at least 1,000 px² (about 33 by 33 px, or 100 by 10 px). `tests/sim/keys-flats-show.test.ts` asks that at least 60 % of the views over the flats (a view every 250 m along every road, three seeds, noon, golden hour and dusk, flats filling at least 1 % of the screen) show a seagrass patch and a sand patch, on the Seven Mile and on Bahia Honda (which holds Spanish Harbor). On main it fails: 0 of 441 views on the Seven Mile and 0 of 225 on Bahia Honda, seagrass in none (its best is 11.2 delta E at noon, 60 m out, under the line of 12) and sand in 267 and 92. Control: #616's tints with the sea's old edge, 0 % again. Also checked: the haze closes over a patch at the fog's end and has taken a good part of it halfway, and the open sea (depth 0.3 and over, 2,702 of 3,721 points around the Seven Mile) carries no patch whatever the seed, while the flats do change with it.
2. The fix, in `SEA_BANDS` (`sea-bands.ts`): patches 90 m (was 60), their edge 7 hundredths of the noise (was 32), the sand tint 6, 1.6, 1.25 (was 1.3, 1.2, 0.95: the water has almost no red, so only a large multiple of it reads pale; the default look's water, #1e8e98, goes to about #57beaf) and the seagrass tint 0.45, 0.5, 0.4 (was 0.5, 0.72, 0.62; about #177364), and the patches give way to the deep between depth 0.05 and 0.3 (was 0 to 0.5, which let a tint of 6 leak into the far sea). Now 412 of 441 views on the Seven Mile (93 %) and 188 of 225 on Bahia Honda (84 %) show both. #616's own proxy test still passes (95 % and 97 % of its frames), and so does `sea-bands.test.ts` with the salt ponds (the pond stays 40 delta E from every sea colour).
3. The cruise ship from abreast (punch item 5): left as it is. In the same ray test the upper decks already show over the roofs at the top left of the frame at s 1469 (10.7, 6.1 and 10.6 % of the screen on seeds 1 to 3; 9 % on seed 1 with the old, smaller ship, so #616 could not have changed it), as run C's own frame at s 1468 shows. A 24 m cross street cut through both rows of fronts beside the stern added 0.2 to 4 points of that for the 0.6 s a rider takes to pass it, and needs a new rule beside the landmark's feature in the road file and the bake config. Not worth it.
