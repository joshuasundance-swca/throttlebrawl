The start screen answers your tap faster. The sound's context (the browser's audio device) used to be made inside the tap, and on the dev machine that one call took about 400 of the tap's 408 ms before the menu could show (an idle tap took a median of 462.5 ms to reach the menu; polish K's check). It is now made as soon as the start screen has painted, still silent, and the tap only switches it on, so the sound still starts from your touch as a phone requires. A device that refuses the early context, or a page that is never shown, falls back to making it in the tap as before.

For devs: `AudioFace.prepare()` in `src/audio/index.ts` makes the context outside the tap and does nothing for an offline render, after the tap, or when it already has one. The engine's context factory now remembers the context it makes, so a late `prepare()` makes no second one. `createApp` in `src/app/index.ts` calls it from a `requestAnimationFrame` then `setTimeout(0)` after `ui.show('start')`, so the screen paints before the work. `src/audio/lazy-engine.test.ts` holds the order on a fake context (the control: without `prepare()` the tap makes it); `tests/e2e/audio-ready-before-tap.spec.ts` holds it in Chromium (a spy on `AudioContext`: one context made outside a click, none in the tap, `resume()` inside the click with the page activated by it, and a control that makes one inside a click and must be seen). The keeper fixed the spec after train 454: the start tap asks for fullscreen before it resumes the sound, which uses up the tap's transient activation (`isActive`), so the spec reads the sticky one (`hasBeenActive`), which is what a context's autoplay check reads; and the control's click handler sits on the clicked element, since one on the window ran after the spy had closed the click. `docs/architecture.md` ("Audio") is updated. The long tasks that hold an early tap during boot (300 to 450 ms each, from about 1.9 s to 3.7 s after navigation in polish K's traces) are `render/`'s road builds as the backdrop's models arrive, not in this lane; they are named in the lane report. Not phone-verified; the browser spec runs in CI only (no local browser, per AGENTS.md).
