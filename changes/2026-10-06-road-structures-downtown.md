The downtowns' buildings are planned by the road now, the first layer of the physical world (the maintainer, 2026-10-06: "consistent physics and gameplay is important here so players know what to expect and how to interact with the world"; "Yeah that sounds good :)"). Nothing in a race changes: the sim does not read the plan yet. San Francisco's downtown looks exactly as before; downtown Portland looks as before but for a few lots on some seeds (below).

- `src/road/structures/downtown.ts` holds render/downtown.ts's placement rules, moved unchanged: San Francisco's front-row towers, plaza towers, headquarters, second row and cross-street buildings, and downtown Portland's fronts, pink towers, second row, closing fronts, food carts and bike racks. Each lot carries its structure at its drawn shape (footprint, base down to the sunk foot, flat roof at the drawn top). Two layers, `downtown-sf` and `downtown-pdx`, fill the structure plan (`STRUCTURE_LAYERS`), and `STRUCTURE_MODELS` gains 16 rows (the headquarters and CX3's 15 tower modules). render/downtown.ts draws the plan's lots and keeps its sidewalks, paving, cross streets and traffic.
- What depended on loading is fixed: San Francisco's towers are always the stacked modules' lots (if the modules ever fail to load, render stretches the old kit to the same box instead of shrinking the lots); Portland's lots take their sizes from the fixed box table, not the loaded kit; and its land comes from `src/road/land.ts` (`landReachOf`, the road scene's land-strip rule read from the network), which matches the drawn scene's reach at every metre of both downtown networks.
- The picture: `src/render/downtown-main.test.ts` holds every drawn building, cart and rack, every stretch of surfaces and every crossing to what main drew (`downtown-main.fixture.json`, San Francisco seeds 7 and 8, Portland seeds 1, 2, 3, 4 and 7, within 2 cm). Four Portland lots on Broadway differ, in seeds 1 and 3 only: one office block's front stands at the sidewalk instead of 4 m back, and two second-row lots swap a loft and a pink tower. Main's lot edge sat exactly on a keep-clear line there and the loaded kit's float error (2 to 4 micrometres over its round sizes) decided it; with the kit's exact sizes the port drew all five seeds as main did.
- Shared config that only this change needs: the planners and the land rule build into their own lazy chunk (`road-structures`, a second code-splitting group in `scripts/sim-chunk.mjs` and `vite.config.ts`), never in the first load, and the sim code hash covers that chunk too, so a planner change still changes the replay key; their dynamic imports bring Vite's preload helper into the sim chunk. The module map lets a module import road's `structures/*` planners directly (`scripts/module-map.mjs`), so the lazy downtown layer can draw one without pulling it into the first load; `docs/architecture.md` records both.

Tests: `scripts/hitboxes.test.ts` holds all 1,625 downtown structures of San Francisco (seed 7, with and without the modules) and Portland (seed 1) to their drawn models' points (worst face 1 cm, tolerance 5 cm), with a control that a structure moved 20 cm, 20 cm wider or a storey short is found; made to place a structure's base at its model's underside, it failed at 5.91 m. `src/render/land-reach.test.ts` (21,682 samples, 0 differ, and a control). `src/road/structures/downtown.test.ts` (the lazy layers fill the registry, one structure per lot; the same plan from a fresh network; another for another seed). `scripts/sim-chunk.test.ts` gains a planner change that moves the hash, and `tests/e2e/dev-report.spec.ts` (the keeper, after train 340 found its replay key wrong) now works out the key's code part as the build does: the sim chunk, then each planner chunk the loaded scripts name, in file name order (on a local build it found the very hash the build wrote into the entry chunk); and `scripts/first-load.test.ts`'s lazy list gains the planners (with the planners put back in the sim chunk, it failed). First-load JavaScript 461.5 KB gzip (38.5 KB headroom); the planners' lazy chunk is 7.5 KB gzip. Not phone-verified.
