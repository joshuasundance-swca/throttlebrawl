Settings has a new Access tab with three options. Text size makes the words bigger (Large or Largest) in the menus, the settings, the ticker along the top, the objective, the heat badge and the speed, position and health boxes. Reduce motion calms the rest: the camera keeps less of its lean roll and speed zoom, the white flash on a hit is gone, the slow-motion tint and the speed lines are half as strong, the cops' red and blue light bar flashes once a second instead of four times, and the HUD stops sliding and flashing. It also turns the screen shake off, and it switches on by itself if your phone is set to reduce motion. Reduce screen shake moved to the same tab and still does only the shake. The wheelie gauge also got easier to read without telling colours apart: a dark tick on each zone boundary, a bracket around the sweet band and a hatch over the loop-out zone. On a short phone the HUD's text grows less than the menus' (a 360 px high screen gets a little over a quarter, a 320 px one none) so the road ahead stays clear.

For devs: the setting names are `reduceMotion` and `textSize` (`normal`, `large`, `largest`) in the settings record, additive (the version stays 1). Text: `src/ui/text-size.ts` (the factors 1, 1.2 and 1.4), every font size in `src/ui` CSS is now in `rem` and `applyTextSize` in `src/ui/index.ts` sets the page's root size; the speed, position and health boxes scale through the layout record's own `scale` (`scaledElement`), and `layoutTop` takes a `textScale` that grows its reserved slots (`hudTextScale` caps it by screen height). `.screen` now scrolls when it must and centres with `safe center`, and the settings rows wrap, so nothing is unreachable at the largest size. Motion: `camera.setMotionAmount` and `camera.motionCalm` (the chase and far views' roll and FOV kick; the helmet view and the moves share the calm), `renderer.setReduceMotion` (`src/render/calm.ts`: no hit flash, half tint and speed lines, the light bar's rate, steady event lights), `ui.setReduceMotion` (`data-motion="reduced"` stops the HUD's animations), and `src/app/motion.ts` (which setting sets which amount; the phone's `prefers-reduced-motion` counts as on). Shape and colour: `src/ui/colour-check.ts` (Machado's protan, deutan and tritan matrices, test-only) and `src/ui/shape-colour.test.ts` found that the wheelie gauge's amber, green and red zones were told apart by colour alone; `gaugeCues` in `moves-gauge.ts` draws the ticks, bracket and hatch. Tests: `hud-layout.test.ts` (the top layout at Large and Largest on every screen in its table, both hands), `camera/reduce-motion.test.ts`, `render/reduce-motion.test.ts`, `app/motion.test.ts`, `shape-colour.test.ts`; browser: `ui-text-size.spec.ts` (menu, every settings tab and the pause menu at Largest on 915x412 and 640x360), `ui-hud-layout.spec.ts` (the HUD at Largest) and the two new probes in `ui-settings.spec.ts`. Browser specs were not run on the dev machine (CI runs them). Not phone-verified. Not covered: the career screens at the largest size, the touch buttons' words, the eight rivals' greyscale check.

Keeper fixes after CI: (1) on a 640x360 phone at the largest size the slow-frames offer's larger words and buttons wrapped into a card 183 px tall in its 148 px column, down onto the health bars; the in-race offer now keeps the Normal size (13 px) at every Text size, and the pause menu's copy of it still grows. (2) `ui-text-size.spec.ts` left its button probes on the last settings tab, so a probe matched a hidden button too; it now clears them before marking the next screen's buttons.
(3) At the largest size on a 640x360 phone the pause menu was taller than the screen and centred, so "Paused" sat 40 px above the top; on a short landscape screen the menu column now scrolls, as the cards beside it do.
