A browser test for the road events (W-P): the bot races three seeded races whose events come up early and meets every kind of piece at least once (a Pacific Northwest race with the hay truck and a speed trap, another with the Logger Days parade and roadwork, and San Francisco's robotaxi incident). Each piece's props reach the snapshot, render reports them drawn with the warning sign's words, and the frame with the piece ahead of the player is not blank and inside the draw budget. The Keys hand-made road is left out: its events come only past 60% of the race, after its long shortcut stretch.
