A new look: Ink + 60s film. Pick it under Settings > Display > Look. It swaps in ink outlines on the riders, cars and road edges, hatched shadows, inked waves on the sea, and a warm old-slide-film colour with grain and a soft dark edge. It changes at once, even mid-race from the pause menu, and it only changes how the game looks, never how it plays. Classic stays the default for now. The ink look's strength, grain and vignette are sliders in the tuning panel.
