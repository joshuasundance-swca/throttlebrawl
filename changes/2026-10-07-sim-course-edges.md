The course has honest edges now: no invisible walls. Ride or fly off the course where nothing stands at its edge (over soft ground, through a gap between buildings, past a smashed fence's yard, down into the lot behind a block, over the water or off a drop) and you are out of bounds: a quick reset with the time penalty, and you wake on the road where you left it. A splash into the water keeps its gator or fisherman, a high fall is a clean cut-away, and anything else (dry ground, a low drop onto a street) is a plain reset. Where something is drawn at the edge (a rail, a wall, a building, the ferns, a fence, the water's edge) it still holds you on the ground, and in the air you clear it only above its drawn height. At a junction or beside a slip road you ride straight onto the other road. Cutting a corner across the roofs counts by where you come back down: land on the road ahead and you keep the gain, come back behind and you gain nothing, and your place in the race waits where you left the road until you rejoin. Rivals and cops ride by the same rule. The row and painted houses in San Francisco's hills are not in the physics yet and stay walls at any height. For developers: the rule is `riders.courseEdges` (sim/riders/course.ts); render's verge layer and road mesh gained three read-only test seams (`edgeKitPoints`, `edgeKitTops`, `drawnBarrierSpans`), and the splash gag and sound now play only for falls into water. The test profile `ISOLATED` (tests/sim/batch.ts) turns the new switch off, and the boat-ramp cut's guard test (tests/sim/road-shortcut.test.ts) now checks how far out a rider holding right rides, since past the marina fence's yard he is reset.
