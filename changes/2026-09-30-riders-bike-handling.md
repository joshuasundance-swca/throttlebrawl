The bike handles like a bike now. Throttle builds speed gradually up to about 85 mph, letting go coasts, and the brake stops you in well under 100 m. The bike leans into turns, the shoulder is slower than the lane, and the barrier bites back: brush it and the bike wobbles, hit it hard (or again while you're still wobbling) and you crash. The tuning panel gains top speed, acceleration and barrier crash speed, and its steering slider now works at low speed too.
