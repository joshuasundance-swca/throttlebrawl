The career's core and data (interview, 2026-10-02: "Network map, tiered" and "The map"). Each region has a career map with ten events on its roads, across every route it has and all four event types (classic races, takedown hunts that end at their count, cop escapes you win by surviving or losing the cops, and grudge matches to the line or by knockdowns), three tiers that open after two wins each, and a boss you beat to the line or knock down three times. Wins claim their roads and open the next ones; each map hides a shortcut, its pirate station and a stash of cash. Races pay their place, every bit of style cash (takedowns, near misses, airtime, oncoming, steals, tricks) and bonuses, and a bust's fine never takes you below $0. Rivals keep their grudges across races and sessions, and the grudges reach the next race. The garage sells the Rustbucket, the Streetfighter 750 and the Superbike 1000, a novelty ride per region (a rental moped, a dirt bike, a chopper) and each region's paints; the golf cart, the riding lawnmower and the mobility scooter are the bosses' prizes. The save gains the export code (`EC1.`, plain or compressed, with a checksum). The career screens and the race-first start land next; until then the free-play races are unchanged.
