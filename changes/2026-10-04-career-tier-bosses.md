The career learns the playtest 3 rules for a climb with some struggle in it. Once a region's map names a boss for each tier, the boss opens after two of the tier's wins, and only beating the boss opens the next tier. The Pacific Northwest opens when Mother Rust falls and San Francisco when Old Growth falls; a region you have already raced in this season stays open, and a tier you have opened never closes again, so no save loses ground. The garage follows: a later region's bikes and paints say "Opens when Mother Rust falls." until then, a bike behind a tier boss says whose fall opens it, and a season's paint waits for its season. The career also works out how strong each race's field is from its tier, never from what you own: the pace is a share of the best bike open at that tier, rivals ride that bike from a region's third tier, the cops are capped just under its top speed, and the fights climb gently (about 10% easier to knock down at a region's first tier, about 20% harder by its last). Nothing races at that level yet: the app-wiring task applies it, the career-screen task locks the region tabs (today only the garage follows the region order), and the new maps, bikes and tier bosses come with the content tasks. Done, not phone-verified. One line in `src/career/index.ts` (the seasons task's file) exports the new `level.ts`.
