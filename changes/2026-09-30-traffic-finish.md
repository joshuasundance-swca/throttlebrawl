Traffic now ends at the finish line. Past the line, finished riders roll to a stop at the road's end, and cars queued behind them reached back over the line, so a rider still racing could be walled off 20 m short of the finish and the race never ended (the M1 track's run-out is 40 m). Cars heading for the line now leave the road there; a car that leaves while its direction is over the density slider's target is retired instead of recycled.
