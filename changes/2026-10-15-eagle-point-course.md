Eagle Point Park adds a compact technical Clinton route through the entry drive, South Loop and Central Loop. The start is single file and the loops carry traffic in one direction. Brake for the tight bends and choose passing room carefully.

The road uses OpenStreetMap geometry and USGS terrain. Lane widths and grass verges are gameplay adaptations; this is one pass, without the North Loop, pedestrian paths or repeated laps.
