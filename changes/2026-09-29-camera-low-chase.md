The camera now rides low behind your bike and stays steady. It eases into bends instead of snapping, widens its view a little as you pick up speed, and settles without bouncing. Under the hood it is ready to tilt with the bike's lean, keep your target rival in frame, look down the road ahead and shake on crashes and hits; those switch on as the rest of the game gets wired up to it. Every camera setting (distance, height, stiffness, lean, field of view, shake) is a tuning value, ready for the tuning panel.
