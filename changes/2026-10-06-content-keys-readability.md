The Florida Keys are easier to read at speed. The Seven Mile Bridge's mile posts are bigger and now show a white number on a green board, so you can see them coming instead of a dark stub. Big Pine's Key deer are larger, stand right at the edge of the verge and now gather in herds of four to six that you ride past, with a lone one here and there. They still never stand on ground you can ride onto. Smathers Beach is a beach on its sea side, with no hotels, and the hotels stand only on the inland side, which now has stretches of shallow salt-pond water where nothing is built.

For devs: three punch items from the playtest-4 live check (run B), lane H2. (1) Mile posts: the board already faced the rider on every road that has posts (the new test asks it of every Keys network, in the routes' own order, with a post turned round as the control), so the dark stub was the paint: a board with no style took the default dark chalk, 0.4 by 0.7 m, 5.5 px tall from the chase camera at 36 m. `text-surfaces.ts` gives `keys_mile_marker_face` white on the model's green, and the posts say `scale` 2 (new `mileposts.scale` in the Seven Mile bake config and in `Milepost` in `network.py`, 1 writes nothing; the seven baked posts were edited to match). The board is 11 px tall (13 for mile 40) from 28 m at 38 m/s on the 915 by 412 screen, in frame and seen from the front, against a floor of 10 px. (2) Key deer: drawn 1.5 to 1.7 times, so every deer is 11 to 24 px tall from 20 m ahead (floor 9 px; a doe at size 1 is 7 px, the control), 0.6 to 1.4 m past the ridable verge (was 0.9 to 2.2), and a new `key-deer-herd` rule runs 4 to 6 deer along the verge, a candidate every 150 m a side, so every race has a herd (seeds 1 to 40: 1 to 5 herds, 9 to 28 deer). The grazers are fewer (every 70 m, 60 %). Both are the kit's last rules, so no other prop moved (tested with the tag taken off). (3) Smathers (sheet K2): `key-resort` is on the inland side only (the line's `sideTags`), so the sea side has no hotel, pool, tiki bar or scooter rack, and the inland side has three `water-shallow` salt-pond runs (s 700 to 1250, 1700 to 2350 and 3000 to 3600, 39 % of the road, clear of its board slots). The sim reads those as the water's edge (the lane's wall, as on a bridge); traffic areas follow the tag on either side. The bake config and the baked road were edited by hand, since the OSM extract is not at hand.

Not done: the sheet's airport fence and runway-light poles (no model for either), and a deer contrast change (tan on tan: a model or palette change, not placement). Budgets: no new draw call (the deer ride the roadside stretches, and the existing test still finds the same stretches and meshes with and without them); the deer add at most about 28 x 180 triangles in view on Big Pine, against 106,839 at the busiest Bahia Honda frame in run B's check and a 150,000 limit; first-load JavaScript gains about a kilobyte of rule data and one style constant (text-surfaces is a lazy chunk), against 27.8 KB of headroom on the live build, not rebuilt here. Tests: `src/render/mile-posts.test.ts`, `src/render/keys-deer.test.ts`, the new `src/render/smathers-beach.test.ts` and `tests/sim/keys-readability.test.ts` (the real chase camera's view of the boards and the deer, in pixels; it lives in tests/ because render may not import the camera), and the new `Milepost` scale case in `tools/gis/tests/test_capabilities.py`. Done, not phone-verified; no browser was run.
