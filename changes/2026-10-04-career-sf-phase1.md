San Francisco's career now climbs. Every tier has its own boss to beat before the next tier opens: Chad in Seed Round, Dial-Up in Series A (a new grudge match, "Buffering", on the Russian Hill streets), Gripman Gus in Series B and Pivot in The Down Round. Four new races join the map: "Burn Rate Row" (a downtown race), "Buffering", "Series C" (a four-takedown hunt downtown) and "Tow-Away Zone" (three of Officer Meter's units on you for 60 seconds). Purses are bigger and rise with each tier, the region's boss pays the most, and the flat podium bonus on Pivot's race is gone. The shop sells the Superbike (from Series A, $48,750) and the chopper ($15,000); paints cost $3,000 to $5,000, and a new gold one opens in Season 2. Each tier also states how hard its field rides: rivals about 10% easier to knock down in Seed Round, about 20% harder by The Down Round.

For devs: this is T10.3, San Francisco's phase 1. The circuit has 14 nodes: the two drift events (Series B, The Down Round) join with the drift lane's content, so two tiers hold two regular races and their boss. Each tier's `boss` and `field` block (pace share, aggression, signature gap, health, power, rival bike) is the progression spec's global tiers 9 to 12. The race pace in each event file is unchanged, because the career derives it from the field level once the app applies it. The purses are the spec's table for tiers 9 to 12, from the playtest 3 progression plan. San Francisco adds no new bike: its one step-up bike is the Superbike, rank 6. Until the Keys and the Pacific Northwest move over, `packs:check` still warns about the Superbike being in more than one shop (an error once all three name tier bosses). `tests/sim/career-field-climb.test.ts` is new and holds every career on the tier-boss format to the climb: the field levels rise, the first tier is easier and the last about a fifth harder, pay rises by tier, a tier boss pays more than its tier's other races. `career-content.test.ts` and `career-screens.test.ts` no longer pin the old ten-event, `3, 3, 3, 1` shape. Not phone-verified.
