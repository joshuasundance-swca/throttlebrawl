Three fixes to the bundle train, so it says what it is doing and always reaches a verdict. A PR whose quick check is green but that cannot ride yet (auto-merge not armed, or a draft) now gets a pending `gate` status that says so ("quick check green: arm auto-merge to ride the next train"); before, nothing on the PR said why it waited. A docs-only PR now takes the quick check as its gate whether the train is on or off (#575 ran the full suite while it was off), and docs-only was re-derived from the deploy and the build: `README.md`, `space/`, `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md` and any `.md` under `public/`, `src/`, `packs/` or `tests/` ship, so they are not docs. A suite job that runs past its time limit now counts as red (GitHub reports it as cancelled, and the train used to conclude nothing and re-run the same bundle on every trigger): a bundle splits, and a lone PR is blamed with "timed out" in its status and comment, within the same cap ladder of at most 4 red trains. Main's own timeout blames nobody: a main run with a timed-out job is red, the train waits for it, and `rerun-main.yml` now re-runs such a run once, as it does a failed one. This PR changes `.github/` (train.yml, rerun-main.yml and ci.yml's comments), so it takes the full gate.
