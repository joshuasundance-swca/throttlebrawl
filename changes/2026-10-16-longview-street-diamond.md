Longview has a separate Street Diamond course. Take 20th and Washington inside,
or continue along Olympia and Kessler on the longer outer route, then meet again
for the bridge and 30th Avenue finish. Both arms carry two-way traffic. Ordinary
streets have recoverable grass margins; the bridge keeps its guardrails.
Civic Triangle and Port Shift remain available.
