Three more things now make the Keys look like the Keys. On the Bahia Honda run the old 1912 rail bridge stands right beside the new one, a rusty steel truss with the old road deck on top and two spans missing, close enough to read from the road. On Big Pine the Key deer are out: tiny deer grazing the verges after a sign that reads "KEY DEER CROSSING. Hip high. The fine is not." And on the Key West run you ride past the two brick Martello forts, East Martello by the airport on South Roosevelt Boulevard and the ruined walls of West Martello at Higgs Beach. The forts are drawn smaller than the real ones so that they fit on the land beside the road.

For devs (playtest 4, P4-19, run B task B5; the identity sheets' fix 5 and item 9, K4). This is the re-run of a lane that hit a session limit; its notes were the head start, and the old bridge needed a different design because B1 (#540) had landed meanwhile.

- **The old Bahia Honda bridge, now beside the road.** B1 had put `old-bahia-honda-bridge` on the horizon at OpenStreetMap's line, 216 m from the baked bridge. The region's piece stays for the Seven Mile (`networks` is now `["osm-keys-seven-mile"]`), where it is a sliver 6 km off. Two small seams in `src/render/backdrop/`: a bridge piece may say its deck's own `widthM` (7 m) and slab `thickM` (1.2 m), the default being the far bridges' 32 m and 4 m or more, and the truss, piers and cables follow.
