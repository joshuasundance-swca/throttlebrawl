The Pacific Northwest opens in the city. Its first tier's third race, Tulip Traffic on I-5, is now Bridge City: downtown Portland at dusk in the rain, over the Burnside and Hawthorne Bridges, with the "Pick your bridge" choice over the Morrison Bridge as its optional bonus. Tulip Traffic is gone; the I-5 road still has two races (tiers 2 and 4).

- **Crown Point Loops.** The third tier gets a drift race on the Historic Columbia River Highway's hairpins. It is a race to the line with a second required goal: $300 of drift style cash (the HUD reads `DRIFT $x/$300`). The target is deliberately small. The progression plan drew $1,750, but a scripted drift bot riding the route alone banks only $562 to $1,093 over the whole race on the bikes a player could own by then. The critic's rule is to cap a drift target at 60 % of that, so it is $300, below the $337 cap set by the weakest bike. A new test, `tests/sim/drift-events.test.ts`, finds every drift event in the packs, runs the bot, and fails any target over its cap, so a drift race cannot be set out of reach. (The bot's code moved to `tests/sim/drift-bot.ts` so both drift tests share it.)
- **Downtown reads as downtown.** Bridge City's sidewalks now hold commuters under umbrellas, rain-shell hikers and, in the cart pod on Broadway, the pod's regulars; before, the same zones could spawn a Bigfoot researcher or a raccoon. Its traffic is the city's: cars, rain-cape cyclists, the new Cargo Bike (kerb rider, weight 0.5) and the new 20 m Streetcar (big, slow, holds its lane), with no log trucks or campers, on the blocks and on the bridge decks. Four invented signs stand on its roads: `BRIDGE LIFT IN PROGRESS. Bring a book.`, `FOOD CART POD, NEXT LOT. Line: 40 min. Rain: now.`, `STREETCAR: SHARE THE LANE. It was here first. It will not say so.` and `TWELVE BRIDGES, ONE RIVER. You get two. Pick fast.`. All are vetoable like any sign. The buildings, the rooftop neon and the lift towers come with the Portland render work; until then the street still draws with the old scenery. The streetcar rides the whole downtown, not only the rail line the plan wanted, because the one baked rail road (Grand Avenue) is on the Morrison Bridge choice, which carries no traffic.
- **A dusk for the region.** The Pacific Northwest gains a rainy `dusk` (its rain was already on in every time of day), used by Bridge City and drawn by the seed in free play.
- **The Supersport 900 costs $36,000** (it was $34,000). A fourth race in the third tier banks enough extra cash that the balance model's typical player bought the Supersport 2 races after it went on sale; the promise is 3 to 4. At $36,000 they take a median of 3, and the Superbike stays at 3.

For devs: the packs' work is `packs/region-pnw` (the event `pnw-t1-bridge-city`, replacing `pnw-t1-tulip-traffic`, whose node is now `bridge-city`; `pnw-t3-crown-point` and its node `crown-point`; four traffic types; the career, region and Bridge City roads). The zones, signs and the new `pdx-deck` tag over the bridge decks are in the bake config `tools/gis/networks/osm-pnw-portland.json` as well as the baked roads (the OSM extract is not in the repo, so they were written by hand to match what `tbgis` writes), and `tools/gis/routes-pnw-dressing.test.ts` holds the two to each other. A bridge deck carries only the `bridge` tag, so without the deck tag the two bridges, almost two fifths of the race, would take the forest's traffic mix. `tests/sim/pnw-real-world.test.ts` checks the rules: the first tier rides Bridge City at dusk with a real branch as its bonus, a drift race holds the third tier, downtown's tags cover every metre of the main path, no forest traffic is in downtown's mix, and every zone names only people. The career check's waiting list `AWAITING_EVENT` loses its Bridge City entry (the Keys and San Francisco entries are their own tasks'). The streetcar's 20 m raises the largest vehicle the rival AI avoids across Pacific Northwest races. Not phone-verified.
