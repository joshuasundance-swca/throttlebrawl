actual-frame sampling, explicit interruption evidence and restoration of the normal menu. Audio
suspension and complete renderer presentation ownership are specified as prerequisites, followed
by APP integration and a separate collector/report consumer. The contract preserves ordinary saves,
settings, seeds and replay state; it does not claim a completed soak tool or phone performance pass.
