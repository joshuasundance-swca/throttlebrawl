Boost pads and a jump truck. Ride over a boost pad for a short burst past your top speed: there is one on the right of the Marina Run that lines you up for the boat-ramp shortcut, and two on the Sandbar Causeway, the last one just before the finish. On the flat stretch of the Pelican Channel Bridge, a car-carrier tow truck is parked on the right shoulder with its deck down: ride straight up it and fly. Hit it from the side and you bounce off. The shapes are placeholders for now.
