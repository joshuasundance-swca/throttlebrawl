The Marin Headlands and the Twin Peaks summit have something on them now. On the Golden Gate route, tufts of coyote brush dot the open grass hills, and low concrete gun batteries with open gun pits stand on the side of Conzelman Road that looks out over the Gate. On the Twin Peaks climb, brush runs the whole hill and outcrops of red chert crop up through the grass over the last stretch to the summit.

For devs: three new scenery kinds, `battery`, `brush` and `outcrop`, draw the roots of Codex CX6's `models/scenery/sf-headlands` (registered as the model kind `sfHeadlands`, loaded for a network with a `headlands` road, as `presidio` loads `sf-identity`; variants 0 to 2 in file order, `HEADLANDS_VARIANT`). `RATE.headlands` was empty and is now brush 55 %, battery 50 % and outcrop 80 % of their candidate spots (`src/render/scenery.ts`). Two rules gate the last two, and neither is a tag, so a rebake cannot lose them: a battery stands only on a side that faces the water the network crosses (`waterCentre`, the mean of its own `water-*` stretches, and `facesWater`: the side's outward normal within about 66 degrees of the line to it), so none stands on Twin Peaks, the bridge or the toll plaza; an outcrop stands only within `SUMMIT_REACH_M` (450 m) of the end of a road that ends there, which is the top of the Twin Peaks climb. The battery is placed like a house (it faces the road, sinks to the lowest of its ends, keeps a few metres of land past each end) but its origin is the middle of its footprint, so a new `FRONT_M` (5.7 m) keeps its front past the ridable band. Everything stands past the band and on drawn land: the scenery geometry sweep now reads the three kinds too (`tests/sim/geometry-scenery.ts`), and `src/render/sf-headlands.test.ts` holds each rule with a control that must find the thing.

Measured with the real models (`src/render/scene-cost.test.ts`, worst view of each route, before then after): Golden Gate 42 then 49 draw calls and 42,408 then 44,723 triangles; Twin Peaks 56 then 60 draw calls and 78,820 then 78,820 triangles. The busiest route anywhere stays at 70 of 120 draw calls (Russian Hill). No download grows: the GLB (7.9 KB) already ships in region-sf's pack. The sheets' cypress windbreak rows are not built.
