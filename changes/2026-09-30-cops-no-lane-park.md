Sgt. Pruitt no longer parks in the middle of the road. If he gets ahead of you (say he rode past while you were down), he pulls onto the shoulder and waits for you to come by, then falls in behind you again; before, he could stop dead in the lane and hold up a queue of traffic, and you behind it, for minutes. He also brakes properly when he pulls up beside a rider who is down, so he stops next to you instead of sailing past, and he rides the centre line through traffic. In the 50 test races nobody now stands still in a lane for more than 5 seconds, no race runs long, and the bust rate is 8 of 50 (it was 4 of 50, mostly because he used to overshoot).
