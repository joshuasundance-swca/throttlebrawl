Groundwork for the Pacific Northwest's ferry, clear-cut and festival street (pitch #12). A road `hazard` marked `solid` is now a real obstacle for a rider: hit a parked pickup or a stump head on at speed and you crash, at a crawl you only wobble, and brushing its side scrapes you along it. The road lint refuses a solid hazard over a lane, since traffic and the rivals cannot see one. The road compiler can raise a stretch of road as a level deck with smooth ramps at each end (the ferry), and three new place tags give the ground beside the road its width: a ferry deck, a festival sidewalk and an open clear-cut. No live road uses any of this yet, so races play as before. Every race's state hash changes once, because the riders keep one more contact flag.
