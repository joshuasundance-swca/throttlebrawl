Clinton's rural roads now have a corn stand, a waiting pickup and a hay stack, each with a short
sign. A race picks at most two from the original procedural scenes, with a different line-up
between seeds. These are generic roadside vignettes, not mapped properties or real businesses.

For devs: rural scenes keep their full footprints on supported ground beyond the riding band and
clear of broadleaf trees. Tests cover actual placement, tree-blocked and unblocked controls,
seeded variety, short signs, cheap near/far geometry and vetoes. Region scene checks require authored
content rather than an unsupported five-scene minimum, respecting entries later vetoed by the
maintainer; the existing geometry and cost checks remain.
