The plant-sale frontage on Kessler Boulevard now has fingerpicked guitar music, using the existing
busker sound. It swells near the market and fades as you leave. Effects and Soundscape control it;
turning the radio's Music volume down keeps this roadside sound audible.

The Portland square test still requires its busker zone to cover the plaza, but no longer requires
Portland to be the only place in the game using that sound. Focused tests cover the authored market,
its distance fade, grounded playback and phrase scheduling. No performer model or recording is added.
