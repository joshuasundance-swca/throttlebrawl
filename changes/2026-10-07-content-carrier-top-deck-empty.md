The car carrier's top deck is empty. The car that rode on a carrier's upper deck was drawn, but a rider flying off the ramp passed straight through it. Making it solid would have crashed every jump off a carrier, so it is gone: nothing is drawn there and nothing is there. The deck itself is a flat top you can land on and ride, and the cab at its front is still solid. This is a default the maintainer can veto (the car can come back, with the question of what happens at the lip).

What changes when you ride up a carrier: a fast jump (about 12 m/s off the lip and up) is the same as before. A slow one used to crash into the car or pass through it; now you come down on the deck, ride it, and the cab's front throws you off. Seven Mile's staging hop left too slow (12 m/s) now ends against the truck's cab rather than in the gap; from 13 m/s up it is the gap as before.

- Sim (`src/sim/riders/features.ts`, `supports.ts`, `index.ts`): a parked truck is its ramp, a lip platform, then the top deck (`truckDeckAt`, `truckDeckBox`, `truckDeckTop`, the lip's height) up to the cab (`TRUCK_CAB_FROM_FOOT_M`, 16.8 m from the foot at the defaults), then the cab (`truckBodyAt`, solid, its top unchanged at 1.16 m over the lip). The deck is a support with key `d:<feature id>` (the cab's stays `t:`). A rider in the air below the deck's top over its footprint hits the truck at any speed (`stepAirborne`); one coming down within `TRUCK_DECK_TOLERANCE_M` (5 cm) of its level lands on it; a rider on the deck who rides into the cab is thrown off at any speed (the old never-stuck rule, `truckContact`; a rider on a top can neither turn round nor back off). A moving carrier has no top deck: its cab starts at its lip platform's end (`cabIntoOf`). `supportAt`'s `accept` now also gets the support's key.
