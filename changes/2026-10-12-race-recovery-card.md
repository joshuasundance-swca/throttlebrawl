The reload-recovery card can distinguish an unfinished race, a result waiting to be saved, a failed result save and unavailable or retryable saved data. It offers the appropriate recovery or abandon action and reports one choice synchronously after hiding. An unavailable recording cannot activate a hidden Resume action.

This is the UI contract for the following app integration. It does not yet enable saved races, recover career progress or prove phone restoration. The default unfinished-race card remains compatible.
