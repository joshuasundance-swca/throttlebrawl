The Pacific Northwest region, crude first, as its own content pack (`packs/region-pnw`, depending on base), per the maintainer's "Pnw and sf first then others". It holds a hand-made forest track (`tools/road/tracks/pnw-c1.ts`, baked into the pack): the Ferry Landing, the Switchback Grade or the gravel Logging Spur shortcut with a log-deck ramp, the twisty Cedar Hollow, a railed timber trestle with the ramp truck on its straight, Espresso Row, Fogline Ridge and the Sawmill Flats, with two boost pads. Its one race, Fogline Run, comes in three lengths (about 2.4, 3.9 and 6.2 km); the dev bot rides the standard in about 2.5 minutes with traffic. The pack adds a grey-green palette with foggy-dawn and overcast-noon lighting names, placeholder region traffic (log trucks, mossy wagons, camper vans, a towed espresso stand, ferry walk-ons, Bigfoot researchers, raccoons and an elk) that reuses the existing shapes, five signs, two billboards, two local rivals (Old Growth and Juniper Moss) with their bark sets, and a local deputy with his own parody sheriff. The game itself still loads only the base pack, so the region is not selectable until the runtime loads region packs; a headless sim test races it through the real race setup in the meantime. The tone guide gains the region's flavour notes.
