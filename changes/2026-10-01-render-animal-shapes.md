Animals and oddities now look like themselves instead of the generic pedestrian or a truck box: a green iguana with an orange crest, a pelican with its long yellow bill, a long low gator, a gator lounging on a pink lawn chair in sunglasses, a runaway mobile home with a pitched roof and windows, and a boat on its trailer. Other animals (the chicken, the Pacific Northwest's raccoon and elk, San Francisco's sea lion) share a plain four-legged shape in their own colour. These animals are still drafts, so they show on the staging build, not the release.
