The roadside reacts to you. Ride past someone close and fast and they hop back from the kerb; a little further out, some shake a fist and some hold up a phone to film you, and anyone you sent diving does the same once they are back on their feet. Cyclists and scooters on the shoulder make people hop back rather than dive. Joggers and walkers can stroll the verge instead of standing still, and a dog may chase you a short way along the verge before trotting home. Nobody is ever hurt, and nobody leaves the verge except to cross.
