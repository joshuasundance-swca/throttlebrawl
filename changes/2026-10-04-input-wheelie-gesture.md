You can pop a wheelie now (playtest 3: "I like the idea of skill"). Double-tap the throttle: tap the stick, then touch it again right away, a little above where you first tapped. The second touch keeps the first tap's stick base, so how high your thumb sits above that spot is your throttle from the very first moment. Then balance it with your thumb: too high and the front loops out, too low and it drops. The sweet band is narrow on purpose (it is a skill move), and it wants a third of the throttle to pop and at least a jog's speed. Lift your thumb and the front comes down.

On a keyboard, tap W (or Up) and then hold it, feathering W to balance; S brings the nose down. On a gamepad, pull R2 twice, and how deep R2 sits is the balance. A long press is just riding, so lifting off the gas for a corner and getting back on never pops one by accident. Auto-throttle turns the wheelie off, since full gas always loops it out. A wheelie into a car's hood launches you into backflips (that was already in the sim). A wheelie does not pay style cash yet: that arrives with the career change that sets the reward numbers.

Your phone buzzes a short tick when the double-tap registers, and the crash buzz if the wheelie loops out. Two new sliders sit with the controls on the tuning panel, the double-tap window (280 ms) and its radius (90 px), and if the sweet band is too fine for your thumb, "Wheelie: balance gain" (under Crashes) makes it lazier and wider. The wheelie gauge beside the stick comes in a later change; until then you balance by feel. Done, not phone-verified.

For devs: the gesture is `src/input/devices/wheelie-tap.ts` (the tap pairing, the 0.3 floor on the quantized throttle, and the latch that raises `ActionState.wheelie` only once the throttle can pop it) plus a path in each of `touch.ts`, `keyboard.ts` and `gamepad.ts`. `TouchState.up` now takes the event time, and `KeyboardState` and `GamepadState` take the thresholds and time themselves from `dt`. Two additions to the spec, both its own risk 2: the first press must itself be a tap (a press that lifts within the window), and the flag waits for the throttle floor instead of firing under it, because the sim pops only on the flag's rising edge. `InputSystem.stickBase()` is for the T6.3 gauge, and `InputSystem.onMoves(snapshot.moves)` feeds a 30 ms warning buzz when the band turns `high`: it is built and tested, but nothing calls it yet, so that buzz needs a one-line call in `app/index.ts` after each step (not in this lane's files). New tests: `src/input/wheelie-tap.test.ts` and `tests/sim/wheelie-gesture.test.ts` (the real sim takes every flag the input raises, including at the 76 and 77 of 255 edge of the floor).
