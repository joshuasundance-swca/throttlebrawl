A bike, rider or traffic model the game server held back is now fetched at your next race instead of staying a stand-in until you reload. The last live check found that a Keys race started while San Francisco's map files had the server asking for a 30 second wait never fetched two of its models (Deacon Vane and the chopper), in that race or in the next, so they stayed plain boxes and stand-in bikes for the rest of the visit. A model, a scenery model or its texture sheet that failed because the server was busy, asked for a wait or could not be reached is now asked for again the next time it is needed, once the wait has passed. A model the server does not have, or one that fails its check, still keeps its stand-in without asking again.

For devs: `AssetLoad` (`src/assets/index.ts`) has a new `retryable` flag, set when a baked file fell back because of an HTTP 408, 425, 429 or 5xx answer (the loader's own hold answers 429 too) or a failed connection; the manifest no longer keeps such a result, so the next `load` of that id asks again, and `retryingFetch` (polish batch F's pacing) still holds that ask until the host's wait has passed. A 404, a hash mismatch and a decode failure are not retryable and stay kept. The callers that kept their own record of a failure now keep it only until the next ask: `RiderRigs` (`src/render/riders/index.ts`) re-requests a part marked `retryable` when a race's looks are set (not from a frame's `rigFor`, and the rider still draws on the starter bike's model meanwhile); `loadSceneryModels` reports `retryable` kinds and `atlasHeld` kinds (loaded without their sheet), and `requestModels` in `src/render/index.ts` drops them from `requested` so the next `setRoad` or `setTrafficTypes` asks again (`retryableKinds` and `mergeModelReports` in `src/render/models.ts`, and the `onHeld` argument of `loadRegionAtlas`); vehicles and landmark kits load per race through the manifest and need no change. Bark voices already asked again after a failed clip; a test now holds them to it. Tests: `src/assets/after-wait.test.ts` (a 429, 503, 500, 408, 425 or lost connection falls back and the next load arrives; a 404, 410, 403, a bad hash and a bad decode are asked once), `src/app/model-after-wait.test.ts` (the real `retryingFetch` over a scripted host: inside a 30 second wait the host is asked nothing, and the model arrives after it), `src/render/riders/after-wait.test.ts`, `src/render/models-after-wait.test.ts`, `src/render/vehicles-after-wait.test.ts` (the real box-truck model through the real manifest) and one case in `src/audio/bark-voices.test.ts`, each with a 404 negative control; on the polish F branch without this change the held-model tests fail (11 cases) and the controls and the voice case pass. Not phone-verified, and not checked against the live host.
