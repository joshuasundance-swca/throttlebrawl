Duval and Whitehead Streets in Key West are a street now, not a palm road. The palms, bait shacks and power poles that lined them are gone; there is a proper sidewalk, and the shops, conch houses and bars stand right on its edge with their balconies hanging out over the pavement. Open-fronted bars with their own invented names (The Missing Wall, No Cover, No Door, Last Call Is a Rumor) sit among the shops, frangipanis shade the sidewalk, and royal poincianas and banyans rise over the rooftops behind. At dusk the string lights now also cross the street, high over the lanes, between the two sides of every party block. The crowds stand on the sidewalk. Key West's other town streets (Truman, White, Bertha, Atlantic, Higgs, Stock Island) have a kerb instead of a strip of sand.

For devs (playtest 4, P4-19, fixes 3 and 9 of the identity study; the maintainer: "The real roads do not have the characteristics of the roads in question in terms of scenery and feel"; P4-16; Codex CX5's wiring list, item 7).

- **Theme and verge order** (`render/scenery.ts`, `road/cross-section.ts`): a new `oldtown` land theme from the `key-oldtown` tag ranks first, then the `commercial` theme (`town`, `strip-mall`, `marina`, `trailer-park`, `landmark`) ahead of `palms`, in the theme order and the verge order alike. Checked over every baked road at 5 m: only Duval, Whitehead, Key West's town streets, Knights Key and 5 m of `m1-marina-bends` change. `oldtown` has no scatter and no poles. Its derived verge is a 4 m kerb to a hard edge; the hand-written 1.5 m verges on both Duval roads (and in the bake config) are gone. `tools/gis/region-routes.test.ts` accepts a network of Old Town tags scattering nothing, as it does for `headlands` and Portland's blocks.
- **Street fronts** (`render/roadside.ts`): a `Frontage` rule's `across[0]` is now the facade's distance past the verge band's outer edge (the sidewalk's), whatever the band is made of, so a front stands 0.3 m past the pavement and its balcony overhangs it. A roadside zone (a crowd) only has to clear a building's body, not its balcony. Planters and scooter racks stand within the sidewalk. A rule may be `first`, running before the others without moving any rule's index (a rule's random stream is its index): the new rules are at the end of the Keys kit, so every other Keys road keeps its props (`roadside.test.ts` keys-m1 guards unchanged).
- **CX5's identity kit** (`models.ts` kind `keysIdentity`, loaded with any network that has `key-oldtown`): `oldtown-bar` (open bars `duval_open_bar_a` and `_b`, a street front 70 to 150 m apart, claimed first), `oldtown-frangipani` (sidewalk), `oldtown-poinciana` and `oldtown-banyan` (the yards behind the row). Their bars' blank name boards are text surfaces painted from six new signs (`duval-open-bar-a-name` and `-2`, `-3`, and `b`), neon at dusk (`text-surfaces.ts`). Not wired from the kit: the Key deer and the mile post, which wait for their roads.
- **Roads** (`osm-duval-street`, `osm-duval-whitehead`, `tools/gis/networks/osm-keys-duval.json`): the 15 pedestrian and animal zones end at 8.5 m (they were 10.6 or 11), on the sidewalk; the Southernmost buoy, the Mile 0 marker and Mallory's pier moved 1.9, 2.4 and 1 m out, past the sidewalk's edge (`landmark-clear`), by hand in the road files, the bake config's `at` points and the network report, as T10.4 did for the pier. The buoy is 2.5 m from the real OpenStreetMap point and the Mile 0 marker 7.8 m from its own (the real one is 2.3 m from the road's centre line), so `routes-keys-pt3.test.ts` allows 8 m (was 7).
- **Party lights** (`render/party-lights.ts`, `render/string-lights.ts`): the lantern-string builder of Chinatown (`chinatown-northbeach.ts`) is lifted into a shared helper (`sagAt`, `stringPoints`, `hangPoints`); `string-lights.test.ts` pins that Chinatown's lanterns come out where its loop put them. The party posts stand on the sidewalk, 6 m high, and a string crosses the street between a pair of posts every 26 m where both sides are party, its lowest point at least 4.8 m over the road (27 strings, about 220 bulbs on Duval; none where one side only is party).
- **Crowd kinds** (`tests/sim/keys-real-world.test.ts`): a zone naming one of the Old Town's people lies inside a stretch of its own road that carries `key-oldtown`, on its side (15 zones; a control without the tag finds all 15). It already held; the test pins it.
- **Budget** (`scene-cost.test.ts`, `osm-duval-run`, seed 1): the busiest view is 68,302 triangles (was 103,726, per #495) and the most draw calls 49 (was 53), against the still scene's 110,000 and 80: the scatter's palms were most of what it drew. `draw-headroom.test.ts` is unchanged and green.
