Traffic looks like traffic now. The rental sedans, pickups, party vans, box trucks and shrimp trucks, the rental convertible, the pickup towing a boat and the snowbird RV in the Keys, the muddy pickup in the Pacific Northwest and the rideshare hatchback and the startup shuttle in San Francisco are real modelled vehicles, with windows, bumpers, lights and wheels, in place of the plain coloured boxes. Each keeps its own size and its own paint colours, so a hit still lands where it did. If a model ever fails to load, the old box is drawn instead. Not phone-verified.

For devs: new `src/render/vehicles.ts` (a lazy chunk: bakes the vehicle GLBs and loads them through the asset manifest, only the ones a race's traffic types name), a small `src/render/views.ts` change (`setVehicleModels`, one instanced mesh per model, the boxes until the model arrives), and a render-only `assets/traffic-models.json` in each of the base, Pacific Northwest and San Francisco packs (never type JSON, so no replay key or content hash moves). Draw calls: with every type of any region's traffic pool in view at once, the models draw at most 2 calls over the boxes they replace (measured per pool in `src/render/vehicles.test.ts`), and `draw-headroom.test.ts` now holds the composed peak at 106 of 120 (it was 104). First-load JavaScript grew by 518 bytes gzip (480,285 to 480,803, the same in-memory build on this branch and on main at 05c2e870); the models' own code is a 1.9 KB gzip lazy chunk. `docs/architecture.md` (render) and `docs/content-packs.md` (asset references) are updated. Not yet drawn: the SUV, the semi and the Keys tour tram have no traffic type to stand for yet, and the pickup-towing-boat and snowbird-rv models are 11 m long against types of 7.5 m, so they draw squashed to the type's length (the content lane can lengthen the types).
