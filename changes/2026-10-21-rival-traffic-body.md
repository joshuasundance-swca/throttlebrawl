Rivals judge each traffic vehicle by its own length and width when choosing a passing line. Previously, adding a long truck or streetcar made every car seem that large, which could leave a rival stopped behind a queue it had room to pass. Unregistered test vehicles keep the conservative fallback. Focused checks cover passing a stationary car queue without body overlaps, an unused truck leaving the driven path unchanged, and keeping stopped behind actual wide blockers.
