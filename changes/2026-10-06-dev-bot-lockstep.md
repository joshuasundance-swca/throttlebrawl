The test bot now rides a seed the same way whatever the frame pacing. Playtest 4 run B's live check saw Bridge City seed 3 ride two different routes (edges 0 to 9 once, 0,1,2,1,2,3,13,10,11,12,14,9 another time) when only its screenshot points differed. The cause was the test handle, not the sim. The check switched the bot on in the menu, started the race, waited until the race passed tick 5 and switched the bot on again. That second `setBot(true)` built a fresh bot mid-race, on whatever tick the runner's pacing had reached. The fresh bot's fight clock (a fight gives up after 14 s) then started on that tick. Its first different input came at tick 841, and the route split later. A headless ride shows it: Bridge City seed 3 with the bot rebuilt at tick 6 rides 0,1,2,3,13,10,11,12,14,9, at tick 9 it rides the live check's 0,1,2,1,2,3,13,…, and at tick 15 it rides 0 to 9.

The handle (`src/dev/handle/index.ts`) now keeps three rules. `setBot(true)` while the bot is on keeps the bot it has. Each race starts a fresh bot at its tick 0, however it was started: before, a race started from the menu's Race button kept the bot's memory of the last race. A repeat look at the same snapshot (a countdown samples the driver at tick 0 again and again) gets the same actions, so the kick a bot pressed on the grid is not dropped.

A new sim test (`tests/sim/dev-bot-lockstep.test.ts`) steps real headless Bridge City races through the real handle, with the handle called only between frames. It checks that seed 3 rides the same inputs and edges when the second `setBot(true)` lands on tick 6 or tick 15, and that a second race from the menu in the same tab rides the same inputs as the first. It also checks that three looks at one snapshot give one decision. On main's handle, the pacing check fails (0,1,2,3,13,… against 0 to 9) and so does the one-decision check. With the fix but without the race-start reset, the second-race check fails (8,793 inputs against 8,853). A negative control checks that seeds 3 and 4 ride different inputs. The docs' test-handle and bot-playthrough lines say the same. Not phone-verified (no game change).
