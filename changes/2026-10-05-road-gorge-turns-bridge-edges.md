The Columbia River Highway's first turns are no longer a near-certain crash, and riding the verge onto a bridge no longer jumps you sideways onto the deck (playtest 4: "the first couple turns are very very prone to crashing"; "when it goes from grass to bridge or whatever the rider clips from open air onto the bridge. You can see it happen if you stay to the far right").

- **The Crown Point loop.** 220 m after the grid the road turns 215° round a 33 m radius on the Crown Point viaduct. Holding the throttle you reach it at about 82 to 118 mph (by bike and line), while full lock holds it only up to about 60 to 62 mph, so the bike runs wide across the oncoming lane into the outer rail. Two kinder rules: a bike the bend carries into the outer rail while you hold the bars into the bend now scrapes along it and wobbles, and crashes only if it hits at three times the old crash speed or more; and oncoming cars now wait short of a tight bend while riders come round it, and never appear right in front of one where they could not stop. Hitting the rail with the bars straight or turned the other way crashes as before, and braking or drifting into the loop is still the quick way round.
- **Bridge ends.** The ground beside the road now narrows into every bridge, 1 m in for every 10 m along, so the band meets the deck's edge where the deck begins; at the far right or far left you are eased onto the deck along it, with no jump and no wobble. The ferns still slow you if you steer out into them.

Measured with a phone-like rider (throttle held, full lock in the bends, no brake for a bend) on the Gorge's first 600 m with the whole field, the cops and traffic, four bikes, six riding habits, 20 seeds each (480 races): before, 329 crashed (68.5 %: 220 into the outer rail, 88 into traffic, 21 into tumbling riders); after, 18 (3.8 %: 13 rail hits, none into traffic). Throttle held in the right lane: 78 of 80 crashed before, 5 after; braking for the loop: 17 of 80 before, 1 after; drifting into it: 19 of 80 before, 1 after. The closing-speed rule (#552) was not merged, so it is not in these numbers.
