The Keys get their own roadside. Sea grape crowds the verge and grows into small trees among the palms. Pastel conch cottages stand on short piers behind white picket fences, with a fish on the mailbox. Lobster traps are stacked with their buoys, skiffs sit on their trailers, and a pelican keeps watch from a piling. Hand-painted BAIT ICE boards and a key lime pie stand turn up by the road.
