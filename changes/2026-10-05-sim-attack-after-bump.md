Ride into a rival's back wheel and hit him straight after, and the blow now lands (playtest 4: "make it so that bumping into a rival while attacking doesn't negate the attack"). The bump shoves him about 2 m ahead with your speeds matched, which was past the punch, the kick and even the straight kick, so a tap or swipe just after the bump swung at air. Now an attack you press up to half a second after a bump with a rider lands on him while he is still within 3 m. Without a bump nothing reaches further than before, and rivals keep their own reach.

For devs: `src/sim/combat/index.ts`. The bump rule from #513 (an attack that touches its rider during its wind-up or active moment lands on him within `BUMP_REACH_M`) now also reaches back: when a player's attack starts, `touchedSince` looks for a contact with a rider (the riding phase's `contactTick`) in the last `BUMP_AFTER_TICKS` (30 ticks, 0.5 s: a felt bump, the reaction and the thumb's way to the button), with the same choice as before (the attack's target first, else a rider on its side, the rider ahead for the straight kick), and the active moment lands on him through `landBump` when the reach box misses. Players only; no new state. Tests: `src/sim/combat/bump-after.test.ts` (the harness, with the contact set by hand: punch, kick and straight kick pressed 0 to 30 ticks after a rear bump land; controls: no bump, a press at 31 ticks, a rider past 3 m, and a rival bumping the player all miss) and `src/sim/combat/timing.test.ts` (the real riders phase: 48 presses 2 to 30 ticks after riding into his back wheel at 4 and 6 m/s closing all land; with the rule switched off the first case, a punch 2 ticks after, misses). Replays: recorded races where the player pressed within half a second of a bump play out differently. Not phone-verified.
