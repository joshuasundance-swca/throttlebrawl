The road fights back. Roadside props now stand on the ground beside the road, and they break. In the Keys there are lobster-trap stacks and mailbox rows, in the Pacific Northwest mailbox rows and honour-system firewood stands, and in San Francisco parking meters, startup pop-up desks and cafe tables (never Chinatown's shops). Ride through one and it flies to pieces: you wobble and lose a little speed, but you never crash. Kick a rival into one and he goes down in slow motion, and the takedown is named after what he hit: CATCH OF THE DAY, RETURN TO SENDER, METER EXPIRED, DOWN ROUND, SEAT YOURSELF, HONOR SYSTEM. The names are pack data, so each one can be cut like a sign. A "Smashables" slider in the tuning panel sets how many there are (0 turns them off).
