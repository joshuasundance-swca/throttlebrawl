Lake Samish looks like a lake and Chuckanut's bay side like a cliff. Beside East Shore Drive the ground now slopes down to the shore, so you see the water from the road, with the cabins on the flat by the water and a dock every hundred metres or so on both stretches of the lake (it used to show as a pale flat plain, and the docks stood hidden behind the edge of the bank). On Chuckanut Drive the drop to the bay is a sheer sandstone face that runs down into the sea, with no green ledges, the stone wall along the edge is taller and paler, and each stretch of rock cut on the hill side ends by sloping into the ground instead of in a flat vertical wall.

For devs (playtest 4, run C's live check, punch items 6 and 7; `[default]`, done, not phone-verified, and no browser was run).

- **The lake's water** (`render/backdrop/builder.ts`, `data.ts`, `shapes.ts`). A far floor is wholly the haze's colour inside the fog's end (`aInfo.y` 1: the near sea and ground cover it there), so Lake Samish, which no near layer covers, was the haze's colour: the pale plain. A water floor above the sea now has floor flag 2 (`NEAR_WATER_FLOOR`): the vertex shader gives it the haze by distance inside the fog's end and the far floor's haze from there on (`floorHazeAt` mirrors it: 0.04 at 60 m, 0.23 at 150 m, 0.68 at 300 m, 1 at the fog's end, 480 m in the Pacific Northwest). No other floor changes. `backdrop/lake-haze.test.ts`: the mirror (a control: the sea's floor is 1 at every distance inside the fog), the flags, and a guard that the shader carries `nearWater`. It failed first (the lake's haze was 1.00 at 20, 60, 150, 300 and 480 m).
- **The lake's bank** (`render/road-mesh.ts`, `scenery.ts`, `roadside.ts`, `index.ts`). The lake's land was a 20 m plateau at the road's height with a sheer drop at its edge, and the water is 3.7 to 9.6 m below East Shore Drive, so a camera 3.2 m (the low chase) or 4.8 m (the far chase) up saw the water only from 40 to 70 m past the verge, and the docks (at the water's level, behind the edge) never. `buildRoadScene` takes `waterAt` (the lake's water, `backdrop/water.ts`); a `lake` row's land is then the verge's 4.2 m at the road's height (`LAKE_BANK_M`: the sim's soft grass is ridable there), a wall facing the lake, and a flat shore 0.3 m over the water (`LAKE_SHORE_OVER_M`), to the same 20 m as before. Where the bank begins or ends (a theme's end, a gap in the water) the wall grows from nothing at 0.5 m per metre, and the plain row beside a bank carries the shore's band one row on at its own height, so the land meets the plateau with no step and no hole: the geometry of all 19 networks without water is identical to main's (a hash of every mesh's vertices, 19 of 19). `RoadScene.landTop` gives the shore's height; the roadside layer stands a prop at it (`RoadsideInput.landTop`), the scatter's conifers on it (`world`), and the cabin and dock only where the shore is flat along the whole prop (`flatShore`), not on the ramp. A dock no longer needs a bank under 6 m (`BANK_MAX_M` holds where there is no shore). The renderer builds the road again when the water arrives (`requestWater`: `buildRoad`, where it built only the roadside). Without the water (every other network, and the race before the water has loaded) the lake's land is the plateau it was.
