On San Francisco's real roads, the row houses at the bridge ends (Hyde Street on Russian Hill, Upper Market on Twin Peaks) no longer stand on thin plates of land with sky and bay showing underneath. Wherever the land beside a road stops, narrows or meets another road, it now ends in a solid bank down to the ground, on every region's roads, and the last house of a row stands a few metres back from the edge. The tests now look under the land, not only down onto it, so a floating plate fails the gate.
