San Francisco's streets fill up between the stoops and the kerb. Parking meters stand on the house plots, street trees grow from their sidewalk cut-outs, lamps light the kerb, and hydrants and dropped rental scooters litter the pavement. The blue, green and black bins are out for collection, and A-frame boards sell AI, AGI and GPUs. Cars park nose to the kerb in the gaps between houses, one of them a driverless taxi with a traffic cone on its bonnet, and a corner store turns up now and then. The cable-car lanes get their cover plates between the slot rails.
