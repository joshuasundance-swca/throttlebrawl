The Race settings now show up and work: Difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard), Race length (Short, Standard, Long, where a region has them, such as the Pacific Northwest), Lower overall speed, Steering assist and Takedown slow motion. Each applies from the next race or restart. Display also gains "Reduce screen shake" (no shake or hit jolt) and the Frame rate cap (Smooth, Half, Third, to save battery). Holding L, or R1 on a gamepad, now really looks behind you. The pause menu's radio panel is live: it shows the station and song, and has Next station, Next song and Cut song.
