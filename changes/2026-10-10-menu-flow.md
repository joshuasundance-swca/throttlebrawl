The main menu now leads with Career, Quick Play and Settings. Quick Play keeps the region, road and race setup together; extra tools live under More. Your previous picks still carry into the next race.

Pause and both results screens keep the main actions separate from scrolling details. Career goals are also available while paused, and results offer a compact Retry, Next or Map row.

Quick Play setup also offers Open field or Same bike competition. Same bike matches rivals to the selected ride, including slower bikes.
