Pick your road. Under the region chips on the menu, "Which road" lists the region's own race first and then its real roads by name: Bahia Honda Run in the Keys, Chuckanut Drive and the Columbia River Highway in the Pacific Northwest, Russian Hill and Twin Peaks in San Francisco. Tap one and the line under it names the real road or streets and how long the run is; tap Race to ride it. Your own region's road stays the default, and it still follows the Race length setting. The real roads run 5.3 to 7.5 km, longer than the regions' own standard races (the test bot takes 4 to 5 minutes on them). The road you picked is saved in the replay with the seed.
