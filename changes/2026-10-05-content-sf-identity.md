San Francisco feels more like San Francisco. Chinatown's Dragon Gate now stands across Lantern Row just past the start line: you ride under its green-tiled roofs in your first second, and its gold plaque reads "WELCOME. STEEP AHEAD." The twin-spired church of North Beach rises over the park at the elbow, dead ahead as you come up Espresso Row, until the road turns right in front of it. Lombard Street has a crowd: tourists with their phones up at each of the eight hairpins, in a queue at the top and at the bottom crossing, stepping back from the kerb when you come close and diving when you come closer. Hyde and California, the real cable-car streets of Russian Hill, now carry cable cars: slow, heavy, and a crash if you meet one, so pass them on the open side.

For devs: the models are CX5's (`sf-landmarks#sf_dragon_gate`, `sf-landmarks#sf_twin_spire`), placed by hand-authored features in `tools/road/tracks/sf-chinatown-northbeach.ts` (re-baked: `sf-cn-lantern-row`, `sf-nb-the-elbow`).
- **The gate**: an `overRoad` landmark at s 52 (footprint 4.4 by 24 m, `yawDeg` 180). Its two inner posts are solid `gate-post` hazards (`sim/riders/features.ts`), so a rider on the pavement meets a post that is drawn. The plaque is the new text surface sign `sf-dragon-gate-plaque` (tags `site`, `surface`, `new`; vetoable) with its own style in `text-surfaces.ts`.
- **The church**: a landmark with `farM` 260 on the elbow, d -36.5 to -18.5, facade to the road. `landmarks.ts` reads one new free-form render param, `frontM`: the model's origin stands that far ahead of the footprint's middle (its origin is its facade, so the footprint is the church's own 30 by 18 m).
- **The districts keep off landmarks** (`chinatown-northbeach.ts`): the frontage closes up 0.3 m short of a footprint, no lantern string crosses the gate, and no park tree, bench or back row stands in the church. `planBlocks` also records `treeSpots`.
- **Lombard's crowd**: the new pedestrian `lombard-photographer` (in no region-wide list; zones name it) on 8 hairpin zones (the outer side of each bend, behind the planter band), plus `lombard-top-queue` and `lombard-bottom-crowd`. Added by hand to `tools/gis/networks/osm-sf-lombard.json` and the baked roads (the OSM extract is not in the repo).
- **Russian Hill's cable cars**: a `cable-route` traffic area (new road tag on `osm-sf-hyde` and `osm-sf-california`, in the GIS lint and the config) with the region's mix plus the cable car at weight 2 of 19.5. The invented `sf-cable-line-grade` of the old standard run carries none. The prop model already draws it (`oddityFigureFor`); the startup shuttle (11 m) stays the race's largest vehicle.
- **Not built**: the sheets' stopped cable car at Hyde and the queue of parked cars at the top of Lombard. A car across the climb would be an invisible wall and Hyde's cross street is not drawn there; the seam is the `cable-route` area. Also not built: the Portland gate and the other CX5 models (other runs wire them).

Tests, all new or extended: `src/render/landmarks-sf-identity.test.ts` (the gate's clearances, solid posts and plaque; the church inside its footprint, past the verge, on drawn land, spires over the roofline, with a control), `src/render/chinatown-northbeach.test.ts` (the districts keep off both footprints, each with a control that plans the same roads without the landmark features), `src/render/landmarks.test.ts` (`frontM`) and `tests/sim/sf-identity.test.ts` (cable cars only on `cable-route`, none in the standard run, the photographers inside their zones, on the outer side of each hairpin). Also run: `tools/road/sf-chinatown-northbeach.test.ts`, `tests/sim/career-headless-sf.test.ts`, `tests/sim/road-real-routes.test.ts`, `tests/sim/region-sf.test.ts`, `src/render/scene-cost.test.ts`, `src/render/draw-headroom.test.ts`, `npm run packs:check`.

Budgets: Chinatown's busiest view is 40 of 120 draw calls and 51,674 of 110,000 triangles, with the gate and the church in the one landmark call (gate 414, church 600 or, past 260 m, 56 triangles). Everything new loads in lazy chunks or pack data; nothing is in the first-load JavaScript. Not covered: how any of it looks on the phone (done, not phone-verified); no browser was run.

Keeper fix: the land sweep (`tests/sim/geometry-land.test.ts`) sent pedestrians from the new hairpin crowds across the street, onto land 0.26 to 0.35 m below the road on the far side of four bends. The photographers stroll, and the sim never sends a stroller across (`src/sim/peds/index.ts`), so the sweep now follows that rule: a zone whose own kinds all stroll is checked on its own side only. Lombard: 12 zones, 2,432 points, 0 wet (the same data with the old rule had 4).

Keeper fix (shift 2): main's sidewalk change (#549) made every traffic type name its roadside class (`tests/sim/roadside-classes.test.ts`), and the new phone photographer had none. It is a pedestrian, so it `dodges`, like San Francisco's dog walker. The roadside-classes test, run alone: 128 passed.
