Cyclists, scooters and e-bikes get out of your way, and clipping one no longer wrecks you (playtest 3: "bike riders cause collisions when they should arguably get out of the way off the sidewalk").

- A bicycle, scooter, e-bike or the cooler on wheels that sees you coming (about two seconds out) steps onto the sidewalk beside the road, slows down while you go by, and drifts back to its kerb afterwards. Where there is no sidewalk (a rail or a wall) it hugs the edge instead.
- If you still clip one, you wobble and keep most of your speed, and the cyclist topples onto the sidewalk and lies there for two seconds. It is never a crash. A slow brush just nudges, as before. The golf cart is not that light: it keeps to the road and a head-on hit still throws you.
- Rivals and cops get the same treatment, so they stop wiping out on cyclists too.

For devs: `src/sim/traffic/kerb-yield.ts` holds the numbers and the pure rules (who threatens a kerb rider, where it dodges to), and `traffic/index.ts` wires them in behind two sliders, `traffic.kerbYield` (0 to 1.5, the look-ahead; 0 turns the dodge off) and `traffic.kerbSoft` (0 or 1). A race whose tuning leaves both out rides exactly as before. The soft contact reuses the `wobble` event with `data.kerb` true (and `data.toppleS` when the cyclist topples), so render's topple (T4.3) reads it from there. No new RNG draws. Two things differ from the build plan: a cyclist slows to 0.6 of its cruise at 6 m/s squared (IDM alone only creeps toward a lower cruise speed, 90 ticks to get within 0.05 m/s of it, not the planned 30), and a clip topples only at 2 m/s closing or more (a kerb rider brushing a cop standing at the kerb toppled over and over, every 2 seconds). Over ten San Francisco bot races (`tests/sim/traffic-kerb-live.test.ts`), crashes into e-scooters and delivery e-bikes go from 2 to 0 and wobbles from 2 to 3, all of them soft. The per-tick check in `tests/sim/traffic-peds.test.ts` swaps seed 5 for seed 6: the yielding reshuffled seed 5, where a rival swung to the road's edge to pass a sedan and rode through a chicken standing there (a 9 cm box overlap for two ticks, no event); pedestrian gap acceptance (T4.2) is the fix for that.
