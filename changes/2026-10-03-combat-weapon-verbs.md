Weapons do something now, not just hit. Kevin's briefcase is thrown: it flies up the road at whoever is ahead and bursts into paperwork, and then it's gone. The bike chain yanks: it pulls a rival across your line and out the other side, into the oncoming lane if you're on that side of him. The campaign sign sweeps both sides at once, so a swing between two riders hits both.

Each region also has its own weapon by the road. In the Keys it's a lawn flamingo: quick and light, and the daze lasts. In the Pacific Northwest it's a canoe paddle, swung in the sweep stroke to hit both sides. In San Francisco it's a dead rental scooter, swung whole: the slowest and hardest hit on the road, and the easiest one to snatch. None of them knocks a rider down in one blow. Two new sliders, Throw speed and Chain yank past you, are in the combat panel.
