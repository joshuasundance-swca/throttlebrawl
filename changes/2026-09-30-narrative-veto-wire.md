"Cut this" works on the phone now. Mid-race, hold a rival's speech bubble with your thumb for half a second and choose "Cut this"; a press that starts where you steer or attack is left alone, so it never costs you a steer. Or pause: the pause screen lists the last 20 lines you saw, and two taps (the line, then "Cut this") cut one. Cuts are remembered on this device after a reload, and "Copy debug report" lists them, so pasting the report tells the agents what to remove. A bubble that is still up when you pause now sits behind the pause menu instead of covering it.
