Two real places join the Keys' route picker (playtest 3: "Duval St, downtown Portland, Golden Gate"; round 3's Seven Mile answer).

- **Duval Street** (3.5 km). From Mallory Square south down Whitehead Street past the Mile 0 marker, a hard left at the Southernmost Point buoy, along South Street, then the whole of Duval north to Front Street, finishing beside Mallory Square. The buoy, the Mile 0 marker and the Mallory Square pier stand where the real ones do (within 4 to 8 m); the marker steps a few metres out onto the sidewalk so it is off the road. Pedestrians gather at the buoy, on Duval and at the sunset end.
- **The Seven Mile Bridge** (11.6 km), at its real length, with the hump over Moser Channel where it really is. The Old Seven Mile Bridge runs beside it to the north, the whole way to Little Duck Key. Keep right to the rail about 600 m onto the bridge and a ramp truck on a repair platform hops you about 30 m over the water onto the old road. The real missing span over Moser Channel is the big jump: a Rustbucket flat out clears it with about 11 m to spare, and a rider well under top speed goes in and wakes up back on the highway. Near Little Duck Key, where the bridges close to about 20 m, a second ramp truck hops you back. The old road is about 50 m longer than the highway; rivals and cops are set never to take it.

Per bike, measured in the sim (`tools/gis/routes-keys-pt3.test.ts`, every bike in the packs): flat out, every bike at least as fast as the Rustbucket clears the Moser gap, the Rustbucket landing 11.5 m past it, the Superbike 112.9 m. The slowest speed at the lip that clears is 39.1 to 39.7 m/s for every bike, so the kicker is 1.6 m over 16 m, not the 2.0 m first planned (at 2.0 m a rider at 80 % of the Rustbucket's top speed still cleared). The dirt bike and slower miss it. Both platform hops clear from the dirt bike up; a moped falls in and wakes on the platform's far side.

For devs:

- **Road data.** Base's real-road data, which loads before every race, grows from 211,218 to 346,818 bytes gzip (+135,600, +64.2 %): Duval 41,139, the Seven Mile 94,461 (the critic estimated about 68 KB at 6 m bridge samples; the land ends, the junction pieces and the 2 m-sampled Moser road add the rest).
- **tbgis.** Two switches, off by default: `elevation.humpAt` (the hump over a real point) and a road's `deckTags` (`old-bridge` on the old road's decks). A stitched kicker's back now falls to the deck over the first half of its gap, and the bake's lint runs the game's grade rule: T9.1's kicker dropped to the deck in one sample past its lip, which the bake passed and the game's road lint failed. The README's new section, "Playtest 3: Duval Street and the Seven Mile Bridge", has the numbers.
- **The turn-off's split zone** is the shoulder's outer edge (d 4.95 to 8, the edge at 5.5), past the lines the AI (0.7 m in) and the dev bot (0.6 m in) aim for. On the travel lane, it swept rivals who were only passing a car onto the old road.
- **Not fixed here (sim/ai and sim/cops own it):** a rival or a cop can still end up on the old road when a kick or a pack passing a car pushes them against the rail inside the zone. On this bake none of 15 races did (seeds 1 to 8, each with and without a rider taking the old road). On an earlier bake with the same zone and fewer pads, which reshuffles every race, 3 of 14 did: a cop on the turn-off for 25 ticks once, and Chad Speedwell down the whole old road twice on seed 8 after a kick put him against the rail. On this bake the dev bot itself was pushed onto the old road once without steering for it. A rider who may not take a branch should never be handed onto it; that rule belongs in the sim.
- **Shared edits riding along:** the Keys region lists the two networks; the region backdrop's two girder bridges, drawn where the real bridges stand, now show only on keys-m1, Bahia Honda and Key West; `tools/gis/region-routes.test.ts` and `tests/sim/road-setpieces-live.test.ts` list the new routes (the platform trucks have no slot, so T5.2's rule leaves them out of the seeded set pieces: they are always there, as round 3 asked); `src/app/routes.test.ts` checks the rule instead of the Keys' exact route list; `src/render/render.test.ts` asks for rails and pylons on raised bridges only (Duval's land reaches 3 m).
