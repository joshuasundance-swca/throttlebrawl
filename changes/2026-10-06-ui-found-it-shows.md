FOUND IT now shows after a clean ride of the Seven Mile's old road. About a second after the second hop's landing the game knew you had found the shortcut, but the AIRTIME chip and the landing's one-liner held the top strip, the "FOUND IT" chip waited its turn for too short a time, and it was dropped unseen. A shortcut found is now always shown: it comes up as soon as the chip showing is done (it takes the strip from a rival's line or a landing line, which come back right after), and nothing drops it. A hop you miss still shows nothing, and a flip's cash chip still comes before its AIRTIME.

For devs: `ui/ticker.ts` keeps the stamp (`kind` `found`, `FOUND_KIND` in `ui/race-feed.ts`) the way it keeps a paid chip: `isKept` (paid, or found) replaces `isPaidStyle` in the stale-waiter filter, the queue limit, the rank (just above a bark; a paid flip still ranks above it), the frozen-on-preempt rule and the landing line kept behind it. A chip showing is still not cut short, so the stamp waits at most that chip's 1.1 s or 1.6 s. `ui/ticker-found.test.ts` plays the real events (tally, then `popItem`) on the model's clock one sim tick at a time: a clean ride at 65 and 69 ticks (the live check's two seeds), every lag from 0 to 400 ticks (shown within 2 s), the flip-before-AIRTIME order, bark and line preemption, the queue limit, and a negative control (a missed hop emits no `shortcutFound`, so nothing shows; 6 of the 8 tests fail on the old ticker and the control and the wait-behind test pass on both). `docs/architecture.md` ("One top ticker") is updated. Not phone-verified.
