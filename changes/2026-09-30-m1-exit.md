Milestone 1 is here: the first build you can play start to finish on your phone. Everything is deliberately crude (boxes, one look, one road), but all of it is in. Tap to start: it goes fullscreen and sideways, with sound. You race about 3.5 km of Keys coastal highway, over bridge humps, against Deacon Vane, Dial-Up, Chad Speedwell and Kevin from Accounting, with traffic both ways and pedestrians who dive clear. Drag up on the left for throttle and sideways to steer; tap the attack button to punch, swipe down on it to kick, and drag off it to pick a side. Kicks land only when you're close and your timing is right. Grab the lead pipe off the road, or snatch a rival's when it glints mid-swing. Crash, run back to your bike (or skip the run), and never get stuck. Sgt. Pruitt turns up about 20 seconds in; go down near him and you're busted. Near the start, hug the right edge at the end of the marina for the boat-ramp cut and its jump. Rivals talk trash in bubbles. The pause screen has restart, copy debug report and save debug file; a long-press on the build number opens the tuning panel (knockback, hit-stop, steering, shake, speed, traffic, the frame-rate cap and more). Add ?debug=1 to the link for a frame-rate readout, or ?selftest=1 to check your phone runs the race the same as ours (it should say MATCH).
