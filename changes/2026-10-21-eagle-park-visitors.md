Eagle Point's entry drive now has a walker or runner on a short stretch of grass above the river. Stay on the racing line for a clean pass, or pass close to the left shoulder and they hop back; a deliberate approach onto their grass makes them dive clear and return to their walk.

These are original park visitors using existing figures and reactions, not mapped walking paths or facility entrances. The highway and county route keep their own people. No new reward, crossing or route is added.
