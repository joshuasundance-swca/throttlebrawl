The sound mix got its M2 pass. Crashes now get bigger the harder you hit: a scrape is a thump and a squeal, and a wall at full speed adds a roar and skittering debris. Takedowns get their own boom-and-ding stinger, and when the slow motion lands the whole world sounds slow too: the effects drop in pitch and go muffled while the music ducks, then whoosh back to speed. Going over the bridge rail clangs, the water splashes, your style cash chimes, a rival's weapon glints when it can be snatched, and a close pass whooshes by. The surf-rock loop is four times longer, with a bridge, drum fills and rhythm-guitar stabs that come in as you speed up. Nothing clips any more in a big pile-up, even at full volume. The slow-motion sounds switch on by themselves once the takedown slow motion lands in the game.
