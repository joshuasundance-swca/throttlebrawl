Places now sound like themselves, not just like their region. On Duval Street a crowd murmurs the length of the street, swells at every bar's open front, and roosters crow now and then. In Bridge City the blocks hum under rain drumming on awnings, a streetcar rings its "ding-ding" as it comes near, the bridge-lift bell and horn sound from up the river when you cross a bridge, and a busker plays a fingerpicked tune at the square. On the Golden Gate and the headlands the wind gusts, harder with speed and out on the open deck, the deck clacks softly at its joints, and the horns are close by. On the Gorge the waterfalls roar as you come up to Latourell Creek and Shepperd's Dell, and fade behind you. All of it sits well under the engine, the wind and the rain, and the Regional sounds slider turns every bit of it off. Two hikers now stand at each of the two falls. Not phone-verified, and no one has heard it yet: the sounds are made in code and tuned by numbers, so expect the maintainer's ear to move a level or two.

For devs (playtest 4 run B, B11; the identity sheets D4, P6, G5, CR4; the existing soundscape from run W-Q and the Duval bar music from P4-16).

- **Beds and events** (`audio/soundscape.ts`, `soundscape-voices.ts`, `system.ts`): the director now returns `beds`, five steady levels (`crowd`, `gust`, `falls`, `city`, `awnings`, 0..1), beside its events. The voices build a bed's loop only when its level first goes above zero and stop it again after `BED_IDLE_S` (8 s) quiet, so a race that never meets a place pays nothing for its sound. Every bed's level at full is under the rain's (`BED_LEVEL`, tested against `RAIN_LEVEL`). Three new events: `rooster`, `gong` and `liftBell`. `inspect().soundscape.beds` shows the levels.
- **What triggers what**, all from the road's own data, never the region alone: `key-oldtown` (crowd at 0.3, up to 1 with the bar music's level; roosters every 7 to 18 s); `pdx-blocks` (city hum and awnings) and `pdx-deck` (a thinner hum, the lift bell and horn every 35 to 70 s); a vehicle whose id has `streetcar` within 70 m (the gong, once per car per 8 s); SF `bridge` over `water-open` (the Gate's open deck: gusts at exposure 1, joints at 0.6 of the Keys', foghorns at 12 to 22 s and louder) and `headlands` (gusts at exposure 0.7); a `roadsideZone` with `params.sound: falls` (the roar, full inside and fading over 140 m, in the Pacific Northwest). A bare `bridge` tag in SF is any overpass and is no Gate.
- **Zone music is now any region's.** `params.music` takes a fourth style, `busker`, and the director chains a zone's phrases in every region, not the Keys only (`bar-music.test.ts` is updated for that). The busker plays in the open; the bar styles still come through a doorway.
- **Data** (added by hand to the baked roads and to their bake configs, the bake not re-run, as in T10.4 and P4-16): two falls zones, `latourell-creek-falls` (s 1150 to 1220) and `shepperds-dell-falls` (s 600 to 660), each on the land just past its road's `bridge` tag, which is the creek bridge in the baked OSM geometry, and each with two rain-shell hikers (a zone spawns at least one person, so the falls carry the hikers a viewpoint would); and `music: busker` on the `courthouse-square` zone, which covers the `square-plaza` landmark. The baked bridges sit at about 45.539 N, 122.218 W (Latourell) and 45.547 N, 122.197 W (Shepperd's Dell), which fits the real creeks to a few hundred metres, checked from memory, not against a map. The older `latourell-falls` pedestrian zone stands 700 m past that bridge; left as it was.
- **Tests** (`place-soundscapes.test.ts`): each trigger and its negative (an overpass is no city, a bare SF `bridge` is no Gate, a zone with any other sound is silent), the exposure ratio, that a gust swings, the bed's lazy start, release and stop, the new events' sources all stop themselves, and the packs: every zone names a style or sound the voices play, each falls zone stands off its road's bridge, within 40 m of it, and is louder than 0.35 on the deck, the busker zone covers the plaza.
- **Budget**: audio only. No draw calls or triangles. First-load JavaScript grows by one inline object in `audio/index.ts`'s stand-in (about 60 bytes; the new code is in the lazy audio engine chunk, held by `lazy-engine.test.ts`); the full build was not run here, so the number is CI's. Not browser-run; not phone-verified.

Keeper fix: the two falls zones first sat over the creek bridges, which stood their hikers over the water and pushed roadside posts onto the deck; CI's land and scenery sweeps on the Gorge caught it. They now stand on the land just past each bridge (Latourell s 1150 to 1220, Shepperd's Dell s 600 to 660, in the baked roads and the bake config), and the roar is still loud on the bridge (0.40 and 0.55 of full at its middle).
