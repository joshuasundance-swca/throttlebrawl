Cops no longer ram the same car again and again. A cop who had moved in beside you kept riding your line after you got away, so from hundreds of metres back he rode a traffic lane at full pursuit speed into the car ahead, got back on, and hit it again. He now drops back once you are out of reach, watches the traffic ahead, and goes round a car or follows it until there is room. Dial-Up's road-wide swerve now shows in traffic too: when an oncoming car blocks the whole road he swerves across his own side instead of riding straight, and he swerves sharply enough to see. And a car no longer appears on top of another car that has just started to change lanes.
