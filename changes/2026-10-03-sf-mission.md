San Francisco has mural alleys. Pick **Mural Alleys** in the San Francisco route picker: one lane each way through a grid of two-storey shopfronts and painted alleys, with five tight corners to brake hard into. Twice the street runs straight at a corner wall where the streaming outfit painted its mascot (a grinning head in a headset, STAY TUNED), and a crew on a scaffold is painting it over while you race: by the time the field reaches the first wall the far end is primed and the thumbs-up is going; by the second it is gone and a sunrise is going up. New deadpan signs (ring-light parking, no drones, wet paint) go with it; long-press any to cut it. In the career, Chad's Collab Gone Wrong now races the mural alleys.
