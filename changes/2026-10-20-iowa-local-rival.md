Rural Rush now fields Mara Vale, a fictional Iowa road surveyor who races with a steady line and rarely starts a fight. She replaces one touring regular without changing the field size, pace, traffic, law or rewards. In Quick Play she joins the Iowa rival pool, whose field is shuffled each race, on either Iowa route. Her rider body and bike reuse existing models with different bike paint, and her new barks are text only.
