Two hidden pages for checking the game on your phone. Open the game with `?debug=1` at the end of the link to see a small readout in the corner: frames per second, frame time, how long the physics takes, the screen's refresh rate, what the renderer is drawing and the graphics chip's name. Open it with `?selftest=1` to run a one-minute robot race behind a panel that says MATCH when your phone computes exactly what the build machine did, or MISMATCH when it doesn't.

For agents (M1 dev-2): `src/dev/perf/` is the perf probe (frame-time percentiles, measured refresh rate, sim step time from the app's new step timer, draw calls, triangles, heap where Chromium shows it), behind the `?debug=1` overlay and the test handle's `perf()`. `src/dev/selftest/race.ts` is the self-test race: the base event with drafts, every rider on the in-sim AI and no player slot, 3,600 ticks; vite.config.ts bakes its hash into the build and `?selftest=1` compares (never the BotController). `tests/perf/perf.spec.ts` is the perf check that `npm run perf` now runs: draw calls and triangles at ticks 120, 480 and 840 of the seeded bot race against the budget, and frame p50/p95 and sim step p95 after 20 s at 4x CPU throttling against twice the baseline, printed with the renderer string. The soft baseline (`tests/perf/baseline.json`, `soft`) is this PR's first CI run: frame p50 33.3 ms and p95 66.6 ms, sim step p95 2.1 ms, on a GitHub runner with SwiftShader. Not active yet: forcing quality tier `low` with dynamic resolution off (render has neither in M1).
