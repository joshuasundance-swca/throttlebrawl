Every region gets one more radio station, each with a sound of its own (playtest 4: "feel free to make more for all regions"). The Keys get Ninety Miles 90.0 FM, Cuban son: a clave tapping its 3-2 pattern all song, congas, a bass that lands just ahead of every chord change, a piano montuno, a trumpet that states a tune, brass that calls and answers, a cowbell and a break where only the percussion plays. The Pacific Northwest gets Sunbreak 92.5, motorik dream pop: a glassy arpeggio and a pad open alone, a steady driving beat comes in under a bass in eighths, a chorus of chiming chords and a soaring lead crests, the drums and bass drop out in a haze, and the crest comes back. San Francisco gets Hella Latency 96.1, Bay Area instrumental hip hop: boom-bap drums with a clap on two and four, an 808 that glides into its notes, a chopped Rhodes and a whistling hook that returns at the end. Each has six songs, all written in code from each song's seed, every one can be cut with "cut this" like any other, the R key tunes through the new stations and Pivot FM's medleys can cut to the new bands too. They sit at the same quieter default mix as every other station. Done, not phone-verified.

For devs: `src/audio/radio-compose-trio.ts` (presets `son-band`, `dream-band`, `beat-band`; sixteen-bar songs; new layers `clave` and `bell`) and `src/audio/radio-rigs-trio.ts` (the rigs, trims in `TRIO_TRIM`), loaded only in the radio's lazy chunk (`radio-band.ts`). Station files: `packs/base/stations/keys-ultramar.json`, `packs/region-pnw/stations/pnw-sunbreak.json`, `packs/region-sf/stations/sf-hella-latency.json`. `src/audio/radio-trio.test.ts` holds each band to its spec and the rule that every station on every region's dial plays through the real band on a band of its own with no rig louder than the loudest shipping rig (read from the station files, so a later station is held to it too). `tests/e2e/audio-radio.spec.ts` renders the three bands and stations in a browser against the 3.5 dB band-to-band bar. Rig trims were set by an offline render (first eight seconds -22.0, -21.2 and -22.0 dBFS against -21.5 for the surf band and -21.6 for swamp). First-load JavaScript: 455.3 KB gzip of 500 (44.7 KB headroom), +752 B against main, which is the three station files and the genre names; the composers ride in the lazy chunk. A small shared edit rides along: `radio.ts` exports `RADIO_LEVEL`, `radio-compose-extra.ts` exports `phrase` and `radio-rigs-extra.ts` exports `lfo` and `fmKeys` for the new files, and the radio-in-the-mixer test picks the surf station by id instead of by dial position.
