Four new roadside weapons: the bike chain (long reach, drags a rider's speed), the driftwood club (splits after a while), the campaign sign (a wide shove, folds after three hits) and Kevin's briefcase (light, a big shove, the latches give out). Weapons also turn up about every 500 m along a race instead of at three fixed spots, and each race lays them in different places.
