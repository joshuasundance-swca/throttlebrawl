A real-race browser check now exercises the shipped voiced lines at Largest text size. After each actual clip transfer, it redisplays the line through the production cache and checks a fresh decoded playback start, the exact subtitle and speaker, and placement clear of the road and HUD. Deliberately clipped text and a hidden speaker prove that the layout check detects missing content.

This covers prepared playback and layout. The existing first-use and late-clip checks remain; cold-download timing, voice acting and real-phone audibility are separate checks.
