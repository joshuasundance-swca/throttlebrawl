Every race now starts from a fresh random seed, so the traffic comes in a different order each time, including when you press Restart (before this, every race until you reloaded the page used the same seed). The seed is saved with the race's recording, so a saved race still replays and resumes exactly, and the copied debug report shows it. Tests and the self-test keep their fixed seeds.
