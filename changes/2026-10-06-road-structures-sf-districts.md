San Francisco's Chinatown, North Beach and the Mission now have their placement in the road's plan, ahead of the work that makes buildings solid (the maintainer, 2026-10-06: "consistent physics and gameplay is important here so players know what to expect and how to interact with the world"; he accepted a road race in a physical world with honest edges: "Yeah that sounds good :)"). The picture does not change: render draws the same layout the plan is made of, triangle for triangle, and the sim does not read the plan yet.

- `src/road/structures/chinatown-northbeach.ts` and `src/road/structures/mission.ts`: the placement that `render/chinatown-northbeach.ts` and `render/mission.ts` did as they drew, moved unchanged (the same rules, the same hash streams, `+ - * /` and core's sin and cos in place of `Math`'s). Each is one kept layout per network and seed (every front, second row, balcony, bay, patio rail, side street, park building; the Mission's wall lines, buildings and mascot wall) and a planner, the layout's solids at their drawn shape: 2158 in Chinatown and North Beach and 846 in the Mission at seed 7. The Mission's walls follow the road, so a building is a run of boxes (a straight stretch is one; 424 buildings in 2879 segments of 2 m make 522 boxes, 1.9 % more floor than is drawn), and the mascot scaffold's planks (3.5, 7, 10.5 and 13.5 m up) are ledges. Two rows join `STRUCTURE_LAYERS`.
- Not planned: anything with no side of 1.5 m (a blade sign, a table, a planter, a bench, a tree trunk), sheets a rider passes under (an awning, a cornice, a lantern string, a railing panel) and the tower 70 m out. Render draws them as before.
- What changed in the picture, once: the layers no longer read `dressing` (the road files plus a career's incident-site boards), only the road's own tags and features, so a plan is the same in every race of one content and seed. In a career race with a bust on a Chinatown road, the old code left a gap in the frontage for the cone (a 6 to 10 m lot), and now the building stands and the cone's placard rests against its front. Nothing else reads `dressing` in these layers.
- The planners build into the road's lazy planner chunk, `road-structures`, outside the sim chunk and never in the first load: `scripts/sim-chunk.mjs` keeps `src/road/structures/` out of the sim chunk's group and hashes that chunk's code with the sim chunk's, so a planner change moves the replay key and a render change does not. (This PR first made its own `structures` chunk; the downtowns' port, #643, landed the same split as `road-structures` first, and the keeper's merge of main took #643's chunk, module-map and test changes, which cover these planners.) First-load JavaScript 461.7 KB gzip in this tree against 462.3 KB on main's (same probe, in-memory builds), 38.3 KB of headroom under 500; its own chunk was 4.8 KB gzip, lazy (both figures from before the merge with #643). The shared-config edits it first carried (`scripts/sim-chunk.mjs` with `vite.config.ts`, and `scripts/module-map.mjs` with the road entry `structures/*`) are main's now, from #643: render's lazy layers import a planner directly and the road's index never re-exports one (a static import from the index puts the planners back in the sim chunk and the first load).
- Docs: the architecture doc's "Physical world" lists the moved layers and what they leave out, and the lazy-loading and determinism notes say where the planners' chunk is hashed.

Tests: `src/road/structures/districts.test.ts` (16: the layers load and keep one plan; the plan is the same for a second network and for the road files as dressing; no lane lies under a structure; boxes cover their run and are 1.9 % over in all; a box moved 1 m, a front moved onto the road, a missing structure are found); `scripts/structures-districts.test.ts`, the twin of the hitbox audit (9: every structure's roof corners are drawn within 2 cm and every drawn roof quad stands under a structure's top; a structure moved, raised, resized or missing is found; the planners import only road modules); `src/render/sf-districts-drawn.test.ts` against a golden made by running main's own code (every soup's triangles exactly, area, mean and bounds within 2 cm, 4238 roof quads for the seeds the other tests use; a different seed, a roof moved and a roof dropped are found). Also run: the two districts' existing render tests (37 tests in the four files that plan them), `scripts/sim-chunk.test.ts` (main's case since #643: a planner change moves the replay key), `scripts/first-load.test.ts` and `scripts/lint-rules.test.ts`.
