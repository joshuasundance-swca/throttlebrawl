A heat meter, shown as a small badge at the top of the screen only while you are hot. Kicking and punching people, taking riders down (cops most of all), riding the wrong way, smashing through roadworks and speeding past a radar trap all raise your heat. At the first level one more cop comes after you from behind, at the second a pair of them, and they come fast. At the top level the cops set up a roadblock across your lanes up the road: go round it, or ride into the back of it and lose your speed. Ride clean for a few seconds and the heat cools, faster on dirt or grass with no cop near; let it run out and the cops chasing you for it give up ("LOST 'EM").
