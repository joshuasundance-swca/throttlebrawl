A heat meter. Kicking and punching people, taking riders down (cops most of all), riding the wrong way, smashing through roadworks and speeding past a radar trap all raise your heat. At the first level one more cop comes after you from behind, at the second a pair of them, and they come fast. Ride clean for a few seconds and the heat cools; let it run out and the cops chasing you for it give up. The roadblock at the top level comes next.
