Two new settings. Display has "View": Chase, Far or Helmet, and it switches at once, even mid-race. Sound has "Radio": the score, a station or off. The pause menu also gets a small radio panel. It shows what is playing, and has Next station, Next song and "Cut song" (tap it, then "Cut it") for a song you never want to hear again. The panel appears once the game hands the radio to the menu.
