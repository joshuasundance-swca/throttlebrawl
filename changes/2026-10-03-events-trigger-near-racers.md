Moving road events now happen where you are, not out of sight. The cable car that loses its grip, the log truck shedding its load and the boat on its trailer used to be put on the road 500 to 600 m ahead. Traffic cleared away anything that far out, so on the Russian Hill route the cable car rolled back in only 4 of 10 races; in the other 6 the signs went up and then nothing happened. A log truck that far ahead could also still be pulling away when its event ended. Now each vehicle appears about 340 m ahead of the leading rider, the event stays on until the riders reach it, and if traffic takes the vehicle early it is put back. The cable car can now also run on San Francisco's walled cable street, so it turns up on the default route too, which it never did before. There are still at most two road events in a race.
