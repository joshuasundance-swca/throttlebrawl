San Francisco has a waterfront. Pick **Waterfront** in the San Francisco route picker: a four-lane boulevard along the bay from the foot of the big bridge, with a palm-lined promenade, numbered pier sheds (even ones before the clock-tower ferry hall, odd ones after), the ferry hall itself with a ferry at its slip, brick lofts, a crab house, an old hotel and short side streets on the city side, and a tight bend at the end down to the sea lions' floats. Ride the promenade if you like: the seawall's edge is a splash, the pier fronts are walls. Only startup pop-ups and cafe tables smash here. New deadpan signs, billboards and four roadside scenes go with it; long-press any sign or billboard to cut it. In the career, the first San Francisco race ("Pier Pressure") now runs the waterfront.
