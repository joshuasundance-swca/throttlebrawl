The sim side of road events (W-P): sim/modifiers now runs the first event-modifier effect, `set-piece`, with five pieces in a closed list: roadwork (a cone taper and line, a sawhorse, a work truck with an arrow board, a flagger), a crash scene (flares, a stalled car and a tow truck with a light bar, a cop waving traffic by; nobody hurt), a parade (floats with their dressing, an optional giant inflatable, marchers, barricades), a hay truck that drops bales as racers close in, and a speed trap whose cop, moved from the lot to the roadside, chases a player who passes him over the limit (siren cause `speed-trap`). Each race rolls them from the `modifiers` stream, places them on open road (no bridge, rail, split, ramp, pad or cop lot), wakes them as racers come within 350 m (`modifierStart`) and clears them once everyone is past (`modifierEnd`). Cones, flares, sawhorses and bales scatter when riders or cars go through; a bale or sawhorse wobbles a rider; people step out of the way. Parked vehicles stand on the verge, because the rival AI and the bot never get round a stopped vehicle in a one-lane direction. The props reach render through `SimSnapshot.props`. A new slider, Road events (`modifiers.setPieceChance`), scales how often they turn up. No pack content uses it yet.
