All four rivals now line up for the Causeway Sprint: Deacon Vane, Dial-Up, Chad Speedwell and Kevin from Accounting race you, and each other, from the start. The hunters (Deacon and Kevin) ease off to let you catch up instead of slamming the brakes, and chase a little harder when you get away. Once you cross the line the fighting stops and the rest hurry home, so the results come sooner.
