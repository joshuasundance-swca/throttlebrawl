The coastal road is three times longer. Past the Sandbar Causeway it now runs through the Mangrove Cut, along Tarpon Flats, through the town of Conch Row and over the Almost Seven Mile Bridge, then out along the Last Resort Causeway. That gives the race three lengths: the short sprint you know (about 3.5 km), a standard race that ends in Conch Row (about 7.4 km), and a long haul to the last causeway (about 11 km). Picking a length arrives with the race-length setting.
