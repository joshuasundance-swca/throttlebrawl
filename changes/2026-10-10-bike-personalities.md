Three existing bikes now offer clearer tradeoffs beyond top speed. The Mudflap has
a punchier initial launch and nimble steering. The Streetfighter accelerates
and brakes more sharply, with quicker line changes than the Grand Tourer.
The heavy Grand Tourer gives up less ground in
rider contact, but needs more time and room to change pace or line.

Top speeds, prices, unlocks and the Rustbucket starter remain unchanged. Wheelies
and airborne tricks still follow the same balance rules on every bike.

Streetfighter steering keeps held-right Seven Mile bridge exits accessible.
The Tourer's softer brake preserves the existing drift-event targets alongside
the handling tradeoffs; road, collision and drift rules remain unchanged.
