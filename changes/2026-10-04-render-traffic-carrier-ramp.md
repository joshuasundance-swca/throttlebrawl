The ramp trucks that drive are in the live game now, with a ramp you can see, and the roads have a few new faces (playtest 3: "the ramp trucks could be in motion", and "bike riders cause collisions when they should arguably get out of the way").

- The car carrier that drives ahead of you in the Keys, the Pacific Northwest and San Francisco is a yellow tow truck. Until you are 200 m behind it, it carries its ramp folded up at the back and is an ordinary big vehicle. Then the whole bed tips down to the road, striped like a hazard sign, and you can ride up it and fly. The three carriers and their three road events are no longer dev-only drafts.
- Clip a cyclist, scooter or e-bike and it topples over onto the sidewalk away from you, lies there for the two seconds the game already gave it, and gets up. A slow brush that does not topple it tips nothing.
- The elk, the raccoon and the sea lion are their own animals now (antlers, a masked face and a ringed tail, flippers), no longer the chicken at a different size. New shapes are ready for the Keys' pedicab, island tram and rooster, Portland's streetcar and San Francisco's parrot flock; nothing places them on the road yet, so you will not see them until the content task adds their types.

For devs: the figures are in `src/render/traffic-figures.ts` (a unit-box figure per type, so each is one draw call in view); `src/render/views.ts` swaps a carrier to its lowered-ramp twin at its `setPieceBeat` `rampDown` and keeps it for the race, and tips a kerb rider's figure on a `wobble` with `data.kerb` and `data.toppleS` (so a nudge with no `toppleS` tips nothing; the plan said `data.kerb` alone, but the sim as built only topples with `toppleS`). The drawn ramp is checked against the sim's own 5 m run and 1.22 m lip by ray-casting the figure (`traffic-figures.test.ts`). `draw-headroom.test.ts` counts the draws: a carrier adds 1 call (2 with one ramp up and one down), a region's new kinds add 1 each (at most 3 in the Keys or the Pacific Northwest). Composed with the plan's figures for today's peak scene (96, from the plan, not re-measured here) and the skids and smoke (2), that is 103 of 120. First-load JavaScript, built here and on main with the same script: 471,388 B gzip against the 500 KB budget (468,528 B on main, so +2,860 B, with 39.7 KB of headroom). The six pack files that were draft are `live`; the docs sections "Jumps, ramps and airtime" and "Event modifiers" say so. The `elk`, `raccoon` and `sea-lion` type files still say "placeholder shape" in their notes and name the chicken preset; the content task can tidy that. Done, not phone-verified.
