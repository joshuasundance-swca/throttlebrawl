On a phone held sideways the chase camera can now sit a little higher and further back than on a laptop, so you see more of the road ahead on the short, wide screen. A laptop-shaped screen keeps today's view. It switches on once the game tells the camera the screen's shape (a small follow-up). The extra height and distance, and the screen shapes it applies to, are sliders in the tuning panel.
