The view, the shadows and the sound now agree with a rider on a roof or over a rail. Riding a truck's roof, the camera looks along the roof the way it looks along the road (it used to aim at the road far below), and it does not tip forward as it does in a flight. Flying over a roof, the camera and the shadow both judge your height from the roof, and the shadow falls on it, not on the road under the truck. Past a rail, the shadow of a falling body is on the water. The road's own sounds (rain on the ground, a bridge's joints) go on while you ride a roof.

A fall over a rail is shown by how far it is. A low splash into water keeps its gag: the water thrown, the ring, the gator or the fisherman, the splash. A high drop (over 25 metres: the Golden Gate, Chuckanut's bluff) is the same fall with the same penalty and respawn, shown as a clean cut-away: the camera holds on the rail while you go over and cuts to you where you wake, with no splash, no gator or fisherman and no splash sound. The Golden Gate's tone-guide line now says so ("(a)": the same physics everywhere, the Golden Gate included). A low splash is also drawn at the water's own level now (Lake Samish's is not the sea's).

For devs: `src/camera/chase.ts` (the aim lift on a support, `floorY` for the air tip, the fall's hold, `FALL_HOLD_MAX_S`), `src/app/camera-target.ts` (the camera's target from the snapshot), `src/render/shadows.ts` (`groundYOf`), `src/render/views.ts` and `effects.ts` (the high splash has no effect; the splash at the water's level), `src/audio/cues.ts` (a high `splash` is silent) and `src/audio/rolling.ts` (`rollingOn`: riding a support is rolling, though `grounded` is false). Data only: nothing is added to the sim, and no budget is touched (no new geometry or draw call).
