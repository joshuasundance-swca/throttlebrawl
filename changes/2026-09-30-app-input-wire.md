The phone now buzzes when you land a hit, take one, score a takedown or crash (a short tap for a jab, longer for a crash). The settings record's control choices (steering method, tilt sensitivity, auto-throttle, pull-back brake, haptics and gamepad remaps) now reach the controls, and tilt steering, once chosen, treats the way you hold the phone at the start of each race as straight ahead. The settings screen that shows these choices comes with the next settings update.
