The radio is on, early. Press R in a race to flip from the original score to Grease Trap 1340 AM (rockabilly), then Low Tide 91.7 (surf), then off, then back to the score; Shift+R skips a song. On a phone, open the tuning panel and move "Radio" (0 off, 1 score, 2 rockabilly, 3 surf). Each station has four short songs written in code: plucked-string guitars, a tremolo-picked lead and a spring reverb on surf, and a slapped upright bass, a shuffle beat and a slapback echo on rockabilly. Every song is generated from its own seed, so it is original and plays the same every time. The music now ducks for a moment under a crash. The station panel on the pause menu, where a long-press cuts a song, comes with the pause menu work; the cut itself is already wired as data.
