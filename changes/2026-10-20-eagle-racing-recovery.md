Eagle Point no longer resets a rider for crossing the last centimetre of a visible shoulder on the way to grass. Physical support now checks the actual simplified shoulder triangles, including the small overlap used to join the road to terrain. Empty wedges, missing ground, water and drops remain hazards.

Deterministic checks ride the Rustbucket, Streetfighter and Superbike from asphalt across grass and back on both park loops, then continue riding after the return and replay the inputs. They approach at half each bike's shipped top speed and brake over the grass. This does not make every bend or uneven offroad line safe at full speed, and it is not phone playtest verification.
