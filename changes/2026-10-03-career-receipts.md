The world keeps receipts (the pitch deck's #14, `[default]` and vetoable): put a rival into a vehicle, or get busted, in a career race, and in that region's next career races the nearest roadside board to the spot says so. A billboard reads, say, "SNOWBIRD RV 1, KEVIN FROM ACCOUNTING 0." and a sign "INCIDENT SITE #3." (numbered by your busts). Each region writes its own: a Keys law office that returns calls eventually, Pacific Northwest moss, a San Francisco pop-up. Only what really happened fills them, at most three a race, each within 600 m of where it happened; long-press one to cut it like any sign. The bust uses an existing roadside sign slot, not a traffic cone yet.
