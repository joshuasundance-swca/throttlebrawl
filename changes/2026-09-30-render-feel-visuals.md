Hits and crashes look like they hurt now. A rider you punch or kick flashes white for a moment and throws a burst of sparks, and a kick throws more. When you crash, the bike flies off on its own and cartwheels end over end before it falls on its side, while the rider tumbles separately and ends up face down. Some pieces are drawn but wait for other updates still in progress. The screen takes a cool blue tint during a takedown's slow motion, which arrives with the takedowns. A rider who goes over a bridge rail makes a splash, and a gator surfaces or a fisherman in his skiff throws his arms up. A knocked-off rival gets up and shakes a fist at whoever did it. Signs and billboards can stand beside the road, and the game can tell which one you are pointing at, ready for the "cut this" long-press. The road's first signs come with the road update.
