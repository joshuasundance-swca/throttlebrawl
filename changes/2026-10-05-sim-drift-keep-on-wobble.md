A drift chain now survives a wobble. Only a crash loses it (the maintainer, playtest 4: "it's too easy to lose a drift. I think crashing should lose it but maybe not just wobbling or bumping"). A wobble, a bump or a brush with traffic, a rival, a wall or a prop, a punch or kick that only staggers you, and a flick of the bars that wobbles you all still end the slide you are in, with no exit boost, exactly as before. But the chain and its cash stay up on the top line, and the next drift within the usual 4 seconds is the next link (x1.5, x2 and so on); if none comes, the chain banks what it has. A crash empties the chain the moment you go down, not when you get back on the bike, and "DRIFT LOST" shows then and only then.

Finishing the race with a chain still open now pays it. Cross the line mid-slide or with a chain waiting for the next corner and its cash drops as a normal "DRIFT" chip at the line, slide in progress included; before, it was forfeited and the strip said "DRIFT LOST". Now "DRIFT LOST" is a crash's alone.

For devs: the rule is in `src/sim/riders/drift.ts` (`crashedLast` empties the chain; `knockedLast`, a wobble or a hit taken, ends a slide through `endDrift`'s new `knocked` path, which opens the chain window and puts `kept` on the `driftEnd` event where a crash puts `lost`) with one new hook, `driftDown`, called from the riders phase for a rider who is not on the road, so a crash empties the chain the tick after the rider goes down (it used to wait for the remount; the remount still empties it as a net). `driftFinish` pays a chain open when a racer stops racing: `closeStyle` in `src/sim/race/style.ts` calls it once per finisher (over the line, or classified at the race end) and scores a `drift` style event; the `driftEnd` it emits carries `finish: true`, which `scoreStyle` skips so nothing is scored twice. `src/ui/moves-meter.ts` (`driftOutcome`, `chainLossShown`) shows "DRIFT LOST" only for a rider who is down. Tests, by sim ticks: `tests/sim/drift-keep.test.ts` (the same wall touch as a wobble or a crash; a rival's bump, a car's side brush and a punch that staggers, each from the real system; a chain open at the finish line, mid-slide or waiting, paid once; the strip; per-tick world hashes equal across two runs) and a new block in `src/sim/riders/drift.test.ts`. Two expectations in `drift.test.ts` changed on purpose: "a wobble mid-drift empties the meter, nothing banks" now says the slide ends and the cash is kept and banks, and the highside (a bar flick at speed) test no longer expects `lost`. Not browser-run; not phone-verified.
