A drift chain now survives a wobble. Only a crash loses it (the maintainer, playtest 4: "it's too easy to lose a drift. I think crashing should lose it but maybe not just wobbling or bumping"). A wobble, a bump or a brush with traffic, a rival, a wall or a prop, a punch or kick that only staggers you, and a flick of the bars that wobbles you all still end the slide you are in, with no exit boost, exactly as before. But the chain and its cash stay up on the top line, and the next drift within the usual 4 seconds is the next link (x1.5, x2 and so on); if none comes, the chain banks what it has. A crash empties the chain the moment you go down, not when you get back on the bike, and "DRIFT LOST" shows then and only then.

One case I kept as it was: finishing the race with a chain still open forfeits it, and the strip still says "DRIFT LOST" for that (it is not a crash; say if you would rather it banked at the finish instead).

For devs: the rule is in `src/sim/riders/drift.ts` (`crashedLast` empties the chain; `knockedLast`, a wobble or a hit taken, ends a slide through `endDrift`'s new `knocked` path, which opens the chain window and puts `kept` on the `driftEnd` event where a crash puts `lost`) with one new hook, `driftDown`, called from the riders phase for a rider who is not on the road, so a crash empties the chain the tick after the rider goes down (it used to wait for the remount; the remount still empties it as a net). `src/ui/moves-meter.ts` (`driftOutcome`, `chainLossShown`) shows "DRIFT LOST" only for a down or finished rider. Tests, by sim ticks: `tests/sim/drift-keep.test.ts` (the same wall touch as a wobble or a crash; a rival's bump, a car's side brush and a punch that staggers, each from the real system; the strip; per-tick world hashes equal across two runs) and a new block in `src/sim/riders/drift.test.ts`. Two expectations in `drift.test.ts` changed on purpose: "a wobble mid-drift empties the meter, nothing banks" now says the slide ends and the cash is kept and banks, and the highside (a bar flick at speed) test no longer expects `lost`. Not browser-run; not phone-verified.
