Ink + 60s film is now the default look for a new save. A save that already has a look keeps it, and Classic is still in Settings, Display, Look. If the game runs slow on an ink look for several seconds of a race, a small note at the top offers "Switch to Classic" or "No thanks". It does not stop the race, the pause menu shows it too, and "No thanks" means it won't ask again.
