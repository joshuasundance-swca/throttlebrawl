Every bike sounds like what it is. A rival on a chopper thumps like a V-twin, scooters buzz, sport bikes scream, dirt bikes rasp, and the lawn mower putters, picked from the bike each rider is drawn on. Rivals' engines now sit to your left or right as they pass (on stereo speakers or headphones), on top of the pitch bend as they go by.
