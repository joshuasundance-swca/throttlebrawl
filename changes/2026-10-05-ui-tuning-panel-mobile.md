The tuning panel no longer covers the pause button or traps you on a phone. It used to sit over the top-right corner, on top of the pause button and the HIT and BRAKE buttons, and its Close button scrolled away with the sliders, so the only way out was to reload the page. Now it stands under the pause button, on the same side (so the left-handed mirror moves it too), and stops above the HIT and BRAKE buttons, scrolling inside that space. Its title bar, with a new Minimise button beside Close, stays put while the sliders scroll, so one tap always gets it out of the way: Minimise leaves just the title bar, Close shuts it. The pause button works with the panel open. The other thumb's side stays free to steer, so the panel is still usable mid-race. The cost is height: about 200 px on a 915 by 412 phone and about 140 px on a 568 by 320 one (the sliders scroll).

For devs: `src/ui/tuning/place.ts` (new, pure: `planPanel` works out the stand from the screen, the safe areas, the mirror and how far the touch buttons reach; unit-tested in `place.test.ts` over 11 screens and both hands, with the old stand as a negative control), `src/ui/tuning/index.ts` (header outside the scrolling part, Minimise, re-placing on open, resize, a new race and the mirror). It reads `--touch-reach` and `--touch-rise` from `#ui` and the pause button's `.mirrored`, so no first-load source file is touched and the panel stays a lazy chunk (`scripts/first-load.test.ts` passes locally; the first-load size itself was not measured here, CI's perf check reports it). Layout check: `tests/e2e/ui-style-popups.spec.ts` opens the panel over a running race at 915x412, 915x412 left-handed, 568x320 (real touch taps) and 412x915 upright (a fine pointer, as the other upright cases use: a touch device held upright shows the rotate screen), measures it against the pause button and the touch buttons, keeps its two header controls on screen and hit-tested before and after the sliders are scrolled to the end, taps the pause button with the panel up, minimises, closes and reopens it, with a negative control and an empty known list. Not covered: the slow-frames offer's two buttons and the pause screen's radio buttons can sit under the panel while it is open. Browser specs were not run on the dev machine (CI runs them). Not phone-verified.

Keeper's fix on this PR (CI's first run): while the pause screen is up the panel docks on the right whichever hand, because the pause menu, Resume first, stands in the left half; left-handed, the panel had covered Resume. `planPanel` takes `paused`, and the panel re-places when the pause screen opens or closes.
