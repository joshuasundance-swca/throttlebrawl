The chase-camera crest test now treats the Jones Street branch's two crests as blind by design: the brink of Jones's 29 % face, and the hump where the branch climbs back onto Leavenworth (about 15 % up, then about 15 % down). There the crest stands above the camera's line of sight, so no aim shows the road past it until the top is about 13 m away (about a third of a second at the test's 1.5x speed). Those frames are counted and printed, capped at 120 per screen shape (measured 51 and 76 over four seeds), and every other road keeps the full check.
