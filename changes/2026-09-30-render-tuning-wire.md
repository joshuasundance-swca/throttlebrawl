The tuning panel has a new "visuals" group with nine sliders for the new hit and crash looks: how long a hit flashes, how many sparks fly and how fast, how strong the slow-motion tint is, how fast a crashed bike and rider spin, how high a splash goes, how long the gator or the fisherman stays, and how long a knocked-off rider takes to get up and to shake a fist. They change only how things look, never the race, and they save into a copied preset like the other sliders.
