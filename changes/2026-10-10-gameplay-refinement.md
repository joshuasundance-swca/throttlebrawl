Takedowns keep a wider forward view of the road, so a rival flying behind you does not pull the camera away from the next corner. The celebration, slow motion and held look-back remain.

Some rivals can choose the Old Seven Mile road, and police can follow. Riders build momentum and line up before its jumps using their own bikes; the missing span, failed jumps and splash penalties still apply. Explicitly closed AI branches remain closed.

Takedown hunts put targets ahead of the player on the starting grid. Cop escapes begin with a nearby officer and an immediate siren, followed by the normal siren lead before pursuit. Objective credit still requires the existing pursuit and escape rules. Ordinary races and grudge matches keep their starting rules.
