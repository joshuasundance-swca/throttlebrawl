Settings now has a steering style, on the Race tab just above Steering assist. **Arcade**, the default, is the steering you have been riding all along under an honest name: the stick sets a heading relative to the road, and letting go lines the bike up with it again. That guidance was never the steering assist, which is why "assist off" still felt like riding on a track. **Free** is the true off: the bike turns at the same rate, so full lock holds the same bends, but nothing turns it back. Hold the lock and the bike keeps turning well past Arcade's limit (about 8° off the road at 40 m/s). Let go and it keeps pointing where it was, so it goes where it points. The steering assist is separate and works with either style. Rivals and cops keep riding Arcade. The choice applies from the next race and is saved in the race's recording, so a replay rides the same way. Not phone-verified.

For devs: `SimAssists.steerStyle` (`'arcade' | 'free'`, absent means Arcade), written from the new `Settings.steerStyle` by `settingsAssists`, so it lands in `SimConfig.slots[i]` and the replay header; the save version stays 1. In `stepGrounded`, Free's turn is `yawTarget × YAW_RESPONSE` with no `− yaw` term. Arcade's expression is untouched, and AI riders (slot −1) always ride it. Arcade is byte-identical to before. In CI, a bot race with the style written as Arcade matches the old slot shape on every one of its 8,531 state hashes. On the dev machine, this branch's default riding matched origin/main on every per-tick hash of two whole bot races (7,795 and 7,146 ticks) and on 2,400 values of a scripted full-lock ride. Tests: `src/sim/riders/steer-style.test.ts` covers held lock past Arcade's cap, no pull back on a straight, the default, the AI and the ±1.2 rad bound. `tests/sim/riders-steer-style.test.ts` covers Arcade hashes, four isolated Free bot races finishing, and Free in the replay header replaying hash for hash. The settings tests in `src/ui` and `src/save` are updated, and `tests/e2e/ui-settings.spec.ts` gets a Free probe that CI runs. Still guided in Free, and unchanged: edge and barrier contact zero the heading, the split-zone guide, the lane-drop funnel, and a jump's flight following the road (forgiving landings). The brief said a "weak" return; Free has none, per the maintainer's "true assist off" and the spec's `[decided]` "no hidden pull toward the road".
