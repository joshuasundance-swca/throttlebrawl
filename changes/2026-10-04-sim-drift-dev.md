The drift (playtest 3, T2.3) fills the contract's stub in `src/sim/riders/drift.ts`. A player who holds the brake at 0.7 or more and half lock or more into a bend (κ ≥ 1/150 within 30 m) for 9 ticks above `riders.driftMinMps` (18 m/s) starts a slide: the steering reaches up to 1.8× farther (`riders.driftSteerGain`), a drag of up to 2.5 m/s² (`riders.driftDrag`) costs speed, and the lean goes to the cap. Letting the bars go, turning them the other way or dropping under `riders.driftExitMps` (10 m/s) ends it. A clean exit boosts 2 to 6 m/s for 1.2 s and opens a 4 s chain window (`riders.driftChainS`); the chain's cash banks as a `driftEnd` with `bank: true`, which sim/race already scores as `drift` style. A crash, a wobble, a hit, a crash and remount, or a flick of the bars at speed (a highside: a wobble, never a crash) empties it. The brake share is 0.7, not the spec's half, so the dev bot's half-brake car follows and 0.6 speed matches never drift. AI riders never drift. The two speed floors are tuning keys for a walking-pace hairpin such as Lombard (the critic's C5); below 12 m/s the brake and full lock make a U-turn instead, so a slow drift turns in short of full lock. Drift style pays nothing in a real race yet: `perDriftSecondCash` reaches the sim once K0b's `buildSimConfig` defaults land. `tests/sim/drift-hairpins.test.ts` rides a drift bot on the drift events' routes and prints the gate G5 figure, the bot's banked drift cash at $1 a drift second. `docs/product-spec.md`'s Drift row gains the `[default]` how.
