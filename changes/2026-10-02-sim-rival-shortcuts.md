Rivals take the shortcuts now, some of the time. Each rival decides at the start which of a route's alleys, spurs and ramps it will try, lines up for it in the last stretch before the split, and vanishes down it. The ones that don't plan to keep out of the turn-off. A rival lining up for one no longer barges a rider riding beside it into the turn-off: it drops in behind you and crosses after.
