Gaps, jumpable walls and the overboard respawn (playtest 3, T3.1) fill the contract's stubs, for the Seven Mile's missing span ("a miss = splash, respawn on the highway") and the static ramp trucks' shortcuts. Nothing a player sees changes until a road file carries a `gap` or a `jumpable` wall (none does yet). New `src/road/gap.ts` answers where there is no surface (`gapAt`), where a rider wakes past a gap (`gapFarSide`, carried onto the next road and past any further gap), the nearest point on the route's main road (`nearestOnEdges`) and the jumpable wall on a side (`jumpableWallAt`). `src/sim/riders/gap.ts`: a grounded rider over a gap takes off, an airborne one never lands on it, and past the gap's kill depth (1.5 m) it crashes with `cause: 'gap'`, `overboard: true`; one more than 0.4 m below the deck on its last tick over the gap hits the far deck's broken end (`face: true`). An airborne rider out at a jumpable wall and higher than it skips the barrier rule, and is never pulled back through once past it. The tumble starts an overboard crash with both bodies falling (a `railOver` with `gap: true` each), and a body that slides into a gap falls through, so none rests on one. The respawn comes after the usual splash penalty: on the far side, or with `respawn: 'main'` on the route's main road nearest the splash, facing along the route; the `respawn` event adds `gap` and `at`. The road lint flags a gap on a main-path road (traffic runs it), a bad gap or landmark param, a jumpable rail, and a landmark footprint on the road or its verge unless it is `overRoad` (rule `landmark-clear`). No tuning, no state, no random draw: a race with no gap hashes as before (tested with a gap nobody reaches). Measured in the sim with the 38 m/s test bike: off a 2.0 m / 16 m kicker a 64 m gap is cleared from 38 m/s (1.98 s of air, landing at 30.8 m/s after the air drag) and missed from 25 m/s, so the Seven Mile bake should measure its own jump speeds per bike rather than use the vacuum figures. `tests/sim/batch.ts` needs no new switch. The docs' Jumps and Crash tumble sections and the road Validation bullet say all this. One shared line rides along: `src/road/index.ts` exports the new queries.
