The rivals can ride like themselves now: turn on "Rival style quirks" in the tuning panel's Rivals group. Dial-Up swerves across the whole road, Chad Speedwell only picks fights that look good on stream (never with whoever is winning, never with anyone healthier than him), Kevin from Accounting hunts you for the rest of the race once you hit him, and Deacon Vane is slow off the line. The switch is off by default for now, so races play exactly as before until the playtest says otherwise. Every regular also has new lines for when they are leading, hurt, near the finish or fighting their own rivals, and three Florida locals, Tammy Two-Stroke, The Mayor and Mother Rust, now exist as riders, ready for the career's events.
