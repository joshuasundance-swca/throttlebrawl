The rivals ride like themselves now. Dial-Up swerves all over the road, Chad Speedwell only picks fights that look good on stream (never with whoever is winning), Kevin from Accounting remembers every hit you land on him for the rest of the race, and Deacon Vane is slow off the line but still comes for you. Each of them also has new lines for when they are leading, hurt, near the finish or fighting their own rivals. Three Florida locals, Tammy Two-Stroke, The Mayor and Mother Rust, now exist as riders, ready for the career's events. A "Rival style quirks" switch in the tuning panel's Rivals group turns the new habits off.
