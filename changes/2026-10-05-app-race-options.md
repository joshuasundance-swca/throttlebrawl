A race from the main menu now has options. Tap **Options** beside Race: pick the bike (any bike in the game, slowest first with its top speed, without a trip to the career garage; the secret joke rides join once your garage owns one), the time of day (any, as before, or one of the region's lights), the weather (the region's own, dry or rain), how many rivals ride (the usual field, or none up to seven), whether the cops ride at all, the difficulty, the race length, and the traffic (none, light, usual or heavy). The region and the road are still picked on the menu, and the options screen says where the race will run. Race on the options screen, or on the menu, starts the race with your picks, and the game remembers them between races and after a reload. They apply to menu races only: a career race keeps its own event, field and bike. Each option is one row with an arrow at each end, big enough for a thumb, and tapping the value steps it too; on a keyboard, Tab to a row and use the arrow keys, and Escape goes back. New race and challenge types are not here yet: the maintainer wants to compare them first, so the options carry an empty slot for them. Laps wait for a road that loops, since every route today runs point to point.

For devs (playtest 4, P4-12 and P4-13).

- **The record** (`src/save/index.ts`): the settings record gains `raceOptions` (`kind`, `bike`, `timeOfDay`, `weather`, `rivals`, `cops`, `traffic`), additive with every default changing nothing, so the version stays 1; `sanitiseRaceOptions` drops a bad field to its default. Difficulty and the race length stay the record's own `difficulty` and `raceLength`, which the options screen also steps.
- **Into the race** (`src/app/race-options.ts`, `src/app/config.ts`): `menuRaceSetup` turns the picks into `RaceSetup` fields, only those a pick changes: `playerBike`, `timeOfDay` (a free-play race's light instead of the seed's draw, so it also picks the road events eligible at that light), `rivals` (the first n of the same seeded shuffle of the region's cast, so the usual count draws the usual field), `cops: false` (no cop riders, and the event's cops block reads `none`) and `trafficScale` (times the `traffic.density` slider, kept in its 0 to 3). Each is in the `SimConfig` the replay header carries; with no picks a config is byte-identical to before. The weather is render only (`showRegion` drops the palette's `rain` colour for dry, or asks for rain). The replay header gains `raceOptions`, the picks as plain values (absent for a career race; `src/replay/index.ts`). No sim contract field changed. The bike that rides wears the garage's paint for it.
- **The screen** (`src/ui/race-options.ts` the rows as data; `src/ui/race-options-view.ts` the DOM, a lazy chunk of 1.7 KB gzip, now in `scripts/first-load.test.ts`'s lazy list): the menu's `Options` button is small, so Start career stays the first big tap. The race-type row stays hidden while there is one type: that is the seam for the `[open]` new types.
- **Checks**: `src/app/race-options.test.ts` on every carried region: a chosen light wins every seed and picks the road events a probe modifier eligible at one light only shows (the carried road events are not limited by light today); a rival count fields that many distinct rivals from the cast, the usual count draws the usual field, and a career race ignores it; cops off fields no law, and 1,200 full-throttle ticks see no law rider and no heat (the same count sees 4,800 law sightings with cops on); the traffic scale moves only the density; at seven rivals all 214 riders across every region's roads start on the road, none within 1 m of another. `src/ui/race-options.test.ts` steps every row through all its choices and back through a save and reload; `src/save/save-race-options.test.ts` and the M2 round trip cover the record; `src/replay/replay.test.ts` the header. `tests/e2e/ui-race-options.spec.ts` (CI only; no browser was run on the dev machine) measures the screen at 568x320, 640x360, 740x360 and 915x412 (Back and Race on the screen, every row's arrows and value on the screen and at least 44 px, a tap at an arrow's centre lands on it, the stamp over no word; a negative control plants a row off the screen and words under the stamp), then picks cops off, no rivals and a bike, races, and reads the field, the player's bike and the replay header's picks, and the picks again after a reload.
