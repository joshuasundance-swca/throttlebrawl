Three places that read wrong in the last live check look better now. On Duval Street at dusk the party lights are now strings: a cord runs from post to post, the bulbs are bigger with a bright centre and a soft glow, and every balcony on a party block has a few people leaning on the rail with their cups up, day or night, so the street looks full at speed. At the Golden Gate the two big grey blocks beside the road where the cables end are gone: each cable now runs into a low, stepped housing in the bridge's own orange. On the Seven Mile the old bridge's repair deck is laid in planks with a kerb along each edge, and pale timber posts stand in the sea beside the rails, so it reads as a deck on piles and not a flat orange slab.

For devs (playtest 4 run A's live check, polish items 7, 8 and 10); `[default]`, done, not phone-verified, no browser run (the numbers are from the unit tests).
- **Duval's party lights** (`party-lights.ts`): the cord is drawn (`CORD_W_M` 0.16 m, 4 triangles a metre), the bulbs are `BULB_SIZE_M` 0.44 m with a hot core, and each has an additive glow (`GLOW_R_M` 0.9 m: two crossed 6-sided fans, bright at the middle and black at the rim, unfogged) in a second mesh, `party-lights-glow`. So the strings are two draw calls, not one. Near one block: 258 bulbs, 7,112 triangles (it was 1,208).
- **The balcony crowd** (`balcony-crowd.ts`, owned by `PartyLights.setFronts`, which `render/index.ts` calls once the street fronts are placed): three revellers over every balcony shop board of a party block, about 600 on Duval, a storey up and 3 m from the verge, so they never touch the sim. One mesh, one draw call, within 130 m; worst view 7,650 triangles. The party chunk now also loads by day (the crowd is there by day; the strings are still dusk and night only).
- **The Golden Gate's anchorages** (`gg-anchorage.ts`): the kit's `gg_anchorage` (two 38 m concrete housings, 8 m high, on a 42 m apron) is not drawn any more; its extras still say where the cable enters. The code-made housing is 20 m at most, steps up to the cable in the bridge's paint, tops at 5.7 m (collar 6.6 m), 14.6 m or more from the centre line, about a quarter of the old volume over the deck. The kit's GLB is unchanged (no Blender).
- **The Seven Mile repair deck**: `road-mesh.ts` lays a seam across a bridge with no drive lane every `STAGING_PLANK_M` (1.6 m) and a `STAGING_KERB` (0.3 m) kerb along each edge, in the road and shoulder strips (no new draw call; 350 seams on the 591 m deck). `bridge-bays.ts` adds `STAGING_LEGS.posts`: pale timber posts 6 m out and 1.3 m over the deck, one per station each side, because the old raked piles were measured at 0 px squared from the chase camera (the deck's own edge hides them).
- **Cost**, from `scene-cost.test.ts` (the commit before vs this one, the busiest still view; limits 80 draw calls and 110,000 triangles): Duval 49 to 50 draw calls and 67,144 to 74,578 triangles; Seven Mile 58 to 58 and 60,032 to 66,872; Golden Gate 42 to 42 and 42,408 to 42,468. The tests with their controls are in `duval-party.test.ts`, `landmarks-sf.test.ts`, `gg-anchorage.test.ts` and `staging-deck.test.ts`.
