The chase camera now sits higher and further back, so you can see oncoming traffic over your rider's head. Before, the camera sat below the rider's helmet, and your own rider hid the road right where oncoming cars appear. A car 125 m ahead, about two seconds away when you are both at speed, is now in view on a straight or a bend. The camera's distance and height are still tuning sliders.
