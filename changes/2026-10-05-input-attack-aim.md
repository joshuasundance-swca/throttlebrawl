Attacks are easier to aim (playtest 4: "I wish I could choose an attack direction"; your "Auto-aim + swipe" call). A tap hits whichever rival is closest, on either side, right away. A small set of amber corner brackets now sits on the rider your next tap would hit, so you can see the auto-aim before you press and swipe for the other side when it is wrong. The brackets show with the keyboard and the gamepad too, which keep their side keys. A swipe down still kicks, a swipe up is still the straight kick, and a quick flat flick left or right still punches that way. The change is in the slanted swipe: a kick swipe used to pick a side as soon as it leaned 20 degrees off straight down, so an ordinary thumb arc sent the kick to the wrong side by accident. Now it takes a clearly sideways lean, 35 degrees, to pick a side; anything straighter stays auto-aimed at the closest rival. Not phone-verified.

For devs: `input.kickSideDeg` goes from 20 to 35 (`src/input/tuning.ts`; the slider's range is unchanged, 5 to 40, and the kick cone stays 60). The 20 to 35 band was the accidental side picks the combat audit measured (21, 30, 45 and 59 degrees all picked a side). The punch flick is untouched: 24 px sideways inside 80 ms, past the 60-degree cone, so it stays reachable; the rules are in `src/input/attack-swipe.test.ts`, run as sweeps over angle and speed through `TouchState`. The marker's rider comes from the sim: `aimPreview` in `src/sim/combat/index.ts` (the attack's own target during an attack, else what `aim()` picks for an auto-sided press, so it can never disagree with the blow), published on the snapshot as `EntitySnapshot.aimId` for a player's rider only (-1 for others; optional on hand-built snapshots). It is presentation only and changes no sim state, no hash and no replay. Render draws it in `src/render/aim-marker.ts`: one mesh (at most one draw call while it shows, none otherwise), facing the camera, a little toward it, growing with distance, drawn over the scene, and hidden on the grid while the countdown holds the sim at tick 0. Tests: `src/sim/combat/aim-preview.test.ts` (the preview equals the press's target for every placement tried, the nearest rider on either side, a rider before a cop, nobody in reach, a downed rival, the snapshot field) and `src/render/aim-marker.test.ts` (the marker follows the id the sim names, between snapshots, hides without one). The contract change rides in this PR because only this change needs it: one optional snapshot field. Not browser-run; not phone-verified.
