The career learns seasons (playtest 3: "Longer + seasons"; "Season 2 on the finished save, plus a 'New career' button"). Once every region boss has fallen, the map gets a Start Season card ("Season 2: Renewed"). Starting it keeps your cash, bikes, paints, grudges, receipts, the secrets and shortcuts you found, and your history, and puts every region's map back to its start roads, with the Keys first again. From Season 2 each event is remixed from a seed saved with the season, so the same save always remixes the same way: a different time of day, the long version of some late events, a fresh field drawn from the region's rivals (with guests from other regions from Season 3), one regular event in three turned into a race, a takedown hunt or a cop escape at its tier's purse, each tier boss handed to the rival who holds the biggest grudge against you, weird events more often, and drift targets 25% higher. Region bosses stay the rematch. A "New career" starts fresh and keeps the old career as a backup code in the save (the newest three). Nothing shows this yet: the app task adds the Start Season and New career buttons and applies the remix to the race, and the career-screen task draws the card, the season name and the tier-boss pins. Done, not phone-verified. One line in `src/career/settle.ts` (the economy task's file) now writes the season on each race result from Season 2, which the per-season region and best-result rules read.
