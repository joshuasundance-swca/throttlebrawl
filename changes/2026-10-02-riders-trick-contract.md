Contract for air control and flips (playtest 2, 2026-10-02: "I love the idea of doing flips"): the snapshot gains `pitch` (the bike's nose-up angle from the horizontal, unwrapped through a flip) and `trick` (the trick in progress), `TRICK_IDS` lists the tricks (backflip, front flip, wheelie, whip), and `STYLE_KINDS` gains `trick` for style cash from a trick landed. The riders lane fills them next.
