Press C on a keyboard, or d-pad up on a gamepad, to step the camera through the low chase, a higher far chase and a helmet view (the pause screen's keyboard list shows it). On a phone, the tuning panel's "View" slider still does it until a settings row lands. The radio now follows the region: the Keys play their own surf and rockabilly stations, and the Pacific Northwest and San Francisco borrow them until they get stations of their own (R switches, as before). In a Pacific Northwest or San Francisco race the local riders now talk too, not just the four regulars.
