Every region's radio gets a third station in a genre of its own. The Keys get Salt Air 98.3, calypso with a steel pan, congas and offbeat guitar chops. The Pacific Northwest gets Stump Radio 1240 AM, slow stoner rock built on one fuzzy riff and a wah solo. San Francisco gets Gold Rush Groove 94.1, funk with a slap bass, chicken-scratch guitar and horn hits. All of it is written in code from each song's seed, so every song is original, and the R key tunes through the new stations like the others.
