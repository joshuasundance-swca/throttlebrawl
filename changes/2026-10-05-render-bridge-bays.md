The Seven Mile Bridge now looks like two bridges. The new bridge stands on its long concrete spans, with taller piers under the hump over the channel. The old bridge beside it runs on arches and steel girders in turn, so it reads as the old one. The ramp trucks' repair decks stand on pipe-pile platforms. Each side of the missing span ends in a broken girder stub under the deck, right where the road's broken end is, so the missing span is where the bridge was cut, not a hole drawn in a deck.

For devs: `src/render/bridge-bays.ts` plans the bays and `models.ts` loads the CX2 kit (`models/scenery/seven-mile-kit`, new model kind `sevenMileKit`, loaded for a network with an `old-bridge` road). Bays stand end to end along each `bridge` stretch from its start (`floor(L / b)` of them, so up to one bay's length stays bare at a stretch's end), each turned to its chord and sheared up its grade (`ScenerySpot.slope`, applied in `scenery-merge.ts`), so joints abut within 5 cm on a hump and piers stay upright. None stands over a gap, the kicker that feeds it, or a gap end; each gap has two gap ends with their far ends exactly at the broken ends road-mesh draws. The spots are kept out of `RoadScene.spots` (the new `RoadScene.bays` lists them), so the roadside layer and the scenery sweeps never see them.

Two things differ from the plan. First, the plan said bays would add no draw calls by merging into the scatter's 160 m blocks. Measured on the real bake that was up to +8 meshes in one view, counting every visible mesh and not only those in the camera's frustum (a bridge crosses a block every 160 m), so the bays merge in their own 320 m squares, drawn within 300 m: the real route adds 2 to 4 meshes in view (frustum-counted, chase camera, eight cameras along it) and the straight 4 km fixture 2 to 3, and nothing is built a kilometre out. Second, the kit's gap end carries a barricade, two posts and a "bridge out" board standing across the lanes at the lip, where riders cross and the sim has nothing, so only its stub under the deck is drawn (`belowDeck`). If the maintainer wants the barricade, it needs its own node in the next Codex batch, set off the lanes. Shared edits riding along because only this change needs them: a `bay` scenery kind in `scenery.ts`, an optional `slope` and `reachM` on a spot, one merged-layer block in `road-mesh.ts`, and the kit's table rows in `scenery.test.ts`.

Not drawn yet from the same kit: Pigeon Key's cottages and dock, and the tug barge. The first-load JavaScript is 470.4 KB gzip of 500 KB after this change (29.6 KB headroom); the size against main was not measured here. Not phone-verified, and no browser was run: the unit tests cover the rules (counts, joints, grade, piers, gaps, gap ends, draw calls, the kilometre cap) on fixtures and on the real Seven Mile bake with the real kit, but how it looks needs the maintainer's eyes or the live check.
