Heights and per-bike hitboxes are in the pack format now, and nothing in the game reads them yet. The hitbox audit found that the sim measures every vehicle at one height (1.2 m), so a rider in the air passes through a 3.3 m truck, and that the lawnmower and the parking trike cannot fit the shared 2.0 x 0.8 rider box. This is the contract change the sim lane builds on, so there is no change to how a race plays (the new fields are in the sim content hash, so a replay key renews, as with any content edit).

What was added, all optional with defaults: `heightM` on a traffic type (a category default when absent: car 1.8, truck 3.1, rv 3.2, oddity 3.1, pedestrian 1.7, animal 0.6), `hitbox` (`lengthM`, `widthM`) on a bike and on a rider (the rider's wins), `heightM` on a region smashable item (its kind's drawn height when absent), and a lint on a hazard's `params.heightM` (above 0, at most 12 m). All 82 shipped traffic types now say their height, the drawn one to 0.05 m; the lawnmower, the mobility scooter and Officer Meter's parking trike say their boxes (his sim bike is the Rustbucket 400, so the box is on his rider file). The defaults, and `trafficHeightM`, `hitboxOf` and `hazardHeightM` to resolve them, are in `src/core/heights.ts`; `buildSimConfig` writes `SimTrafficTypeDef.heightM`, `SimSmashableDef.heightM` and `SimRiderDef.hitbox`. Migration note and the full table: `docs/content-packs.md` ("Heights and hitboxes"); `docs/architecture.md` lists the new config fields.

`scripts/hitboxes.test.ts` now holds every height within 0.15 m of its drawing: the 82 traffic types, 6 smashable kinds, 5 set-piece props and the solid hazards that have a model (the model scaled as `views.ts` scales it; a smashable's umbrella or banner pole over a rider's head is left out). It also pins each category default to the median of the drawn heights, and checks per rider that its hitbox is within 0.15 m of the bike it is drawn on: the lawnmower, mobility scooter and trike are no longer exceptions. Negative controls: the old flat 1.2 m and the tumble's 1.5 and 3.2 m boxes fail it (a box truck, a log truck, a pickup towing a boat), and so does a height 0.2 m off. New tests: `src/content/schema/height-contract.test.ts` (each field passes, and fails when out of range; they fail on the old schema), `src/app/config-heights.test.ts` (the config carries them) and the hazard lint in `src/road/validate.test.ts`. Two known exceptions stay on the test's list with their reasons: the festival bear (file 2.3 m, drawn 2.03 m) and the log pile (2.4 m, drawn 2.05 m), because the sim already reads a solid hazard's height and fixing the file is a sim change for the sim lane. `views.ts` now exports `CAR_PARTS`, `TRUCK_PARTS`, `PED_PARTS` and `SHAPE_HEIGHT` for the test; nothing else changed there. Not phone-verified (no game change).
