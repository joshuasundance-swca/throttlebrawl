Four San Francisco places that did not read from the road now do (playtest 4, run C's live check).

- **Coit Tower is seen whole when you finish Lombard Street.** The tower stands at its real place, beside the end of the climb, and it is far taller than the screen can show from 25 m, so the old finish only ever showed its base. Now, as you cross the line, the camera stops where it is and tips up to the whole tower for the two seconds before the results, with the field of view widened just enough to fit it. It is a taste call: the maintainer may veto it, and removing it is one number in the tower's data.
- **The Mission chapel shows before the finish.** The painted wall on the left of the last alley used to hide it until the last 40 m. The wall now opens for 80 m before the chapel, so its front is seen down the alley from 120 m.
- **The headland batteries look like gun emplacements.** They stand half as big again (45 m long, 5.6 m of concrete wall) and each gun pit has its gun: a dark barrel laid over the wall.
- **The Twin Peaks summit has something on it.** A paved viewpoint lot on the right of the last stretch, with a cream and teal tour bus, a stone wall and rail with two coin binoculars, two lamps and a few tourists at the rail taking photos. The radio mast on the skyline is drawn smaller on this road (it used to stand so high on the screen that its top was cut off), so you see the whole mast when it is ahead of you.

Not built: batteries standing on a rise (the headlands' ground beside the road is flat) or facing the sea (they still face the road); a closer mast at the finish (the real mast stands behind the rider there).

For devs: run C fix lane J2; every item is a `[default]` taste call. Details are in `docs/content-packs.md` ("Run C's J2 fixes") and `docs/architecture.md` (Camera).
- **The finish shot** (`src/camera/finish-shot.ts`, wired in `src/app/index.ts`). A landmark feature with `params.frameHeightM` is framed whole when the player finishes within 90 m of it: the app holds the chase pose at the finish tick and `shotPose` eases it over 24 ticks to look at the landmark, widening the field only as far as its height needs (72 to 86 degrees at the Lombard line, never past 100). Camera only, no sim state, no bust. `coit-tower` says 64 (`osm-sf-lombard-telegraph-hill.json`, `tools/gis/networks/osm-sf-lombard.json`; `frameHeightM` is in the bake schema and lint, `tools/gis/src/tbgis`); `landmarks-sf.test.ts` holds it equal to the model's own height. `src/camera/finish-shot.test.ts` projects the tower through the real chase camera at the real finish, on a phone and on 16:9: without the shot its top lands at NDC y 3.05 (the frame ends at 1), with it the whole tower stands within 0.77 of the frame's middle from the end of the ease to the results.
- **`sightM` for the Mission** (`src/render/mission.ts` `wallLines`): a landmark that says `params.sightM` opens its side of an alley that far before its footprint, as the downtown's roof sign already does. The chapel says 80 (`sf-mi-last-coat-alley.json` and `tools/road/tracks/sf-mission.ts`). `mission.test.ts` ray-tests the chapel's front against the real walls: 100 % seen from 60 and 120 m, 33 % from 245 m; with the opening shut (the control) 13 % from 120 m.
- **Batteries**: `BATTERY_SIZE` 1.5 in `scenery.ts` scales the spot and every footprint number (`DEPTH_M`, `HALF_ALONG_M`, `FRONT_M`, the clear radius, and the merged block's `reachM`); `src/render/battery-guns.ts` adds two code-made guns (96 triangles) to the battery variant at bake (`models.ts`), inside the model's front so the band rule holds. `sf-headlands.test.ts` holds the size, the gun in each pit (with a control: the file's own vertices have none) and the footprint. Golden Gate over five seeds: 33 batteries.
- **The summit lot** (`src/render/summit-lot.ts`, composed as `sf-landmarks#summit_lot` in `landmarks.ts`, about 750 triangles, sheared to the road's 3.5 % grade). The feature `summit-lot` is on `osm-sf-twin-peaks-climb` (s 1866 to 1954, d 12.5 to 28.5, `yawDeg` 180; in the road file and `tools/gis/configs/osm-sf-twin-peaks.json`), the straightest 80 m of the last stretch, over the finish at s 1931. The `summit-lookout` zone moved onto its rail and names the phone photographers (`maxPeds` 5). `isComposite` in `landmarks.ts` lets the every-landmark test accept code-made nodes. Tests: `src/render/summit-lot.test.ts` (place, one draw call, triangle budget, inside the box and past the verge, grade with a flat control, the bus, the rail and the binoculars, the zone) and `tests/sim/sf-identity.test.ts` (five tourists at the rail at the start of a seeded race; the Golden Gate event has none).
- **The mast**: the region's `radio-mast` piece names the other eight networks, and `osm-sf-twin-peaks` draws `radio-mast-near` at 0.7 times (`assets/backdrop/san-francisco`). `summit-lot.test.ts` holds one mast per network; `tests/sim/twin-peaks-mast.test.ts` puts the real chase camera on the road: the whole mast is in the picture at s 1700 and 1990 to 2005, and the old 1.3 times piece loses its top at every one (the control). At s 1650 and 1750 the camera, tipped down the grade, still cuts its top.
