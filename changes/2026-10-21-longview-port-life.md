Port Shift's Industrial Way now passes open freight sheds and timber decks, with solid posts, walls and roofs. These are original game scenery, not recreations of individual properties.

Loading-line signs point to an optional right-shoulder boost and parked carrier jump. The carrier changes parking spots between races; through traffic keeps its ordinary lanes. The real road alignment, freight traffic and town return remain in place.
