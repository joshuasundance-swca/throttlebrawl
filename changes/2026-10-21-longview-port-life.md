Port Shift's Industrial Way now passes open freight sheds and timber decks, with solid posts, walls and roofs. Visible paved loading aprons join their ground to the road verge, so entering a yard does not cross an invisible out-of-bounds strip. These are original game scenery, not recreations of individual properties.

Loading-line signs point to an optional right-shoulder boost and parked carrier jump. The carrier changes parking spots between races; through traffic keeps its ordinary lanes. The real road alignment, freight traffic and town return remain in place.
