Your bike sounds like a bike now: a lumpy, rough big single whose pitch climbs with the revs, tuned so a phone speaker can actually play it. A crude surf-rock loop plays while you race, and a lead guitar comes in once you are going fast. Punches, kicks, weapon hits, misses, crashes, oncoming-car horns and the cop's siren are made in code too; you will hear them as soon as the fights, traffic and the cop are switched on in the race.
