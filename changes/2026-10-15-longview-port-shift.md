Added Longview: Port Shift to the Pacific Northwest's Quick Play route picker. Ride from Industrial Way through Mount Solo Road, Ocean Beach Highway and 38th Avenue, with freight traffic giving way to wider town roads. It is one pass with separate start and finish lines.

The road alignment comes from OpenStreetMap and the ground heights from USGS 3DEP. Corners are smoothed and lanes widened for arcade racing; the course uses existing industrial, forest and town scenery rather than copying individual properties. It reuses the PNW cast, traffic and race objective.
