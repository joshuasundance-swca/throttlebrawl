Two new camera views next to the chase camera: a far chase view, higher and further back, and a helmet view from the rider's own eyes. For now you pick one with the "View" slider in the camera section of the tuning panel (0 is chase, 1 is far, 2 is helmet). A C key to cycle views is built in the camera and switches on once the game connects it (a small follow-up), and a proper settings row comes later. In the helmet view, the reduce-shake setting also calms how much the view tilts and widens with speed. Every distance, height and angle is a slider. Done, not phone-verified.
