San Francisco has a downtown. Pick **Downtown** in the San Francisco route picker: a four-lane avenue from a waterfront plaza between invented AI-startup towers, giant screens (AGI SOON, SERIES Z), lamps, plazas and a deadpan headquarters at the finish. Every block has a cross street with its own traffic queued at the red light as you blast through, and two steep cable-car streets climb the hill with a cable car on each. Cable cars now run only there: they no longer drive along the race roads (or through the forest). New deadpan signs and billboards go with it; long-press any to cut it. In the career, the San Francisco finale against Pivot now runs the downtown and ends at the headquarters.
