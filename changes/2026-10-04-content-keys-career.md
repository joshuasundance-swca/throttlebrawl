The Keys get a longer, harder career. The map is now sixteen races in four tiers, three regular events and a boss each: Kevin from Accounting closes Tourist Season, Chad Speedwell closes Hurricane Season, Dial-Up closes Off Season and Mother Rust still closes the Drawbridge. Six races are new: Rental Return, Spring Break Hunt, Speed Monitored by Aircraft, Hurricane Party, Auntie's Rules (a grudge match with Tammy Two-Stroke) and Mile Marker Zero. The rivals get quicker tier by tier, and the money is tighter: a first-tier win pays about $900 and the Drawbridge about $4,100, where the old career paid $1,500 to $8,000. The shop changes with it: the Moped is $1,500, a new Sport 600 (about 112 mph) opens in Hurricane Season for $9,750, and the Streetfighter 750 waits for the Drawbridge tier at $16,750. The Superbike moves to San Francisco's shop. A new paint, Hurricane Chrome, sits at $15,000 for the late game.

For devs: `packs/base/careers/keys-circuit.json` names each tier's `boss` and `field` (the pace share of the best open bike, aggression, signature gap, health, power and `rivalBike`; the values are the spec's defaults, written out), and moves to `requiredWins: 2` per tier. The ten old events are retuned to the purse and pace tables (the Mother Rust finale loses its flat podium bonus), and the six new events use only the region's one time of day for now (`golden-hour`; the region's own list comes with the real-world content task). `sport-600` is a new bike file with a byte copy of the stickered sport bike's model under its own id; its catalog row in `tools/blender/catalog/keys.mjs` reuses that bike's script, so a rebuild writes the same bytes. Tests: the new `tests/sim/career-ladder.test.ts` checks the ladder's rules (structure, purse by global tier, pace and field climb, step-up bikes with models) on every career that names its tier bosses, so the other regions meet them as they move over. `career-content.test.ts`, `career-screens.test.ts` and `tools/career/pack-rules.test.ts` no longer pin today's node counts, tier shape, garage list or prize, which the new Keys changes; they assert the rules instead. Until the career core lands, the tier boss still counts as a win toward opening the next tier, and the Pacific Northwest's shop still sells the Streetfighter and the Superbike (a warning in `packs:check` until it moves over).
