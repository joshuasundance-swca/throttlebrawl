San Francisco's own art for playtest 3 (Codex batch CX3), and region-pack models now ship with the game.

- **The atlas.** `packs/region-sf/assets/textures/atlas/san-francisco.png` (15,930 bytes): 22 greyscale facade tiles (Victorian and Edwardian walls, curtain walls and stone drawn four storeys to a tile, fire escapes, brick, and the Golden Gate's tower panels and truss, so the region's `bridgePaint` repaints them) and 21 picture tiles (storefronts, six murals, Lombard's brick and flower beds, cable-car trim). No lettering of any kind: `cx3-sf.test.ts` fails the sheet if a tile draws glyphs.
- **The Golden Gate kit** (`models/landmarks/golden-gate`), built to the contract `src/render/landmarks.ts` already composes: `gg_tower_lod0/1` (origin at the waterline, `top_m` 225, `cable_saddle_x_m` 17.5, nothing in y 55 to 82 inside |x| 14.5, so the 61 to 69 m deck passes between the legs), `gg_bay_lod0/1` (deck top at the bay's start, +Z for 15.24 m; no railing, because the road's railing look draws it), `gg_anchorage` (origin at the cable entry, block behind it under the road, `cable_entry_m` 6), plus `gg_approach_pier`, `gg_fort_arch` and `gg_suspender`.
- **`gg_suspender` is a seam, not drawn yet.** The cable's height above the deck runs from about 3 m to about 155 m, and the game places bays without stretching them, so suspenders cannot live in the bay. `gg_suspender` is one bay's ropes at 1 m tall, for the landmark layer to stretch from the deck to the cable; until it does, the near bridge shows towers, bays, anchorages and cables without suspenders.
- **San Francisco landmarks** (`models/landmarks/sf-landmarks`): `coit_tower` and `coit_tower_lod1` for the baked Telegraph Hill feature (64 m, inside its 22 m footprint), and `toll_gantry` (posts outside the 27.6 m toll-plaza road, 6 m clear over it, a blank sign text surface) for a later feature to place.
- **Stackable downtown towers** (`models/scenery/sf-tower-modules`): five styles, each a 7 m base, an exact 14 m four-storey mid that stacks seamlessly and a crown, so a later downtown change can stack instead of stretching windows.
- **Lombard planting** (`models/scenery/lombard-kit`): mirrored brick-edged hairpin beds (3 m radius, for the 5.1 m hairpins) and a 6 m hedge.
- **SF traffic**: `sf-streetcar` (a streamlined invented two-tone streetcar), `sf-trolleybus`, `robotaxi` and `startup-shuttle` (11 m by 2.6 m, matching the pack's type), on the CX1 vehicle contract.
- **Region-pack models ship.** The build listed only the base pack's GLBs in the asset manifest, so a region pack's models (playtest 3's step-up bikes in `region-pnw`, and now these) never loaded. `src/content/base-pack.ts` `assetIndex` now lists every carried pack's GLBs under that pack's id; only a URL string per model rides in the first load. `base-pack.test.ts` checks every committed GLB is listed and that an uncarried pack contributes none. Docs: architecture.md, "Asset manifest".

Coit Tower and the Golden Gate kit are the only ones a scene uses yet: the landmark layer draws them from the baked features. `src/render/landmarks-sf.test.ts` loads the committed kits through the asset manifest onto the real baked networks: each feature finds its kit and node, the layer draws in one call, and no tower triangle enters the deck's corridor (the road's width, from 1 m under its surface to 5 m over it) at the deck's real height at each tower (69.1 m and 60.8 m). Widening the corridor to 16 m makes it fail on the legs. Not phone-verified.
