The rivals got brains. Deacon Vane now hunts you down, pulls up alongside and swings; if you get ahead he waits for you, and if you're not around he picks on whoever is. Three more regulars join the roster: Dial-Up (fast, swervy, kicks first), Chad Speedwell (races, and swings at whoever is leading) and Kevin from Accounting (always comes at you from the left, kicks a lot). Rivals slow down or swerve around cars in their way, dodge through the oncoming lane only when they're the risky type, and get a slight push to stay near you. Punches and kicks start landing once combat arrives. Two new tuning sliders: rival pace and rival aggression.
