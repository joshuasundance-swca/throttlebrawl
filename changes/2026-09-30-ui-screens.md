The screens around the race got real. The start screen shows a small controls card. The menu has Settings: four volume sliders (master, music, effects, voices), mute, and a left-handed option that swaps the thumb buttons to the other side. The race HUD now shows your health beside speed and position, and the touch buttons and a ring under your left thumb are drawn where they work. Press Esc, or the pause button in the top corner, to pause: resume, quit to the menu, or copy a debug report. After the race you can go straight into another one. Turn your phone upright and it asks you to turn it back. Pausing does not stop the race clock yet, and the volume sliders are not connected to the sound yet: both get wired in the next small change.
