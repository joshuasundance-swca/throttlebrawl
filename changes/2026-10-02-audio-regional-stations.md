Each region now sounds like itself. A race starts on its region's own station instead of the same score everywhere: the Florida Keys play surf and rockabilly, the Pacific Northwest plays grunge (Drizzle 89.3) and indie folk (the new Salal 102.7), and San Francisco plays psychedelic rock (Fog Bank 1550) and synth (the new Burn Rate 104.5). A region's dial only carries its own stations. The score is still one press of R (or the Radio row) away, and if you picked it, it stays picked.
