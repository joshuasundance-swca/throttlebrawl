The road and the riders look more like a place now. Where the road rises high over the water it gets rails, a thick deck and pylons standing in the sea; the road has white edge lines and a yellow centre line; low stretches sit on a causeway bank. Riders lean, throw a punch toward whoever they are aiming at, swing a leg out to kick, and wobble when hit. A held pipe glints while its owner winds up, which is your cue to snatch it. The cop's bike has a flashing red and blue light bar, cars and trucks are coloured boxes of their real size, and pedestrians throw themselves flat when they dive clear. Most of these only appear once the traffic, cop and fight lanes land. If the phone briefly loses its graphics (for example after switching apps), the scene comes back on its own.
