The ramp truck shows its parked car on the top deck again. Two fixes for the same problem landed at the same moment: the riders lane made the car solid (ride up slowly and you bump it and fall off), and the render lane had removed the car and drawn a flat deck over the cab. Together they made an invisible car you could crash into. The ride is the one that counts, so the truck looks as it did: ramp, a short platform at the top, then the car you hit if you are too slow to clear it.
