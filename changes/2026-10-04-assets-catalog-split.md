The model pipeline is ready for the playtest 3 Codex batches. The Blender catalog is split into one file per batch (`tools/blender/catalog/`: every existing row moved unchanged into `base.mjs`, with empty `traffic`, `keys`, `sf` and `pnw` files), and each row can name its `pack` and `region`, so a region's models are committed to its own pack. The score gains the asset plan's rules: atlas UVs must stay inside one tile of the region's atlas layout, the traffic `vehicle` contract (size extras, a hood marker for the wheelie launch, one white-painted body), bridge bays of an exact length, far-detail models at most 30% of the near one's triangles, outward winding on convex parts, and a text surface read by its winding once its normals are gone. Ten material roles are added (`art`, `bridge_paint`, `concrete` and others). The game's GLB reader now gives UVs the `uv` name materials sample, and reads signed and normalised data. The perf check counts a region pack's models and its atlas as that region's own bytes. Nothing in the game changes yet: all 35 committed models still pass, and no model or atlas uses the new rules until the batches land.
