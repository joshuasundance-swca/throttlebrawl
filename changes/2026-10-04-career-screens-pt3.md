The career screens now show playtest 3's career. Each tier's header counts its wins, then says its boss is open or beaten, and a closed tier says which boss to beat. Tier bosses are level squares on the map and the region boss a diamond (a small legend names them), and their cards are tagged TIER BOSS or REGION BOSS. A region you cannot enter yet (the regions open in order) now says so: a LOCKED banner names the boss who opens it and where that boss waits, its tab is marked locked, every event in it is locked, and Ride stays off. A season badge sits by the career's name, and the line under it names the highest tier you have reached ("Tier 2 of 4"). The Start Season card and the New career panel are laid out to keep every line on a 320 px phone. The garage numbers the six step-up bikes ("Step 3 of 6"), calls the slower ones novelty rides, and shows under each bike you do not own its price in races ("about 7 races to afford", from what one race pays at your tier), with the bike you ride showing what a crash costs to fix. A line on the results screen that takes money away now reads "-$150", not "$-150", next to the fine.

For devs: the words are pure functions in `src/ui/career-show.ts` (`money`, `ledgerNode`, `tierHead`, `tallyLine`, `regionLockNode`, `shutRegionView`, `seasonCardNode`, `bikeNotes`, `racesToAfford`), read by `src/ui/career-screens.test.ts` without a browser. `shutRegionView` marks a shut region's events locked on the screen, because the career's per-region node state cannot see the chapter order (the race itself was already refused by the ride gate). A small shared-seam edit rides in this PR: `garageView` in `src/app/career-flow.ts` now also hands the screen `racePay` (`typicalRacePay`: the purse of the highest open tier in the season's scale, an estimate by the purse table, not a promise), each bike's ladder step and its repair cost per crash; `tests/sim/career-screens.test.ts` pins them to the career's own functions. The new `tests/e2e/career-layout.spec.ts` measures the shut-region banner, the Season 2 card (its title inside its box, whole and level) and the garage at 320x568, 360x640 and 568x320; the Season 2 card's title is no longer the shared rotated one. `career.spec.ts` also checks that the results ledger never writes "$-". Not phone-verified, and not run in a browser by the lane: CI runs the browser tier.
