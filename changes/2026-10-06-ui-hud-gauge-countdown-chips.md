Three phone HUD fixes from playtest 4's live check. The wheelie gauge now stands by the wheelie button, where your right thumb is, instead of by the left stick across the screen: it sits level with the button on a wide phone and just over it on a squarer one, clear of the road ahead, HIT and BRAKE. The 3-2-1 countdown reads over a pale building: a warm-white number with a dark outline, a little less see-through; it is the same size and in the same strip beside the road, so it still never covers the road ahead. And a flip's cash chip ("DOUBLE BACKFLIP +$240") no longer waits behind its landing's AIRTIME chip: the flip shows first, then the airtime.

For devs: `ui/moves-meter.ts` `placeGauge` takes the settled wheelie button's box (`button`, set by `ui/index.ts` only on a coarse pointer) and picks the cheapest clear spot beside it, in the same three tiers; a keyboard or gamepad rider has no button and keeps the stick's spot. Within 24 px of the button on every screen in the layout table except 740x360, 640x360 and 568x320 (43 to 57 px: the button is boxed in by HIT, the road ahead and the heat badge); `ui/moves-meter.test.ts` sweeps all of them, both hands, with a negative control. `ui/countdown-view.ts`: rest opacity 0.6 to 0.9, fill `#fffbe8`, an eight-step 2 px outline; `countdown-view.test.ts` holds a contrast ratio of 7 (edge and silhouette) over pale and dark backgrounds, pins the size, strip and clip (the two vetoes), and `tests/e2e/ui-menu-first.spec.ts` now asks for 0.85 to under 1. `ui/ticker.ts`: a paid trick chip ranks above other paid chips and takes the strip from one (the frozen chip keeps its remaining time). `docs/architecture.md` ("One top ticker", the wheelie button) and `docs/product-spec.md` (the countdown) are updated. Not phone-verified.
