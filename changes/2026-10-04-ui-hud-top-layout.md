The top of the race screen now settles itself so nothing sits on anything else (playtest 3: "The race objective sits over the heat meter"). On a phone held sideways the ticker line runs along the top between your position and the rival's health bar, and the career objective and the heat badge sit one above the other in the right-hand column (the left-hand one if you play left-handed), clear of the road ahead. The objective shows up to three lines there, then trails off. On a narrow window the ticker gets its own row under the top buttons, and the objective and the heat badge share the row under it. A rival's health bar that would land on your position badge drops down into the column instead. Your health bar lifts off the touch buttons where they used to touch. The "Running slow?" offer moved to the column under your position badge on a wide screen, so it no longer pushes the ticker out of the way.

For devs: `src/ui/hud-layout.ts` is a pure function (`layoutTop`, `settleLifts`) that `placeAll` calls; it sets `--hl-*` variables on `#ui`, which the ticker, the offer, the objective and the heat badge read, and every slot is reserved while its widget is hidden. The media-query positions for those four are gone. `hud-layout.test.ts` is table-driven over twelve screens with and without the mirror (no overlap, nothing in the road ahead from 390 px wide up); `ui-hud-layout.spec.ts` measures the real widgets in their slots. The known layout list in `ui-style-popups.spec.ts` stays empty. The career prompt box and the floating offer stay until the ticker-integration task moves them into the ticker. Not phone-verified, and not run in a browser by the lane: CI runs the browser tier.
