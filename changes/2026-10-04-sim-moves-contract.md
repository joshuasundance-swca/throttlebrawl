The sim and input contract for playtest 3's new moves (the wheelie, the hood launch, the drift, road gaps and jumpable walls) lands first, as stubs, so those lanes can build in parallel without touching the shared riding and traffic files. Input flags widen to 16 bits (`quantizeInput` keeps `0xffff`) and `InputFlag.wheelie` is bit 256, set from the action state's `wheelie`. The contract adds the snapshot's `wheelie`, `drift` and `moves` fields, the `hoodLaunch`, `wheelieEnd`, `driftStart` and `driftEnd` events, the `wheelie` and `drift` style kinds with their optional per-second cash, and the moving-deck registry type. `src/sim/riders/index.ts` now calls hooks in new `wheelie.ts`, `drift.ts` and `gap.ts`: steering scale, drag, lean, pitch, take-off, a hazard launch for parked cars, the jumpable-wall skip in the air, and the gap fall. `src/sim/traffic/index.ts` calls the hood-launch hook on a first contact and skips a vehicle with a live moving deck. sim/race already scores a clean wheelie, a banked drift chain, and a hood launch's landed trick, the last at `race.styleHoodScale` (1.5). Every hook returns its neutral value. Over 4 headless races of 4,000 ticks, every snapshot and event matches main's exactly; the state hash moves only because the two new style keys (`race.styleWheelieMinS`, `race.styleHoodScale`) sit in the tuning. One small shared edit rides along: `src/sim/create.ts` fills the new snapshot fields.
