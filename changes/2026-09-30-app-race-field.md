The race is now the full M1 field: you against all four regulars (Deacon Vane, Dial-Up, Chad Speedwell and Kevin from Accounting), with Sgt. Pruitt of the county deputies starting behind you. He turns up with his siren about 20 seconds in, hangs back, then comes alongside now and then; you can hit him too (aim at him by dragging off the attack button toward his side). Go down near him and you're busted: the results say "Busted" and show the fine. The position readout and the results count only the racers, never the cop. The results now come up two seconds after you cross the line (or get cuffed), instead of after the whole field is home.
