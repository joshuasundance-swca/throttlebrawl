Two new real-road races drawn from real road networks, each with a four-lane highway and a real junction choice. In the Keys, "Key West" runs the Overseas Highway four lanes wide over Stock Island and Cow Key Channel into Key West, then South Roosevelt Boulevard along Smathers Beach to Higgs Beach; at the Triangle you can keep right onto North Roosevelt Boulevard, the strip-mall boulevard (four lanes, empty for the race), which comes back down Truman Avenue and White Street about 170 m shorter. In the Pacific Northwest, "I-5 by Lake Samish" races Interstate 5 over the Chuckanut Mountains, four lanes and a grass median with traffic to split; at exit 246 you can keep right off the interstate and take the lake's north and east shore roads instead, about 250 m longer and no traffic, and back on at the Nulle Road on-ramp. Signs at both splits say which way. Both are in the "Which road" picker, and the career races them: Chad Speedwell's grudge match now rides Key West, and Deputy Lindqvist's cop escape now runs on I-5. Done, not phone-verified.

For developers: `tools/gis` gained network bakes (`uv run tbgis network networks/<id>.json`). A network config names several real paths ("lines") and the junction choices between them; each line is pinned back onto the real map at every junction it meets (the bake's smoothed line drifted 0.4 to 43 m off the real junctions on these two networks, and about 6 m per city block before), and the bake writes the junction roads, split zones and named branches the hand-made tracks use. Key West's four-lane beach road narrows to two lanes at Bertha Street in the race direction, the first lane drop a racer meets (the W-R funnel eases riders over).

Fixes found by this PR's first CI run (the lander's pass):
- The turn-off at exit 246 was a 16 m radius bend at interstate speed, and a rider holding the steer through it could fly off the edge and land sideways. The connector now starts 70 m earlier and lands 60 m sooner on the lake road (`shiftM` 90, `insetM` 140, `turnsM` 40): a 66 m radius bend. The alternate is now 250 m longer, not 256.
- Road events (roadwork, crash scenes, parades, speed traps) never appeared on either new route, and a patrol cop could find no spot on I-5, because both systems kept clear of everything a shortcut bypasses, and these junction choices bypass about 5.5 km of a 7.2 km race. A bypass longer than 2 km now counts as a junction choice like any fork: a road event or a patrol may stand on the main way, and only 200 m at each end stays clear (`src/sim/modifiers/setpieces.ts` `SET_PIECE.longBranchM`, `src/sim/cops/index.ts` `PATROL.longBranchM`). Every hand-made shortcut bypasses under 1.3 km, so nothing moves on the other routes. Over 40 seeds, 36 Key West races and 35 Lake Samish races now get a road event (0 of 40 each before).
- The career map showed the new networks' names with "(OSM network bake)" on the end; that tag is now dropped like "(OSM bake)".
- Tests: the two hand-made tracks' "every sign has a slot" checks accept a junction sign that stands on the region's real-road network; the route picker and career browser specs know the two new routes.
