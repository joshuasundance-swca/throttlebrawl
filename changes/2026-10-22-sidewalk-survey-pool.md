sim job. The mandatory same-process survey reporter pools the complete cohort before applying the
original overall and regional dodge bands, exposure floors, crash rule and braking rule. Missing,

observations and the optional before-control rides are unchanged. No speedup has been measured.

new event files use the ordinary unmeasured fallback until valid CI timings replace it. Partial
selections still fail, and the current-file partition and budget checks remain in place.
