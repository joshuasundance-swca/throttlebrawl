Things now happen on the road. Most races bring one or two road events, placed by the race's seed and different in each region: roadwork with a cone taper and a flagger (cones scatter if you ride through them), a fender-bender with flares, a tow truck and a cop waving you by, a parade on the shoulder (a flamingo and conch costume parade in the Keys, a logging-days float with a carved bear in the Pacific Northwest, a startup's launch parade with a giant balloon in San Francisco), a hay truck in the Pacific Northwest that drops bales in front of you, and a speed trap: pass the radar over about 65 mph and the law comes after you. Each one has a warning sign ahead of it. A Road events slider in the tuning panel sets how often they turn up.
