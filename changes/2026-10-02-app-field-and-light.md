Every free-play race (the menu's Race) is a different crowd. It picks its four rivals from the region's whole cast, so Mother Rust, Tammy Two-Stroke and The Mayor finally ride in the Keys, and the Pacific Northwest and San Francisco locals turn up in new combinations. In San Francisco and the Pacific Northwest it also picks its light: dawn one race, golden hour the next. A career race keeps its event's own rivals and light.
