Going fast now looks fast. Past about 45 mph, thin white speed lines start rushing past the edges of the screen, brighter and quicker the faster you ride, and they never cover the road ahead. Palm trees on little sand islands now line the road, so the world streams past. The speed lines' strength, count and speeds, and how many palms there are, are sliders in the tuning panel.
