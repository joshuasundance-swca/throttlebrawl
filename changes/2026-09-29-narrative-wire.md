Rivals talk now. At the start of a race, when they pass someone and when they land a hit, the four regular rivals say short, in-character lines in a speech bubble at the top of the screen, one at a time and never the same line twice in a row. Deacon Vane is the only one in the current race; Dial-Up, Chad Speedwell and Kevin from Accounting have their lines ready for when they join.
