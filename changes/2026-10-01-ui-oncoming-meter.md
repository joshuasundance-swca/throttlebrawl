Ride in the oncoming lane and a live counter under your position now ticks up as you go, such as "ONCOMING 2.4s +$24". It stays dim until the stretch has lasted long enough to pay, and when you leave the lane it lands on the cash you earned and becomes that pop-up. Jumps count up their airtime the same way. A new "Style pop-ups" switch on the Display settings page turns the pop-ups and the counter off.
