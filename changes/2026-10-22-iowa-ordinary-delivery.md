Iowa's Middle Bends and County Join highway stretch now occasionally includes an ordinary box truck among the pickups and rental sedans. It uses the existing truck and traffic rules, with no extra traffic density or population allowance. Trucks keep driving along the highway after leaving those spawn areas; the county detour and Eagle Point keep their existing ordinary traffic mix.

Focused simulation checks cover both spawn roads and directions, each admitted truck's movement through the highway to recycling, full-body pavement at internal joins and opposing-traffic clearance, and an input-driven following and passing encounter through a real finish and replay. These checks do not establish phone performance or human fun.

The county vehicle traversal check now shares the ordinary county ride's existing nine-minute simulation guard. This removes the former five-minute complete-trip guarantee: an unchanged vehicle that was still queued at that cutoff later completed the whole route. The original seed cohort and both directions still require natural admission, continuous ordinary traffic, the same non-retired vehicle generation, and every street and connection in order. An incomplete journey still fails at the finite guard; it is not automatically called a permanent deadlock. A controlled check that substitutes the historical faulty merge rule still fails on the original reverse-direction seed cohort under the shared guard.
