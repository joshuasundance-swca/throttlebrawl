Braking into a hairpin no longer turns the bike round (playtest 4: "Lombard's hairpins feel impossible to control smoothly"; "maybe I just need to slow waaaaaay down. It's also just very windy and tight"). A U-turn now has its own gesture: tap the brake, then press it again and hold it with the stick at full lock, at about 27 mph or slower, and the bike pivots round. Braking with the stick over, held or tapped through the bend, is just braking. On a keyboard it is S (or the down arrow), then S held with A or D; on a gamepad L2, then L2 held with the stick; the pause screen's key legend names it on the brake row. Lombard Street's camera is calmer too: on a tight bend it aims closer, over the bend you are in, and turns more lazily, so the hairpins do not whip the view from one apex to the next. Not phone-verified.

For devs: the old rule (`riders.uturnMps` or slower, brake 0.5 or more, steer 0.85 or more) turned a phone rider round on the first bend of Lombard's crooked block in all 9 audit runs (lock 0.86 to 1, hairpin speeds 6, 8 and 10 m/s). The gesture is read in `sim/riders/uturn.ts` from the brake and the bars alone, as plain numbers per rider (`uturnTap`, `uturnClock`, `uturnDown`): a first press under 0.35 s with the bars under half lock, a second press within 0.45 s of the first lifting, then held with full lock at `riders.uturnMps` or slower (the speed gate and the turn rate are unchanged; a gesture made too fast waits, held, until the bike is slow enough). No new input flag and no new setting, so every recording replays as before apart from a held brake with full lock, which no longer turns round. Tests: `src/sim/riders/uturn.test.ts` (the gesture, and held, stuttering and single-tap brakes with full lock at 0, 4, 9 and 12 m/s never flip); `tests/sim/lombard-uturn.test.ts` (a phone-like rider, the brake a button and the stick at fixed lock 0.86, 0.9 and 1, rides Lombard's crooked block at 6, 8 and 10 m/s on a held brake and on a stuttering one with no flip, no crash and a clean finish of the block; the gesture still turns a bike round there); `tests/sim/uturn-devices.test.ts` (the real touch, keyboard and gamepad code, through `toSimInput`, make the gesture and turn the bike round; the same devices holding the brake at full lock without the tap do not). The camera: `camera.tightBendRadiusM` (30 m, 0 is off) and `camera.tightBendMinShare` (0.3) scale the look-ahead by the tightest bend within it and slow the bearing spring by the same share (`src/camera/tight-bends.test.ts`: the aim's arc is the look-ahead times radius over 30 m, floor 0.3; on R 5 m hairpins 21 m apart at 9 m/s the view's peak swing falls from 100 to 75 degrees a second and the bike's furthest distance from the middle of the view from 72 to 41 degrees; `modes.test.ts`'s sensitivity script now rides a bend of R 8 m so the two new sliders move its trace). Nothing in `src/render`, `src/ui` layouts or the browser tiers changed; browser specs were not run on the dev machine. Not phone-verified; a "U-turn" cue on the HUD when the second press arms it is not built.
