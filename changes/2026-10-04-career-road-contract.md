The career, road, content and save contract for playtest 3 (K0b) lands before the lanes that build on it. Nothing a player sees changes yet. The career gets its race types (`FieldLevel` and `EventPatch` in new `src/core/career-race.ts`, re-exported through `sim/api`), `SimEventDef.level` (the rivals' aggression and signature scales, absent means 1 and 1), and `RaceSetup.fieldLevel` and `eventPatch` as types only; the app-wiring task applies them. `buildSimConfig` now writes the wheelie's and the drift's style cash per second, an event's own or 2 and 3 times its oncoming rate [default]. Road files gain the `landmark` feature kind, the meaning and params of a `gap` (kill depth 1.5 m, wake on the far side 10 m past it, or `respawn: 'main'` on the highway for the Seven Mile's Moser gap, per the maintainer's answer), `jumpable` walls and the barrier `look` `railing`, with readers in `src/road/types.ts` that give every bad value its default; a route branch gains `aiTake`, which `RouteBranch` carries. The schema adds all of these, the wheelie and drift reward fields, a career tier's `boss` and `field`, the paints list with `unlockSeason`, and the `tourer` bike class. The career lint checks tier bosses, and a priced bike in more than one region's shop: a warning while a career selling it has no tier bosses (today's three files: the Streetfighter and the Superbike, 6 warnings), an error once all do. The profile gains `season`, `seasonSeed`, a result's `season` (written from Season 2 only) and `careerBackups` (the newest 3 finished careers as backup codes), all additive, so the version stays 1 and the v1 fixture loads as Season 1. `tools/packs/invented-brands.test.ts` adds a list of 19 real marks near the new places that no pack text may name; San Francisco's transit name is left off, as a shipped pirate-radio track uses it. The docs' contract sections say all this (architecture: sim contract, jumps, features, save format; content-packs: bike class, event, career, road file, route branches; product spec: rivals, cash, career; tone guide: a Real places subsection and a hard line on neighbourhoods). Small shared edits ride along because only this change needs them: the base pack's engine voice for `tourer` and its model in `src/render/rider-looks.ts` (the class-exhaustive tests want both), the career lint in `tools/career/pack-rules.ts` (where the career lint lives, rather than `src/content/lint.ts`), and two lines in `src/road/route.ts` and `src/road/index.ts`.
