A jump no longer passes through a truck. Every car, truck, pedestrian, animal, roadside smashable and road-event prop now has its drawn height, and you only clear it when you are above that height. Before, anything above 1.2 m sailed through every vehicle, even a 3.3 m box truck. If you come down on a car's roof, it is judged like any other traffic hit: a gentle touch-down is a wobble and you ride off the roof, and a hard drop is a crash. Flying into a car's side or tail below the roof is a hit too, a wobble or a crash depending on how fast you close on it. A wheelie into a car's hood or trunk still launches you clear of it. The lawnmower and Officer Meter's parking trike now hit things at their own size. The carved bears and the log piles in the Pacific Northwest are now as tall as they are drawn (2.05 m and 2.1 m, down from 2.3 m and 2.4 m), so a jump that clears the drawing clears them. Not phone-verified.

For devs: `src/sim/world` gains `riderHitbox(config, riderIndex)` and `vehicleHeightM(type)`, the resolvers for the hitbox audit's contract (`src/core/heights.ts`). `src/sim/traffic` `contacts()` measures each vehicle at its own height. Its rider view now holds every rider on the corridor whatever its height, and it carries `airborne`, `h` and the rider's own box. In the air below the top, a contact that came from above is `hit: 'top'`, and its closing speed is the fall rate, read by name from the riders' `vy` (`roofContact`). A wobble keeps the rider on the roof (`onRoof` writes `m.h`, the riders' `yAbs` and `vy`). Any other air contact is classed as on the road. All of them carry `data.air`. `TrafficState.launchedOff` lets a hood or trunk launch, which starts 1 m up under the roof, fly clear of the car it left. Without it, both trunk-launch tests fail. Near misses stay with riders on the road. `TRAFFIC.maxContactH` (1.2 m) is now only the over-another-road surface tolerance and the driving reads' view. `vehicleInfo` gains `heightM`. Other systems: `src/sim/peds` uses each type's height in place of the flat 1.5 m (rider above pedestrian; 1.5 m is kept for a pedestrian mid-dive above a rider). `src/sim/smash` uses each item's height in place of 0.8 m. `src/sim/modifiers` adds `riderMeetsProp`, so cones, flares, sawhorses and bales meet riders in the air as well as on the road, below `SET_PIECE_PROP_HEIGHT_M`. `src/sim/tumble/contacts.ts` takes each vehicle's height in place of 1.5 m or 3.2 m by class, and takes the rider's own box. `hazardTop` falls back to `HAZARD_OBJECT_HEIGHT_M`; every shipped hazard gives a height, so nothing shipped moves. In `tools/road/tracks/pnw-c1.ts` the bears go to 2.05 m and the log piles to 2.1 m: render builds the pile from its height, and any height from 2.08 to 2.53 m draws the same 2.05 m pile. Both are rebaked. `scripts/hitboxes.test.ts` has no height exceptions left.

Tests, each written failing first: `src/sim/traffic/air-height.test.ts` covers these cases. A jump clears a 1.5 m sedan, and the same jump crashes into a 3.3 m truck's tail at 24.9 m/s. Control: the same jump at 3.6 m clears the truck. At 1.3 m the jump meets the sedan. A 3.3 m/s touch-down on the roof wobbles and rides it; it is held at 1.500 m and lands past the car. A 12.2 m/s drop crashes onto the roof. An air brush into the side at 2.8 m/s wobbles, and at 11.6 m/s it crashes. Control: the same drift above the roof touches nothing. A trunk launch from 1 m clears its car, and its control rear-ends it. The lawnmower's 1.15 m box clips a car that the default box passes 0.1 m clear of. Further tests: smash (1.2 m smashes a mailbox, 1.7 m clears it), peds (over an alligator and through a tourist, each with a control), the tumble's boxes, set-piece props and the hazard fallback. On the old code the six traffic rule tests failed; the five controls passed.

Seeded races change. Measured on the dev machine with the bot riding, this branch against its base. The shared batch's base race, seeds 1 to 30: the same event stream in 15 of 30, another finish order in 9, and the player's place changed in 7. No rider met traffic in the air there. One free-play race at every career node of the three regions (48 races): the same event stream in 15, another finish order in 20, and the player's place changed in 6. There were 10 contacts in the air, in 7 races, all in San Francisco: 5 frontal crashes, 1 crash onto a roof, 3 grazes and 1 side wobble. Smashables were hit 41 times, against 34. In most races the first difference is an existing event a few ticks earlier or later (an overtake, a get-up, a pedestrian's dive), not a new contact. The likely sources are the tumble's new boxes and pedestrians judging a rider by their own height, but these were not traced one by one. In 4 of the 48 career races, the first difference is a new contact in the air. The final state hash differs in every race, because the traffic state has a new field.
