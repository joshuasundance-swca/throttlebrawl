The one-liner after a clean landing ('TEN OUT OF TEN, SAYS A PELICAN') now shows in every look, and you can read it on a phone. In the default Ink + 60s look it never showed at all: the film pass paints the sky over anything that sits in front of the sky without depth, and the line did. On a sharper screen only the part of it over the sea showed. In the classic look it drew, but its letters were about 6 px tall.

The line now draws over the finished frame, after the film pass. It is chalk on a dark plate, with letters about 6.5% of the screen's short side: never under 18 px, and no more than 30 px. It sits over your bike and stays inside the screen. On a narrow screen, such as a phone held upright, it wraps onto two rows instead of shrinking. It still lasts 2 seconds and fades out, and "cut this" still lists it. The draw cost is the same as before: one draw while it shows.

New tests check the size, the fit and the contrast. The line draws outside the 3D scene, so the film pass cannot reach it. The tests lay out the longest line the content rules allow (40 characters) with a stand-in letter width (the game measures the real font). It fits every screen tested at 18 px or more: 20 px on two rows on a 320 by 568 phone, 22 px on two rows on a 360 by 740 phone held upright, 23 px on one row on an 800 by 360 phone held sideways, and 30 px on a laptop. The chalk on its plate keeps 10.3:1 contrast over a white sky and 16.4:1 over a black sea; the test asks for 7:1. Done, not phone-verified.
