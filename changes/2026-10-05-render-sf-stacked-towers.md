San Francisco's downtown towers now have proper windows. They used to be one tall kit stretched to different heights, which stretched every window with it; now each tower is built from stacked floors (a lobby, four-storey blocks and a crown, in five styles), so a window is the same shape on a short tower as on a tall one, and the glass and stone wear the San Francisco texture sheet. The skyline still varies (towers from about 30 m to 190 m), and the headquarters, the lamps and the cross streets are as they were. The towers are a little wider than before, so the front row has a few more gaps. If the new towers or their texture sheet fail to load, the downtown draws the old towers instead.

For devs: `src/render/downtown.ts` (`planDowntown` takes `stacked`; a tower item is `model: 'tower'` with `mids` and `targetM`, drawn as the base, `mids` copies of the mid and the crown, every part at scale 1 in y; `towerFootprint`; `DowntownLayer` takes the modules as a last argument and puts the sheet on each stretch's one material), `src/render/models.ts` (`sfTowerModules`, `models/scenery/sf-tower-modules` in `region-sf`, its atlas `san-francisco`, loaded with the downtown's models), one argument in `src/render/index.ts`. `docs/architecture.md` has the paragraph.

Tests (`src/render/downtown.test.ts`, "stacked towers"): each tower's stacked height is within half a mid (7 m) of its target; the lots are the baked modules' own footprints and no two towers in a row overlap; every facade triangle in a built stretch is exactly one mid tall (a mutation that scales the mids by 1.1 fails it); every stretch's material has the atlas as its map; stacked costs no more draws and at most 1.3 times the triangles of the stretched kit along the route; and the fallbacks (no modules, no sheet) build without throwing. The front-row frontage floor drops from 85% to 80% (it measured 85.2% and now 84.7%: the lots are 24 to 34 m wide, so a run's last gap can be a lot wide), and the front-row count floor from 200 to 100 (183 now, same rule). A new region model kind lives in a region pack, so `scenery.test.ts`, `scenery-merge.test.ts` and `regions.test.ts` read each GLB from the pack that carries it (`model-files.test-util.ts`), and the manifest test lists every carried pack.

Cost on the busiest view of the downtown route (`scene-cost.test.ts`, the real road, the real models, a camera every 25 m): 50 draw calls and 58,898 triangles, against 50 and 62,270 for the stretched kit; the still scene's share is 80 and 110,000, and the frame's caps are 120 and 150,000. Browser checks were not run on the dev machine. Not phone-verified.
