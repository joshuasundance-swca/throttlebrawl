Each region's roads get their own traffic and people. The Keys: golf carts and beach cruisers at the kerb, rental convertibles, pickups towing boats, convoys of snowbird RVs, sunburnt joggers, dog walkers and a dive-bar dog. The Pacific Northwest: wagons with kayaks on the roof, muddy pickups, cyclists in rain capes, motorhome convoys, hikers in rain shells, trail runners and a wet dog. San Francisco: Hesitron robotaxis (driverless, made up, travelling in pairs), delivery e-bikes and weaving e-scooters at the kerb, professional dog walkers and a doodle.
