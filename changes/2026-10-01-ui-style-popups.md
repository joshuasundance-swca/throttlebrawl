The style cash pop-ups (near miss, oncoming, airtime and the rest) no longer sit in the middle of the road. They are now small chips under your position, on the same side (they swap sides with the left-handed layout), with the cash in yellow, gone in about a second. A run of near misses adds up on one chip, such as "NEAR MISS ×3 +$75", instead of stacking.
