Cops now patrol every race. One or two of them wait at the side of the road ahead of you, light up their siren as you come up the road and pull out after you as you pass, so you meet the law within the first minute or so. A patrol chase lasts about 40 seconds unless you go down beside him. The cop who starts behind the grid still comes after you too. Before, he was the only one, and barely faster than you, so most races never saw him.
