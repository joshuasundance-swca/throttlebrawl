San Francisco's last tier goes to the real places. Series C, the downtown takedown hunt, is now International Orange: a dawn race down Conzelman Road's hairpins from Hawk Hill, past Vista Point, across the Golden Gate and through the toll plaza, with the whole pack, the heat and six lanes of traffic. Lombard Street joins as The Crooked Mile, a race down the real one-lane brick block with its eight hairpins to Telegraph Hill. The third tier gets a drift race, Figure Eight, up the Twin Peaks hairpins.

- **Figure Eight.** A race to the line with a second required goal: $300 of drift style cash (the HUD reads `DRIFT $x/$300`). The target is deliberately small. The plan drew $3,950, but a scripted drift bot riding the route alone banks $523 to $661 over the whole race on the bikes a player could own by then. The critic's rule is to cap a drift target at 60 % of that, so it is $300, under the $314 cap the Superbike sets. `tests/sim/drift-events.test.ts` finds every drift event in the packs and fails any target over its cap.
- **Lombard is a race, not a drift race.** The T2.6 probe found a drift holds on its hairpins only at lowered `riders.driftMinMps` and `riders.driftExitMps` floors, and an event cannot set those. The plan's second drift race, "Crookedest Street, Allegedly" (`sf-t4-crookedest`), is not built: a tier holds three regular races (`tests/sim/career-ladder.test.ts`), and a second drift race on the Twin Peaks hairpins would add nothing. Its slot is Lombard's.
- **Conzelman Road is the Marin Headlands.** The Golden Gate's land roads (Hawk Hill, Conzelman Road, Vista Point, the toll plaza) were tagged `forest` as a stand-in, which drew the Pacific Northwest's dense pines, log fences and ferns. `headlands` is now a land theme of its own: grass hills with no trees, no poles and a soft grass edge. It is a small change in `src/render/scenery.ts` and `src/road/cross-section.ts` (the new tag only reaches these four roads), checked by `src/render/headlands.test.ts` against a control that shows the pines when the old tag is put back. The bake config `tools/gis/networks/osm-sf-golden-gate.json` and the four baked roads were edited to match by hand (the OSM extract is not in the repo).
- **Lombard's one-way block no longer jams.** An oncoming car left the two-way street beyond the block too fast to stop at the end of its lane, rolled into the one-lane one-way block and stopped there head-on with the cars coming down, so about one race in four left the bot, and so a player, stuck behind a wall of stopped cars for good. A road vehicle is now held, stopped, at the end of the last lane that goes its way (`laneRunBoundary`). `tests/sim/traffic-one-way.test.ts` checks that no vehicle is ever on a stretch with no lane its way over six seeds (it failed before the change); the dev bot finished 24 of 24 races on the Crooked Mile (three bikes, eight seeds), 7 of 24 stuck before.
- **#476's waiting list is empty for San Francisco.** `AWAITING_EVENT` in `tests/sim/career-content.test.ts` loses the Golden Gate and Lombard routes (the Keys and Portland entries are their own tasks').
- **The economy holds.** With the new map the balance model's typical player still waits a median of 3 to 4 races for each step-up bike, over a season of 90 races (promise: 3 to 4, and 60 to 100).

For devs: the packs' work is `packs/region-sf` (the events `sf-t4-golden-gate`, `sf-t4-crooked-mile` and `sf-t3-figure-eight`, replacing `sf-t4-series-c`; their nodes `golden-gate`, `crooked-mile` and `figure-eight`). `tests/sim/sf-real-world.test.ts` checks the rules: the Golden Gate is a fourth-tier dawn race whose win claims every road of the route, Lombard is a classic race with no drift objective and a small field, the drift race is on the Twin Peaks route and never Lombard, and the finale's road is still opened by a win in its tier. The Golden Gate keeps no fall or rail joke and stays out of the logo (the coordinator's default for the maintainer's "Golden Gate"). Not phone-verified.
