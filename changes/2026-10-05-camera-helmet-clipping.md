In the helmet view the camera no longer ends up inside the cars you pass. It was only what you saw: the game never let the bike through a car. The helmet camera rode in the rider's head, and leaning hard the head swings more than a metre out to the side (at the full lean, 1.28 m out and 1.24 m up), over a car the bike itself was passing clean, so the view went through its door. Now the eye leans out at most 0.2 m, inside the rider's own space; the horizon still rolls with the lean as before. At a hard lean a glove and the end of the bar can show in the corner, as they already could. Some regional vehicles were also drawn a little bigger than the space the game gives them (worst the parked boat on its trailer, 0.88 m past each end, and the Pacific Northwest wagons with kayaks, 0.43 m): they are now drawn exactly inside it, so you no longer ride into the end of one before you hit it.

For devs: `src/camera/chase.ts` (`helmetPlacement` holds the eye's sideways offset to the new `camera.helmetReachM` slider, 0.2 m `[default]`; docs/milestones/M3.md updated) and `src/render/views.ts` (`fitUnitFootprint`: a code-made vehicle figure that overhangs its unit box is shrunk and slid into it, axis by axis; one that fits is unchanged). Tests: `tests/sim/helmet-traffic.test.ts` races the batch's seeds 1 to 6 with the bot and two helmet cameras: 38,695 frames on the road, 346 vehicle-frames within 1 m alongside; the rider's contact box sat inside a vehicle 0 times (the sim had no gap), the new eye 0 times (closest 0.19 m outside), the old reach 122 times (the negative control, which must stay above 0). `src/render/traffic-footprint.test.ts` draws all 50 road vehicle types of every pack and checks each drawn footprint against its sim box plus 5 cm: 17 failed before the fit, 0 after. `src/camera/views.test.ts`'s helmet test now allows the eye beside the helmet past the reach and checks the helmet is then outside the phone-shaped view.
