A PS4-style gamepad now works: left stick steers, R2 throttles, L2 brakes, Cross attacks, Square and Circle attack to the left and right, Triangle kicks and R1 looks back. Connect the pad (Bluetooth on the phone works if the browser sees it) and press a button before the race. The controls module also gained tilt steering (thumb, tilt, or both), auto-throttle, a pull-back brake and phone buzzes on hits, takedowns and crashes. Those switch on once the settings screen offers them.
