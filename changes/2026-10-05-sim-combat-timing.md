Fights answer your thumb faster (playtest 4: "reduce delay between tap and attack and make it so that bumping into a rival while attacking doesn't negate the attack"). A kick now lands 0.12 s after the tap, as fast as a punch; it took 0.22 s. You can kick again as soon as the leg is back, about 0.8 s between kicks (it was 1.3 s, and a swipe in the last half second came out as a punch); that is your "No wait" call. A tap or swipe made in the last moment of an attack (about 0.17 s) is no longer lost: it fires the instant the attack ends, and a kick stays a kick. Bumping into the rider you are hitting no longer makes the blow miss: it lands on him. A press made before a rival is close enough to aim at now picks him, and his side, once he is, instead of swinging right. The punch still lands 0.12 s after the tap: any sooner and a sideways flick to pick its side would arrive after the punch had already gone. Rivals kick as often as before.

For devs: `src/sim/combat/index.ts`. The press buffer: a player's press in the last `PRESS_BUFFER_TICKS` (10) of a recovery is kept, like the stagger queue, with its kick flag sticky and the latest side (`pendingFlags`), and starts on the first legal tick. A player's kick sets no cooldown; rivals keep the data's 30 ticks, so their AI's kick cycle (13 + 6 + 27 + 30 = 76 ticks before, 7 + 6 + 33 + 30 = 76 now) is unchanged. `packs/base/weapons/kick.json`: wind-up 0.22 to 0.12 s (13 to 7 ticks), recovery 0.45 to 0.55 s (27 to 33), so the swing is as long as before. Seven ticks is the floor that `src/input/gesture.ts`'s invariant allows (the 80 ms side drag plus a sampling tick is 6 ticks), and the punch is already there. An attack with no target re-aims every wind-up tick (`reAim`), and one still without a target swings at either side (side 0) rather than defaulting to the right; the audit's probe (16 m/s closing, rival on the left, M1's 13-tick kick) went from 5 landing press ticks to 12, the same as a kick forced left. The bump rule: the riding phase's contact holds two bikes 2 m apart nose to tail, merges their speeds and glances them apart, so a rider you ride into was pushed out of a punch's 1.2 m or a kick's 1.0 m reach before the blow landed. A player's attack that touches its target (or, with none, a rider on its side) in its wind-up or active moment now lands on him while he is within `BUMP_REACH_M` (3 m) along and across, also on the tick the active moment ends. Players only, like the buffer: rivals keep their reach box and drop presses as before. Tests: `src/sim/combat/timing.test.ts` (the buffer for punch and kick in each order, the sticky kick, the edge of the buffer, rivals unbuffered; the player's kick-to-kick and the rival's kept wait; the harness against the pack; the re-aim windows; a bump sweep through `createSimWithWorld` with the real riders phase: every attack that touched its rival lands: 52 bumped attacks riding into his back wheel, every one a miss before this change, and 2 steering in from alongside, which already landed and stay as a guard). The older combat tests now read the kick's wind-up from the harness instead of 13; three keep M1's 13-tick kick where the rule needs the longer wind-up (the brake-out-of-reach case) or show the re-aim. Replays: recorded races where the player pressed late in an attack, kicked twice, or swung before a rival was in reach play out differently; new recordings replay exactly (the new state is in the hashed combat state). Not changed: an attack pressed after a rear bump still misses (the rival is 2 m ahead, the straight kick's reach), and the dev bot still leads its kicks by M1's 13 ticks and waits 78 between them (`src/dev/bot`). Not phone-verified.

Keeper fix that rides along: the new timing moved the career's first race on seed 4 so the bot never landed a jump clean, and the HUD layout check that measures the ticker after a paid landing (`tests/e2e/ui-style-popups.spec.ts`) waited for one until the race ended. That check now starts the career's first race afresh on the next seed when a race ends without one, up to six seeds, instead of relying on seed 4.

Also the forgiving-landings check (`tests/sim/landings-held.ts`): a crash into traffic just after a clean landing now counts as the road's, not the landing's. With the kick held over the Fogline Run's log hop, the race had parted ways with its no-command control long before, the landing was clean, and five ticks later an oncoming log truck hit the rider, which the control could not show.
