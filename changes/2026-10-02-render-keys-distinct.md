The Keys race now hops between keys that each look different. You start in Chum Key, a fishing village with shrimp boats up on blocks, fish houses on pilings, buoys hung on lines and stacks of lobster traps. Over the bridge is Lime Wedge Key, a resort strip of pink and aqua hotels, pool decks, a tiki bar and rows of rental scooters. Further on, Salvage Key racks old boats three high, stacks retired buses and keeps a junk-art garden. The long race ends on Last Resort Key the morning after the party: bunting, coolers where they fell, the bar closed and a pool flamingo face down on the sand. Each key has its own signs and billboards, and the conch cottages now stand only in Conch Row.
