Pick your kick's direction as you ride up behind someone. On the phone, swipe down on the attack button and lean the swipe left or right to kick to that side; a straight swipe down still kicks whoever is nearest. Swipe up for a straight kick at the rider right in front of you, which reaches further ahead. On the keyboard, hold U or O with K to kick to a side, and press I for the straight kick. On a gamepad, Triangle with Square or Circle kicks to a side, and L1 is the straight kick.
