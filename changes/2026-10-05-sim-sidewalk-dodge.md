Cyclists, scooter riders and delivery e-bikes now jump out of the way when you come up the sidewalk at them. Before, they only moved over for a rider on the road, so a rider charging up the kerb line often clipped them; now, if there is room, they get to the far side of a wide sidewalk, and if there is none, they step into the road clear of you. People and animals on the sidewalk already dived, and still do. Seeded rides up the busy sidewalks of Key West, San Francisco and Portland: 44 of the 47 things in the rider's way got out of it (94 %), where the same rides without the new dodge saw 40 of 59 (68 %).

For devs: every traffic type now says what it is for sidewalks and verges in a new `behaviour.roadside` (`dodges`, `yields` or `solid`), and the sim reads that instead of `hazard` and a width cut-off: a `dodges` thing never costs the rider a crash, a `yields` thing (the gator, the elk, the golf cart, the pedicab) gets out of the way but a hit is a crash, a `solid` thing does not move (the road's vehicles and parked oddities). All 80 shipped types carry a class chosen to match what they did before, so nothing else changes. The table with the reasons is in `docs/content-packs.md` ("Roadside classes"). Hydrants, benches and poles are drawn only, with no hit shape; that is listed as the open follow-up for the hit-shape lane. New tuning key `traffic.kerbDeep` (on; absent in an old recording means off, so old replays are unchanged). Tests: `tests/sim/roadside-classes.test.ts` (every type has a class that fits, each class behaves as its row says, behaviour follows the data), `tests/sim/traffic-sidewalk-dodge.test.ts` (the share that dodge is at least 85 % overall and 75 % per region, no crash into a `dodges` thing, a braking rider is never punished). Not phone-verified; no browser was run.
