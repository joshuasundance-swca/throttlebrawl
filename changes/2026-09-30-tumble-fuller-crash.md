Crashes are bigger. On a hard crash the bike cartwheels end over end and the rider flops like a ragdoll, and a flying bike or rider can knock over someone riding behind or make a car brake. On the Pelican Channel Bridge you can now go over the rail: a splash, a 4-second wait, and you're back on the bridge. A rival you knock off gets up, shakes a fist at you and notes a grudge.
