Buildings are solid now, and you can land on their roofs. Fly over the sidewalk higher than a building and you come down on its roof and ride it: the same landing as on the road (a clean flip is clean, nose first is a crash), the gas and the brake work up there, and riding off the edge is a jump. A row of roofs runs on across the narrow cracks between them; a taller neighbour is a wall, a lower one a drop, and a gap between two buildings is open (roll into it slowly and you fall through, hit it fast and you jump it). A pitched roof is a slope you ride up like a ramp and leave from its ridge. Below a roof's edge the building is a wall, met the way a lamp post or a car is: brush along it and you wobble, ride into it square at speed and you crash. That holds for every building and landmark that is drawn beside the road (downtown's towers, Chinatown's and the Mission's fronts, Old Town's shops with their porches and balcony posts, the waterfront's sheds, the festival's shops, the landmarks), for you, the rivals and the cops alike; a tower is a wall at any height you can reach. The chalk mark shows where you will come down on a roof. A crash on a roof stays on the roof. The rivals and the cops steer round the posts, porches and racks that stand on a sidewalk. The row and painted houses beside some roads are not in the physics yet, so their fronts stay walls at any height. Not phone-verified.

For devs: `src/sim/riders/structures.ts` (the maintainer, 2026-10-06, `[decided]`: a road race in a physical world with honest edges; "I've been high enough to land on buildings but the wall prevented it"). The race reads the road's structures plan through `raceStructures` (new in `src/road/structures.ts`: the kept plan, or the plan made at once when every planner its network needs has loaded; it throws otherwise, so a race never rides a world with a piece missing). The app loads the planners in the background at boot and every race start waits for them as it waits for its roads (`loadStructurePlanners`, exported through `src/sim/api.ts`); the tests load them in a setup file (`tests/setup/structures.ts`). Walls: `structureContact` and `airStructures` in `src/sim/riders/index.ts` meet a structure's footprint, laid out in the rider's frame in metres (`localShape`, `inMetres`), by `meetSolid` (the one rule, `traffic.solidHitMps`), on the ground and in the air, from its base to its roof; a top no more than a kerb over the feet is a step, not a wall; a small top (a post, a rail, a parapet) from above is the fall speed (`hit: 'top'`). Roofs: a structure top that holds the bike is a support (`structureSupportAt` in `supports.ts`, kind `structure`): landed through `land()` (a pitched roof judged against its own slope), ridden (its grade along the heading, not the road's), crossed to a neighbour within a kerb with no hop (a crack narrower than the bike is bridged by its wheels), and ridden off as a take-off. On a structure's top the band's edge does not hold the rider (`barrierContact` skipped; combat's shove and the riders' bumps leave him there, `outPastEdge`). A building front whose buildings are planned (`frontTagAt` in `src/road/beyond.ts`, `plannedFrontAt`) is no wall in the air (`overStep` takes the edge's top as 0 there, `edgeTopOf`, for the flight and the chalk mark's forecast alike; the buildings stop the rider; the floor below a rider in the air, `floorOf`, is a roof he is over past the edge), and on the ground a rider held at it meets the building by the one rule where one stands (a gap keeps the old edge). The tumble rests a crashed body on a structure's top (`bodyTopsOf`, `stepCluster`'s `tops`), and the rival AI and the cops see the structures in a ridable band (`bandPiecesOf`, `furnitureSeen`). Switch `riders.structures` (on; absent, the old rules), off in `ISOLATED`; per-rider state (`structures`) only after a first contact. Tests: `src/sim/riders/structures.test.ts` (fixture street, each with a control), `tests/sim/structures-solid.test.ts` (Duval, the Mission, downtown's towers), `scripts/hitboxes.test.ts` (the footprint the contact meets is the plan's box at every rider's spot).
