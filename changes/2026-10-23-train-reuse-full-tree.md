The train can reuse a full-suite result for its exact assembled tree after independently checking the original main train's plan, official record and upload. Artifact names alone and PR-run artifacts cannot authorize reuse; full-PR landings may therefore need another full main suite. Reuse checks complete current job evidence, rebuilds the bundle, and reads current readiness after proof work. Missing or uncertain proof during planning runs the normal full suite. If the report cannot verify it again, it posts no success. Landing checks and deadlines stay in place, and skipped tests create no fresh proof.
