Rivals take your hits like themselves. Each one now has a toughness a little above or below average: Deacon Vane, Old Growth and Gripman Gus soak up a fourth kick and shake off your blows sooner, while Dial-Up, Pivot and Chad Speedwell go down quicker and wobble longer. Each rival also carries a punch strength; for now it stays out of their hits on you (they hit as hard as before), and the tuning panel's "Rival power on you" slider turns it on. Cops are average on both.
