No more power poles, palms or mangroves standing in the sea. On the inside of tight bends (San Francisco's switchbacks, the Pacific Northwest grade) the land beside the road used to fold over itself and leave a sea-coloured hole, and scenery could stand in it; the land now stays narrower there. Scenery also keeps its whole footprint on the land where the land ends or narrows. A test now checks every spot on 24 race seeds in the Keys, the Pacific Northwest and San Francisco.
