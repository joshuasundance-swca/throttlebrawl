The radio learns four new bands, all still written as code: a grunge band (quiet verses into loud, distorted choruses), an indie folk band (stomps, claps, tambourine, strummed acoustic, banjo and glockenspiel), a synth band (drum machine, pumping bass, pads, arpeggios and a gliding lead) and a psychedelic band (a phased organ, a fuzz guitar and tambourine). They play slower than the Keys' surf and rockabilly and at the same loudness. The next change gives them to the Pacific Northwest and San Francisco stations.
