Nine rival voice lines that came out garbled or off-voice are redone, and every other voice line was checked and kept. All 273 spoken barks were run through two speech recognisers and a voice-likeness check on the dev machine. Four were garbled: the Mayor's "Dodged it like a subpoena" was heard as "Daws did like a subpoena", Juniper ad-libbed a "Mm-mm" before "Last call", Pivot's "risks slide" came out as "wrist slide", and Deacon's "Last rites" slurred to "Lass rites". Five said the right words but sounded unlike their rival's other lines (Tammy's "Bless your heart", Kevin's "pin in you", Pruitt's "walk it off", Deacon's "ring light" and Dial-Up's "Buffering"). All nine are now spoken by the rival's own reference voice, made for free on the dev machine's graphics card; the other 264 stay as they were, and none were cut. Each redone line says why in its pack file, so any of them can still be vetoed with "cut this" or in chat. The clips are about the same size as before (the nine total 56.8 KB, down from 57.6 KB). For developers: `tools/voices/scripts/check_local.py` and `redo_local.py` run the check and a redo locally, a set's `meta.voice.review` records the picks (`tbvoices apply` now keeps it), and a stale `tools/voices` test that expected every bark set to be cast now allows the uncast DJ and road-event sets.
