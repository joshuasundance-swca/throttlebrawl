The Columbia River Gorge and Chuckanut Drive look more like themselves. On the Gorge run, Vista House stands on Crown Point near the start, the green-domed stone rotunda the real highway loops round, and long runs of the old highway's grey masonry guard wall, with its arched openings, line the cliff side of the road where the ground drops away to the river. On Chuckanut Drive, red-barked madrones lean out over the bay side of the road. Multnomah Falls and its lodge are not on the Gorge route: they are about 5.6 km past where it ends, so they wait for a longer route.

For devs (playtest 4, P4-19, run B task B9; the identity sheets' CR3, CR1 and H4, with Codex CX5's models). Everything is data plus two kit rules; there is no new render system.

- **Vista House** is a `landmark` feature of Crown Point Loops (`gorge-landmarks#vista_house`, scale 1, `farM` 200), written in the Gorge bake config and re-baked. Its place was checked against OpenStreetMap, not remembered: the building (way 145892556, centre 45.5395671, -122.2443784, `ele` 223) projects onto the baked road at s 275, 24.4 m to its right, where the road is 223.9 m high; the real highway passes it 28.3 m off. The road wraps the site in a 28 m bend, and the land strip cannot fold over the inside of a bend, so at the real point a corner of the 22 m footprint would stand 24.7 m past the drawn land. It stands 24.1 m from the real point along the road (s 250, d 30; the nearest place whose whole footprint is on drawn land, 1.0 m to spare at the worst corner). The visitors' pedestrian zone stood on the cliff side; it is now `vista-house-visitors` on the building's side. One landmark draw call, 600 triangles near and 154 past 200 m.
- **Guard walls and madrones** are two rules at the end of the PNW kit (`src/render/roadside.ts`, `gorge-wall` and `madrone`; `first: true`, so they claim their ground without moving any other rule's seeded places) drawing the new scenery model kind `pnwIdentity` (`models/scenery/pnw-identity`, 9.5 KB, loaded only for a network with either tag). The wall is CX5's 6 m section laid every 6 m on its own slots, at the verge's outer edge (where a rider is stopped), following the road's grade; the madrone stands 1.5 to 7.5 m past the verge with its back to the road, so its lean (the model's +Z) hangs out over the water, through one new rule option, `away`. Seeds 1, 7, 42 and 99: 309 to 319 wall sections on the Gorge, covering 87 to 90 % of the six-metre slots of the long runs; 86 to 93 madrones on Chuckanut, in 32 of 33 hundred-metre windows of the long runs.
- **Where they stand is data**, found from the ground: two one-sided district tags, `guard-wall` and `bay-bluff`, written over runs of s by a new bake-config field, `sideRuns` (`tools/gis/src/tbgis/config.py`, `emit.py`; never over a deck; the lint's tag list names both). `tools/gis/src/tbgis/drops.py` found the runs: it samples the USGS 3DEP ground 14 m and 30 m to each side every 10 m and calls a side a drop where it is 5 m or more below the road at 14 m and 12 m or more at 30 m (runs of 30 m or more, gaps under 40 m joined). Run on all six roads it put every Gorge drop on the left (north, the river) and every Chuckanut drop on the right (the bay) and none on the other side of any road: 15 guard-wall runs, 2,148 m of the Gorge's 7,629 m; 8 bay-bluff runs, 3,760 m of Chuckanut's 7,624 m. Both bakes were re-run from a fresh OSM extract; the baked files differ from main only in those tags, the Crown Point features, `provenance` and `2` written `2.0`.
- **Multnomah Falls and the lodge (the decision)** `[default]`: neither is placed. OpenStreetMap has the falls at 45.5759525, -122.1151867 and the lodge at 45.5773652, -122.1174658, 5.6 km from the route's last point (nearly seven times the landmark layer's 820 m reach). A route extension is at least 5.6 km of new road, 73 % of today's route, and a far landmark at the route's end would stand 5.6 km from the real falls, a place error of the kind the maintainer's note is about. The nodes stay in the kit; the extension is its own task, starting at the bake config's `pathTo`.
- **Tests, each with a control**: `src/render/gorge-identity.test.ts` (37: Vista House's feature, its place against the OSM point, footprint on drawn land with the real point as the failing control, keep-off, near and far forms; the side runs equal the config's and sit off decks; walls only in `guard-wall` runs, left, past the verge, laid end to end; madrones only in `bay-bluff` runs, right, leaning away; none with the tag taken off, the model unloaded or on any other network; the same stretches and no more meshes in view), `tools/gis/tests/test_side_runs.py` (5). `scenery.test.ts` counts the new model kind's three variants. Also run: roadside, verge, landmarks (all), bridge-supports, the PNW route and region-route tests, road pack rules, verges and board slots, the geometry land, bridge-end, backdrop and scenery sweeps, `pnw-real-world`, `road-setpieces-live`, `gorge-first-turns`, ruff, mypy and typecheck.
- **Budgets** (`scene-cost.test.ts`, same machine, still-scene share 80 draw calls and 110,000 triangles): the Gorge at most 62 draw calls and 88,138 triangles in a view (87,918 with the walls and madrones switched off and Vista House still in; main at #553's merge: 61 and 88,038), 18 draw calls and 21,862 triangles of headroom; Chuckanut 53 and 51,630 (53 and 50,978 switched off), 27 and 58,370 of headroom. First-load JavaScript from one local build: 467.1 KB gzip of 500 KB (32.9 KB headroom); the change against main was not measured here (CI's perf check reports it).
- **Not done.** No browser was run; not phone-verified, and how the wall and the madrones read on screen is unseen. Chuckanut's `bluff` land and rock cuts (H1, H2), the Gorge's falls and wind (CR4, CR6) are other rows of the sheets. The wall stands at the verge's outer edge, 6 m off the asphalt, because a nearer wall would be a new hard edge for the sim; tightening it is a verge change that the Gorge's turn work should weigh, not this one.
