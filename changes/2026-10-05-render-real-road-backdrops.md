The Golden Gate now shows from the headlands. Ride Hawk Hill and Conzelman Road and the bridge stands out over the strait in International Orange, towers and cables and its traffic crawling across, where before the fog hid it until you were almost on it. Close up it is still the one detailed bridge: the far one takes over only where the near one has faded into the fog, so you never see two. Coit Tower now stands on top of Telegraph Hill over the city from the other San Francisco routes, and on the Bahia Honda run the old 1912 rail bridge stands beside the new one, its rusty truss under the old road deck and a span missing near each end.

For devs (playtest 4, P4-19, run B task B1; the identity sheets' G1, W2 and B1).

- **The near fade** (`src/render/backdrop/`, G1). A backdrop piece may now carry `nearFadeM`: it is the far form of a model the near world also draws. No fragment of it is drawn nearer, in view depth, than the near fog's end (or the camera's far plane, if nearer), where three's fog has the near model wholly hidden; past that it comes out of the haze over `nearFadeM` metres. It is a per-vertex attribute (`aFade`, from `Soup.fade`) and a `discard` in the backdrop's one material, so no draw call is added; `nearFadeAt` and `fadeFrom` in `builder.ts` mirror the shader. A suspension or girder bridge may also slope end to end (`deckEndM`), its deck, piers, towers, cables and traffic following it. Both are checked in `backdropProblems`.
- **The far Golden Gate** (`packs/region-sf/assets/backdrop/san-francisco/networks/osm-sf-golden-gate.json`, `golden-gate-far`). It is laid on the near kit's own line in the network's metres (`frame: local`), from anchorage to anchorage: its ends and towers are 0.0 to 0.5 m from the kit's, and its deck is 71.2, 69.0, 60.8 and 58.6 m at the Marin end, the towers and the plaza end, against the road's 71.2, 69.1, 60.8 and 58.6 m. It fades over 250 m. The other networks keep the region's `golden-gate-bridge` piece as before (unfaded, exaggerated 1.3 times).
- **Coit Tower and Telegraph Hill** (`coit-tower`, `telegraph-hill` in the San Francisco backdrop's `region.json`), on every San Francisco network except Lombard's, whose route ends at the near kit's tower. Its spot (Wikipedia's 37.8025 N, 122.40583 W) is 23 m from where the baked Telegraph Hill Boulevard ends, standing on 85 m against the road's 87 m. It stands on 7 of the 8 networks; Chinatown's lantern row passes 60 m from it, so the keep-out leaves it out there.
- **The old Bahia Honda bridge** (`old-bahia-honda-bridge` in the Keys backdrop's `region.json`, on the Bahia Honda and Seven Mile networks): a `truss` with `deckOnTop`, 57 m spans, and the two removed spans (the westernmost and one near the east end) as `gaps`. Its line is OpenStreetMap's way of the old bridge (ways 11115598, 360080957 and 45528505, read from the Overpass API); Wikipedia puts it south of the new one. It stands 216 m from the new deck at the nearest and 203 m from any road (keep-out 210 m). From the Seven Mile's west end, 5.5 to 7 km off, it is a sliver on the horizon at most.
- **Tests.** New `src/render/backdrop/real-road-backdrops.test.ts`, each rule with a control: the fade's cut and ramp at four fog ends (a piece with no fade is drawn at any depth); the material carries the attribute, the uniform follows the fog (480 m, and 750 m for a 900 m fog) and the shader discards; only the far Golden Gate's 2,016 vertices fade; the far bridge lies on the kit's line (above); its towers are seen past the fog from 145 of 145 cameras on Hawk Hill and Conzelman Road, up to 9.1 degrees (without the piece: no tower over the strait); from cameras every 25 m along the whole route at fog ends of 300, 480 and 700 m, 0 of 245,553 far-bridge vertices inside the fog are drawn (all 245,553 without the fade); Coit Tower's spot, and its list is exactly the networks with no near one; the old bridge's truss under its deck and its distance from the roads. `bridge-styles.test.ts` adds the sloping deck (control: level without it) and the two data checks. `landmarks-sf.test.ts` gains the rule that every far Golden Gate vertex on its own route fades (2,016 of 2,016; 0 of 2,052 from Lombard). `backdrop.test.ts` lists the Golden Gate network's signature pieces and movers.
- **The geometry sweep** (`tests/sim/geometry-backdrop.test.ts`). A standing vertex that fades is exempt from the 150 m keep-out, since it is never drawn inside the fog; a new negative control takes the fade off the Golden Gate's and finds a vertex 5 m from the road (236 m with the fade on). Every route's network stays green with its known list empty: closest standing vertices 203 m on Bahia Honda, 236 m on the Golden Gate.
