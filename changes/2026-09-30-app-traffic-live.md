Traffic and pedestrians are in the real game now, not just the test builds. Both ways along the causeway: rental sedans, pickups with kayaks, and box trucks. Cars brake behind you, never appear right on top of you, and oncoming ones show up with time to react. Brush a car and you wobble; touch another while still wobbling, or touch a truck at all, and you go down. At the marina boardwalk, the fishing rail, the sandbar beach and the tiki stand, tourists with coolers, fishermen and the odd chicken wander about and dive out of your way, cartoonishly; nobody gets hurt. Each direction's traffic density is a tuning slider, so oncoming traffic can be turned down or off.
