A gap in the road now reads as a broken bridge end you can see coming, not a hole in the deck (playtest 3, T11.1, the render half of "the 80 m missing span is the big jump"). Next to nothing a player sees changes until a road file carries a `gap` that spans the drive lanes (none does yet; the Seven Mile bake waits for this). In `src/render/road-mesh.ts`: no surface, marking, fascia, rail, post, pylon or trestle bent is drawn over a gap's box, and the deck stops exactly at each side whatever the 2 m sampling. Each side ends in a raw-concrete face as deep as the fascia beside it with a jagged bottom, two tufts of rebar stick 0.6 m out into the gap, and three orange lip stripes like a ramp's mark it from a distance. A `ramp` that ends within 8 m of a gap's start is its kicker: its cheeks close the wedge under the lifted road down to the level it rises from. All of it is in the road's own layers (deck, rampMark), plus one small `road-rebar` layer that only a chunk with a broken end has and the far chunks leave out: at most one extra draw call in a chunk that holds a gap, none anywhere else. `RoadSceneStats` gains `gapEnds` and `kickers`.

In `src/render/backdrop/`: three new bridge styles, flat-coloured plates in the one backdrop mesh (no draw call). `truss` (camelback spans, over the deck or, with `deckOnTop`, under it, honouring `gaps`), `lift` (towers joined at the top, counterweights, hoist ropes) and `arch` (a tied arch with hangers). A `gaps` hole in any bridge is now exactly as long as its shares say: the slabs used to round it to a whole number of about 120 m slabs (the Keys' existing old Seven Mile girder piece, one `gaps` entry, moves each edge of its hole by up to a slab, and every bridge with a hole now shows a broken end at each side). A 1.5 km truss costs about 2,300 triangles, a lift 300 and an arch 400, against 230 for a girder. The data check refuses a bad `style`, `gaps`, `archAt`, `spanM`, `trussM`, `archM` or `deckOnTop`. No pack data uses the new styles yet; the real-world content tasks place them.

Docs: the Backdrop piece list and the Gaps bullet in `content-packs.md` say how all this is drawn. New checks: `src/render/gap-ends.test.ts` (no deck triangle between a gap's ends and the same check finding them on an unbroken deck, the deck reaching each end to the millimetre between two samples, the faces, the rebar clusters, the stripes, the kicker's cheeks, an unbroken edge left as it was) and `src/render/backdrop/bridge-styles.test.ts` (each hole's length to a metre for each style, the camelback and the deck-on-top truss, the lift's towers and counterweights, the arch's rise and hangers, one mesh, the data check). Not phone-verified.
