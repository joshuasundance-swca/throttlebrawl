Russian Hill in San Francisco is now a real road network with a junction choice. Climbing Union Street, one block before the crest, a sign on the right says "JONES STREET: KEEP RIGHT. 29% grade. Not a typo." Keep right and you turn onto the real Jones Street, which drops straight down the hill's north face (fast riders leave the ground at the top), then run along Chestnut and back onto Leavenworth for the finish. It is about 28 m shorter than staying on Union over the top and diving down Leavenworth, but it is steeper, has two more corners, and misses the boost pad the race sometimes puts on Leavenworth. The Russian Hill races in the career (The Collab, Your Meter Expired and Russian Hill) all ride the new network, and winning Russian Hill claims the new streets on the map. Done, not phone-verified.

For developers: Russian Hill's stretch bake (`tools/gis/configs/osm-sf-russian-hill.json`) became a network config (`tools/gis/networks/osm-sf-russian-hill.json`) on exactly the same path, with the same network, route and road ids, so career positions and tests hold. Union and Leavenworth are cut at the junctions (`osm-sf-union-crest`, `osm-sf-leavenworth-north`), and Leavenworth's billboard moved onto the north road. The network bake's report now carries each line's fun numbers (corners, grades, launch crests), as a stretch bake's did. New: `uv run tbgis loops <config>` lists where the real map offers a junction choice along any real route (`tools/gis/reports/*.loops.json`). It found none on Chuckanut Drive, one long detour on the Columbia River Highway, and only detours or a tiny alternate on Twin Peaks, so those routes keep no choice for now; Bahia Honda's extract holds only US 1, so it can't say there. The SF track's sign-slot test accepts a junction sign that stands on the region's real-road network, as the Keys' and the Pacific Northwest's already do. A new sim test (`tests/sim/road-sf-network.test.ts`) rides the choice end to end.
