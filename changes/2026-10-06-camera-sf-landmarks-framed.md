Three San Francisco sights read better. The Coit Tower shot at the Lombard finish now hides the race readouts and the touch buttons while it plays, so the picture is the tower alone (they are back with the next race, and the results screen follows two seconds later as before). It also bends the houses less: the camera backs off along the road and rises a little instead of widening the view to 86 degrees, so the whole tower is still in frame with the chase view's own field. The Twin Peaks radio mast stays whole in the picture from the lane to the verge and at every speed on the climb: it is drawn smaller than before (about 120 m, where the real one is 298 m) because its top was running off the top of the screen. The headland gun batteries on the Golden Gate's Marin side show a bold gun from far down the road: a thick barrel lifted over the wall against the sky, a dark pit under a dark collar, and paler concrete against the grass.

For devs: `shotTarget` and `shotPose` in `src/camera/finish-shot.ts` back the camera off up to 90 m (0.3 m of rise per metre) to fit the landmark at the held field, capped at `SHOT_MAX_FOV_DEG` 75 (was 100; the Lombard shot used 86); `finishShotHudOn`, `FINISH_SHOT_CSS` and `GameUi.setFinishShot` in `src/ui/finish-shot-hud.ts` and `src/ui/index.ts` hide `#hud`, `#touch-surface` and `#look-offer` through a `data-finish-shot` flag on the root that `show()` clears; `app/index.ts` sets it each frame. The Twin Peaks backdrop's `radio-mast-near` is at `exaggerate` 0.4 (was 0.7); `tests/sim/twin-peaks-mast.test.ts` rides the real chase camera over 16 speed and offset pairs and holds the top under 0.9 of the frame wherever the mast is in it (the 0.7 and 1.3 times pieces are the controls). `src/render/battery-guns.ts` has the new `BATTERY_GUNS` (pedestal, 0.78 m barrel, 30 degrees, dark collars, dark pit insides, paler concrete) and keeps run C's guns as `BATTERY_GUNS_FIRST`; `src/render/battery-silhouette.test.ts` measures the real baked battery's screen size from 60, 100 and 150 m (16.6 px of gun over the wall at 150 m against 4.1 before; 272 triangles a battery against 96, no new draw call). The maintainer kept the Coit Tower framing on 2026-10-06 (`[decided]` in `docs/architecture.md`). Not phone-verified; no browser was run.
