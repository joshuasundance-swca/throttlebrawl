The Keys' golden-hour palette no longer sets the sea to orange (`#e08a5b`). The renderer starts reading region palettes now, so PNW and San Francisco get their own colours; without this edit the Keys' sea would have turned orange, a colour the game has never drawn. The Keys keep the teal sea they have always had. Presentation data only: the palette is outside the sim content hash.
