cops-1 (M1): Sgt. Pruitt, the first cop, as sim code and pack data. He is not in a race yet: the integration lane adds him to the field in `buildSimConfig`, and the race lane ends the race for a busted player.

- `src/sim/cops/` is his controller and the crude bust. He waits where he starts for `cops.spawnDelayS` (20 s, divided by the difficulty's cop frequency), sounds the siren (`siren` event, `on: true`) and chases the nearest player, or whoever landed a hit or kick within 60 m of him in the last 10 s. While his target rides, he closes to `cops.followGapM` (40 m) behind and holds there instead of shadowing; once the target is down he pulls up beside him. A player who is down (tumbling or on foot) within his `law.bustRadiusM` × `cops.bustRadiusScale` for `law.bustDwellS` × `cops.bustDwellScale` of scaled time is busted: a `bust` event with `fineCash`, once per player, and the siren goes off. He only busts while he is up and chasing, so knocking him down works. Every timer counts scaled time, so a hit-stop freezes the dwell.
- Pack data: `packs/base/riders/sgt-pruitt.json` (a cop with a `law` block: 14 m, 1 s, a $400 fine, pursuit speed ×1.05) and his agency `packs/base/crews/keys-county-deputies.json`, a parody name on real county-deputy structure.
- Tests: 19 unit tests beside the code (the bust inside and outside the radius and dwell, a knocked-down cop, the scales, scaled time, the spawn delay and siren, chaos targeting, holding the gap, pulling up and busting, a same-hash chase), and `tests/sim/cops-bust-rate.test.ts`, which runs 50 seeded races with him appended and prints the player-bust rate (fails above 30%). It is 0% today because nothing knocks a rider down until tumble-1 and combat-1 land.
