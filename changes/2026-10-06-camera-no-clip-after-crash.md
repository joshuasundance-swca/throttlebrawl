After a crash on a city sidewalk the camera no longer ends up inside a building. On Duval Street it used to sit inside an upper-floor balcony railing, or cut through a shopfront, after you went down beside the planters and trees (the solid-world check: "the camera ends up inside buildings after a Duval sidewalk crash"). The camera now keeps out of the fronts on every street that has them at the sidewalk: it swings to the open side, toward the road, and your bike stays in view. Not phone-verified.

For devs: a crash throws the rider to the back of the sidewalk and the walk back to the bike swings the chase camera round the rider, so the eye went past the sidewalk's outer edge, where the buildings stand. `src/camera/fronts.ts` holds the camera at most `camera.frontMarginM` (0.5 m) inside a sidewalk's outer edge on any side whose scenery tags name a street (`FRONT_TAGS`, the tags of the street themes in `road/themes.ts`: Old Town, downtown, Portland's blocks, the mural alleys, Chinatown and North Beach, row houses, cross streets), and on Old Town further in by `camera.frontReachM` (3.2 m, the open bar's 3.1 m, less the 0.3 m the facade stands past the edge). It is a sideways shift along the road's across direction; the aim and height are unchanged, the helmet eye is not moved, and `camera.keepClearOfFronts` at 0 turns it off. The push is on in the frame it is needed and eases off at 4 per second. The camera still imports only types from `road`, so the list of tags is its own data, and `src/camera/fronts.test.ts` holds it to `LAND_TAGS`. `tests/sim/camera-fronts.test.ts` rides a scripted rider along the sidewalk of Duval, Russian Hill and downtown until it crashes, with the old camera and the new one following each ride, and checks the eye from each crash to half a second after the rider is back on the road against the fronts the render layers draw (their own plans: Old Town's balconied shopfronts and open bars with their balconies, the scatter's row houses and apartments, the downtown towers), each a box from its base up padded by the 0.3 m near plane. Measured: the old camera was inside a front in 2,107 of 5,691 frames on Duval (4 rides, 35 crashes), 10 of 2,103 on Russian Hill (3 rides, 14 crashes) and 5 of 3,155 downtown (3 rides, 19 crashes); the new camera in none of them. Controls: on every street the old camera pushed 5 m toward the front is inside one (so the check can see that street's fronts), and with the reach set to 2.3 m the open bars are found (263 frames on Duval) and the reach test fails. Not covered by the drawn-front test: the mural alleys, Chinatown and North Beach, Portland and the waterfront, which follow the same rule from the tags. Not phone-verified; browser tiers not run on the dev machine.
