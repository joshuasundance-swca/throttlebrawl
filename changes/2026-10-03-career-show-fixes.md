Two career fixes, both found by playing the live game.

- The producer no longer asks for something the event already pays for. In each region's first grudge race (Per My Last Email, and its Pacific Northwest and San Francisco twins), the producer could ask for "$300 of style. +$200", the same as the race's own bonus. The results then showed that goal once met and once failed. An ask now never repeats a goal the event sets. A place ask still comes up in a race to the line, but only when it asks for a better place than the race does (top 2 in a top-3 race).
- Last place no longer reads as a win. The Shakedown asks you only to finish, so 5th of 5 clears it, but the results said "WON" and the paper said "won, 5th of 5". A race to the line cleared below first now reads "5TH OF 5. CLEARED." on the results, and the paper says "cleared, 5th of 5" with a finishing-place headline instead of a winner's one. First place, and any grudge, hunt or escape you win, still says won. The career still counts the event as won: the map, the roads and the save are unchanged.
