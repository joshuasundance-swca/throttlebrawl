Road files can now describe the whole width of a road: one to three lanes each way, an optional median, and a band of ground beside each side (its width, what it is made of, and what stops you at its far edge: open ground, ferns, water, a building, a fence, or a rail on bridges and drops). A road can also say what its lanes are made of, so a marked dirt shortcut is a road with a dirt surface. Every existing road gets its bands worked out from its scenery, matching what is drawn there: ground beside land, water beside the sea and causeways, rails where rails stand. Nothing rides differently yet; the off-road work builds on this.
