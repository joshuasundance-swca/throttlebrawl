Choose Eagle Point North Run in Iowa for a course through Central Loop and the long northern park arc. Traffic shares the one-way roads, with soft grass verges and park scenery. The original Serpentine Drive course remains available.

Crashing off the road beside another bend no longer lets recovery skip most of the course.

Waiting cops park on firm grass clear of traffic when the narrow park roads have no safe paved spot.
