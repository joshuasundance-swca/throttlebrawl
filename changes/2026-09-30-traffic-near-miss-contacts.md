Hitting a car no longer bounces you off it. Plough into the back of a car or meet one head-on and you come off the bike; brush along a car's side or clip its corner and you wobble and lose speed. Near misses now only count when you pass fast, so creeping past a stopped car scores nothing. The tuning panel gains three traffic sliders: the speed that turns a car hit into a wipeout, the closing speed a near miss needs, and an optional ease-in that starts a race with fewer oncoming cars (off by default).
