You can now change every key and gamepad button. Settings has a new Keys tab: pick Keyboard or Gamepad, tap a slot beside an action and press the key or button you want. Every action is there: steering, throttle, brake, the U-turn, the wheelie, punch, kick and its sides, the straight kick, look back, the camera view, pause and skipping the run-back. If a key ends up doing two jobs, both rows say so ("Also: Punch"), and Reset puts the defaults back. Your keys are saved with your settings, and the pause screen's keyboard list shows your own keys. On a phone nothing changes: the tab only shows where there is a keyboard or a gamepad.

Drifting on a keyboard is comfier too: Space now brakes as well as S and Down, so you can hold Space with your thumb while you steer and hold the throttle (W and A or D, or the arrow keys). Every old key still works. There is also a U-turn button, Q on the keyboard and d-pad down on a gamepad: hold it and steer the way round. The double tap of the brake still works too. Options on a gamepad now pauses.

For devs: `src/input/bindings.ts` (new: the Keys rows, the saved record to a KeyMap, conflicts, one slot's change, labels), `src/input/uturn-macro.ts` (new: the U-turn button writes the brake's double tap into the action state, so the SimInput is the gesture the sim already reads), `devices/keyboard.ts` and `devices/gamepad.ts` (the `uturn` and `pause` actions, Space as a brake, `setMap`), `src/save` (`keyBindings`, additive, version stays 1), `src/ui/settings-screen.ts` (the Keys tab), `src/ui/index.ts` (the legend redraws from the player's keys; any pause key pauses, Esc always), `src/app` (the pad's pause press opens the pause screen). Tests: `src/input/bindings.test.ts` lists every action and asserts a default binding on the keyboard and the pad; `tests/sim/uturn-devices.test.ts` rides the U-turn button (and remapped ones) through the riding model, and fails if the button does not straighten the bars for its tap; `tests/e2e/ui-keys-remap.spec.ts` is the layout check for the Keys tab at 915x412 and 1280x720 plus the remap flow. Browser specs were not run on the dev machine (CI runs them). Not phone-verified. The pad's pause button only pauses; resuming from the pad is not built.

Keeper fix: the new U-turn row put the open keyboard legend and the first "cut this" row 2 px past the bottom of a 915x412 touch phone (CI: 414.6 against 412.5). On a short landscape screen the legend's lines are now a little tighter (line height 1.15, was 1.3), which wins back about 2 px a line; the font size is unchanged.
Keeper fix 2: the build-stamp check (`tests/e2e/ui-style-popups.spec.ts`) pressed every settings tab, including the Keys tab this change hides on a touch-only phone, and timed out on it; it now presses the tabs the device shows.
