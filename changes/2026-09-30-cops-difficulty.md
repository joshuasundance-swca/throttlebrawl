Sgt. Pruitt now follows the difficulty. On Easy he stays in his lot in about half your races, and when he does come out it takes him 40 seconds. On Hard he always comes out, after about 13 seconds. Normal is as before: he always comes out, after 20 seconds. His siren now starts 3 seconds before he pulls out, so you always hear him coming, and he can't bust anyone until he is actually on the road. Knock him off his bike and he can't bust you until he is back on it.
