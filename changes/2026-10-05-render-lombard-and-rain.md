Three looks fixes from the last live check. Lombard's crooked block now draws in red brick, with a light mortar joint across the lane every metre, in all four looks; before, it drew as grey asphalt. Its garden verge is now a low clipped hedge that follows each hairpin from one end to the other, where it used to be big green fern clumps that piled up inside every bend and stood up as X-shaped frames. When a rider or a rival clips the hedge, a few small flat leaves tumble off instead of green blocks. And the Pacific Northwest's drizzle no longer falls inside the car ferry: it thins out as the camera goes under the passenger deck and comes back at the far end.

For devs: `src/render/road-mesh.ts` (a new `brick` material kind and `road-brickCourse` joints for any road with `surface: "brick"`, except a `boardwalk`), `src/render/look.ts` and `looks/recipes.ts` (the brick colour per look), `src/render/verge.ts` (hedge panels where a `gardens` road ends in `brush`, `verge-leaves`, and the ground band sampled at the road's own stations so its edges turn as the road does), `src/render/roofs.ts` (new: where a roof stands over the road, and whether the camera is under one) with `rain.ts` and `index.ts`, and the ferry's builder in `pnw-places.ts` now takes its roof numbers from `roofs.ts`. `docs/content-packs.md` (Surface) is updated. Not phone-verified: the checks are headless (what lies under a point on the crooked block, panel position and height, the band's turning angle, the rain count under the ferry's deck); the look of the brick, the hedge and the leaves in the four looks wants a glance on the phone.
