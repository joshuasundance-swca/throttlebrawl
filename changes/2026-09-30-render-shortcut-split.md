The boat-ramp shortcut no longer flickers where it peels off the main road. The stretch of road where you choose the shortcut is now painted in the shortcut's colour, with arrows and a solid white line on its inside edge: be to the right of that line when the road splits and you take the shortcut. Past the split the same line keeps going, so the brown surface starts exactly where the game sends you onto it. Roadside posts no longer stand in the other road's lane where the two overlap.
