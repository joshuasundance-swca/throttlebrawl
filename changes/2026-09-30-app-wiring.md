Several parts of the game that were built but not switched on now are. The chase camera looks ahead along the road instead of straight down the bike's nose, leans the framing toward whoever you're about to hit, and shakes on hits, crashes and landings. You now hear the other riders' engines, the cop's siren, car and truck horns, and a sound for every punch, kick, miss and crash (before this only your own engine and the music played). A rider who gets hit wobbles, and a kick shows the kicking leg. The tuning panel now holds every slider, including the touch thresholds and how often rivals talk; the frame-rate cap really caps the frame rate; and "Save preset" keeps your tuning on the phone. If you switch apps or lock the screen mid-race, you come back to the pause screen instead of straight into the race.
