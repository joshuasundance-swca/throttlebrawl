The Pacific Northwest and San Francisco have radio stations of their own, written in code like the Keys' ones. Drizzle 89.3 FM plays slower, darker surf for wet two-lanes: Two-Hour Ferry Wait, Moss on the Mirror, Log Truck in the Fog and Holding Lot Stomp. Fog Bank 1550 AM plays rockabilly for steep blocks and crests: Exact Change Boogie, Crest and Drop (at 202 bpm, for the hill jumps), Gripman's Shuffle and Pre-Revenue Bop. In those regions R tunes the local station first, then the Keys' surf and rockabilly stations further down the dial, then off; in the Keys nothing changes. "Cut this" on a song works the same everywhere.
