Hits now jolt the camera: when you take a punch or a kick, the view gets knocked away from the rider who hit you and springs back, and your own hits give a smaller recoil. Two more camera moves are built and switch on as the rest of the game connects to them. Holding look-back shows the road behind you. A takedown gets a short, wider shot that keeps the rider you knocked down in view through the slow motion, then eases back to the normal view. The reduce-screen-shake setting will turn the shake and the jolt down, or off.
