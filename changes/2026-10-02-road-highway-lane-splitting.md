The first multi-lane highway: San Francisco's race now finishes on a freeway onto the bridge. Out of the fog a short on-ramp opens into two lanes each way, then three, full of shuttles, robotaxis and rideshares in every lane, both ways. Thread the gap between two cars side by side and it counts as a lane split: each car of the pair pays double near-miss cash. Cars whose lane ends merge over in time instead of piling up, and bikes at the kerb move in before the road narrows. If you ride a lane that ends (turn round on the freeway and head back), the edge eases you over instead of walling you.
