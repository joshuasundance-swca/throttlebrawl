Three real roads look more like their places. The Twin Peaks climb in San Francisco is open grass hill now, not a pine forest with ferns. The Golden Gate's toll plaza runs through the Presidio's Monterey cypress and blue gum eucalyptus instead of bare grass. Bridges are held up by what they really stand on: the Columbia River Highway's three bridges (Crown Point, Latourell and Shepperd's Dell) are concrete deck arches, and I-5's bridges and Upper Market's bridge stand on concrete pylons instead of timber trestles. The Pacific Northwest's own Timber Trestle and its ferry slip keep their timber.

For devs: a bridge's supports follow its own deck's tag, no longer whether the network has `forest` anywhere (the identity sheets' cause C5). `trestle` stands a deck on timber bents, `arch-bridge` gives it Codex CX5's deck arches from the Gorge kit (`src/render/bridge-bays.ts` `planArches`: a deck under 70 m takes the one 46 m span, a longer one 24 m bays end to end, centred; a 2.4 m concrete column carries each footing down to the ground where the deck is higher than the pier is deep), and any other deck keeps the plain pylons. The arch nodes load as the scenery model `gorgeArches` through the landmark kit's own decoder, since the asset manifest hands every caller of a file the first caller's decoded value (`src/render/models.ts` `KIT_MODELS`). A new land tag and theme, `presidio`, scatters the new `coastTree` kind (CX5's `models/scenery/sf-identity`), with a soft grass verge and no poles (`src/render/scenery.ts`, `src/road/cross-section.ts`). The tags live in the bake configs and were re-baked through `tools/gis` (`osm-sf-twin-peaks`, `osm-pnw-gorge` with `deckTags`, `osm-sf-golden-gate`); a control bake of all three from a fresh extract first matched the committed files exactly apart from `provenance`, so the re-baked files differ from main only in their tags and their provenance (new extract dates and hashes). `pnw-c1`'s decks are tagged `trestle` in its track source. The bake lint's closed tag list gains `presidio`, `arch-bridge` and `trestle`. Docs: `docs/content-packs.md`, the scenery tags.
