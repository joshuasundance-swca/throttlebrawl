The first race you can play. Tap to start, pick Race, and ride a crude box bike down a short Keys-flavoured coastal road against one box rival, Deacon Vane, to see your place at the finish. Keys: W or Up to ride, S or Down to brake, A and D to steer. On a phone, drag up with your left thumb to ride and sideways to steer. Everything is boxes on purpose; the real track, traffic, fights, crashes and cops come next.
