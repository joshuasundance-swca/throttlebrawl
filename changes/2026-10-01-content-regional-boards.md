Each region gets its own new road signs and billboards: hurricane routes, the Conch Republic and charter boats in the Keys; espresso merge rules, full trailheads and smug rain gear in the Pacific Northwest; robotaxi zones and invented AI startups in San Francisco. They fill the sign and billboard slots that pick from a region's pool, and each can be cut with "cut this" like any other board.
