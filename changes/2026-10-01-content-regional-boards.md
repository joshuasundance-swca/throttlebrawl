Each region gets its own new road signs and billboards beside its hand-made road: hurricane routes, the Conch Republic and charter boats in the Keys; espresso merge rules, full trailheads and smug rain gear in the Pacific Northwest; robotaxi zones and invented AI startups in San Francisco. Every one has its own spot on the road (the two older Keys signs that never showed now do too), and each can be cut with "cut this" like any other board.
