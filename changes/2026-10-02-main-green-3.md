Main fix-forward (main went red after #339 and #303). Three checks, one PR:

- **Draw calls at road events** (`road-events.spec.ts`: 122 in the Pacific Northwest, 129 in San Francisco, budget 120). The game regressed, so the game is fixed. A one-frame breakdown at the San Francisco crash scene found draw calls spent on things a phone cannot show: roadside weapons 350 to 670 m away (two draw calls each, a pixel or less; #303 put one about every 500 m), a weapon in a far rival's fist, and billboards and signs past the scenery draw distance (two draw calls each, standing where the houses round them were already hidden). Now a weapon, on the road or in a fist, is drawn only within 200 m of the camera, and a board only within `render.sceneryDrawM`, like the scenery. On the dev machine, at the same moment of the same seeded race, San Francisco's crash scene went from 123 draw calls to 110 and the Pacific Northwest's speed trap from 102 to 96. The budget is unchanged.
- **The first-load JavaScript** (500.4 KB gzip, budget 500 KB): #341's fix, carried here because each PR's gate needed the other's fix. The packs' `meta.notes` stay out of the bundled pack JSON (see `2026-10-02-pack-notes-out.md`); about 485 KB.
- **cops-2's patrol arrivals** (`cops-difficulty.test.ts`: "expected 12 to be greater than 12"). The game did not regress, so the test's assumption is fixed. The patrol cop's siren still leads his pull-out by 5.3 s or more in every race (the bar is 3 s). What fell is how many of the 24 races see him ride up within 20 m of the bot in 100 s: 14 at the last green main, 13 after #331 (each rival takes hits his own way) and 12 after #303 (bisected over every merge in between). No cop behaviour changed in those merges (#330 only added the heat meter's state and snapshot); every race's siren is at the same second and every pull-out within 0.1 s of where it was. The two races that flipped turn on whether the bot crashed near him, or whether he got within 20 m before his 40 s chase ran out. The old floor (more than half the races) sat one above the measured rate. Now the lead is checked on every patrol cop's pull-out (all 24 races), and the arrivals are a sample floor of a third of the races (8). Meeting a cop in view in every race stays `cops-patrol.test.ts`'s check.
