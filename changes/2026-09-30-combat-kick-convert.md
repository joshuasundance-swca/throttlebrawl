A swipe down on the attack button now turns into a kick even when the swipe takes a moment: the game now listens for the kick for a quarter of a second after the press, instead of only during the punch's short wind-up, and the kick still lands as soon as a straight kick would. If the punch already connected, the kick follows it. The screen jolt and phone buzz on a hit now scale with how hard the hit was. The swipe itself gets its longer window from the controls side; the grace time is a slider in the tuning panel's combat group.
