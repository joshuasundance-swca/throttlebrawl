Coming down at the very end of a truck's roof no longer leaves you hanging in the air. If the middle of your bike is over the roof you land on it; if it is past the edge you fall off, at the speed that fall gives. Before, you could hang at the tail of a truck for up to 2 seconds and then hit the road far too hard (a crash from a 3 m drop). Riding off the front, back or side of a truck you now drop at once. A rider who comes down just behind a truck while catching it bumps its tail, the same as on the road. And braking right after a big landing on a truck now keeps you on it: the landing's surge no longer beats the brake, so you can ride the truck instead of being pushed off its front. Not phone-verified.

For devs: the support and barrier run's live check (2026-10-07), under the maintainer's rule of 2026-10-06 (`[decided]`: one consistent physics for everything a rider meets). One rule decides whether a rider coming down at a roof that holds the bike is on it: the riders' own, his middle over it (`overVehicleTop`, new in `src/sim/riders/supports.ts`). Traffic's `contacts()` (`src/sim/traffic/index.ts`) used to hold any overlapping rider at the top (`holdAtTop`, keeping his fall speed) while the riders landed only a rider whose middle was over it, so a rider between the two hung there while gravity built. Now traffic holds a rider only when his middle is over the top; with his middle past the edge, moving into the vehicle he meets its end below its top by the closing speed (end on or side by the shorter overlap), and level with it or falling behind he slides clear of it the short way with no contact. A rider who leaves a vehicle's top is clear of it until they are apart (`clearOfVehicleTop`, called from the riders' `toWorldFrame`; traffic's existing `launchedOff`, as after a hood launch). On a top a held brake holds back a boost's push by the brake's share (`stepGrounded`'s support branch), so the surge no longer outruns the brake. Tests in `src/sim/riders/supports.test.ts`, each but the control run failing first on the code before this change: 70 come-downs at a box truck's and a semi's tail (the live check's 5 speeds × 7 offsets per truck: the longest hold went from 141 ticks to 0, the hardest landing from 20.3 m/s over a fall's speed to 0.14); riding off a semi's front (a 10-tick hold before, none now, no contact with the semi); braking from a surge landing on the semi (carried 0, 0.43 and 1.78 m at 0, 3 and 6 m/s over it, against 0, 0.42 and 1.72 m with no surge; 1.77 m at level before) and on a box truck (rode it 59 ticks before, 177 now); and the control that the surge still pushes off the brake. Races where nobody comes down at a vehicle's edge, rides off one or brakes during a boost on a top hash as before.
