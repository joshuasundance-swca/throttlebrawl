The turn-offs and rejoins of the real-road shortcuts are smoother to take. The Key West Boulevard, Bridge City's Morrison Bridge, Lake Samish and Russian Hill's Jones Street shortcuts now leave and rejoin the road in wide, gentle curves a bike at full speed can follow, instead of corners it ran wide of, and the San Francisco park cut has a looser entrance too. At the places where a shortcut met a real road, the last two metres of the turn-off were missing, and you could see the ground (or the water) through a wedge-shaped hole in the road; they are filled in. Jones Street no longer dips under the edge of Union Street before it comes out, and a thin gap along the edge where a shortcut's paving meets the main road is closed.

For devs: the road lint has a new `shortcut` rule (`src/road/validate.ts`, the riding audit's F8b, `[default]`): the connector, plus the first 80 m of the road a turn-off leads onto, holds a 30 m/s arrival (radius 42.5 m or more); a split zone starts at least 1.5 m inside the rider's reach; and where a connector's lanes lie over a main road's surface, at the centre and both lane edges, they stand within 0.15 m of its height. The bake takes a branch's `minRadiusM` (`tools/gis`, tried in small steps, refused with the radius it reached), raises `ROAD_LINT.junctionRadiusM` to 250 m for the longer connectors, and `curve_piece` makes a connector follow the main road's height while a lane is over its surface and keep the branch road's height at the end that joins it. Five baked networks were re-baked; only the `-in` and `-out` connector roads changed (plus the earlier wide-turn data of the same branches). `src/render/junction-sweep.test.ts` is the sweep for visible junction artifacts: it reads the built scene's ground faces at every junction of every network for holes, steps and floats, and flickering pairs, with controls showing each check can find what it looks for. Its first run found 601 faults; the causes were the pinched end row of a shortcut that meets a road head on (`road-mesh.ts` clipped it against the road it joins at the one point where they meet), a connector hidden under the verge of a steep road, and slivers a few centimetres wide at the clip edge (now refined and laid 10 cm over the edge). `tests/sim/camera-crests.test.ts` raises the Jones Street blind-crest cap from 120 to 160: the crest moved from the rejoin to the leave connector (135 and 145 hidden frames at the two aspects, 0 on the rejoin), with the reason in its comment. The audit's other flagged shortcuts (the Seven Mile's run-up and zone) were fixed in PR 516. Not phone-verified.
