The Pacific Northwest roads are no longer bare grass between the asphalt and the trees. Sword ferns and salal crowd the verge, mossy stumps and rocks sit in the woods, split-rail and log fences run along the road, and mailboxes stand by stacks of firewood. Bigleaf maples and red alders grow among the firs, drive-through espresso huts turn up now and then, and a yellow warning sign with a big-footed silhouette has gone dark in the rain. The props are laid out by the race's seed, stand only on land, and thin out with distance, so the frame costs a few extra draws.
