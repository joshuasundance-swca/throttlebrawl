San Francisco has Chinatown and North Beach. Pick **Chinatown & North Beach** in the San Francisco route picker: a narrow street of steep blocks under strings of lanterns, with awnings, balconies and a crest lip at every cross street to catch air off, then a street of cafes with striped awnings and patios, a hard right-hand elbow to brake for, and a climb through the park to a finish under the tower on the hill. Nothing smashes in Chinatown; in North Beach the pavement cafe tables do ("TABLE FOR NONE"). New deadpan signs and two billboards go with it, aimed at robotaxis, shuttles and the startups in the cafes; long-press any to cut it. In the career, Gripman Gus's grudge match ("Hold On to Something") now runs these streets.
