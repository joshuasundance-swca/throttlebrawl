Iowa is now the career's fourth chapter, the County Line Circuit. It opens once Pivot falls in San Francisco, and San Francisco's ending now points there. It has four tiers named for the farm year: Planting, Knee High, Harvest and First Frost. Across them are twelve races on IA 136 and Eagle Point Park: races to the line, takedown hunts, chases by the county patrol, and grudges against Chad Speedwell (a "content farm" collab scored on style), Dial-Up (No Bars, his dropped-signal trick on the highway), and Deacon Vane (a tent revival on the Serpentine). The finale is Benchmark: Mara Vale, the county road surveyor, on the whole of IA 136 at dusk. The chapter has its own paints (Barn Red, Seed Corn Yellow, River Mud, Grain Elevator Grey, and Blue Ribbon from Season 2), its own paper, the Co-op Bulletin, and three secrets: the county detour, a coffee can of seed money, and a pirate funk station broadcasting from somebody's pole barn. Every bike is already on sale by then, so the shop sells paints only.

Longview joins the Pacific Northwest chapter. Port Shift, a chase along Longview's port roads, replaces Fir County Pursuit in tier 3. Master Plan, a race through the planned civic streets, replaces Rainout in tier 4. Each keeps the pay and difficulty of the race it replaced. The two replaced races were on the invented sawmill road, which keeps its other two.

Quick Play in Iowa now also has a hunt (Scenic Overlook) and an escape (County Line).

For developers: Iowa's chapter sells no step-up bike and its boss grants no joke ride, and the ladder and content tests now allow both for a chapter after the last step-up bike goes on sale. Two Iowa encounter tests now expect Iowa's career grudges after the Survey Run in Quick Play. The economy model's "not a grind" season band is now per chapter: 20 to 33⅓ races a chapter, which is the old 60 to 100 over three chapters. A typical season goes from 92 races to 113.5, about 28 a chapter. The bike-saving waits are unchanged. An asset brief for Longview's squirrel bridge and Iowa's joke ride (a pedal tractor) is in `docs/milestones/longview-iowa-assets.md`. The cop parking check holds an escape's lead cop, who starts behind the player in a one-lane road's only lane (the North Loop, through the route picker), to pulling out within 4 s. The new tests are `career-iowa-chapter.test.ts` and `career-headless-iowa.test.ts`. The headless career depends on the test bot turning round when it faces away from the finish (#853), and on cops no longer crawling ahead on a road with no shoulder (#857).
