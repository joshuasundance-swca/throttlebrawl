The geometry invariant sweeps now run in the per-PR gate (sim tier) on every network a route in any region pack races on: 14 networks from 18 route files, read from the packs, not from a hand-written list. They check that nothing stands over water (scenery, roadside props, every place a pedestrian can reach in a route's roadside zones), that every scenery spot and roadside prop stands on the drawn land at its height (24 seeds, split over two files), that no land ends in mid-air or opens at a junction and the land strip meets its slope with no gap of any width, and that every backdrop vertex is checked: standing pieces keep 150 m from the roads, and floors lie under the sea as the vertex shader draws them (the backdrop unit test's "floors may pass beneath" exemption is gone; its road check moved here). Each sweep has a negative control that breaks its input and shows it fires. Main had two kinds of violation: verge grass standing in I-5's painted Lake Samish split zone (fixed: roadside props now keep out of split zones) and three junction land gaps (two at Russian Hill's Jones Street choice, one at the Park Cut turn-off), kept on a known list that can only shrink.
