Rivals now react to what just happened. Each of the four regulars has new lines for scraping past a car, crashing on their own, being knocked off their bike by you, and knocking someone into traffic, and a rival you knock down twice in one race gets a sharper line the second time. A line that fits the moment is now picked ahead of a generic one. The first piece of "cut this" is in too: on a computer, hold a speech bubble with the mouse for half a second and choose "Cut this", and that line is gone for the rest of your visit. Follow-up changes bring the rest: the same hold with your thumb on the phone (it stays off until the game knows where your steering and attack thumbs are, so it can never steal a steer), a "recently seen" list on the pause screen where two taps cut a line, cuts that are remembered after a reload, and cuts listed in the copied debug report.
