Bridge City now looks like downtown Portland. Broadway and the streets round it are lined with brick lofts, cast-iron fronts, office blocks and the odd tall pink tower, with a cross street every block and bike racks on the sidewalk, where there used to be open ground, bait shacks and palm trees. Pioneer Courthouse Square is a small brick square under a rain cloud. Just past the Burnside Bridge a neon salmon leaps over a roof, above a sign that reads STILL RAINING. The food-cart pod on Broadway is a row of carts now, each with a name board: THE ONE WITH THE LINE, DUMPLINGS, MOSTLY DRY and SOUP FOR THE DAMP. The roof sign and the carts' names are new, and you can cut any of them like any other sign: long-press the board in the game, or pick it from the recently seen list, and its board stays blank.

For devs (playtest 3, wave C, T12.6; Codex batch CX4's kits; wave B's punch list, item 3).

- **The ground.** `pdx-blocks` is a land theme of its own (`blocks`, `scenery.ts`): the `town` tag beside it no longer scatters palms and bait shacks (`commercial`), and no pole stands there. `src/render/portland-blocks.test.ts` builds Bridge City and finds none of the four; the old tag alone draws them.
- **The blocks** (`downtown.ts` `planPortland`, drawn by the same `DowntownLayer` as San Francisco's, one mesh per stretch with the Pacific Northwest atlas as its map). A front row of cast-iron fronts, brick lofts, office blocks and pink towers (a 7 m base, whole 14 m mids and a crown at scale 1) on every `pdx-blocks` side, a cross street's asphalt every 61 m, bike racks, and paving. Each building is placed from its model's bounding box on land the road scene drew (`landReach`), past a 3.4 m sidewalk, its back corners at most 5 m past the land, and its base sinks 0.3 m so a facade's picture is not stretched. It keeps clear of every zone, sign, pad, cop lot and landmark the road names (it moves back behind one that only crosses its front, or gives its lot up), of every road (the inside of a bend, a junction's other road) and of every other building. The tests hold each of those, and each was seen to fail with its rule taken out (the theme, the land check, the pod lot, the building overlap and the road clearance). Broadway's fronts are built along 53 to 79 % of each side (the rest is the pod, the square, cross streets and lots).
- **The cart pod.** A pedestrian zone with `params.dressing: "food-carts"` (the `cart-pod` zone on Broadway, edited in the bake config and the baked road) is a lot with no building, where seven carts, three models in turn, stand behind its diners, serving side to the road.
- **The roof sign and the square** are two `landmark` features, written by hand into `tools/gis/networks/osm-pnw-portland.json` and the baked roads as T10.5's dressing was (no OSM extract in the repo): `roof-sign` on West Burnside, on the right as the real sign stands, scale 0.8, turned so its board faces a rider coming off the bridge; `square-plaza` on Broadway South, over the courthouse-square zone, scale 0.45 so it stands on the land the road draws. The Hawthorne lift towers needed no rename: T9.4's `pdx-landmarks#pdx_lift_tower` is the node CX4 built, and a test lists all four Portland nodes against the kit.
- **Text surfaces** (`text-surfaces.ts`, a lazy chunk; `docs/content-packs.md`, Text surfaces). A model's blank board (a node with the extras `text_surface`, `width_m`, `height_m`) stays in the model as its blank panel, and the game paints the words of the region sign named for the node (`pdx_roof_sign_words` shows `pdx-roof-sign-words`), in one canvas on one quad mesh for the whole road scene: one draw call, within 420 m. The words are pack text in `region.json`: `STILL RAINING` and the three cart names, tagged `site` (no pooled slot can pick them), `surface` (words for a board, not a board, so `boards.test.ts` does not ask for a kicker) and `new`. `status` has no `new` value, so the tag and the note's `NEW` are the mark. The renderer's `visibleContent`, `pickContentAt` and `hideContent` carry the surfaces beside the boards, so the veto's list, its long-press and a cut all work on them; a cut leaves the blank panel, never a hole.
- **Cost** (`scene-cost.test.ts`, which now puts the landmarks and the words in the frame and gives the downtown one build a frame for a ride's worth of frames). The busiest view on Bridge City anywhere, a junction connector (`osm-pnw-portland-morrison-in` at s 35, 12 of its draws downtown stretches): 70 draw calls and 64,467 triangles; the main path alone 62 and 59,363. The still scene's share is 80 and 110,000, the frame's caps 120 and 150,000; before this change, with the landmarks and words not counted, the busiest view drew 62 and 59,978. San Francisco's downtown route is unchanged (50 and 58,898).
- **Tests** (new: `portland-blocks.test.ts`, `text-surfaces.test.ts`; changed: `scenery.test.ts` the kit's ten variants, `scene-cost.test.ts`, `boards.test.ts` (a `surface` sign is no board), `app/regions.test.ts` (the surface signs are items, in no pool, and cuttable), `tools/road/board-slots-on-land.test.ts` (a `surface` sign needs a model that paints it, not a road slot), `tools/gis/region-routes.test.ts` (city blocks scatter nothing, as the Marin hills' grass does), `tools/gis/routes-pnw-dressing.test.ts` (the landmarks the bake places from a lat/lon are its list; the roof sign and the square are road features), `scripts/first-load.test.ts` (the two lazy modules)). The per-PR geometry, land and reachability checks on the Pacific Northwest's networks stay green with their known lists empty.
- **Not done.** `pdx_bascule_pier` (the Burnside operator towers) and the St. Johns towers are not placed: the bake config holds no position for either, and the St. Johns Bridge is not on the route. Street trees are not drawn. The Keys' shop-name panels and San Francisco's signs can use the same mechanism by adding a region sign named for the node, but Old Town's merged roadside layer does not list its surfaces yet. The renderer wiring in `render/index.ts` (the layer's build, the veto's three calls) has no unit test: it needs WebGL, so the browser tiers see it. Not phone-verified, and no browser was run.
