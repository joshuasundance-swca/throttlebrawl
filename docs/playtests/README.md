# Playtests: the maintainer's feedback and decisions

> **In plain words.** The maintainer plays the live game on their phone and says what they think in chat. Agents then ask follow-up questions in short rounds. Each page here keeps the maintainer's words as they were written, the answers to the follow-up rounds, and where each decision now lives in the design docs. The design docs carry the decisions; these pages are the source they cite.

Tags mean what they mean in [the product spec](../product-spec.md#how-to-read-this-spec). Every answer quoted here is `[decided]` on the date given, unless it says it is a coordinator default (`[default]`). A later playtest can supersede an earlier answer; the page then says so.

## Decision rounds between playtests

Two rounds of answers came without a playtest. They are carried in the design docs:

- **2026-10-01** ("the maintainer's answers"): build out the three regions instead of adding new ones ("polish and build out the regions we have now (more roads, GIS-based network maybe, better visuals and experience, stuff like that)"); the four staged real roads become routes ("Yes, add as routes"); Ink + 60s film is the default look ("ink+60s but may change later based on improvements, opinions, etc"); voices go in, from open models only, never cloning a real person, one consistent voice per rival, with a Voices volume, an off switch and "cut this" for voice lines; the code-made music stays ("I actually like the music lol"); race types and new regions stay held. See [the roadmap](../roadmap.md#regions-alongside-the-milestones) and [the product spec's settings](../product-spec.md#settings).
- **2026-10-01b** ("the worlds just feel very empty"): the maintainer picked every missing piece: roadside density close to the road, traffic and people, distance and skyline, events and set pieces, and "better models, textures, etc etc", plus "I want unique regional flavor everywhere, like NW tree species, SF AI stuff, etc". See [the tone guide's taste log](../tone-guide.md#taste-log) and [the content-pack doc](../content-packs.md).
