Maintain project standards etc but be cautious about hard-coded limits in tests, testing implementation details, things like that. Let me know if anything if you have questions. Thanks :)

Actual source geometry may change which alignment comes first. Subsequent park routes, bridges and additional courses remain opportunities rather than prerequisites.

## Riding feedback and road choices

Coordinator summary of the maintainer's feedback: Eagle Point's narrow, winding road was too unforgiving to finish, even on the Rental Moped. Crossing into roadside grass produced an out-of-bounds reset with little recovery space and no protective rail. The initial San Francisco grass/hill concern was subsequently clarified:

> actually to be clear I am able to navigate the grassy hilly sf stuff at speed if I'm very careful. the eagle point stuff is way way harder because of how narrow and windy it is. the sf stuff isn't nearly as bad.

The maintainer clarified the intended edge behavior:

> I think the main thing is making sure there are rails where appropriate but not necessarily everywhere or just by default. they should be contextually fitting and sometimes there really should be opportunities to go out of bounds. but right now it's just two different shades of grass lol it shouldn't make you crash.

`[decided]` A change in the shade of continuous roadside grass must not cause a crash. Rails belong at meaningful, contextually fitting hazards, with genuine opportunities to leave the course. Preserve San Francisco's navigable high-speed challenge; Eagle Point is the urgent playability problem.

Coordinator summary of the maintainer's location direction: show Iowa as the region, with Clinton-area routes as choices within it. Longview's triangular and diamond street patterns should create actual route choices. The original Clinton highway needs more interest, including regionally appropriate real-feature side roads. These are future playable-road opportunities, not claims that the current courses already offer them.

`[default]` The immediate edge repair first opts in the existing connected land strip beyond a soft rural grass verge, with grass grip and speed penalties. Other scenery cohorts retain their existing limits. Rural render-only trees and staged scenes move beyond the playable strip onto existing supported flat ground; sparse copses and regional vignettes remain where full footprints fit, with no invented platforms. Counts and positions may change. It retains Eagle Point's lane width, centreline, turns and grades. It adds no blanket rails. Riding terrain skirts, general hillside height-field riding, broader scenery contacts and new regional branches remain separate work; this repair does not claim to finish them or to be phone-verified.
