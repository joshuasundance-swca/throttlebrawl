## Evidence and guardrails

The interview reports voluntary repeated play and enjoyment of aggressive racing, drift exits, launches, underdog wins, geography and earned region unlocks. Reported friction includes takedown cutaways hiding hazards, hunts/escapes that require waiting for participants, limited slow-bike competition, and crowded menus/results. These are player observations; causes and fixes require verification.

Preserve viable clean racing in ordinary events and combat requirements only in explicitly combat-centered events. Preserve creative physical discoveries and fair crash consequences. Free roam, new objective kinds, persistent police heat, multiplayer, paid generation, naming and hosting changes are not authorized by this round.

The interview requests a holistic menu review including main, Quick Play, pause and results. Phone rendering/readability, expressive voices, distinct bosses/bikes and further geographic experiments remain valuable follow-up work rather than automatic additions to this batch.
