# Road development and testing clarification, 2026-10-20

This is development feedback, not phone verification of the current changes. No build or device
was identified for these clarifications.

> I only mentioned it to emphasize that even slow on that particular road was very difficult. I didn't mean it should be a standard or anything like that lol.

`[decided]` The Rental Moped example emphasized Eagle Point's difficulty; it did not establish a
standard QA bike. `[default]` Choose vehicles and speeds that exercise the behavior being changed.
For road recovery, include controlled excursions on ordinary and faster racing bikes, retain real
out-of-bounds controls, and distinguish a false ground reset from a genuine landing crash. A bot
or geometry check does not establish human or phone playability.

> btw it really seems like we could be getting much more done if we had agents working in parallel.

> well I wasn't saying you should park the CI optimization :/ why can't that be done as well? that seems like important enablement work

`[decided]` Continue useful parallel game work and CI optimization together. The existing
[delivery rules](../engineering.md#parallel-agent-lanes) still apply: independent file ownership,
separate worktrees, one integration owner and coordinated local browser/build use. This does not
authorize bypassing acceptance or publishing an unverified integration.

On mid-air rotation, the maintainer asked what combinations of backflip, frontflip, spin-only and
combined spins/flips would remain possible, and said:

> I would be okay with postponing this feature for right now until we're sure how we want it implemented.

`[default]` Postpone spin implementation while those combinations, controls, collision orientation
and landing behavior are designed. Assisted full turns were a proposal, not an approved control
scheme. Existing air controls are not changed by this batch.

## Regional content priority

> those all sound good. I'm most excited about fleshing out the content we've added recently and continuing to add more. the game was fun, then we added longview and iowa, but those are not as good as the others that we already had, and I'm eager to get more, with everything the very best it can be.

`[decided]` Lead the next batch with richer Longview and Iowa content and continue purposeful
content expansion. The maintainer finds those additions less compelling than the older regions;
earlier automated acceptance does not establish comparable fun or completeness. This feedback
does not identify a particular missing feature or a new benchmark device.

`[default]` Give the locations independent owners, compare their actual roads, world and
encounters with the stronger existing regions, and deliver bounded playable improvements.
Judge route choices by what makes them worth riding, with regional identity, discoveries and
readable encounters rather than decoration count. Keep CI enablement alongside content work.
The replay-viewer prototype follows this content push; spin implementation remains postponed.
Existing restrictions on new event kinds, paid generation and public hosting changes still apply.

In the follow-up about regional character and life versus riding choices and encounters, the maintainer answered:

> Both matter about equally

`[decided]` Give regional character and life, and riding choices and encounters, about equal priority.
`[default]` Pair local
places and life with usable riding spaces, discoveries or encounters, while preserving the
ordinary route. This answer sets priorities; it does not phone-verify a new feature or establish
that Longview and Iowa have reached the older regions' quality.

## Overall goal

> excellent, thank you. and just remember above all else we want the game to be consistently fun :)

`[decided]` Consistent fun is the overriding goal. `[default]` Evaluate content and technical
guardrails by whether they help deliver interesting riding choices, readable hazards, fair recovery,
variety and responsive play. Automated acceptance supports that goal; human play remains necessary
to judge the experience.
