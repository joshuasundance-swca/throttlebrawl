# Playtest 2 and its interview (2026-10-02)

> **In plain words.** The maintainer played every road on the phone and found the game smooth but "primitive and monotonous at times". Seven short interview rounds the same day turned that into decisions: the career goes ahead, built on each region's road network; you can ride off the road; cops are met in every race; rivals get real models and visible personalities; the radio stays code-made.

Cite the feedback as "playtest 2, 2026-10-02" and the answers as "interview, 2026-10-02". Every answer is `[decided]`; the bracketed readings after an answer are what the maintainer was shown and chose.

## The maintainer's words

"It all looks and runs smoothly. I've played it extensively across all roads. I have various bits of feedback. Some about visuals, some about the experience.
Feel free to proceed to career.
Mostly it just still feels primitive and monotonous at times. Maybe not enough variety. Plus it feels too linear with no freedom. The edge horizons have nothing interesting to encourage immersion of any kind, and the barriers are too obvious. It would be nice to be able to go off road. It's too easy to crash after a jump and sometimes it feels like it takes too long to get back to racing. The cable cars are just driving around including in forests which doesn't make sense. I expected some city feeling not just all row houses. Maybe islands in the Keys. The billboards render with their legs blocking text and you pass to fast to read them. There should be different stations and music in different regions. The riders and bikes are blocky and uninteresting. I think I've only ever encountered cops once even though I've played a lot. Its surprising how long it takes to knock people down. It would be nice if everything was more interesting, varied, polished, etc. I think i have other points I'll need to remember but please feel free to interview extensively, pitch ideas, etc etc. The game should always be fun never boring always creative and original always entertaining etc etc"

## What it settled at once

- **Phone-verified:** the Ink + 60s look "looks and runs smoothly" on every road. Keep it that way.
- **The career is un-held** ("Feel free to proceed to career"): build the career decided on 2026-09-29.
- **The bar:** "The game should always be fun never boring always creative and original always entertaining". Variety and surprise beat polish on one thing.
- **Defects, no taste call needed:** billboard legs block the text and billboards pass too fast to read; cable cars belong on San Francisco's cable lines only, never in forests; cops are met too rarely; knockdowns take too long; crashing after a jump is too easy; getting back to racing takes too long; each region needs its own stations and music.

## Interview answers

- **Round 1.** Freedom: "Off-road riding" + "Branching roads" + "This may suggest a map somewhere on the playscreen or pause but it seems like that could take up a lot of room. I love the idea of a road network from which races are drawn and which can be connected. This lends itself to future roaming etc and it makes for good progression, career, etc. I basically want to use all races in career probably. Open areas and roaming sound good but maybe later. Right now there's no way to turn around and go the other way as far as I know lol. Maybe we're less constrained than I currently feel." Crash flow: "Forgiving landings" + "Maybe just limit the time running to the bike. Sometimes it's far away and sometimes it's close. Maybe we shouldn't change respawn idk. What's normal? What's like road rash? I love the idea of doing flips." Knockdowns: "Kick is great because it makes them move but it should take a few hits even if they are kicks. Weapons should do more. But yeah shorter more frequent brawls (but still not usually one hit) and yeah different racers different stats might make sense (not totally sure)". Other: "Rivals feel samey" + "Steering or controls" + "HUD or menus" + "Sounds" + "Engine monotonous and maybe too loud idk. Kick timing requires the ability to choose kick direction as you ride up behind someone (directional swipe). Braking around tight corners was satisfying. Surprised no 4 lane highway. It may be nice to know how far back people are, maybe a rear view. The music is impressive lol. Riders could look more realistic? At least interesting and distinctive"
- **Round 2.** Run-back: "Road rash with variable penalty as it is currently until further discussion". Rider look: "Real proportions, loud costumes". San Francisco: all four districts (downtown towers, the waterfront and piers, Chinatown and North Beach, the Mission and its murals). The Keys: all four (distinct keys, beaches and sandbars, mangrove back roads, a secret island).
- **Round 3.** Awareness: "Unsure. Maybe just map on pause" [a network map on the pause screen; nothing permanent on the HUD]. Roads: all four [4-6 lane highways with lane splitting, junction choices in races, map-based networks, U-turns]. Engine: "Richer and quieter". Rivals: "Visible personalities" [moderate stat differences shown through how they ride and fight].
- **Round 4.** Career map: "Network map, tiered" [each region's road network is its career map; events on its roads; wins open nearby roads and the next tier, then the boss; every race type and route is in the career]. Cops: "Mix of 2 and 1 (reliable but rich)" [one or two cops on patrol in every race, plus a heat meter: chaos raises heat, which brings more cops and then a roadblock; lose them by riding clean or going off-road]. Off-road: "Anywhere with ground" [dirt, grass, sand and gravel with their own grip and speed; cliffs, buildings, deep water and fences stop you; some fences smash]. Between races: "Maybe all of the above in whatever mixes make sense and actually work well" [stream chat, rival texts and taunts, a local newspaper, side gigs].
- **Round 5.** Off-road: "2 and 1" [a ridable ground band beside most roads now, with water, ferns and kerbs as the real edges, and marked dirt shortcuts such as sandbars, fire roads and clear-cuts; open hillsides and real terrain later]. Barriers: "Both" [the invisible wall at the road edge goes where ground is drawn; rails only on bridges and drops; posts only on highways]. San Francisco first: "Downtown towers" [a four-lane avenue between invented AI-startup towers, cross traffic at intersections, cable cars on the steep cross streets only]. The Keys first: "Distinct keys" [a fishing village, a resort strip, a junkyard key and a party key].
- **Round 6.** Riders: "Real models now" [real 3D riders with limbs and flapping clothes, and real bike silhouettes per rival, with a loading path for big files]. Radio: "More code-made music only" [more stations plus a hidden pirate station per region; no DJ and no AI songs for now]. The career's personality: "The map" [claim roads, find secrets and shortcuts, a set-piece finale per region]. The streaming outfit: "A quiet frame" [between races, plus at most one producer ask during a race].
- **Round 7.** The asset repo: "I'm fine with it but what about offline play?" [the public dataset repo was created; the build copies the pinned files into the game, so offline play works the same]. Run-back: "Trim only the waiting" [the tumble hands back once nearly stopped, at most about 3.5 s; Skip is never slower than running; remount rolling]. Busts: "I kinda think it should only be a bust if the cop knocks you down (not just proximity and timing). Maybe I'm wrong about that" [a bust only when a cop's hit knocks you off; revisit after a playtest]. The Rerun (your last attempt rides in the field as a ghost rival you can punch): "Later".
