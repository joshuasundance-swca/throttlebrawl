rider `region-sf:pivot` and Pivot signature. Ordinary Pivot AI remains unchanged. First readiness
240 and tell48 ticks; act45/open120; gap540/spread360. First grace and phase durations are unscaled.
Initial readiness adds floor(roll * scaled spread); completion adds scaled gap plus
floor(roll * (scaled spread + 1)); interruption keeps max(existing next, tick + scaled gap) without
drawing again. Capture an opening ceiling below actual speed; ordinary throttle/brake causes sag.
Tell and recovery allow no new swings. Retain busy suppression and navigation/avoidance priority.

Every Pivot rig uses nondirectional preparation because snapshots do not expose the committed
line or encounter identity. Existing combat poses take precedence throughout windup, active and
recovery; the preparation returns afterward. No duration fingerprint, event field, new public
contract, content, asset, dependency, budget, physics change or forced acquisition.
